How to read this page

  • LiveRuns on a schedule and was confirmed by a log or a recent run at the last review.
  • In progressBeing built or worked. The card says where it stands.
  • PausedNot started, or stopped for the reason on the card.
  • CompleteFinished and moved to the archive.

Cards marked hand-tracked are projects a person moves forward. Their status comes from the last recorded step, not from a log. The scheduled work is AI-assisted and a caveat is written on the card wherever a person still has to step in.
