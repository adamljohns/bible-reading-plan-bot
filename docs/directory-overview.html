Methodology Note

Each church record carries a 10-dimension theological score (Christology, Scripture, Gender, Leadership, Soteriology, Cultural, Preaching, Mission, Men's Discipleship, Denominational), seven public-statement signatory cross-references (Warhurst Protest 2020, AMR 2026, Letter of Lament 2025, Revoice, Dallas Statement 2018, Nashville Statement 2017, CBE Egalitarian 2026), pastor and pulpit-transition tracking, and a personal-engagement record. Records are progressively enriched. Scoring is conservative-orthodox; cultural drift, female-senior-pastor SBC churches, and Revoice-adjacent congregations are flagged transparently with documented reasons.

Sources include denominational find-a-church locators (sbc.net, pcanet.org, opc.org, epc.org, arpchurch.org, urcna.org, etc.), individual church websites for pastor and theological-statement verification, and seven hand-curated public-statement signatory lists maintained alongside the directory. Where verification is incomplete, records are flagged needs_review rather than guessed.
