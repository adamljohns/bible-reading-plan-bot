Autonomy: what a BGAV label does and does not tell you

The BGAV holds no creed but the Bible. It affirmed the 1963 Baptist Faith and Message, not the 2000 revision, and leaves that choice to each church.

Whether a church ordains women, or calls a woman as pastor, is decided by that church, not by the BGAV. Gender and Leadership are graded YELLOW for that reason: check the church's own page.

The BGAV's published marriage and sexuality resolutions define marriage as one man and one woman. A church's own practice can still differ, and the church's own evidence decides its rating.
