Governance strength

autonomous Each church is autonomous and the body disclaims any authority over it; its confession binds no church. Cooperation or affiliation can still require agreement with that confession → AUTONOMOUS.

“A New Testament church of the Lord Jesus Christ is an autonomous local congregation of baptized believers” — bfm.sbc.net/bfm2000/
“While independent and sovereign in its own sphere, the Convention does not claim and will never attempt to exercise any authority over any other Baptist body” — www.sbc.net/about/what-we-do/legal-documentation/constitution/

Autonomy and cooperation

Every Southern Baptist church is autonomous. The Convention states it claims no authority over any church, so nothing it adopts binds a congregation.

Cooperation is a different matter. The SBC Constitution counts a church as cooperating only when its faith and practice closely identify with the Baptist Faith and Message, and it names affirming homosexual behavior as one example of a church that is not in cooperation.

The Baptist Faith and Message limits the office of pastor/elder/overseer to men. A given church's own practice still decides its own rating; check the church's page.

“A New Testament church of the Lord Jesus Christ is an autonomous local congregation of baptized believers” — bfm.sbc.net
“While independent and sovereign in its own sphere, the Convention does not claim and will never attempt to exercise any authority over any other Baptist body” — sbc.net
“(By way of example, churches which act to affirm, approve, or endorse homosexual behavior would be deemed not to be in cooperation with the Convention.)” — sbc.net
“While both men and women are gifted for service in the church, the office of pastor/elder/overseer is limited to men as qualified by Scripture.” — bfm.sbc.net
