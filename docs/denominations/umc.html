The 2024 General Conference changes

The UMC is connectional: the Constitution and the Book of Discipline bind every conference, and General Conference sets church law for all of them. Governance is graded BINDING.

In 2024 the General Conference removed the restrictions on non-heterosexual clergy and revised the Social Principles. Marriage is now defined as a covenant between a man and a woman or between two adult persons, effective in full from January 1, 2025.

The UMC has ordained women with full clergy rights since 1956. A church's own evidence decides its own rating.

“The Constitution is universally-binding to all United Methodist conferences (annual, jurisdiction and central) and entities worldwide.” — umc.org
“the 2024 General Conference removed all of those restrictions and replaced them with a series of affirmations and agreements about how all persons will be” — umc.org
