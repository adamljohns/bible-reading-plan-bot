Governance strength

autonomous Each church is autonomous and the body disclaims any authority over it; its confession binds no church. Cooperation or affiliation can still require agreement with that confession → AUTONOMOUS.

“While autonomous in its own affairs, Southern Baptist Conservatives of Virginia, recognizing the autonomy of other Baptist bodies, disclaims all right of exercising authority over any church, association, or convention.” — www.sbcv.org/wp-content/uploads/2021/06/sbcv_constitution_revised_2011__bylaws_revised_2010.pdf
“The doctrinal position of the Southern Baptist Conservatives of Virginia shall not be binding upon any local church; however, the SBCV recognizes its right and responsibility to determine its identity, including doctrinal parameters, and to include within its affiliation those churches who can freely agree with it” — www.sbcv.org/wp-content/uploads/2021/06/sbcv_constitution_revised_2011__bylaws_revised_2010.pdf

What SBCV affiliation means

The SBCV is a state convention of Southern Baptist churches in Virginia. Its constitution adds an explicit inerrancy clause to the Baptist Faith and Message 2000, which it adopts as its statement of fundamental truths.

Its doctrinal position binds no local church, but the SBCV sets the doctrinal parameters for affiliation and can rescind a church's affiliation. Churches stay autonomous; affiliation is the accountability.

Categories shown as unknown here are covered by the Baptist Faith and Message on the Southern Baptist Convention page; this page grades only what sbcv.org itself publishes.
