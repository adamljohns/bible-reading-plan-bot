Connectional, without a trust clause

The GMC is connectional: its General Conference legislates for the whole church and the Book of Doctrines and Discipline binds its conferences, clergy and congregations. Governance is graded BINDING.

Unlike the UMC, the GMC has no trust clause. Local churches hold their own property.

The GMC defines marriage as one man and one woman, and it ordains women. Gender and Leadership are graded RED on that line; a church's own evidence still decides its own rating.

“The General Conference shall have full legislative power over all matters that are distinctly connectional” — irp.cdn-website.com
“There is no trust clause for property held by local churches, annual conferences, connectional commissions, or any other entities of the Global Methodist Church.” — irp.cdn-website.com
