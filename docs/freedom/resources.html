Accountability Software

Technology That Helps

These tools are not optional for Freedom Group participants. Every man in the group is expected to use at least one accountability software solution.

Covenant Eyes

The gold standard in accountability software. Covenant Eyes monitors your browsing and sends a report to your accountability partner. It also includes a screen accountability feature that captures screenshots. Required for all group participants.

covenanteyes.com →

Accountable2You

A strong alternative or supplement to Covenant Eyes. Accountable2You monitors device activity across all platforms — iPhone, Android, Mac, Windows, Chromebook — and reports to your accountability partner in real time.

accountable2you.com →

Proven Men App

A companion app to the Proven Men Bible study. Includes daily devotions, accountability check-ins, and progress tracking. Free and available on iOS and Android.

provenmen.org/app →
