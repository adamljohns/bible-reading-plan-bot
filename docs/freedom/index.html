The Mission

What Is the Freedom Group?

The Freedom Group is a closed, confidential men's group designed to help men walk out of sexual brokenness and into the freedom God intends. This is not a lecture series — it is a brotherhood.

Over 12 weeks, men meet together to confront strongholds honestly, apply Scripture practically, and hold each other accountable with grace. Everything shared in the group stays in the group.

The Freedom Group operates under the pastoral covering of Spotswood Baptist Church, Fredericksburg VA, and is facilitated by Adam Johns.

The Journey

12-Week Overview

Each week builds on the last. This is not a sprint — it is a disciplined march toward freedom.

  • 01
    Honesty & Assessment
    Where are you really? Naming the stronghold.
  • 02
    The Biology of Brokenness
    Understanding how the brain works against you — and with you.
  • 03
    Biblical Foundation for Purity
    What does God actually say? Building a theology of the body.
  • 04
    Triggers & Temptation Patterns
    Identifying your personal weakness map.
  • 05
    Accountability Structures
    The Cord of Three: accountability that actually works.
  • 06
    Identity in Christ
    You are not your sin. Reclaiming who God says you are.
  • 07
    Wounds, Shame & the Root System
    What's feeding the stronghold? Getting to the root.
  • 08
    Marriage, Relationships & the Stakes
    The real cost — and the real reward — of purity.
  • 09
    Practical Tools for Ongoing Battle
    Covenant Eyes, device boundaries, prayer rhythms.
  • 10
    Confession, Forgiveness & Restoration
    The mechanics of grace — and how to receive it.
  • 11
    Becoming a Man Who Leads
    Sexual integrity as the foundation of family leadership.
  • 12
    Commissioning & Ongoing Brotherhood
    The group ends. The brotherhood continues.
