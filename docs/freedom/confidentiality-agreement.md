# Freedom Group — Confidentiality Agreement

## USMC Ministries | Spotswood Baptist Church
### Men's Sexual Integrity & Accountability Group

---

## Purpose

The Freedom Group exists to provide a safe, confidential environment where men can pursue freedom from sexual sin through biblical truth, accountability, and brotherhood. Confidentiality is the foundation of trust in this group. Without it, no man will be vulnerable enough to heal.

---

## Agreement

I, _________________________________ (printed name), understand and agree to the following:

### 1. What Is Said Here Stays Here

Everything shared within the Freedom Group — including personal stories, struggles, confessions, prayer requests, and discussions — is **strictly confidential**. I will not share any participant's disclosures with anyone outside the group, including my spouse, friends, family members, church staff, or any other person.

### 2. No Identifying Information

I will not identify any group member as a participant in the Freedom Group to anyone outside the group. Membership itself is confidential.

### 3. Digital Security

- I will not record (audio, video, or written) any group session without explicit written consent from all participants.
- I will not share group content via text, email, social media, messaging apps, or any other digital platform.
- I will not take screenshots or photographs of any group communications.

### 4. Exceptions to Confidentiality

I understand that confidentiality may be broken **only** in the following circumstances:
- **Imminent danger:** If a participant expresses intent to harm themselves or others.
- **Abuse of a minor:** If a participant discloses ongoing abuse of a child, the facilitator is legally and morally obligated to report.
- **Legal requirement:** If compelled by law enforcement or court order.

In these cases, the group facilitator will consult with Pastor Dan Cook (covering pastor) before taking action, except where immediate safety requires immediate response.

### 5. Accountability Partners

If I choose to establish an accountability partnership with another group member outside of scheduled meetings, we will mutually agree on what may be discussed and the boundaries of that relationship. This does not extend to sharing a third party's disclosures.

### 6. Breach of Confidentiality

I understand that a breach of this agreement:
- Undermines the safety and trust of every man in the group
- May result in my removal from the Freedom Group
- May result in a pastoral conversation with group leadership

### 7. Facilitator Obligations

The group facilitator (Adam Johns) and covering pastor (Dr. Dan Cook) are also bound by this agreement. Pastoral conversations between the facilitator and covering pastor regarding group health are permitted, but individual disclosures will not be shared without the participant's consent except as noted in Section 4.
