How We're Funded

Transparency is a core value. Here's exactly how U.S.M.C. Ministries operates, what services cost, and where your support goes.

We Are Not a 501(c)(3)

U.S.M.C. Ministries operates under Bow & Arrow Studio LLC, a for-profit limited liability company. We chose this structure intentionally — it keeps us accountable to results, not paperwork.

What this means for you:

  • Individual donations are NOT classified as charitable contributions for personal income tax purposes.
  • Businesses and organizations: Payments to USMC Ministries can typically be classified as legitimate business expenses — consulting, training, or professional development. We provide itemized receipts for every transaction.
"We believe in transparency. You should know exactly where your money goes and what it pays for."
