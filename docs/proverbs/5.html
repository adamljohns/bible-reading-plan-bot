Day 5: Proverbs 5

M — Manage Faithfully

REAL MAN Focus

God hands every man a set of assets he never created and does not ultimately own — a body, a marriage, a sexuality, a good name. To Manage Faithfully is to steward them the way a trustee handles a fortune that belongs to someone else. Proverbs 5 aims that stewardship straight at the place men most often squander it: the marriage bed.

🔑 Key Verse

“May your wellspring rest under God's blessing, and take your gladness in the bride of your early years — a loving doe, a graceful mountain deer; let her love fill you always, and be captivated in it at all times.”
— Proverbs 5:18–19

Observation

Proverbs 5 is blunt about a slow-motion disaster. The forbidden woman's words "drip honey, and her mouth is smoother than oil" (v.3), but the exit is bitter — "in the end she is bitter as wormwood, sharp as a two-edged sword" (v.4). Solomon is not being a prude; he is being an accountant. He shows a man the true cost of an asset that looked free: "lest you give your honor to others... and at the end of your life you groan, when your flesh and body are spent" (vv.9, 11). Faithless management always looks affordable at the moment of purchase and ruinous at the audit.

But the chapter does not only warn against the counterfeit — it commands enjoyment of the real thing. "Drink water from your own cistern" (v.15); take your gladness in the bride of your early years, "let her love fill you always, and be captivated in it at all times" (vv.18–19). This is stewardship as pleasure, not merely restraint. A man who manages his marriage faithfully does not just avoid the other woman; he actively tends, delights in, and pours himself into his own wife. Faithfulness is a garden you cultivate, not only a fence you maintain.

And then the reason underneath it all: "for a man's ways are before the eyes of the LORD, and He weighs out all his paths" (v.21). You are not managing these assets in the dark. Every path is weighed. The man who forgets he is a steward — that a day of reckoning is coming for what he did with his body, his marriage, his private hours — will "die for lack of discipline" (v.23). The man who remembers manages faithfully, because he knows the Owner is watching and will one day ask for an account.

⚡ Application

Do one concrete thing today that tends the real thing instead of merely fencing out the counterfeit. Plan an actual time with your wife, write her something true, or break off whatever private appetite has been quietly draining the marriage. If you are not yet married, steward your body and your eyes now as a man already keeping covenant with the wife you do not yet have.

🙏 Prayer

Father, everything I have is Yours on loan — my body, my marriage, my desires, my name. Forgive me where I have managed them as though no one were watching and no account were due. Teach me to drink from my own well with gratitude, to delight in the wife You have given, and to guard what is holy because You are holy. Let me feel the weight of Your eyes on all my paths — not as a threat, but as the mercy that keeps me faithful. In the name of Your Son Jesus — Amen.
