Orphan Care
/OR-fuhn KAIR/
noun phrase
Greek orphanos (parentless) plus care. The biblical mandate to defend and provide for the fatherless.

📖 Biblical Definition

Orphan care is the biblical mandate to defend and provide for the fatherless — paired consistently in Scripture with widow-care and stranger-welcome. "Pure religion and undefiled before God and the Father is this, To visit the fatherless and widows in their affliction, and to keep himself unspotted from the world" (James 1:27). The LORD is named "a father of the fatherless, and a judge of the widows" (Psalm 68:5); His people imitate Him. The Mosaic law commanded specific protections: "Ye shall not afflict any widow, or fatherless child" (Exodus 22:22-24); the gleaning corners belonged to them (Deuteronomy 24:19-21); the third-year tithe fed them (Deuteronomy 14:29; 26:12-13). Adoption, foster-care, and gospel orphan-ministry are the modern church’s same obedience.

Old Testament references are dense: Ex 22:22-24 (forbids afflicting widow or orphan); Deut 10:18 (the LORD doth execute the judgment of the fatherless and widow); Deut 14:29 (third-year tithe for Levite, stranger, fatherless, widow); Isa 1:17 (relieve the oppressed, judge the fatherless, plead for the widow).

James 1:27 brings the mandate into the New Covenant: pure religion and undefiled. Orphan care is not auxiliary; it is one of the marks of true religion.

📖 Key Scripture

Psalm 68:5 — "A father of the fatherless, and a judge of the widows, is God in his holy habitation."

Deuteronomy 10:18 — "He doth execute the judgment of the fatherless and widow, and loveth the stranger, in giving him food and raiment."

James 1:27 — "Pure religion and undefiled before God and the Father is this, To visit the fatherless and widows in their affliction."

Isaiah 1:17 — "Learn to do well; seek judgment, relieve the oppressed, judge the fatherless, plead for the widow."

⚠️ Modern Corruption

Modern Christianity often outsources orphan care to nonprofits; Scripture commands the church and household to do it directly.

expand to see more

James 1:27 puts orphan-and-widow visitation as a mark of pure religion. Not as supplementary giving; as core practice. The modern outsourcing to specialized agencies is helpful but does not discharge the personal mandate.

Adoption, foster care, support of orphans through the church's own networks, regular visiting of fatherless families — each is the household's direct fulfillment of James 1:27. The Lord's own father-of-the-fatherless heart shapes the saints'.
