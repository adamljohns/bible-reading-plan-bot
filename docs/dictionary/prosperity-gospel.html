Prosperity Gospel

Biblical Definition

The true gospel is that Christ died for our sins, was buried, and was raised on the third day (1 Corinthians 15:3–4). It calls sinners to repent and believe (Mark 1:15). Scripture warns against teachers who treat godliness as a means of gain (1 Timothy 6:3–10) and against those who refuse the cross: “If anyone would come after me, let him deny himself and take up his cross daily and follow me” (Luke 9:23). God may prosper His people in wisdom and provision; He never authorized a gospel that makes health, wealth, and breakthrough the covenant center. That is another gospel (Galatians 1:6–9).

Webster 1828 Definition

PROSPER'ITY, n. Advance or gain in any thing good or desirable; successful progress in any business or enterprise.

PROSPER'ITY, n. Advance or gain in anything good or desirable; success.

Note: Webster defines prosperity as a *condition*. He does not define a religion of guaranteed wealth. The corruption is turning a possible providence into a promised product.

Key Scripture

• 1 Timothy 6:3–10 — godliness is not a means of gain; love of money.

• Luke 9:23 — deny yourself, take up the cross.

• Matthew 6:19–21 — treasures in heaven, not earth.

• Philippians 3:18–19 — enemies of the cross; minds set on earthly things.

• 2 Corinthians 11:4 — another Jesus, another spirit, another gospel.

• Galatians 1:8–9 — accursed is another gospel.

• 1 Corinthians 15:3–4 — the real gospel.

Modern Corruption

The prosperity gospel (health-and-wealth, seed-faith, “speak it / sow it”) replaces the cross with a transaction: give money, claim the breakthrough, name the blessing.

Public tells: seed-faith appeals as normal giving theology; guaranteed health/wealth as covenant right for “faithful” givers; gospel summarized as elevation, success, and breakthrough without repentance; pastors as life-coaches of accumulation. This error is deeply ingrained in much of the NAR world, but it also stands alone in seeker and celebrity churches. Either way, when prosperity *is* the gospel, the church is not merely “imbalanced” — it is preaching darkness in kingdom language.

Usage

• “If the altar call is ‘sow a seed for your breakthrough,’ you are not hearing the apostolic gospel.”

• “Christ purchased sinners with His blood — not luxury with your offering.”

• “Prosperity as a providence is one thing. Prosperity as the gospel is another religion.”
