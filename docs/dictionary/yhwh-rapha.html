YHWH-Rapha
yah-WEH RAH-fah
compound divine name
Hebrew Yahweh-Rapha (יְהוָה רֹפְאֶךָ) — "The LORD Who Heals You." Declared by God Himself at Marah after the bitter waters were sweetened (Exod 15:26).

Biblical Definition

YHWH-Rapha (יְהוָה רֹפְאֶךָ) — "the LORD that healeth thee" — is the covenant self-revelation God gave Israel at Marah, after sweetening the bitter waters with a tree cast in: "If thou wilt diligently hearken to the voice of the LORD thy God... I will put none of these diseases upon thee, which I have brought upon the Egyptians: for I am the LORD that healeth thee" (Exodus 15:26). The Hebrew rapha covers physical healing, spiritual healing, and the binding-up of the broken-hearted. God is the comprehensive Healer: of Naaman’s leprosy (2 Kings 5), of David’s soul (Psalm 41:4; 147:3), of the nations’ wounds (Isaiah 53:5). All medicine, all comfort, all resurrection terminate in this name.

KJV Continual Tense

In KJV: healeth — God's healing is a sustained name, not an event.

expand to see more

The Hebrew imperfect in Exodus 15:26 — "I am the LORD that healeth thee" — is continuous-aspect. Healing is not an occasional act of God; it is His name, His character, His standing relationship to His people.

Psalm 103:3: "Who forgiveth all thine iniquities; who healeth all thy diseases." Continuous forgiveness paired with continuous healing — the same divine character on both sides.

Some healing is now (medical, prayer-answered, miraculous), some healing waits (the resurrection body). YHWH-Rapha is real in both timeframes; the name does not collapse if a particular healing is delayed.

Webster 1828 Definition

The LORD Who Heals — declared at Marah.

expand to see more

The compound covenant name God Himself pronounces at Marah after the bitter waters were sweetened: "I am the LORD that healeth thee." Hebrew rapha covers physical healing (disease cured), spiritual healing (sin's wound bound up), relational healing (covenant restored), and ultimate eschatological healing (the resurrection body). The Healer is one Healer working at every level.

Key Scripture

Exodus 15:26 — "I am the LORD that healeth thee."

Psalm 147:3 — "He healeth the broken in heart, and bindeth up their wounds."

Isaiah 53:5 — "But he was wounded for our transgressions, he was bruised for our iniquities: the chastisement of our peace was upon him; and with his stripes we are healed."

Modern Corruption

Either commercialized by prosperity-healing (where the absence of physical healing is read as lack of faith) or dismissed as primitive superstition.

expand to see more

Word-of-faith teaching weaponizes YHWH-Rapha as a guarantee of physical healing on demand. Naturalist medicine dismisses the name as pre-scientific. Scripture sits between: God is the Healer; some healing is now, some waits; suffering is not always lack of faith.

Recover the four-tier healing: physical, spiritual, relational, eschatological. YHWH-Rapha is at work in all four. The resurrection body is the final stripe-by-stripe healing.
