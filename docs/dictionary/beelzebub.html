Beelzebub
bee-EL-zuh-bub
proper noun
Hebrew Baʾal-zevub (H1176), “lord of the flies.” The Philistine god of Ekron and a derisive name for Satan in the Gospels — the prince of the devils.

📖 Biblical Definition

Beelzebub (or Beelzebul, the Greek form) was originally the Philistine god of Ekron consulted by King Ahaziah of Israel after his fall through the lattice — "Beelzebub the god of Ekron" (2 Kings 1:2-6, 16). The Hebrew form (Baal-zebub) means "Lord of the flies"; the Greek (Baal-zebul) may mean "Lord of the dwelling" — perhaps a deliberate Jewish corruption of the Philistine title. In the Gospels the name is used by Christ’s opponents and by Christ Himself as a name for the prince of demons. The Pharisees attributed His exorcisms to "Beelzebub the prince of the devils"; Christ exposed the absurdity (Matthew 12:24-27; Mark 3:22; Luke 11:15-19). "It is enough for the disciple that he be as his master... if they have called the master of the house Beelzebub, how much more shall they call them of his household?" (Matthew 10:25).

⚠️ Modern Corruption

Christ was called Beelzebub by His enemies; modern saints should not be surprised when slandered.

expand to see more

Matthew 10:25 is a sober warning: if they have called the master of the house Beelzebub, how much more shall they call them of his household? The Pharisees accused Christ of demonic empowerment; modern equivalents accuse His followers of bigotry, hatred, fascism, or whatever epithet the cultural moment supplies. Christ's warning is permanent: do not expect different treatment than the Master received.

Christ's answer in Matthew 12:25-29 is also instructive. He does not deny the accusation tearfully; He demolishes it logically. A kingdom divided against itself cannot stand. If Satan were casting out Satan, his enterprise would already be collapsing. The accusation is not just false; it is incoherent. When the world calls you Beelzebub, do not despair; do not over-defend; answer with logic, with Scripture, and with the steady deeds of the kingdom. The truth survives the slander.
