Nation
/ˈnā-shən/
noun
From Latin natio, nationis (birth, race, people), from nasci (to be born); a people group defined by shared origin, language, land, and culture — born, not merely constituted

📖 Biblical Definition

In Scripture, the nations (goyim in Hebrew, ethnē in Greek) are the peoples of the earth as ordered by God. God Himself established the nations at creation (Acts 17:26), fixing their times and boundaries. The call of Abraham was a call through which "all nations of the earth shall be blessed" (Gen 12:3) — nations exist within God's redemptive purpose. The Great Commission is explicitly to "all nations" (Matt 28:19), and Revelation's culmination is "a great multitude that no one could number, from every nation, tribe, people, and language, standing before the throne" (Rev 7:9). Nations are not accidents or mere political constructs — they are God's ordering of humanity, and they will be healed and honored in the New Jerusalem (Rev 21:24, 26).

📜 Webster 1828 Definition

NATION, n. A body of people inhabiting the same country, or united under the same sovereign government, and distinguished from others by similar language, customs, or institutions. In Scripture, the nations often denotes the Gentiles, the nations of the world, as opposed to Israel. Also, the people of God are called a holy nation. "Ye shall be unto me a kingdom of priests, and a holy nation." — Exodus 19:6.

⚠️ Modern Corruption

Globalist ideology treats the nation as an obstacle to progress — a source of dangerous tribalism to be dissolved into a borderless world order. From this view, national identity, particularity, and sovereignty are evils to be overcome. Biblically, the obliteration of nations is not God's design — it is Babel's ambition (Gen 11), which God judged. The nations persist through Revelation and into eternity (Rev 21:24). Conversely, nationalism divorced from God's sovereignty and the brotherhood of all peoples in Christ becomes idolatry. The biblical tension holds: nations are real, ordained, and honored — and all nations owe allegiance to the King of kings.

📖 Key Scripture

Acts 17:26 — "And he made from one man every nation of mankind to live on all the face of the earth, having determined allotted periods and the boundaries of their dwelling place."

Matthew 28:19 — "Go therefore and make disciples of all nations, baptizing them in the name of the Father and of the Son and of the Holy Spirit."

Revelation 7:9 — "A great multitude that no one could number, from every nation, from all tribes and peoples and languages, standing before the throne."

Revelation 21:24 — "By its light will the nations walk, and the kings of the earth will bring their glory into it."
