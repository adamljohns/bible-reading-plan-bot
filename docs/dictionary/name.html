Name
/neɪm/
noun
Old English nama, from Proto-Germanic namnaz; related to Latin nomen and Greek onoma. In ancient Near Eastern thought, a name was not merely a label but a disclosure of essence, character, and authority. To know a being's name was to have access to its nature.

📖 Biblical Definition

In biblical thought, a name carries the full weight of a person's identity, character, and reputation. God's name is His self-disclosure — "I AM WHO I AM" (Exodus 3:14) — the most profound statement of divine self-definition in Scripture. To act "in the name" of someone is to act with their full authority and as their representative. Prayer "in Jesus' name" is not a magic formula but submission to His character and purpose. God's name being "hallowed" means His character being honored, protected, and not misrepresented. Name-giving in Scripture is often transformational: Abram becomes Abraham, Jacob becomes Israel, Simon becomes Peter — a new name signals a new identity and vocation.

📜 Webster 1828 Definition

NAME, n. 1. The word or words by which any person or thing is called and distinguished from others. 2. Reputation; character — "a good name is better than great riches." 3. Fame; renown. 4. Power; authority; as, "in the name of the king." 5. In Scripture, the name of God signifies God himself, his attributes, his will, his word, his authority, and whatever by which he makes himself known to men.

⚠️ Modern Corruption

The phrase "in Jesus' name" has been reduced to a liturgical tag appended to prayers as an afterthought — a password rather than a posture. The prosperity gospel weaponizes the "name of Jesus" as a formula to extract divine goods, divorcing it from submission to His will and character. Meanwhile, the Third Commandment — "Do not take the name of the LORD your God in vain" — is typically reduced to prohibiting swearing, when it actually forbids the far deeper sin of claiming to represent God while living in contradiction to His character.

📖 Key Scripture

Exodus 3:14 — "God said to Moses, 'I AM WHO I AM.' Say this to the people of Israel: 'I AM has sent me to you.'"

Philippians 2:9–10 — "God has highly exalted him and bestowed on him the name that is above every name."

Proverbs 22:1 — "A good name is to be chosen rather than great riches, and favor is better than silver or gold."

Acts 4:12 — "There is salvation in no one else, for there is no other name under heaven given among men by which we must be saved."

Psalm 20:7 — "Some trust in chariots and some in horses, but we trust in the name of the LORD our God."

🔗 Greek & Hebrew Roots

H8034 — shēm (שֵׁם): name, character, reputation, fame; in Hebrew thought, the name IS the person's essential nature disclosed.

G3686 — onoma (ὄνομα): name, character, authority; "in my name" = acting with the full authority and representing the full character of Jesus.

H3068 — YHWH (יְהוָה): the Tetragrammaton — God's personal name, revealed to Moses; "He who is," "He who causes to be" — the self-existent, covenant-keeping God.
