Except
/ɪkˈsɛpt/
verb / conjunction
Latin excipio: ex + capio, to take out. Webster: to exclude; also equivalent to unless.

Biblical Definition

Except is to take something out of a stated whole — or, as a hinge, to mean unless. Paul is careful: when Scripture says all things are put under the Son, He who put all things under Him is excepted. Jesus is equally careful: whoever divorces his wife, except for sexual immorality, and marries another, commits adultery. The word is not a loophole factory. It is a fence. Do not flatten except into “rules are optional for me.”

Webster 1828 Definition

EXCEPT, v.t. To take or leave out of any number specified; to exclude. Also: unless.

EXCEPT', verb transitive [Latin excipio; ex and capio, to take.]

1. To take or leave out of any number specified; to exclude; as, of the thirty persons present and concerned in a riot, we must except two.

2. To take or leave out any particular or particulars, from a general description.

When he saith, all things are put under him, it is manifest that he is excepted who did put all things under him.

EXCEPT', verb intransitive To object; to make an objection or objections; usually followed by to; sometimes by against.

EXCEPT', participle passive Contracted from excepted. Taken out; not included. Hence except is equivalent to without, unless, and denotes exclusion.

Key Scripture

• Luke 13:3 — “I tell you, no; but unless you repent you will all likewise perish.” (BTE NKJV)

• Matthew 19:9 — “And I say to you, whoever divorces his wife, except for sexual immorality, and marries another, commits adultery; and whoever marries her who is divorced commits adultery.”” (BTE NKJV)

Modern Corruption

The age treats except as a personal exemption from repentance. Jesus treats it as a real fence — and as unless.

expand to see more

Soft religion says, “God understands; I am the exception.” Luke 13 will not have it: unless you repent you will all likewise perish. The other mash is to treat Matthew 19:9 as if except erased the rest of the sentence. The exception names one crime. It does not license serial covenant-breaking.

Usage

• “Except is a fence, not a hall pass.”
