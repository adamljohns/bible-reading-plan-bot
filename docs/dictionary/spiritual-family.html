Spiritual Family / Household of God
/ˈspɪr.ɪ.tʃu.əl ˈfæm.ə.li/ · household of God
noun phrase · dual headword
Household of God is the New Testament phrase (Eph 2:19; 1 Tim 3:15). Spiritual family is ordinary pastoral English for the same reality. Also called the family of God and the household of faith — the redeemed household entered by new birth and adoption, not by bloodline or sentiment.

📖 Biblical Definition

Spiritual family and household of God name one thing: the church as God’s family and dwelling — all who are born again, adopted by the Father, united to Christ, and bound as brothers and sisters under one Lord. Scripture’s controlling language is household of God; pastoral speech often says spiritual family. This dictionary keeps them as one dual headword so the vernacular and the text stay locked together. It is not a second natural household, a lifestyle brand, or a substitute for marriage. It is the people of God formed by the Spirit — also called the family of God and the household of faith.

Paul writes that believers are “no more strangers and foreigners, but fellowcitizens with the saints, and of the household of God” (Ephesians 2:19), and that the house of God is “the church of the living God, the pillar and ground of the truth” (1 Timothy 3:15). Jesus marks the bond: “Whoever does the will of my Father in heaven is my brother and sister and mother” (Matthew 12:50). Believers are charged to do good especially to the household of faith (Galatians 6:10). The image gathers adoption, dwelling, and order: sons under one Father; a habitation of God through the Spirit; officers, duties, and discipline — not a club of customers.

Nuance that must be kept: this family is not identical to every visible congregation, every membership roll, or every room that uses the word “church.” The true spiritual family / household of God is the company of the regenerate — what theology has long called the invisible church. That household is made visible, trained, and ordered through the visible church, the local and historic assemblies of professing believers. On earth that people still wars against sin, the world, and the devil (church militant); in glory the same people rest and reign with Christ (church triumphant). Local church membership is the ordinary commanded form of household life — not optional, and not a consumer upgrade.

📜 Webster 1828 Definition

Webster has no headword “spiritual family.” The sense is recovered from his definitions of family, household, and church as God’s people.

FAM'ILY, n. The collective body of persons who live in one house and under one head or manager; a household. Also, those who descend from one common progenitor; a race; a tribe.

HOUSE'HOLD, n. Those who dwell under the same roof and compose a family.

CHURCH, n. In its most general sense, the collective body of Christians, or of those who profess to believe in Christ.

Note: “Spiritual family” is later pastoral English for the church as God’s household — a family entered by regeneration and adoption, not by natural descent alone. Scripture’s controlling phrases remain household of God and family of God.

⚠️ Modern Corruption

“Spiritual family” is flattened into warm vibes, chosen community, or a rival to marriage and the natural household — and then used to baptize whatever feels loving.

expand to see more

Three distortions now ride the phrase.

1. Sentiment without new birth. Spiritual family becomes any affirming group that uses Jesus-language. Scripture knows no such family: entrance is by regeneration and adoption, not by feelings of belonging.

2. Rival to God’s first institutions. Some treat “spiritual family” as license to neglect spouse, children, or parents, or to redefine marriage and household. That is rebellion. The natural family is creation ordinance (Gen 1–2; Deut 6; Eph 5–6). Spiritual family does not abolish it; it outlasts it and judges it. A man who will not provide for his own has denied the faith (1 Timothy 5:8).

3. Soft cover for soft churches. When “spiritual family” means unconditional affirmation without repentance, truth, or discipline, the household of God is swapped for a club. The house of God is the pillar and ground of the truth (1 Tim 3:15). Love that blesses what God calls sin is not household loyalty; it is house-wrecking. Context, marriage, and church order matter precisely because God’s family is ordered under His Word — not under the spirit of the age.

Recover the phrase as covenant reality: one Father, one elder Brother, one Spirit-born people — with duties, holiness, local church commitment, and no permission to rewrite creation.

Usage

• “Spiritual family is Scripture’s household of God — entered by new birth, not by bloodline or branding.”

• “Your spiritual family is not every person in a building; it is the regenerate people of God, ordinarily joined to a faithful visible church.”

• “Spiritual family never cancels marriage or the natural household; it forbids using ‘family’ language to bless what God forbids.”
