Bottomless Pit
/ˈbɒt.əm.lɛs pɪt/
noun phrase
Translation of Greek abyssos (ἄβυσσος) — "without bottom, boundless depth." The English "bottomless pit" appears seven times in the King James Version of Revelation.

Biblical Definition

The bottomless pit is the place of restraint for demonic powers — a kind of prison from which they cannot escape except by divine permission. In Revelation 9:1-2, a fallen star is given the key to the bottomless pit, and out of it comes smoke and locust-creatures that torment the unsealed. In Revelation 11:7, "the beast that ascends out of the bottomless pit" makes war against the two witnesses. In Revelation 17:8, "the beast that you saw was, and is not, and will ascend out of the bottomless pit." Most significantly, Revelation 20:1-3 shows an angel with "the key of the bottomless pit" binding Satan himself for a thousand years. The bottomless pit is not hell proper (the lake of fire) but a place of demonic confinement. The demons Jesus cast out of the Gadarene demoniac begged "that He would not command them to go out into the abyss" (Luke 8:31) — they knew the place and feared it. The bottomless pit is one more reminder that every hostile spiritual power operates on a leash held by Christ.

Key Scripture

Revelation 20:1-3 — "Then I saw an angel coming down from heaven, having the key to the bottomless pit and a great chain in his hand. He laid hold of the dragon, that serpent of old... and he cast him into the bottomless pit."

Revelation 9:1-2 — "Then the fifth angel sounded: And I saw a star fallen from heaven to the earth. To him was given the key to the bottomless pit."

Luke 8:31 — "And they begged Him that He would not command them to go out into the abyss."
