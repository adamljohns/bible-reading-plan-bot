Armor
/ˈɑːr.mər/
noun
Old French armure, from Latin armatura — "equipment, arms"; from armare, "to arm." Greek: panoplia (πανοπλία) — "full armor, complete weaponry"; from pan (all) + hopla (arms). Paul's Ephesians 6 passage draws directly on the image of a Roman legionary's full battle kit.

📖 Biblical Definition

The armor of God (Ephesians 6:11–17) is the complete spiritual equipment provided by God for the believer engaged in cosmic warfare against "the rulers, against the authorities, against the cosmic powers over this present darkness." Each piece corresponds to a spiritual reality: the belt of truth (doctrinal integrity), the breastplate of righteousness (righteous standing and conduct), shoes of readiness (the gospel of peace as firm footing), the shield of faith (trust in God's promises that extinguishes flaming accusations), the helmet of salvation (assurance protecting the mind), and the sword of the Spirit (the Word of God as the believer's only offensive weapon). Notably, every piece except the sword is defensive — the Christian life requires protection, not just attack. The armor is not self-generated but divine: it is "the armor of God."

📜 Webster 1828 Definition

ARMOR, n. 1. Defensive arms; any covering worn to protect the body in battle; formerly of leather, plate, or chain mail. 2. In Scripture, the armor of God is applied to the Christian's spiritual equipment for the warfare against sin and Satan — truth, righteousness, faith, salvation, the Word of God.

⚠️ Modern Corruption

Contemporary Christianity has largely abandoned the warfare worldview that makes the armor necessary. The spiritual life is presented as a therapeutic journey toward self-improvement rather than a battle on enemy territory. When the church forgets it is at war, it stops putting on armor — and unarmed soldiers are casualties. Conversely, some traditions weaponize "spiritual warfare" language into paranoid, unhealthy obsession with demons in every situation, losing the grounded, disciplined posture Paul describes. The balanced view: we are in a real war, the armor is real, the enemy is real — but the victory is already secured in Christ.

📖 Key Scripture

Ephesians 6:11 — "Put on the whole armor of God, that you may be able to stand against the schemes of the devil."

Ephesians 6:14–17 — Belt of truth, breastplate of righteousness, shoes, shield, helmet, sword of the Spirit.

Romans 13:12 — "Let us cast off the works of darkness and put on the armor of light."

Isaiah 59:17 — "He put on righteousness as a breastplate, and a helmet of salvation on his head." (God Himself wearing armor — the origin of Paul's imagery.)

🔗 Greek & Hebrew Roots

G3833 — panoplia (πανοπλία): the full armor of a Roman heavy infantryman — used twice in Ephesians 6 for the complete spiritual kit God provides.

G3696 — hoplon (ὅπλον): weapon, implement of war; used in Romans 13:12 and 2 Corinthians 10:4 for weapons of righteousness.

H5402 — nesheq (נֶשֶׁק): armor, weapons — the equipment of a warrior prepared for battle.
