Sovereignty

/ˈsɒv.rɪn.ti/

Etymology

From Old French soverain, from Vulgar Latin *superanus, meaning "chief, principal." The root is Latin super, "over."

Biblical Definition

The sovereignty of God is His absolute right and power to govern and control all things. It means that God is the supreme authority in the universe, and nothing happens that is outside of His ultimate control or that can thwart His divine purposes.

Webster's 1828 Definition

Supreme power; supremacy; the possession of the highest power, or of uncontrollable power. The sovereignty of God is a sublime and indisputable doctrine of revelation.

Modern Corruption

Modern thought often limits "sovereignty" to political entities (national sovereignty) and radical individualism (personal sovereignty). This focus on human autonomy stands in direct opposition to the biblical concept of God's absolute and comprehensive sovereignty over all creation, including human will.
