Idolatry
/aɪ·ˈdol·ə·tree/
noun
From Greek eidōlolatreia (εἰδωλολατρεία) — worship of images; eidōlon (image, phantom) + latreia (service, worship). Hebrew: avodah zarah (עֲבוֹדָה זָרָה) — strange/foreign service; also gillulim (גִּלּוּלִים) — dung-idols (Ezekiel's contemptuous term).

📖 Biblical Definition

Idolatry is the worship, trust, or ultimate devotion given to anything or anyone other than the living God. It is the foundational sin — the root from which all other sins spring — because it places a creature in the place that belongs to the Creator. The First and Second Commandments address it directly and at length, suggesting God treats it with extreme gravity. The prophets mock idolaters with devastating irony: the craftsman cuts down a tree, burns half of it to warm himself, roasts meat over it, and then bows down to the other half and calls it "my god, deliver me!" (Isaiah 44:16–17). But the New Testament expands the definition: covetousness is idolatry (Colossians 3:5); whatever you serve with your deepest loyalty is your god. John Calvin called the human heart a "perpetual factory of idols."

📜 Webster 1828 Definition

IDOLATRY, n. The worship of idols, images, or any thing made by hands, or which is not God. Idolatry is of two kinds; the worship of images, statues, pictures, etc. made by hands; and the worship of the heavenly bodies, the sun, moon and stars, or of demons, angels, men and animals. Excessive attachment or veneration for any thing, or that which borders on adoration.

⚠️ Modern Corruption

Modern people think idolatry is an ancient problem — something primitive peoples did with golden calves. But contemporary idols are simply better dressed: career, appearance, romantic fulfillment, political ideology, nationality, self. The diagnostic question is not "Do you bow to a statue?" but "What do you turn to for ultimate security? What are you willing to sin to obtain? What loss would unmake you?" The answers reveal your functional god. The modern West does not lack religion — it is saturated with it. It worships at the temples of the gym, the stock market, the university, and the mirror.
