Christianese Decoded

Why this page exists: the church has its own dialect, and like every dialect it reveals a heart. Some Christianese is sound shorthand for real truth; some is harmless sentiment; but a great deal of it has been quietly weaponized — biblical-sounding words bent to silence the faithful, dissolve clear commands, and smuggle another gospel past the gate. “Image-bearer” is made to forbid all moral judgment; “winsome” becomes a muzzle; “love is love” collapses agape into appetite. Here every term is weighed against Scripture and ruled — is it real gold, or fool’s gold? Each entry is verdict-tagged Redeemable, Neutral, Examine, or Reject.
