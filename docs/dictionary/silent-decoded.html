Silent Generation Decoded

Generation 1928–1945. The swing-band jive, GI talk, home-front words, and soda-fountain slang your grandparents grew up on — most of it clean, some of it carrying a minced oath or a sneer they never noticed. Here is what a grandfather can hand down with a smile, and what he would do well to set down for good.
