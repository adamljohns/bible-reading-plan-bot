Restoration of Peter
PEE-ter
Christ event
Greek event in John 21:15-19. The risen Christ's threefold reinstatement of Peter beside the Sea of Galilee, asking three times Lovest thou me? for the three denials, and commissioning Peter with three commands: feed my lambs, feed my sheep, feed my sheep.

See also: Restoration of Peter

📖 Biblical Definition

John 21:15-19 records one of the most pastorally healing scenes in the Gospels. After breakfast on the shore of the Sea of Galilee, the risen Christ took Peter aside. Three times He asked: Simon, son of Jonas, lovest thou me? Three times Peter answered yes. Three times Christ commissioned him: feed my lambs; feed my sheep; feed my sheep. The three questions matched the three denials; the three commissions matched the three calls (Mark 1:17; 3:16; Luke 5:10). Then Christ predicted Peter's death by martyrdom, and concluded: follow me.

⚠️ Modern Corruption

Modern Christianity often retires fallen leaders; Christ restored Peter and gave him the church.

expand to see more

Modern Christianity often retires fallen leaders permanently. The fall is treated as terminal; the restoration is incomplete; the calling is forfeited. John 21 disagrees. Peter's denial was as public as it gets, and his restoration was equally specific: three questions for three denials, three commissions for three falls. Christ then made him the chief shepherd of the early church.

The pattern is not blanket; not every fall is restorable in the same way. But the principle stands: the Lord is in the restoration business. He restored Peter; He restores you. Receive the three questions: lovest thou me? Receive the three commissions: feed my lambs. The work is not over because of the fall; the call is renewed at the breakfast fire.
