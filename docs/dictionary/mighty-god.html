Mighty God
MY-tee GOD
proper noun (Christ-title)
Hebrew El Gibbor (Isa 9:6 / Isa 10:21). One of the four throne-names Isaiah gives the Messianic Child — an unambiguous declaration of His full deity.

📖 Biblical Definition

Isaiah's Messianic name El Gibbor, “Mighty God,” applied to the Child to be born and the Son to be given (Isa 9:6). The same name is used of God Himself two chapters later in Isaiah 10:21. Isaiah is unambiguous: the Messiah is not a powerful angel or an exalted human; He is God, mighty in battle, the warrior-King of redemption.

📜 Webster 1828 Definition

MIGHT'Y, a.

1. Having great bodily strength or physical power; very strong. 2. Powerful; able to produce great effects. 3. Strong in wealth and influence. 4. Mighty God — a title of Christ in Isa. 9.

📖 Key Scripture

Isaiah 9:6 — "His name shall be called... The mighty God."

Isaiah 10:21 — "The remnant shall return... unto the mighty God."

Jeremiah 32:18 — "The Great, the Mighty God, the Lord of hosts, is his name."

Titus 2:13 — "Looking for that blessed hope, and the glorious appearing of the great God and our Saviour Jesus Christ."

⚠️ Modern Corruption

Modal Christianities try to turn Christ into less than God; Isaiah ruled it out before He was born.

expand to see more

The four throne-names of Isaiah 9:6 are not poetic adjectives; they are deity claims. Wonderful Counselor — God's wisdom incarnate. Mighty God — not a mighty god but the Mighty God, the same El Gibbor used of Yahweh in chapter 10. Everlasting Father — eternal sourcing, not subordinate origin. Prince of Peace — throne authority. Together: the Messiah is God Himself walking in human form.

Every reduced Christology the centuries have produced — Arianism, modern Watchtower theology, Mormonism, Unitarian-progressivism — has had to wave a hand at Isaiah 9:6 to get past it. The hand never quite obscures the verse. Christ is the Mighty God. Worship Him as such, or do not worship Him at all.
