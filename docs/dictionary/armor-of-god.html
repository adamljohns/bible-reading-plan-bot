Armor of God
/ˈɑːr.mər əv ɡɒd/
noun phrase
From Paul's description in Ephesians 6:10-18. The six-piece spiritual equipment the Christian must "put on" to stand against the schemes of the devil: belt of truth, breastplate of righteousness, sandals of the gospel of peace, shield of faith, helmet of salvation, and sword of the Spirit (which is the Word of God).

📖 Biblical Definition

The Armor of God is Paul's extended metaphor at the close of Ephesians for spiritual warfare. Writing from Roman imprisonment, quite possibly chained to a Roman soldier, Paul used what he saw before him to describe what the Christian must wear to stand in the spiritual battle. "Finally, my brethren, be strong in the Lord and in the power of His might. Put on the whole armor of God, that you may be able to stand against the wiles of the devil. For we do not wrestle against flesh and blood, but against principalities, against powers, against the rulers of the darkness of this age, against spiritual hosts of wickedness in the heavenly places" (Ephesians 6:10-12). The six pieces: (1) Belt of truth (6:14) — the foundational piece; the Roman soldier's belt held everything else together. Truth — God's revealed truth and the believer's personal integrity — is what holds the Christian's life together. (2) Breastplate of righteousness (6:14) — protects the vital organs. The imputed righteousness of Christ, lived out in daily obedience, guards the heart. (3) Sandals of the gospel of peace (6:15) — Roman soldiers' hobnailed boots gave them traction. The gospel gives the Christian sure footing and readiness to move. (4) Shield of faith (6:16) — the large Roman thureos that could be soaked in water and used to extinguish flaming arrows. Faith in God's promises extinguishes Satan's fiery doubts and temptations. (5) Helmet of salvation (6:17) — protects the head, the mind. The assurance of salvation keeps the believer's thinking intact. (6) Sword of the Spirit, which is the word of God (6:17) — the only offensive weapon in the armor. Note: rhema theou — the spoken word, Scripture quoted and wielded. Jesus used it against Satan in the wilderness. And Paul closes with prayer (6:18) — the invisible infrastructure that connects all the armor to God. Every piece of the armor is Christ Himself. We are not decorating ourselves with pieces of equipment; we are putting on Christ and walking into battle under His orders.

📖 Key Scripture

Ephesians 6:10-11 — "Finally, my brethren, be strong in the Lord and in the power of His might. Put on the whole armor of God, that you may be able to stand against the wiles of the devil."

Ephesians 6:14-17 — "Stand therefore, having girded your waist with truth, having put on the breastplate of righteousness, and having shod your feet with the preparation of the gospel of peace; above all, taking the shield of faith with which you will be able to quench all the fiery darts of the wicked one. And take the helmet of salvation, and the sword of the Spirit, which is the word of God."

Ephesians 6:18 — "Praying always with all prayer and supplication in the Spirit, being watchful to this end with all perseverance and supplication for all the saints."
