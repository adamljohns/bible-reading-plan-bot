Abortion
/uh-BOR-shun/
noun
From Latin abortio (miscarriage), from aboriri (to miscarry, perish), from ab- (away from) + oriri (to arise, be born). The word originally described any premature termination of life or process. In all historical usage prior to the twentieth century, abortion was understood as the destruction of a living human being in the womb.

Biblical Definition

Scripture treats the child in the womb as a living person created in the image of God. God declares that He knew and formed the child before birth: "Before I formed you in the womb I knew you" (Jeremiah 1:5). The unborn are described with the same Greek word (brephos) as born infants. The intentional destruction of the unborn is therefore the shedding of innocent blood, which God declares He hates (Proverbs 6:16-17). The Mosaic law assigned penalty for harm done to an unborn child (Exodus 21:22-25), affirming the personhood of the child in the womb. Abortion is an offense against the Creator who alone has authority over life and death.

Webster 1828 Definition

The act of miscarrying, or producing young before the natural time, or before the fetus is perfectly formed.

ABOR'TION, n. [L. abortio, a miscarriage.] 1. The act of miscarrying, or producing young before the natural time, or before the fetus is perfectly formed. 2. The fetus brought forth before it is perfectly formed. 3. Any fruit or produce that does not come to maturity. 4. Failure of a plan or project. Note: Webster's definition treats abortion as the premature termination of life. There is no pretense that the unborn child is mere tissue or a "clump of cells."

Modern Corruption

Abortion has been reframed from the destruction of a human life to a "healthcare right."

expand to see more

The modern corruption of language around abortion is among the most deliberate in history. The child is called a "fetus," a "clump of cells," or a "product of conception" to strip away personhood. The act of killing is called "reproductive healthcare," "choice," or "termination of pregnancy." The woman is told she has "bodily autonomy" as though the child's body is her own. Scripture recognizes no such right. God alone is the author of life. The euphemisms exist to anesthetize the conscience and suppress the truth that every human being bears the image of God from conception. The church that refuses to call abortion what it is — the shedding of innocent blood — has already compromised with the spirit of the age.
