Emet
/ eh·ˈmet /
noun
Hebrew ʾemet (אֱמֶת) — "truth, reliability, trustworthiness." From the root ʾāman (to be firm, to support). Emet shares its root with emunah (faithfulness) and amen. The Hebrew letters aleph-mem-tav span the entire Hebrew alphabet (first, middle, last), leading ancient rabbis to say that truth encompasses all things from beginning to end. Truth in Hebrew is not abstract correspondence but concrete reliability.

📖 Biblical Definition

Emet is truth understood as firm, stable reality — that which is reliable and can be depended upon. When Scripture declares that God is a "God of emet" (Psalm 31:5), it means He is the God who can be trusted completely, whose word will not fail, whose character does not shift. Truth in the Bible is not a philosophical abstraction or a proposition floating in logical space; it is a Person. Jesus declared "I am the way, the truth [aletheia/emet], and the life" (John 14:6). Emet is relational before it is propositional — it is the quality of a father who keeps his promises, a king whose word is his bond, a God who cannot lie (Titus 1:2). The opposite of emet is not merely falsehood but instability — the shifting sand of a life built on anything other than God's unchanging word. To walk in emet is to walk in reality as God defines it, not as man imagines it.

📜 Webster 1828 Definition

TRUTH — Conformity to fact or reality; exact accordance with that which is, or has been, or shall be. Veracity; purity from falsehood; practice of speaking truth; habitual disposition to speak truth. In theology, God is truth. His word, his promises, are truth. "Thy word is truth." John 17:17.

⚠️ Modern Corruption

Postmodernism has declared war on emet. "My truth" and "your truth" have replaced the truth. Truth has been relativized into a personal narrative, a social construct, or a power play. Even within the church, "speaking your truth" has replaced "speaking the truth in love" (Ephesians 4:15). The therapeutic model says truth is whatever feels authentic to you; the biblical model says truth is whatever God has declared, regardless of how it feels. The Hebrew concept of emet is stubbornly objective — it is firm ground, not shifting sand. You do not create emet; you discover it, submit to it, and build your life upon it. A culture that has lost emet has lost its foundation. When truth becomes personal opinion, every man does what is right in his own eyes (Judges 21:25), and civilization collapses.
