Intercession
/ˌin-tər-ˈse-shən/
noun
From Latin intercessio — "a going between," from inter- (between) + cedere (to go). The act of one standing in the gap between parties.

📖 Biblical Definition

The act of pleading on behalf of another before God. Intercession is one of the highest expressions of love — to stand in the presence of God and advocate for someone who cannot or will not advocate for themselves. It is not merely asking; it is standing between a person and judgment, a nation and destruction, a sinner and wrath. Abraham interceded for Sodom. Moses interceded for Israel. Christ intercedes perpetually at the right hand of the Father for all who are His (Romans 8:34, Hebrews 7:25). The Holy Spirit intercedes within believers with groans too deep for words (Romans 8:26). Intercession is the ministry of the Cross continued in prayer.

📜 Webster 1828 Definition

INTERCESSION, n. The act of interceding; mediation; interposition between parties at variance, with a view to reconcile them; prayer or petition to God in behalf of another. "He made intercession for the transgressors." Isaiah 53:12.

⚠️ Modern Corruption

Modern Christianity has reduced intercession to a bullet point on a prayer list — a brief mention of someone's name followed by a request. Biblical intercession, however, involved fasting, weeping, and persistent pressing before God (Daniel 9, Nehemiah 1). The culture has further corrupted it by replacing prayer with activism: "sending thoughts," signing petitions, or publicly advocating — all human effort substituted for the divine transaction of standing before the Throne.

📖 Key Scripture

Romans 8:26 — "The Spirit himself intercedes for us with groanings too deep for words."

Romans 8:34 — "Christ Jesus is the one who died — more than that, who was raised — who is at the right hand of God, who indeed is interceding for us."

Hebrews 7:25 — "He is able to save to the uttermost those who draw near to God through him, since he always lives to make intercession for them."

1 Timothy 2:1 — "I urge that supplications, prayers, intercessions, and thanksgivings be made for all people."

Isaiah 53:12 — "He poured out his soul to death and was numbered with the transgressors; yet he bore the sin of many, and makes intercession for the transgressors."

🔗 Greek & Hebrew Roots

G1783 — enteuxis (ἔντευξις): petition, intercession; an approach or meeting with a superior on behalf of another.

G5241 — hyperentynchanō (ὑπερεντυγχάνω): to intercede with exceeding earnestness; used only of the Spirit's intercession in Romans 8:26.

H6293 — pāga' (פָּגַע): to meet, reach, entreat; used for intercession and for the Servant's bearing of sin (Isaiah 53:12).

✍️ Usage

A man of God does not merely pray about his children — he intercedes for them, standing before God as a priest of his household.

The church's greatest weapon is not political influence but prevailing intercession — the sustained, faith-filled cry of believers before the Throne of Grace.

Christ's intercession is not past; it is present and perpetual. Every moment, the Son pleads the blood before the Father on behalf of every believer.
