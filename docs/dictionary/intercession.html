Intercession
/ˌɪn.təˈses.ən/
noun
From Latin intercessio — a going between, a mediation; from intercedere (inter + cedere), to go between, to intervene on behalf of another. Hebrew: pāgaʿ (H6293) — to meet, encounter, reach; to lay a burden upon another, to plead. Greek: entynchanō (G1793) — to meet with, to plead for; to make petition on behalf of another.

📖 Biblical Definition

Intercession is the act of standing before God on behalf of another — pleading for their need, their sin, their situation. It is at the heart of priestly ministry: the Old Testament high priest entered the holy of holies carrying the names of the tribes on his breastplate. Christ is the supreme intercessor — He ever lives to make intercession for His people (Heb. 7:25). The Holy Spirit also intercedes for us with groanings too deep for words (Rom. 8:26). Intercession by believers is an expression of love in prayer — standing in the gap (Ezek. 22:30) for others before the throne of grace.

📜 Webster 1828 Definition

INTERCESSION, n. Mediation; interposition between parties at variance, with a view to reconciliation; prayer, petition or entreaty in favor of another. The intercession of Christ constitutes one of his most important offices. He was made a priest after the order of Melchizedek, and his intercession for sinners is perpetual and efficacious. "He ever liveth to make intercession for them." Hebrews 7:25.

⚠️ Modern Corruption

In much contemporary Christianity, intercession is treated as either a special gift reserved for a small cadre of "prayer warriors" or reduced to vague "thoughts and prayers" with no real expectation of divine action. Catholic tradition risks compounding error by directing intercession through saints and Mary — adding human mediators where Christ alone stands between man and God (1 Tim. 2:5). The biblical model is bold: Christ has opened the way into the holy of holies (Heb. 10:19), and every believer can approach the throne of grace directly — and is called to do so on behalf of others.

📖 Key Scripture

• Hebrews 7:25 — "He is able to save to the uttermost those who draw near to God through him, since he always lives to make intercession for them."

• Romans 8:26 — "The Spirit himself intercedes for us with groanings too deep for words."

• 1 Timothy 2:1 — "I urge that supplications, prayers, intercessions, and thanksgivings be made for all people."

• Ezekiel 22:30 — "I sought for a man among them who should build up the wall and stand in the breach before me for the land."

• John 17:9–23 — Christ's High Priestly Prayer: the supreme model of intercession for His people.
