Intercession
/ ˌin-tər-ˈse-shən /
noun
From Latin intercessio — a going between, mediation; inter- (between) + cedere (to go, yield); Hebrew paga (פָּגַע) — to meet, to intercede, to fall upon; Greek enteuxis (ἔντευξις) — a petition, an appeal made on behalf of another

📖 Biblical Definition

Intercession is prayer offered on behalf of another — standing in the gap between a holy God and those in need of His mercy, grace, or intervention. Moses interceded for rebellious Israel (Exodus 32:11-14) and God relented. Abraham interceded for Sodom (Genesis 18). Isaiah 53:12 prophesies of the Suffering Servant that "he bore the sin of many, and makes intercession for the transgressors." The New Testament reveals a magnificent intercessory structure: Christ is the perfect Intercessor at the Father's right hand (Romans 8:34; Hebrews 7:25), and the Holy Spirit intercedes within believers "with groanings too deep for words" (Romans 8:26). Believers are called to intercede for one another, for rulers, and for all people (1 Timothy 2:1).

📜 Webster 1828 Definition

INTERCES'SION, n. The act of interceding; mediation; interposition between parties at variance, with a view to reconciliation; prayer, petition or entreaty in favor of another. In theology, the prayer of Christ in favor of his disciples and of sinners; also, the prayer of saints in favor of others.

⚠️ Modern Corruption

Roman Catholicism has extended intercession to deceased saints and to Mary as "mediatrix," contradicting 1 Timothy 2:5: "there is one mediator between God and men, the man Christ Jesus." Praying to the dead has no biblical basis and diverts intercession from the one fully qualified to act on it. On the Protestant side, intercession has been reduced to a polite inclusion in prayer meetings — something tacked onto personal petition — rather than treated as the serious, faith-demanding work the Bible presents it to be. True intercession changes things. It is the prayer that bends history (James 5:16-18).

📖 Key Scripture

Exodus 32:11–14 — Moses intercedes for Israel and God relents from destroying them.

Isaiah 53:12 — "He bore the sin of many, and makes intercession for the transgressors."

Romans 8:26 — "The Spirit himself intercedes for us with groanings too deep for words."

Hebrews 7:25 — Christ "always lives to make intercession" for those who draw near to God through Him.

1 Timothy 2:1 — "I urge that supplications, prayers, intercessions… be made for all people."
