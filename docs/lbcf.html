About this confession

The 1689 London Baptist Confession of Faith was the work of English Reformed Baptist pastors who met privately during the years of state persecution and published it openly in 1689 once the Toleration Act made it legal. The confession affirms the same Reformed soteriology as the Westminster Confession, but parts company on the doctrines of the church, baptism, and the role of the civil magistrate in matters of religion.
