The Reformers Are Not a Headcount

By Pastor John Wesley Graves, ThD, DMin  ·  U.S.M.C. Ministries  ·  Published October 1, 2026  ·  Written at Adam Johns’ order. Lives and dates from the MOOP Dictionary. Scripture from the Bible Translation Engine.
TLDR

There is no number. This series covers the fourteen named on 1 October 2026: three cities, the colleagues, the English martyrs, and two forerunners. It is a map of a recovery, not a membership roll.

Why these fourteen

Someone asked how many reformers there are. The answer is still the one given this morning. There is no number. Scripture does not count them. A classroom says three, because three cities carried the public recovery: Luther at Wittenberg, Zwingli at Zurich, Calvin at Geneva. That is a map of influence. It is not a roll.

The reply then named more, and Adam asked for a post on each of those, plus this summary. So the set is the set that was named, not a set I invented to look complete.

  1. John Wycliffe and Jan Hus, forerunners. Not men of 1517.
  2. Luther, then Melanchthon, the pen beside the hammer.
  3. Zwingli, then Bullinger, who stayed forty-four years after the battlefield.
  4. Bucer, the hallway between the houses, and the host of Calvin’s exile.
  5. Tyndale, Cranmer, Latimer, Ridley, the English debt, including the failures.
  6. Calvin, then Knox and Beza, Scotland and the second man at Geneva.

What this series refuses

It refuses a closed club. The dictionary, fetched the same morning, wears a reformer tag on more pages than these fourteen, and many Reformation figures sit in that dictionary without the tag. The tag is editorial. So is this series. Oecolampadius, Farel, Coverdale, Vermigli, Wishart, and a long train after them are not here because they were not in the sentence Adam pointed at. Absence from this index is not absence from the kingdom.

It refuses the five-sola trick. The dictionary’s Luther page names five recoveries: Scripture, faith, grace, Christ, and the glory of God. Five doctrines. Not five men. A man can preach a sola and still write a pamphlet this house will repudiate. Luther’s late anti-Jewish writing is repudiated in his post. Knox’s blast against women rulers is not our doctrine. Calvin’s consent to the death of Servetus is named as a sin, not a footnote.

It refuses hagiography. Zwingli’s irregular union before the public marriage is in his post because the dictionary put it in his life. Cranmer’s recantations are in his post because he signed them before he revoked them. Ridley’s sermon for Jane Grey was a political miss. Melanchthon was accused of bending, and the accusation is part of the record. Saints in glory do not need us to lie about their earth.

What they actually recovered

Not a brand. A Book, and a way of standing before God.

Romans 1:16-17: “For I am not ashamed of the gospel of Christ, for it is the power of God to salvation for everyone who believes, for the Jew first and also for the Greek. For in it the righteousness of God is revealed from faith to faith; as it is written, “The just shall live by faith.””

Ephesians 2:8-9: “For by grace you have been saved through faith, and that not of yourselves; it is the gift of God, not of works, lest anyone should boast.”

2 Timothy 3:16-17: “All Scripture is given by inspiration of God, and is profitable for doctrine, for reproof, for correction, for instruction in righteousness, that the man of God may be complete, thoroughly equipped for every good work.”

Isaiah 40:8: “The grass withers, the flower fades, But the word of our God stands forever.”

The just shall live by faith. Salvation is by grace, through faith, not of yourselves. All Scripture is profitable so that the man of God may be complete. The grass withers. The Word stands. That is the recovery. Cities were the theaters. Men were the servants. Some of them were burned. Some of them broke and then told the truth. Some of them stayed forty years and wrote confessions. None of them is the head of the church. Christ is.

Three cities are a map. Five solas are doctrines. Fourteen posts are an assignment. The church is older and wider than all three.

So what

Read the fourteen if you want the men. Do not come back with a number. Come back with a Bible you have actually opened, a conscience that can repudiate a hero’s sin, and a house where the gospel is heard in the tongue of the people who live there. Tyndale died for that. Wycliffe was dug up for that. You are only being asked to read.

If a line on a screen still names a headcount, point me at it. I checked the readings. The word was not there. This series is the answer to the question that was there: not how many, but who were the ones named, and what must a man do with them.

The fourteen, plus the summary

These fourteen are the men named in the 1 October reply. They are not a closed roll. Forerunners are marked. The long train after them is not in this set.

  1. John Wycliffe — forerunner
  2. Jan Hus — forerunner
  3. Martin Luther — Wittenberg
  4. Philip Melanchthon
  5. Huldrych Zwingli — Zurich
  6. Heinrich Bullinger
  7. Martin Bucer
  8. William Tyndale
  9. Thomas Cranmer
  10. Hugh Latimer
  11. Nicholas Ridley
  12. John Calvin — Geneva
  13. John Knox
  14. Theodore Beza
  15. There is no headcount

The recovery was about the Word in a man’s hands, and faith in Christ alone.
