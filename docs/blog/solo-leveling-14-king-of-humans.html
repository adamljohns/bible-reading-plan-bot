Biblical Manhood · Solo Leveling · Part 14

Are You the King of Humans?
The One Worthy Man

By Adam "MOOP" Johns  ·  U.S.M.C. Ministries  ·  Written for my son Gideon  ·  July 2026

Gideon, last week we watched the strongest men on earth fall on Jeju Island, and I told you that every human champion has an expiration date. This week the story turns that hard truth into a question, and it is one of the most important questions a man will ever be asked. So sit down close, because I want you to hear it the way I heard it, and then I want to show you Who has already answered it.

Here is where the story stands. The giant ants have overrun Jeju, and above them all stands their king, a creature of terrifying power. But the Ant King is not merely rampaging. He is hunting. As he tears through the mightiest hunters humanity can throw at him, he keeps searching the wreckage with something like disappointment, looking for one human strong enough to be worth his attention. He wants a real fight. He wants to know if there is anyone on this whole planet worthy to stand across from him. And so, as the smoke rises off the island, he asks the question that names this whole chapter: is there a king of the humans, or are you all just prey?

The Question the Whole World Is Asking

Son, I want you to notice that the Ant King's question is not a weird monster question. It is the oldest question there is, dressed up in armor. Underneath every empire and every arena and every stadium, the human race has always been asking the same thing: is there a worthy man? Is there anyone who measures up? Is there one who can stand where the rest of us fall?

We ask it about presidents and generals. We ask it about the strong man in the gym and the smart man in the office and the brave man in the firefight. We build statues to men we hope were worthy, and then we read their biographies and find the cracks, every time. Because here is the thing the Ant King is about to learn the hard way, and the thing I need you to learn young: measured against the full weight of what "worthy" really means, not one of us passes. Scripture says it flat out, that when God searched the earth for a righteous man, He found none (Romans 3:10). The island is bigger than Jeju, Son. The whole earth is the arena, and the whole race has been weighed.

There is a scene in the Bible that reads almost exactly like the Ant King's hunt, except it happens in the throne room of heaven, and the stakes are not one island but the entire future of the world. A mighty angel stands up and calls out across all creation, hunting for one worthy man. And the answer that comes back is the most crushing silence in the whole book:

“And I saw a strong angel proclaiming with a loud voice: "Who is worthy to open the scroll and to break its seals?" And no one in heaven, nor on earth, nor under the earth, was able to open the scroll or to look into it. And I began to weep greatly, because no one was found worthy to open the scroll or to look into it.” (Revelation 5:2-4)

Read that weeping, Gideon, because it is honest. The apostle John is watching the same search the Ant King is running, and coming up empty, and it breaks his heart. No one steps forward. No president, no general, no philosopher, no priest, no strong man on any island anywhere. The arena goes quiet. If the story stopped there, it would be the most hopeless verse ever written.

Jinwoo Steps Onto the Beach

Back on Jeju, the story does not stop there either. Just as the Ant King is deciding that humanity has nothing to offer him, a man walks onto the shore. Jinwoo has come, the fight the whole season has been building toward finally beginning. Someone has stepped into the arena. For a moment the show lets you feel the relief of it: at last, here is a man who might actually be worthy to stand across from the king.

And Son, I love that beat, and I want you to love it too, because that longing is planted in us by God. We were made to look for the worthy man to walk onto the beach. But I have to tell you the truth about Jinwoo, the same truth I told you last week about all the fallen champions. He is a shadow. A powerful, thrilling shadow, but a shadow. He walks onto that shore already having died to his old self, already raised into new strength, already collecting the fallen and making them his own. Do you hear it? The story keeps reaching for the shape of a Savior and can only ever sketch it. Jinwoo can win a fight on an island. He cannot answer the angel in the throne room. There is only One who can do that, and He is the reason the beach scene stirs you at all.

The One Worthy Man

Go back to heaven with me, to that unbearable silence where no one was found worthy. Watch what happens next, because this is the hinge of the whole Bible and the whole of your life:

“And one of the elders said to me, "Stop weeping. Behold, the Lion that is from the tribe of Judah — the Root of David — has overcome, so as to open the scroll and its seven seals."” (Revelation 5:5)

Do not weep. There is a Man. The search that swept heaven and earth and came up empty was not empty after all, because there is One who steps forward, the Lion of the tribe of Judah, Jesus Christ. He does not walk onto a beach; He walks out of a tomb. And when John turns to see this conquering Lion, he sees a Lamb who was slain, and all of heaven falls down and sings that He alone is worthy. That is the answer to the Ant King's question and to yours and to mine. Are you the king of humans? No. But He is. There is exactly One worthy Man, and His name is Jesus.

Why Him and no one else? Because He is the second Adam. The first Adam was our king, our head, the man who stood for all of us, and he failed; he was found unworthy, and we fell in him. But God sent a second Man to stand in the arena the first one lost, and this Man did not fall:

“For as through the one man's disobedience the many were made sinners — even so through the obedience of the One, the many will be made righteous.” (Romans 5:19)

That is the whole gospel in one line, Son. One man's disobedience sank us; one Man's obedience raises us. And Paul draws the two heads up side by side so you can never forget which King you belong to:

“For as in Adam all die — so also in Christ all will be made alive.” (1 Corinthians 15:22)

The Ant King wanted to know if there was a human worthy to stand across from a king. Heaven had already answered before Jeju ever burned. There is a Man on the throne right now, a real Man with real scars, crowned over all creation, worthy. This is exactly the wonder that made an old poet stare up at the night sky and marvel that God would put such honor on the sons of men:

“what is man, that You take thought of him? And the son of man, that You care for him? Yet You have made him a little lower than the angels — and You crown him with glory and majesty. You make him to rule over the works of Your hands. You have put all things under his feet —” (Psalm 8:4-6)

The writer of Hebrews takes that very psalm and tells us we do not yet see everything under man's feet the way it was meant to be; but we do see Jesus, crowned with glory and honor, the one Man in whom the psalm comes true. The crown the first Adam dropped is on the head of the second Adam, and He wears it worthily. He is the King of humans.

What We Confess

Our fathers in the faith did not leave this to a feeling; they nailed it down in ink so that no smooth talker could ever talk your inheritance away from you. The 1689 London Baptist Confession, in chapter 8, "Of Christ the Mediator," tells you exactly who this worthy Man is and why He alone could fill the office:

Of Christ the Mediator.
"It pleased God, in His eternal purpose, to choose and ordain the Lord Jesus, His only begotten Son, to be the Mediator between God and man; the Prophet, Priest, and King; the Head and Saviour of the Church, the Heir of all things, and Judge of the world... The Lord Jesus, in His human nature thus united to the divine, was sanctified and anointed with the Holy Spirit above measure... to the end that being holy, harmless, undefiled, and full of grace and truth, He might be thoroughly furnished to execute the office of a Mediator and Surety." (1689 LBCF 8.1–3)

Hear what that confession is guarding, Son. Jesus is not the best of a bad lot of human kings. He is God the Son taking on real human nature, holy and harmless and undefiled, so that a true Man, our Man, could finally stand in the arena and win. Prophet, Priest, and King. When the angel cries "who is worthy?" the answer is not a committee and not a hero we hope in and lose. It is Him. He was found worthy, and because you are hidden in Him by faith, His worthiness is counted as yours. You will never have to walk onto that beach and prove you measure up. Your King already did.

Father & Son Talk

  • The Ant King wanted to know if there was a human worthy to stand against him. Where in your own heart are you still trying to prove that you are worthy, strong enough, good enough, on your own?
  • John wept because no one was found worthy, and then the elder said, "Do not weep, there is a Lion." How does it change your week to know the search for a worthy man is already over, and He won?
  • The first Adam was our king and fell; the second Adam is our King and stands. What does it mean for you today that you belong to the second one, not the first?

Today's Quest

Read Revelation 5 slowly, out loud if you can, the whole chapter. Do not rush it. Sit in the silence where no one is found worthy, and feel John's tears; then read on to the Lion and the Lamb, and watch heaven erupt in worship because the worthy Man has come. When you finish, close your eyes and tell the King, in your own words, that you are done trying to be worthy on your own and you are trusting His worthiness instead. That is what the whole scroll was waiting to hear.

Gideon, the world will spend your whole life asking the Ant King's question, hunting for a worthy man and coming up short, and it will keep pointing you to the newest strong man on the newest beach. Do not fall for it. You already know the answer that swept heaven and earth and came back with exactly one name. There is a King of humans. He is a real Man with nail scars in real hands, and He was found worthy when no one else in all creation could be. Bow to Him, follow Him, and one day you will see Him crowned with your own eyes. Until then, walk like a son of the worthy King, because that is what you are.

“And I saw, in the midst of the throne and the four living creatures, and in the midst of the elders, a Lamb standing — as if slain — having seven horns and seven eyes, which are the seven Spirits of God sent out into all the earth. And He came and took the scroll out of the right hand of the One sitting on the throne. And when He took the scroll, the four living creatures and the twenty-four elders fell down before the Lamb, each having a harp and golden bowls full of incense — which are the prayers of the saints. And they sang a new song, saying: "Worthy are You to take the scroll and to break its seals — for You were slain, and You purchased for God by Your blood people from every tribe, tongue, people, and nation.” (Revelation 5:6-9)
