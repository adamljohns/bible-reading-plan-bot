Heinrich Bullinger: Forty-Four Years After the Battlefield

By Pastor John Wesley Graves, ThD, DMin  ·  U.S.M.C. Ministries  ·  Published October 1, 2026  ·  Written at Adam Johns’ order. Lives and dates from the MOOP Dictionary. Scripture from the Bible Translation Engine.
TLDR

Heinrich Bullinger (1504–1575) led Zurich for forty-four years after Kappel. Decades, Consensus Tigurinus with Calvin, Second Helvetic Confession. The unifying voice is not the loudest voice.

The man who stayed

When Zwingli died at Kappel, Zurich could have folded. Heinrich Bullinger is the reason it did not. 1504 to 1575. Trained with the Brethren of the Common Life at Emmerich and at Cologne. Converted through Luther’s books and through Scripture itself. Ordained in 1528. Pastor at Hausen and Bremgarten. Called as Antistes, head pastor, in December 1531. He kept that post until he died. Forty-four years.

That is the part of the Reformation a headline cannot use. No theses on a door. No stake. A man who buries the founder and then preaches for half a century until the doctrine has a spine.

What he actually built

The dictionary names the work, and I will not inflate it. The Decades, five volumes of sermons, 1549 to 1551, the pastoral compendium of that Reformed world. The Consensus Tigurinus of 1549, the Supper agreement with Calvin that kept the Swiss from spending the next generation on Marburg. The Second Helvetic Confession of 1566, the principal Reformed confession of that hour, taken up widely. More than twelve thousand surviving letters. Refugee scholars housed, among them Peter Martyr Vermigli. Zurich stood with Wittenberg and Geneva because a pastor stayed.

2 Timothy 3:16-17: “All Scripture is given by inspiration of God, and is profitable for doctrine, for reproof, for correction, for instruction in righteousness, that the man of God may be complete, thoroughly equipped for every good work.”

Psalm 119:105: “Your word is a lamp to my feet And a light to my path.”

A recovery that cannot survive the founder’s funeral was a personality, not a church.

So what

You do not have to be the man who starts the thing. You may be the man who keeps it after the funeral, the deployment, the split. Stay. Write it down so a younger elder can preach it when your voice is gone. Unite where the gospel is the same, and do not call every difference a war. Bullinger’s long post is a better model for most husbands and pastors than Luther’s hammer or Knox’s trumpet.

The fourteen, plus the summary

These fourteen are the men named in the 1 October reply. They are not a closed roll. Forerunners are marked. The long train after them is not in this set.

  4. Philip Melanchthon
  5. Huldrych Zwingli — Zurich

The recovery was about the Word in a man’s hands, and faith in Christ alone.
