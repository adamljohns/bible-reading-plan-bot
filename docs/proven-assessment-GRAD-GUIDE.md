## What P.R.O.V.E.N. means

Six axes. Whole-life after-action review (AAR) for course grads.

| | Axis | What you’re scoring |
|---|------|---------------------|
| **P** | **Passionate for God** | Fire for the Lord — not cool indifference |
| **R** | **Repentant in spirit** | Real turn, not vague guilt |
| **O** | **Open & Honest** | Accountability that can handle hard questions |
| **V** | **Victorious living** | Body and spirit under discipline, not drift |
| **E** | **Eternal perspective** | Earthly work aimed heavenward |
| **N** | **Networking with PROVEN men** | Brotherhood that sharpens; not lone-ranger faith |

Score each 1–10. Rubrics sit under every slider. Radar shows the picture. Formation plan hits your **three weakest** axes with micro-habits, prayer, and reading.

Scores, XP, and history stay **on this device only**. Re-take monthly as a course-grad cadence.

## How to run the mission timer

1. Open the assessment page.
2. **Mission Briefing** — pick a clock:
   - **18 min** — standard AAR (default)
   - **12 min** — focused sprint
   - **30 min** — deep work
   - **No clock** — self-paced
3. Hit **Begin PROVEN Mission**. The HUD sticks under the nav: ring, MM:SS, XP, axis dots.
4. Work the hard questions while the clock runs. Wall-clock time (`Date.now` end) — not a fake spinner.
5. HUD controls:
   - **Pause / Resume**
   - **+5 min** if you need more runway
   - Under **2 minutes** remaining → urgent (red) state
6. Finish before zero → **Under Fire Finish** badge. Then generate **Formation + Reading Plan**.

Do not game the timer. The clock is pressure for honesty, not a high score.

## How to share results

1. After **Generate Formation + Reading Plan**, open **Share with a PROVEN Brother**.
2. Copy the AAR text (no name unless you add one).
3. Send it to your accountability partner, small group, or Adam **before the next huddle**.
4. Optional: **Print Summary** and bring it to mentoring / counseling intake.

Formation without brotherhood dies in private. Share the weak flanks, not just the green axes.
