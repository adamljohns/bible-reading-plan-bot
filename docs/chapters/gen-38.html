  13. And it was told Tamar, saying, "Look, your father-in-law is going up to Timnah to shear his sheep."
  14. So she took off her widow's garments, covered herself with a veil and wrapped herself, and sat at the entrance to Enaim which was on the road to Timnah, for she saw that Shelah had grown up and she had not been given to him in marriage.

  19. So she arose and went away, and removed her veil and put on her widow's garments.
