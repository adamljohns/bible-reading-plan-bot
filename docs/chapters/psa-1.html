  1. Blessed is the man who walks not in the counsel of the wicked, nor stands in the path of sinners, nor sits in the seat of scoffers.
  2. But his delight is in the law of the LORD, and in His law he meditates day and night.
  3. He shall be like a tree planted by the rivers of water, that brings forth its fruit in its season, whose leaf also shall not wither; and whatever he does shall prosper.
  4. The wicked are not so, but are like the chaff which the wind drives away.
  5. Therefore the wicked will not stand in the judgment, nor sinners in the congregation of the righteous.
  6. For the LORD knows the way of the righteous, but the way of the wicked shall perish.
