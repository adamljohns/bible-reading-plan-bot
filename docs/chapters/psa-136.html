  1. Oh, give thanks to the LORD, for He is good! For His faithful love endures forever.
  2. Oh, give thanks to the God of gods! For His faithful love endures forever.
  3. Oh, give thanks to the Lord of lords! For His faithful love endures forever.
  4. To Him who alone does great wonders, for His faithful love endures forever;
  5. To Him who by wisdom made the heavens, for His faithful love endures forever;
  6. To Him who laid out the earth above the waters, for His faithful love endures forever;
  7. To Him who made great lights, for His faithful love endures forever—
  8. The sun to rule by day, for His faithful love endures forever;
  9. The moon and stars to rule by night, for His faithful love endures forever.
  10. To Him who struck Egypt in their firstborn, for His faithful love endures forever;
  11. And brought out Israel from among them, for His faithful love endures forever;
  12. With a strong hand, and with an outstretched arm, for His faithful love endures forever;
  13. To Him who divided the Red Sea in two, for His faithful love endures forever;
  14. And made Israel pass through the midst of it, for His faithful love endures forever;
  15. But overthrew Pharaoh and his army in the Red Sea, for His faithful love endures forever;
  16. To Him who led His people through the wilderness, for His faithful love endures forever;
  17. To Him who struck down great kings, for His faithful love endures forever;
  18. And slew famous kings, for His faithful love endures forever—
  19. Sihon king of the Amorites, for His faithful love endures forever;
  20. And Og king of Bashan, for His faithful love endures forever—
  21. And gave their land as a heritage, for His faithful love endures forever;
  22. A heritage to Israel His servant, for His faithful love endures forever.
  23. Who remembered us in our lowly state, for His faithful love endures forever;
  24. And rescued us from our enemies, for His faithful love endures forever.
  25. He gives food to all flesh, for His faithful love endures forever.
  26. Oh, give thanks to the God of heaven! For His faithful love endures forever.
