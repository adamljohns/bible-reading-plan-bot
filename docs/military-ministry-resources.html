Military Ministry Resources

Organizations serving military personnel, veterans, and families through faith-based discipleship, fellowship, and support.
Compiled by U.S.M.C. Ministries · Updated March 27, 2026

🤝 Personal Connections (Moop's Network)

Navigators Military
navigators.org/ministry/navigators-military
Life-to-Life® discipleship, small group Bible studies, and family support for active duty, National Guard, and ROTC. One of the original military discipleship ministries — deeply relational, focused on making disciples who make disciples.
Personal connection — Moop has history with the Navigators
Personal Connection
Officers' Christian Fellowship (OCF)
ocfusa.org
Ministry to military officers and their families since 1943. Bible studies, conferences, small groups, and leadership development for commissioned officers across all branches. Known for deep theological grounding and officer-specific discipleship.
Personal connection — Moop has been involved with OCF
Personal Connection
The Traveling Team / Titus Task Force (TTF)
ttf.org
Mobilizing Christians toward global missions with a focus on the unreached. Moop has been close with TTF for years — a missions-minded organization that connects military believers to the Great Commission worldwide.
Personal connection — close relationship for years
Personal Connection
Cru Military Ministry
cru.org/communities/military
Part of Cru (formerly Campus Crusade for Christ). Reaches military personnel at installations worldwide through Bible studies, retreats, marriage enrichment, and evangelistic outreach. Strong emphasis on evangelism and spiritual multiplication.
Personal connection — Moop has worked with Cru Military
Personal Connection
Cadence International
cadence.org
Hospitality houses near military installations worldwide. Cadence missionaries open their homes to service members for meals, Bible studies, mentoring, and genuine community. A unique model — incarnational ministry through radical hospitality.
Personal connection — Moop has been close with Cadence

🦅 Veteran Service Organizations

Marine Corps League
mcleaguelibrary.org
Founded 1923 by MajGen Lejeune. Congressionally chartered 1937. Preserves USMC traditions, strengthens Marine fraternity, fosters Americanism and patriotic volunteerism. 501(c)(4) nationwide.
Local: Jack Maas Detachment 1379, Fredericksburg VA (est. 2011). Hosting VA Dept Spring Convention May 29-30, 2026 in FXBG.

Operation We Are Here
operationwearehere.com
Comprehensive resource hub connecting churches with military ministry resources, tools, and best practices for serving military families in their congregations.
