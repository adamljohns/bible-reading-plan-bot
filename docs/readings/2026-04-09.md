## Morning Wisdom — Proverbs 9:1-12

**REAL MAN Letter:** A — Accountability

⚓ MORNING WISDOM — Thursday, April 9, 2026

📖 Scripture — Proverbs 9:1-12

Wisdom has built her house; she has hewn out its seven pillars. She has prepared her meat, mixed her wine, and set her table. She has sent out her servants, and she calls from the highest point of the city: "Let all who are simple come to my house!" To those who have no sense she says, "Come, eat my food and drink the wine I have mixed. Leave your simple ways and you will live; walk in the way of insight."

Instruct the wise and they will be wiser still; teach the righteous and they will add to their learning. The fear of the LORD is the beginning of wisdom, and knowledge of the Holy One is understanding. For through wisdom your days will be many, and years will be added to your life. If you are wise, your wisdom will reward you; if you are a mocker, you alone will suffer.

🧭 Context: Solomon personifies Wisdom as a woman who builds, prepares, and invites. This isn't passive knowledge sitting on a shelf — it's active, generous, and public. Wisdom goes looking for people who need her.

🛡️ REAL MAN — A is for ACCOUNTABILITY: A man who can receive correction is a man who is still growing. Accountability isn't weakness — it's the mechanism by which wise men stay wise.

⛏️ Application: Identify an accountability partner. Receive correction without defending. Set the table for your family. Audit your inner circle.

🙏 Prayer: Lord, make me a man who can be corrected. Kill my pride before it kills my growth.

⚓ Helm Command: "Instruct the wise and they will be wiser still." — Today, receive one correction without defending yourself.

## 2nd Watch — Father's Charge

⚓ **2nd Watch — Father's Charge** | 1100 Hours
📅 Thursday, April 9, 2026
📖 **Scripture: Judges 14**

And Samson went down to Timnath, and saw a woman in Timnath of the daughters of the Philistines. And he came up, and told his father and his mother, and said, I have seen a woman in Timnath of the daughters of the Philistines: now therefore get her for me to wife. Then his father and his mother said unto him, Is there never a woman among the daughters of thy brethren, or among all my people, that thou goest to take a wife of the uncircumcised Philistines? And Samson said unto his father, Get her for me; for she pleaseth me well.

But his father and his mother knew not that it was of the LORD, that he sought an occasion against the Philistines: for at that time the Philistines had dominion over Israel. Then went Samson down, and his father and his mother, to Timnath, and came to the vineyards of Timnath: and, behold, a young lion roared against him. And the Spirit of the LORD came mightily upon him, and he rent him as he would have rent a kid, and he had nothing in his hand: but he told not his father or his mother what he had done.

And he went down, and talked with the woman; and she pleased Samson well. And after a time he returned to take her, and he turned aside to see the carcase of the lion: and, behold, there was a swarm of bees and honey in the carcase of the lion. And he took thereof in his hands, and went on eating, and came to his father and mother, and he gave them, and they did eat: but he told not them that he had taken the honey out of the carcase of the lion.

🧭 Context: Judges 14 opens the Samson saga in earnest. A man set apart from birth — a Nazarite, consecrated by God — begins making choices driven by what "pleaseth" his eyes. His parents push back. They counsel him. They ask the hard question: "Is there not a woman among your own people?" But Samson insists. What we see here is a father who spoke up, who walked with his son even into a decision he disagreed with, and a son whose gifting was extraordinary but whose discipline was fragile. God was working behind the scenes, but Manoah's grief as a father was real.

👨‍👧‍👦 **F.U.L.F.I.L.L.E.D. Father Reflection**

**F — Faithfulness in the Hard Conversations.** Manoah did not stay silent. He told his son the truth. A father's job is not to win the argument — it is to make sure your sons and daughters heard the truth from YOUR mouth before the world gave them its version.

**U — Understanding That Gifting Is Not Character.** Samson could tear a lion apart with his bare hands. He could not govern his own appetites. Father, never confuse your child's talent with their maturity. A son who can dominate the field but cannot control his eyes is not ready. A daughter who excels in every room but crumbles under pressure needs more from you, not less.

**L — Leading by Presence.** Manoah went with Samson to Timnath. He did not approve of the mission, but he did not abandon his son to it either. Sometimes fatherhood means walking beside your children into their bad decisions — not to endorse them, but so they are not alone when consequences arrive.

**F — Forming Boundaries That Outlast You.** The Nazarite vow was a boundary God set. But Samson treated it like a suggestion. What boundaries have you established in your home? Are they living realities your sons and daughters can articulate, or are they rules they have already learned to work around?

**I — Investing in Their Discernment.** "Get her for me, for she pleaseth me well." That is a man making decisions by appetite, not by wisdom. Father, are you training your children to evaluate by character, by covenant, by calling — or are you letting the culture train them to evaluate by appearance and feeling?

**L — Lamenting What You Cannot Control.** Manoah could not stop Samson. There comes a point where your children will choose. Grieve when they choose poorly — but do not stop praying and do not stop showing up.

**L — Leaving the Outcome to God.** Scripture tells us plainly: this was of the LORD. Manoah could not see it. You will not always see it either. Father, do your part faithfully and leave the sovereignty to the One who holds your children closer than you ever could.

**E — Enduring as a Witness.** Manoah's name means "rest." The irony is thick — this father had no rest watching his son's choices. But he endured. He stayed. That is the call. Not comfort. Endurance.

**D — Declaring the Standard Even When It Is Rejected.** "Is there never a woman among thy brethren?" Manoah asked the question. Samson rejected it. The standard still stood. Father, keep declaring the standard. Your sons may roll their eyes. Your daughters may push back. Declare it anyway. Truth spoken faithfully has a longer shelf life than you think.

⛏️ **Application:** This week, have one honest conversation with your children about a hard topic — not to lecture, but to speak truth and then listen. Walk with them, even when you disagree with the direction. Set a boundary and explain why it exists. Pray for their discernment more than their success.

🙏 **Prayer:** Father God, give me the courage of Manoah — to speak the truth to my children even when they do not want to hear it. Give me the humility to walk beside them even when I cannot control them. Form in my sons and daughters a discernment that runs deeper than desire. And when I cannot see Your hand, help me trust Your sovereignty over the ones I love most. In Jesus' name. Amen.

⚓ **Helm Command:** "Is there never a woman among thy brethren?" — Today, declare the standard in your home. Speak truth. Walk with your children. Trust God with the outcome.
