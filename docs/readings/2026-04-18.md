## 🛡️ 2nd Watch — Father's Charge (1100)
**📖 1 Samuel 9:1–17 · KJV**

### Scripture
And there was a man of Benjamin, whose name was Kish, a mighty man of power. And he had a son, whose name was Saul, a choice young man, and a goodly: and there was not among the children of Israel a goodlier person than he: from his shoulders and upward he was higher than any of the people.

And the asses of Kish Saul's father were lost. And Kish said to Saul his son, Take now one of the servants with thee, and arise, go seek the asses. And he passed through mount Ephraim, and passed through the land of Shalisha, but they found them not: then they passed through the land of Shalim, and there they were not: and he passed through the land of the Benjamites, but they found them not.

And when they were come to the land of Zuph, Saul said to his servant that was with him, Come, and let us return; lest my father leave caring for the asses, and take thought for us. And the servant answered him and said, Behold now, there is in this city a man of God, and he is an honourable man; all that he saith cometh surely to pass: now let us go thither; peradventure he can shew us our way that we should go.

Then said Saul to his servant, But, behold, if we go, what shall we bring the man? Then answered the servant, I have here at hand the fourth part of a shekel of silver: that will I give to the man of God, to tell us our way. And Saul said unto his servant, Well said; come, let us go.

Now the LORD had told Samuel in his ear a day before Saul came, saying, To morrow about this time I will send thee a man out of the land of Benjamin, and thou shalt anoint him to be captain over my people Israel, that he may save my people out of the hand of the Philistines: for I have looked upon my people, because their cry is come unto me. And when Samuel saw Saul, the LORD said unto him, Behold the man whom I spake to thee of! this same shall reign over my people.

### Context
Yesterday the elders demanded a king. God granted their request — not as a blessing, but as a permission. Now watch how He works *within* human failure. Saul began his story on an ordinary errand — looking for his father's lost donkeys — not a throne. His father Kish simply sent him out to do a job. He didn't know what was waiting. God's hand was already moving before Saul took a single step.

### F.U.L.F.I.L.L.E.D. Reflection
- **F — Faithfulness in the Ordinary:** Kish sent his son on a mundane errand. That *is* fatherhood. Your sons and daughters won't receive their calling in a blinding flash of glory — they'll find it on the road you send them down.
- **U — Understanding the Weight:** Even in discouragement, Saul thought of his father. "Lest my father take thought for us." Your children are watching whether you carry that same tender weight for them.
- **L — Letting Them Go:** You can't walk the road for them. You send. You pray. You trust.
- **F — Forming Them for Counsel:** The servant's wisdom saved the mission. Raise children who know how to ask for wise counsel — and who honor the man of God when they meet him.
- **I — Identity Before Crown:** Saul was anointed before he knew it. Your child's identity in God is already written. Your job is to point them toward the Author.
- **L — Legacy Is a Road, Not a Destination:** Character is forged between your sending and their returning.
- **L — Listening to God First:** God told Samuel before Saul arrived. The Lord is working on your child's story before they take a step.
- **E — Equipping, Not Engineering:** Your job is not to control the outcome. It's to send them out right.
- **D — Discipline of Presence:** Stay in the fight — on the errand with them in spirit, even when they're miles away.

### Application
This week, assign your children a real task — something that requires persistence, asks them to seek help, and pushes them past the point where they want to quit. Don't rescue them from the difficulty. Equip them for it. Tell them to report back. Their character is forged on the road between your sending and their returning.

### Prayer
Father, You sent Your Son on a road that led to a cross — and beyond it, to a crown. Help us trust You with our sons and daughters the same way. Give us wisdom to send them out with purpose, courage to let them struggle without rescuing them too soon, and faith to believe that You are already moving on their road before they take a single step. Guard their way. Raise up wise counselors to speak into their lives. Bring them home to You. In Jesus' name, Amen.
