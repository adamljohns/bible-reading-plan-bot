## ✝ Morning Wisdom — Sunday, May 31st, 2026

**📖 Proverbs 31**

### 📖 Scripture
The words of King Lemuel, the utterance which his mother taught him: What, my son? And what, son of my womb? And what, son of my vows? Do not give your strength to women, nor your ways to that which destroys kings.

It is not for kings, O Lemuel, it is not for kings to drink wine, nor for princes intoxicating drink; lest they drink and forget the law, and pervert the justice of all the afflicted. Give strong drink to him who is perishing, and wine to those who are bitter of heart; let him drink and forget his poverty, and remember his misery no more.

Open your mouth for the speechless, in the cause of all who are appointed to die. Open your mouth, judge righteously, and plead the cause of the poor and needy.

Who can find a virtuous wife? For her worth is far above rubies. The heart of her husband safely trusts her; so he will have no lack of gain. She does him good and not evil all the days of her life. She seeks wool and flax, and willingly works with her hands. She is like the merchant ships; she brings her food from afar. She also rises while it is yet night, and provides food for her household, and a portion for her maidservants. She considers a field and buys it; from her profits she plants a vineyard. She girds herself with strength, and strengthens her arms. She perceives that her merchandise is good, and her lamp does not go out by night.

She stretches out her hands to the poor, yes, she reaches out her hands to the needy. She is not afraid of snow for her household, for all her household is clothed in scarlet. She makes tapestry for herself; her clothing is fine linen and purple. Her husband is known in the gates, when he sits among the elders of the land. She makes linen garments and sells them, and supplies sashes for the merchants.

Strength and honor are her clothing; she shall rejoice in time to come. She opens her mouth with wisdom, and on her tongue is the law of kindness. She watches over the ways of her household, and does not eat the bread of idleness. Her children rise up and call her blessed; her husband also, and he praises her: “Many daughters have done well, but you excel them all.”

Charm is deceitful and beauty is passing, but a woman who fears the Lord, she shall be praised. Give her of the fruit of her hands, and let her own works praise her in the gates.

### 🧭 Context
Proverbs 31 is a unique conclusion to the book of wisdom, featuring an oracle from King Lemuel's mother. It begins with a stern warning to leaders about the dangers of lust and intoxication, then pivots to an exaltation of the "virtuous wife"—a portrait of diligence, wisdom, and fear of the Lord.

### 🛡️ REAL MAN Reflection
**L — Lead Courageously (Day 31 mod 7 = 3)**

Leading courageously is not just about the bold charge into battle; it is about the steadfastness of a man who refuses to let his home or his character drift. King Lemuel was warned by his mother that the greatest threats to a leader's strength are not external enemies, but internal appetites—lust and excess. A man who cannot lead himself in the secret places of his heart is unfit to lead others in the public gates.

True courage is found in the discipline of "no." It is the strength to reject the distractions that destroy kings and the resolve to build a life centered on righteousness. When we see the virtuous woman in this passage, we see the fruit of a home where leadership is defined by service and wisdom. A courageous leader doesn't seek the applause of men or the fleeting charm of the world; he seeks the approval of God and the trust of his family. Stand your post, reject the poison of complacency, and lead with a heart anchored in the fear of the Lord.

### ⚒️ Application
• **Audit Your Appetites:** Identify one area of "excess" or distraction (digital, physical, or emotional) that is draining your strength and commit to cutting it out this week.
• **Protect the Gates:** Like Lemuel, ensure your leadership is not compromised by secret sins that would pervert your judgment or damage your integrity.
• **Honor the Virtuous:** If you have a woman of God in your life, explicitly praise her "fruit"—her works and her character—rather than just her appearance.
• **Advocate for the Weak:** Find one way today to "open your mouth" for someone who cannot speak for themselves.

### 🙏 Prayer
Father, I thank You for the warning and the wisdom found in Your Word. Grant me the strength to lead my home and my life with courage and discipline. Deliver me from the traps of lust and the fog of excess. Help me to be a man of integrity, whose strength is used for Your glory and the good of those I lead. May my life be a reflection of Your truth, and may I walk in the fear of the Lord that brings true praise. In Jesus' name, Amen.

### ✝ Helm Command
Reject the distractions that destroy kings; lead with a disciplined heart.

### 🧭 Context
Jehu is executing a divine judgment upon the house of Ahab and Jezebel, purging Israel of the idolatry of Baal. This passage depicts the ruthless efficiency of Jehu's purge and his insistence on absolute loyalty to the mission.

### 💍 H.H.A.A.P.P.Y. Husband Reflection
**Adoring (May 31st)**

To adore your wife is to see her through the lens of God's grace and to actively pursue her joy. In this passage, we see Jehu’s singular focus on a mission of judgment and purity. While the context is one of war and execution, the principle for the husband is clear: a man must have a singular focus. For the husband, that focus isn't the destruction of enemies, but the elevation of his wife.

Adoration is not merely a feeling; it is a disciplined action. It is the decision to look past the frictions of daily life—the laundry, the arguments over the schedule, the stresses of work—and see the daughter of the King. When a husband adores his wife, he creates an environment where she feels safe to bloom. He doesn't just "tolerate" her or "appreciate" her in a general sense; he actively pursues the things that make her feel cherished.

Think of your wife as a garden entrusted to you by God. If you neglect the soil or ignore the weeds of bitterness and exhaustion, the garden withers. But when you adore her—when you speak words of affirmation, anticipate her needs, and protect her heart—you are reflecting the way Christ adores His Bride, the Church. Adoration is the antidote to neglect. It transforms a marriage from a functional partnership into a sanctuary of love. Today, ask yourself: "Does my wife feel adored by me, or does she simply feel managed by me?"

### ⚒️ Application
- **Identify one specific quality** in your wife that you often take for granted and tell her exactly why you adore it today.
- **Perform a "silent service"**—something she needs done that you do without being asked and without seeking credit.
- **Guard her reputation**—speak highly of her to your children, your parents, and your friends, ensuring she is honored in the home.
- **Set aside 15 minutes** of undistracted time today just to listen to her heart, without trying to "fix" anything.

### 🙏 Prayer

Grant me eyes to see her beauty, a heart to cherish her soul, and the strength to lead our home in love. Protect her heart from anxiety and let her feel secure in my affection. May our marriage be a living testimony of Your grace. In Jesus' name, Amen.

### ✝ Helm Command
Stop managing your wife and start adoring her.
