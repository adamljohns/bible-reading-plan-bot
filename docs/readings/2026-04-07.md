
🌙 **Evening Peace — Psalm 86**

📖
Incline your ear, O Lord, and answer me, for I am poor and needy.
Preserve my life, for I am godly; save your servant, who trusts in you—you are my God.
Be gracious to me, O Lord, for to you do I cry all the day.
Gladden the soul of your servant, for to you, O Lord, do I lift up my soul.
For you, O Lord, are good and forgiving, abounding in steadfast love to all who call upon you.
Give ear, O Lord, to my prayer; listen to my plea for grace.
In the day of my trouble I call upon you, for you answer me.
There is none like you among the gods, O Lord, nor are there any works like yours.
All the nations you have made shall come and worship before you, O Lord, and shall glorify your name.
For you are great and do wondrous things; you alone are God.
Teach me your way, O Lord, that I may walk in your truth; unite my heart to fear your name.
I give thanks to you, O Lord my God, with my whole heart, and I will glorify your name forever.
For great is your steadfast love toward me; you have delivered my soul from the depths of Sheol.
O God, insolent men have risen up against me; a band of ruthless men seeks my life, and they do not set you before them.
But you, O Lord, are a God merciful and gracious, slow to anger and abounding in steadfast love and faithfulness.
Turn to me and be gracious to me; give your strength to your servant, and save the son of your maidservant.
Show me a sign of your favor, that those who hate me may see and be put to shame because you, Lord, have helped me and comforted me.

🛡️ **Evening Reflection: A Heart Inclined to Grace**
As husbands, fathers, and citizens, we often find ourselves carrying burdens—for our families, our work, and the communities we serve. Psalm 86 is a powerful reminder that in our weakness, we have a God who inclines His ear to us. He is merciful, gracious, slow to anger, and abounding in steadfast love. This Psalm calls us to lean into His strength, knowing that our trust in Him is never misplaced. Let us strive to walk in His truth, uniting our hearts to fear His name, so that our lives reflect His goodness to all those around us. In every role, let His steadfast love be our foundation and guide.

**Night Orders:**
*   **Reflect:** Consider one area where you need God's strength this week.
*   **Pray:** Lift up your family and community to the Lord, trusting in His steadfast love.
*   **Serve:** Look for an opportunity to demonstrate God's grace to someone tomorrow.

🙏 **Prayer:**
Heavenly Father, we come before You, humbled and grateful for Your boundless mercy and steadfast love. As we conclude this day, we lay before You our anxieties, our hopes, and our responsibilities as husbands, fathers, and citizens. Grant us the wisdom to walk in Your truth, the strength to lead our families with grace, and the courage to be Your light in our communities. Unite our hearts, O Lord, to fear Your name alone. May Your presence be our comfort and Your promises our peace through the night. In Jesus' name, Amen.

Grace and Peace,
Pastor John Wesley Graves
