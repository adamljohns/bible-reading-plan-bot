Morning Wisdom

Brother, hold fast to the truth of God’s nature, for it is the bedrock of your spiritual defense.

📖 1 John 4:1-12

Beloved, do not believe every spirit, but test the spirits, whether they are of God; because many false prophets have gone out into the world. By this you know the Spirit of God: Every spirit that confesses that Jesus Christ has come in the flesh is of God, and every spirit that does not confess that Jesus Christ has come in the flesh is not of God. And this is the spirit of the Antichrist, which you have heard was coming, and is now already in the world. You are of God, little children, and have overcome them, because He who is in you is greater than he who is in the world. They are of the world. Therefore they speak as of the world, and the world hears them. We are of God. He who knows God hears us; he who is not of God does not hear us. By this we know the spirit of truth and the spirit of error. Beloved, let us love one another, for love is of God; and everyone who loves is born of God and knows God. He who does not love does not know God, for God is love. In this the love of God was manifested toward us, that God has sent His only begotten Son into the world, that we might live through Him. In this is love, not that we loved God, but that He loved us and sent His Son to be the propitiation for our sins. Beloved, if God so loved us, we also ought to love one another. No one has seen God at any time. If we love one another, God abides in us, and His love has been perfected in us.

🧭 Context Summary

John addresses the church to ensure their loyalty rests solely on the incarnate Christ against the rising tide of docetic error. He establishes a clear doctrinal test: true faith confesses that Jesus Christ has come in the flesh, denying the spirit of antichrist that denies His humanity. The apostle urges believers to recognize the voice of truth by their allegiance to the apostolic witness and to respond to God’s initiating love by loving their brothers.

🛡️ Reflection for a REAL MAN — Reject Passivity

John commands, “do not believe every spirit, but test the spirits, whether they are of God.” Testing is active work. A passive man lets whatever voice is loudest into his home: the teacher on Boaz’s phone, the podcast Gideon keeps quoting, the cultural script about manhood and marriage that drifts in through every screen. In the garden the first Adam said nothing while the serpent talked theology with his wife. Do not repeat his sin. When a lie is being spoken in your house, the man of the house speaks first.

You can afford to speak, because “He who is in you is greater than he who is in the world.” Passivity is often fear dressed up as peacekeeping: you do not want the argument, you do not want to look narrow, you do not want to be wrong in front of your sons. But you do not go into this fight alone or unarmed. Test the claims by Scripture, name the error plainly, and lead your family back to the confession that “Jesus Christ has come in the flesh.”

Then John shows you the pattern behind all of it: “In this is love, not that we loved God, but that He loved us and sent His Son.” God did not wait for us to move toward Him. He moved first. A man who has been loved like that cannot sit back and wait to be pursued by his wife, his kids, or his brothers. Make the first move toward reconciliation, the first word of encouragement, the first prayer at the table. John concludes, “if God so loved us, we also ought to love one another.” In a man, love looks like initiative.

⛏️ Personal Application
  • Ask Gideon or Boaz which voice he has been listening to most this week, listen to it yourself, and talk it through with him by Scripture.
  • Turn off or call out one message in your home today that contradicts God’s word, instead of letting it slide to keep the peace.
  • Make the first move toward someone you have been waiting on to come to you: Maria, one of your sons, or a brother.
🙏 Prayer

Father, I thank You for the gift of Your Son, Jesus Christ, who came in the flesh to save me from my sins. Grant me the discernment of a sober mind to test every spirit and to reject the lies of this world. By the power of Your Holy Spirit, give me the courage to break my silence when error enters my home, to move first in love as You first loved me, and to hold fast to the truth. May my life be a testament to Your grace, guarding my heart and my home against all deception. In Jesus' name, I pray. Amen.

🛡️ Watch Charge: Brother, inspect every spirit at the gate and cast out the impostors that deny the flesh of Christ.
0700

First Watch — The Husband's Post

Brother, man your post: as you prepare for the day, let the Apostle Paul’s example of patient endurance and strategic prayer anchor your spirit before you engage the world.

📖 Romans 15:22-33

For this reason I also have been much hindered from coming to you. But now no longer having a place in these parts, and having a great desire these many years to come to you, whenever I journey to Spain, I shall come to you. For I hope to see you on my journey, and to be helped on my way there by you, if first I may enjoy your company for a while. But now I am going to Jerusalem to minister to the saints. For it pleased those from Macedonia and Achaia to make a certain contribution for the poor among the saints who are in Jerusalem. It pleased them indeed, and they are their debtors. For if the Gentiles have been partakers of their spiritual things, their duty is also to minister to them in material things. Therefore, when I have performed this and have sealed to them this fruit, I shall go by way of you to Spain. But I know that when I come to you, I shall come in the fullness of the blessing of the gospel of Christ. Now I beg you, brethren, through the Lord Jesus Christ, and through the love of the Spirit, that you strive together with me in prayers to God for me, that I may be delivered from those in Judea who do not believe, and that my service for Jerusalem may be acceptable to the saints, that I may come to you with joy by the will of God, and may be refreshed together with you. Now the God of peace be with you all. Amen.

🗺️ Briefing Summary

Paul reveals his strategic intent to visit Rome, not as a conqueror, but as a servant seeking fellowship and support for his mission to Spain. He highlights the sacred duty of Gentile believers to support the Jewish saints in Jerusalem, illustrating the tangible outworking of spiritual unity. The passage concludes with an urgent plea for the church’s intercession, grounding his future plans in the sovereignty and peace of God.

❤️ Reflection for Your Wife — H.A.P.P.Y. Husband — Honoring

Notice how Paul writes to a church he has never visited. He tells them his plans, his hopes, and his dangers, and then he asks them to “strive together with me in prayers to God for me.” The great apostle did not carry his burdens alone or keep his plans to himself. He honored those believers by bringing them into the fight. A husband honors his wife the same way. Maria is not a spectator to your life and calling; she is a fellow heir of grace, as Peter tells husbands in 1 Peter 3:7, and her prayers for you are a gift God has put in your hands.

Many men honor their wives in public with a kind word and then shut them out of the real decisions. Paul laid out his whole route: Jerusalem, then Rome, then Spain. Lay out yours for Maria. Tell her what you are carrying at work, in ministry, and as a father. Ask her what she sees. Weigh her counsel seriously, and then lead. Honoring her counsel does not reverse your headship; it shows that you know God gave you a wife with wisdom, and that you are not too proud to hear it.

Paul also speaks well of others. He commends the believers of Macedonia and Achaia for their gift (“It pleased them indeed”), and he longs to “be refreshed together with you” among the saints in Rome. Speak of Maria the way Paul spoke of the saints he loved: with gratitude, in front of others, and in specifics. Let your sons hear you thank her. Let your brothers hear you praise her. A wife who is honored by her husband carries herself with the dignity God gave her.

⛏️ Personal Application
  • Tell Maria tonight the heaviest thing you are carrying this week and ask her to pray for you by name.
  • Bring one upcoming decision to Maria, ask what she sees that you might be missing, and thank her for it.
  • Thank Maria at dinner, in front of the kids and in specifics, for something she did this week.
🙏 Prayer

Father, I thank You for the Apostle Paul’s example of patient endurance and strategic dependence. Forgive me for my haste and for the times I have carried my plans alone and shut Maria out of them. By the power of Your Holy Spirit, teach me to honor my wife as a fellow heir of Your grace, to seek her prayers and her counsel, and to speak well of her before my children and my brothers. Align my heart with Yours, that I may lead my household with humility and obedience. May Your peace rule in my heart and home. In the name of Jesus Christ, I pray. Amen.

🛡️ Watch Charge: Bring your wife into the fight as a fellow heir of grace, and honor her before all.
1100

Second Watch — The Father's Charge

Brother, take up the charge: as you keep guard over your household, remember that the Lord has entrusted you with the stewardship of His people.

📖 Romans 16:1-16

I commend to you Phoebe our sister, who is a servant of the church in Cenchrea, that you may receive her in the Lord in a manner worthy of the saints, and assist her in whatever business she has need of you; for indeed she has been a helper of many and of myself also. Greet Priscilla and Aquila, my fellow workers in Christ Jesus, who risked their own necks for my life, to whom not only I give thanks, but also all the churches of the Gentiles. Likewise greet the church that is in their house. Greet my beloved Epaenetus, who is the firstfruits of Achaia to Christ. Greet Mary, who labored much for us. Greet Andronicus and Junia, my countrymen and my fellow prisoners, who are of note among the apostles, who also were in Christ before me. Greet Amplias, my beloved in the Lord. Greet Urbanus, our fellow worker in Christ, and Stachys, my beloved. Greet Apelles, approved in Christ. Greet those who are of the household of Aristobulus. Greet Herodion, my countryman. Greet those who are of the household of Narcissus who are in the Lord. Greet Tryphena and Tryphosa, who have labored in the Lord. Greet the beloved Persis, who labored much in the Lord. Greet Rufus, chosen in the Lord, and his mother and mine. Greet Asyncritus, Phlegon, Hermas, Patrobas, Hermes, and the brethren who are with them. Greet Philologus and Julia, Nereus and his sister, and Olympas, and all the saints who are with them. Greet one another with a holy kiss. The churches of Christ greet you.

🗺️ Field Notes

Paul’s letter concludes with a dense roll call of believers, illustrating that the church is a vast, interconnected family bound by shared suffering and service. He commends Phoebe, a servant of the church at Cenchrea; Priscilla and Aquila, his fellow workers who risked their necks for his life; and Andronicus and Junia, who are of note among the apostles, showing that the gospel calls every member to serve the body according to their gifting. His closing call to greet one another with a holy kiss reminds us that we are one family in Christ.

👨‍👦 Reflection for Your Children — F.U.L.F.I.L.L.E.D. Father — Fun

Romans 16 is the part of the letter most men skim, a long list of names. Read it slowly and you hear a man who delighted in people. “Greet my beloved Epaenetus.” “Greet Amplias, my beloved in the Lord.” “Greet Rufus, chosen in the Lord, and his mother and mine.” Paul did not only teach doctrine; he enjoyed the company of the saints and remembered them with warmth and good humor. Your children should be able to say the same of you: that their father was not only the one who made the rules, but the one who made the house glad.

Priscilla and Aquila had “the church that is in their house,” a home people wanted to come to. A house like that is not built on order alone. Gideon at twenty will keep coming home if home is a place where he laughs with his father. Boaz at fifteen needs adventure as much as instruction: a hike up a hard trail, a day on the river, a contest he has a fair chance of winning. Shiloh at five needs a father who will be silly with her, chase her around the yard, and make up ridiculous stories at bedtime.

Joy is not a distraction from raising children in the faith; it is part of it. The gospel is good news, and a home with no laughter tells your children that following Christ is only a burden. Paul could write about the gravest matters and still greet his friends with delight. Put the fun on the calendar this week the way you would anything else that matters. Let your children see that their father enjoys them, and they will find it easier to believe that their heavenly Father does too.

⛏️ Personal Application
  • Take Boaz on an outing he would call an adventure this week (a hike, fishing, go-karts, the range) with no lesson attached.
  • Have a game night or a backyard chase with Shiloh, and let her win at least once.
  • Invite Gideon to something you both enjoy (a ballgame, a meal out, a round of golf) just for the laughs.
🙏 Prayer

Father, I thank You for the gift of the church and the example of faithful servants like Phoebe and Priscilla who have labored for the gospel. Fill my home with the warmth and gladness I see in Paul’s greetings, and make me a father who laughs with my children and enjoys their company. By the power of Your Holy Spirit, help me to hold fast to the truth and to extend the holy kiss of peace to all who are in Christ. Let my children find joy at home, and through it taste the goodness of the gospel. Through Christ my Savior, I pray. Amen.

🛡️ Watch Charge: Extend your hand to lift the burden of the faithful sister, ensuring her service is honored with the dignity of a patron who has sheltered the saints.

Third Watch — The Citizen's Stand

Brother, stand firm: The watchman stands firm against the encroaching darkness, guarding the truth of the Gospel with the gravity of a soldier who knows the cost of compromise.

📖 Romans 16:17-27

Now I urge you, brethren, note those who cause divisions and offenses, contrary to the doctrine which you learned, and avoid them. For those who are such do not serve our Lord Jesus Christ, but their own belly, and by smooth words and flattering speech deceive the hearts of the simple. For your obedience has become known to all. Therefore I am glad on your behalf; but I want you to be wise in what is good, and simple concerning evil. And the God of peace will crush Satan under your feet shortly. The grace of our Lord Jesus Christ be with you. Amen. Timothy, my fellow worker, and Lucius, Jason, and Sosipater, my countrymen, greet you. I, Tertius, who wrote this epistle, greet you in the Lord. Gaius, my host and the host of the whole church, greets you. Erastus, the treasurer of the city, greets you, and Quartus, a brother. The grace of our Lord Jesus Christ be with you all. Amen. Now to Him who is able to establish you according to my gospel and the preaching of Jesus Christ, according to the revelation of the mystery kept secret since the world began but now made manifest, and by the prophetic Scriptures made known to all nations, according to the commandment of the everlasting God, for obedience to the faith— to God, alone wise, be glory through Jesus Christ forever. Amen.

🛰️ Situation Report

Paul issues a final, stern warning to the Roman saints regarding false teachers who seek to undermine the unity of the church for selfish gain. He commends their reputation for obedience, urging them to maintain their spiritual vigilance and purity in the face of such deception. The chapter closes with personal greetings and a benediction, grounding the believers in the grace of Christ and the fellowship of the saints.

🛡️ Reflection for a R.E.S.O.L.U.T.E. Citizen — Steadfast

Fredericksburg (local)
Paul warns about men who “by smooth words and flattering speech deceive the hearts of the simple.” Every city has them: the developer who flatters the council, the activist who flatters the crowd, the online voice that tells you exactly what you already wanted to hear. A steadfast citizen is not moved by smooth words. When a vote comes before the Fredericksburg City Council, weigh the matter against what is true and good, not against who spoke most warmly. Notice too that one believer in Paul’s greetings is “Erastus, the treasurer of the city.” The gospel had men in city hall, and they did not have to bend.

Virginia (state)
“I want you to be wise in what is good, and simple concerning evil.” Trends sweep through Richmond like weather, and every session brings pressure to follow whatever is fashionable that year. Steadfastness is not stubbornness about everything; it is a settled refusal to be moved off what God has called good. When a bill touches life, marriage, or the freedom of churches in Virginia, do not let fear of being called names change your vote or your voice. Paul says God is “able to establish you.” A man established by God can afford to be unpopular in his own state.

United States (nation)
“And the God of peace will crush Satan under your feet shortly.” That promise is why you need not be governed by fear of where the nation is headed. Headlines are built to frighten you into following someone. A steadfast citizen reads the news, prays, votes, and speaks, but his footing does not move when the polls do. The Roman believers’ “obedience has become known to all”; their faith was public and it lasted. Let yours be the same, unmoved by trends and fixed on the “everlasting God,” who will have the last word over every nation.

⛏️ Personal Application
  • Before the next Fredericksburg City Council vote that concerns you, read the agenda packet yourself rather than relying on anyone’s summary of it.
  • Name one issue before the Virginia General Assembly where you have kept quiet for fear of being called names, and send your delegate a clear, respectful letter this week.
  • Turn off cable and social media news for three days this week, and spend that time praying by name for Fredericksburg, Virginia, and the nation.
🙏 Prayer

Father, I thank You for the gift of Your Word, which stands as a firm foundation in a shifting world. Grant me the grace to be steadfast, not swayed by the smooth talk of the enemy or the pressure of the age. Keep me wise in what is good and simple concerning evil, unmoved by flattery in my city and by fear in my nation, with my eyes fixed on Jesus, the author and finisher of my faith. By the power of Your Holy Spirit, give me the courage to confront error with truth and to love the saints with sincerity. Strengthen my hands to build up the body of Christ and to stand firm against the schemes of Satan. In the name of the risen Lord Jesus, I pray. Amen.

🛡️ Watch Charge: Cut the cord of flattery and isolate the divisive agent before his smooth words infect the fellowship.

Evening Peace

The light of Christ shines upon the blind to reveal His glory and to break the chains of spiritual darkness.

📖 John 9:1-23

Now as Jesus passed by, He saw a man who was blind from birth. And His disciples asked Him, saying, “Rabbi, who sinned, this man or his parents, that he was born blind?” Jesus answered, “Neither this man nor his parents sinned, but that the works of God should be revealed in him. I must work the works of Him who sent Me while it is day; the night is coming when no one can work. As long as I am in the world, I am the light of the world.” When He had said these things, He spat on the ground and made clay with the saliva; and He anointed the eyes of the blind man with the clay. And He said to him, “Go, wash in the pool of Siloam” (which is translated, Sent). So he went and washed, and came back seeing. Therefore the neighbors and those who previously had seen that he was blind said, “Is not this he who sat and begged?” Some said, “This is he.” Others said, “He is like him.” He said, “I am he.” Therefore they said to him, “How were your eyes opened?” He answered and said, “A Man called Jesus made clay and anointed my eyes and said to me, ‘Go to the pool of Siloam and wash.’ So I went and washed, and I received sight.” Then they said to him, “Where is He?” He said, “I do not know.” They brought him who formerly was blind to the Pharisees. Now it was a Sabbath when Jesus made the clay and opened his eyes. Then the Pharisees also asked him again how he had received his sight. He said to them, “He put clay on my eyes, and I washed, and I see.” Therefore some of the Pharisees said, “This Man is not from God, because He does not keep the Sabbath.” Others said, “How can a man who is a sinner do such signs?” And there was a division among them. They said to the blind man again, “What do you say about Him because He opened your eyes?” He said, “He is a prophet.” But the Jews did not believe concerning him, that he had been blind and received his sight, until they called the parents of him who had received his sight. And they asked them, saying, “Is this your son, who you say was born blind? How then does he now see?” His parents answered them and said, “We know that this is our son, and that he was born blind; but by what means he now sees we do not know, or who opened his eyes we do not know. He is of age; ask him. He will speak for himself.” His parents said these things because they feared the Jews, for the Jews had agreed already that if anyone confessed that He was Christ, he would be put out of the synagogue. Therefore his parents said, “He is of age; ask him.”

🌾 Reflection for a Man of God

As a HAPPY husband, brother, I see in this account the profound mystery of how God’s sovereign grace operates not through human merit but through His pure will. You are called to lead your household with the same quiet authority that Christ exercised, not by demanding sight, but by providing the means for restoration. Just as the Savior did not debate the theological merits of the beggar’s condition but acted with decisive love, you must lead your wife with a spirit of sacrificial provision. Your headship is not a tyrannical rule but a Christ-like stewardship, guiding your family toward the light of truth even when the world around you questions your authority.

As a FULFILLED father, brother, recognize that your children, like the neighbors who questioned the healed man, will often look for signs and demand explanations. Your duty is not to appease their curiosity with worldly philosophy but to point them steadfastly to the Source of all light. Teach them that wisdom comes from fearing the LORD and that true sight is a gift of grace, not a result of human effort. Guard their hearts against the spirit of the age, which seeks to confuse the roles God has ordained, and raise them to respect the order of creation where the father leads in faith and the mother supports in wisdom.

As a RESOLUTE citizen, brother, stand firm when the cultural Pharisees demand you abandon your convictions for the sake of social approval. The healed man knew who he was and what he had received, even when his own parents trembled at the threat of exclusion. You must be equally resolute, knowing that your citizenship is in Heaven and your accountability is to the King of Kings. Do not compromise the truth of Scripture to fit the narrative of the world, but speak with clarity and courage, affirming that Jesus is the Christ and the only hope for a broken world.

🙏 Prayer

Father, I thank You for the light of Your Son, Jesus Christ, who illuminates the darkness of my heart and guides my steps. Grant me the strength to lead my home with wisdom and grace, to raise my children in the discipline and instruction of the LORD, and to remain steadfast in my civic duties without fear of man. By the power of Your Holy Spirit, strip away my pride and replace it with a humble reliance on Your sovereignty, that I may reflect Your glory in all I do. For the sake of Christ our King, I pray. Amen.
