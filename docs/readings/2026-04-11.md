
## 🛡️ 2nd Watch — Father's Charge (1100)
**📖 1 Samuel 2:12–36 (KJV)**

### Scripture
Now the sons of Eli were sons of Belial; they knew not the LORD... [full passage 1 Samuel 2:12-36]

### Context
Eli was the high priest of Israel at Shiloh — entrusted with the tabernacle, the sacrifices, the spiritual life of an entire nation. His sons Hophni and Phinehas were wicked — stealing offerings, defiling the tabernacle. Eli knew, rebuked once, but never enforced consequences. He honored his sons above God. That indictment cost his household its future. Meanwhile young Samuel grew in favor with God and man. Same tabernacle. Different trajectory. The difference was fathering.

### F.U.L.F.I.L.L.E.D. Reflection
- **Faithfulness** — Private fathering must match public faith
- **Understanding** — Action born from awareness, not just awareness
- **Leadership** — Passive fatherhood is destructive, not neutral
- **Fortitude** — Loving children enough to endure their anger for their souls
- **Intentionality** — What are you building into your children on purpose?
- **Legacy** — Whether they know how to walk with God because they watched you
- **Love** — Love without discipline is indulgence
- **Eternity** — "Them that honour me I will honour" — an eternal equation
- **Devotion** — A home where devotion to God is the oxygen everyone breathes

### Helm Command
"Thou honourest thy sons above me." — 1 Samuel 2:29
