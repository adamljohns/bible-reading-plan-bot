## Morning Wisdom

⚓ **Morning Wisdom** — Thursday, March 26, 2026
📖 Proverbs 26:17-28

He who passes by and meddles in a quarrel not his own is like one who takes a dog by the ears.

Like a madman who throws firebrands, arrows, and death, is the man who deceives his neighbor, and says, "I was only joking!"

Where there is no wood, the fire goes out; and where there is no talebearer, strife ceases. As charcoal is to burning coals, and wood to fire, so is a contentious man to kindle strife. The words of a talebearer are like tasty trifles, and they go down into the inmost body.

Fervent lips with a wicked heart are like earthenware covered with silver dross.

He who hates, disguises it with his lips, and lays up deceit within himself; when he speaks kindly, do not believe him, for there are seven abominations in his heart; though his hatred is covered by deceit, his wickedness will be revealed before the assembly.

Whoever digs a pit will fall into it, and he who rolls a stone will have it roll back on him.

A lying tongue hates those who are crushed by it, and a flattering mouth works ruin.

---

🧭 **Context Summary**

This passage comes from the second collection of Solomon's proverbs, focusing on the destructive power of words and interpersonal conflict. Written in the 10th century BC for training young men in wisdom, these verses address meddling, deception, gossip, and concealed hatred—all forms of relational toxicity that undermine community. The ancient Near Eastern context valued honor and reputation highly, making these warnings about speech particularly urgent.

---

🛡️ **REAL MAN — Accountable**

A real man owns his words and guards his tongue. This passage exposes the coward's toolkit: meddling in others' business, disguising cruelty as humor, spreading gossip, and hiding hatred behind false smiles. These aren't just character flaws—they're acts of relational warfare that destroy trust and community.

Solomon warns that deception is self-destructive: the pit-digger falls in, the stone-roller gets crushed. You can't sow chaos and expect to harvest peace. Your words either build or burn—there's no neutral ground.

Accountability means taking responsibility for the impact of your speech. Before you speak, ask: Is this true? Is it necessary? Does it build up? If you've weaponized your words—gossiped, joked cruelly, stirred needless conflict—own it. Apologize specifically. Make it right. A man who can't control his tongue can't be trusted with anything else.

---

⛏️ **Application**

• **Stay out of quarrels that aren't yours.** Don't insert yourself into conflicts where you have no stake or responsibility. Meddling masquerades as concern but usually feeds your ego or entertainment. Focus on your own walk.

• **Cut the fuel supply to gossip.** When someone brings you a story about someone else, refuse to engage. "Have you talked to them directly?" is a conversation-ender. Gossip dies without an audience.

• **Test your humor for cruelty.** "I was just joking" is the refuge of cowards who want to inflict pain without accountability. If your humor leaves wreckage, it's not humor—it's vandalism. Speak to build, not to destroy.

• **Confront passive-aggressive hatred.** If someone's actions don't match their words, don't ignore the discrepancy. Address it directly and privately. Flattery from a hateful heart is poison—don't drink it, and don't serve it.

---

🙏 **Prayer**

Father, guard my tongue. Convict me when I meddle, gossip, deceive, or disguise cruelty as humor. Give me the courage to speak truth that builds, the wisdom to stay silent when I should, and the humility to own my words when they've caused harm. Make me a man of integrity—let my words and heart align. Help me to fuel peace, not strife. In Jesus' name, amen.

---

⚓ **Helm Command**
*Guard your tongue. Own your words. Build, don't burn.*

📖 **SCRIPTURE** (Joshua 10:1-14, excerpts)

When Adonizedec king of Jerusalem heard how Joshua had taken Ai and how Gibeon had made peace with Israel, he feared greatly. He allied with four other Amorite kings and made war against Gibeon.

The Gibeonites sent urgent word to Joshua: "Come up to us quickly, and save us!" So Joshua ascended from Gilgal with all his warriors. The Lord said, "Fear them not: I have delivered them into thine hand." Joshua came upon them suddenly, marching all night.

The Lord discomfited them with great slaughter, and as they fled, the Lord cast down great stones from heaven—more died from hailstones than by the sword.

Then Joshua spoke to the Lord: "Sun, stand thou still upon Gibeon; and thou, Moon, in the valley of Ajalon." And the sun stood still, and the moon stayed. The sun stood still in the midst of heaven about a whole day.

There was no day like that before or after, when the Lord hearkened unto the voice of a man: **for the Lord fought for Israel.**

---

🧭 **CONTEXT**

After Israel's victories at Jericho and Ai, five Amorite kings allied to punish Gibeon for making peace with Israel. Despite the questionable treaty origins (Gibeon's deception in ch. 9), Joshua honored his covenant and marched all night to defend them. God answered with Scripture's most dramatic miracle: extending daylight to allow complete victory.

---

💍 **H.A.P.P.Y. HUSBAND — Y: Yielding to God's Timing**

Brother, notice what didn't happen: Joshua didn't wait for perfect conditions. He didn't complain about Gibeon's inconvenient distress call. He moved with immediate obedience—an all-night forced march—trusting God's promise.

But when Joshua needed more time, he asked boldly: "Sun, stand thou still." That's faith-filled partnership with a God who **fights for His people**.

In your marriage: **move when God says move, ask when you need provision**. Don't procrastinate when your wife needs you to step up—even if it's inconvenient, even if the timing feels off. Honor your covenant. March through the night if needed.

And when you're in the battle and need resources beyond yourself? Ask your God. He commands the sun and moon. He can handle your need for patience, wisdom, or strength to love well when exhausted.

Your wife needs a Joshua who moves in obedience and prays in faith. God will fight for your marriage—but you have to show up to the battle.

---

👨‍👧‍👦 **F.U.L.F.I.L.L.E.D. FATHER — F: Fight for Your Covenant Commitments**

Joshua honored his word to Gibeon despite their trickery. Why? **A man's word is his bond**, and God holds us to our commitments even when we wish we hadn't made them.

Dad, your kids are watching how you keep your word. When you promise to play after work but you're tired—do you follow through? When you commit to family time but something "more important" comes up—what do you choose?

**Your integrity is built in small moments of follow-through.** Every kept promise trains your children that commitments matter, that character is forged in consistency, and that God's people make midnight marches when duty calls.

Teach them to pray audacious prayers. Joshua didn't accept time constraints—when he needed more daylight, **he asked God to stop the sun**. That's the faith-filled boldness you want in your children's hearts.

Train them: obey immediately, honor your word fiercely, and when you need a miracle—**ask the God who moves heaven and earth**.

---

⛏️ **APPLICATION**

1. **Audit your commitments.** What covenant obligations feel "inconvenient" right now? Honor them anyway.
2. **Don't wait for energy.** What "midnight march" is God calling you to today? Move in obedience.
3. **Pray a Joshua prayer.** Where do you need God to "extend the daylight"? Ask boldly.

---

🙏 **PRAYER**

*Father, give me Joshua's immediate obedience and audacious faith. When You call me to the inconvenient battle—defending my marriage, protecting my family, honoring commitments—strengthen my resolve. And when I need more than I have, remind me to ask boldly. Fight for my family, Lord. In Jesus' name, Amen.*

---

⚓ **HELM COMMAND**
**"Move in obedience. Pray in faith. God fights for those who honor covenant."**

Stand your watch, brother. The battle is the Lord's, but you still have to show up. 🎯

## 2nd Watch — Father's Charge

⚓ **2nd Watch — Father's Charge** | March 26, 2026 | 1100 Hours

📖 **Joshua 11 — The Northern Coalition Destroyed**

When Jabin king of Hazor heard of Israel's victories, he assembled a massive coalition—kings from the north, the hill country, and coastal regions. The Canaanites, Amorites, Hittites, Perizzites, Jebusites, and Hivites united with armies, horses, and chariots as numerous as sand on the seashore. They gathered at the waters of Merom to fight Israel.

Yahweh said to Joshua, "Don't be afraid because of them; for tomorrow at this time, I will deliver them up all slain before Israel. You shall hamstring their horses and burn their chariots with fire."

Joshua came suddenly against them and attacked. Yahweh delivered them into Israel's hand. They struck them and chased them until no one remained. Joshua hamstrung their horses and burned their chariots, just as Yahweh commanded.

Joshua captured Hazor and struck its king with the sword, for Hazor was the head of all those kingdoms. He utterly destroyed every soul, leaving no one who breathed. He burned Hazor and captured all the cities of those kings, utterly destroying them as Moses commanded.

Israel took the plunder and livestock for themselves, but every person they struck with the sword until destroyed. As Yahweh commanded Moses, so Moses commanded Joshua, and Joshua did so—he left nothing undone.

Joshua captured all that land and made war a long time with all those kings. Not one city made peace with Israel except the Hivites of Gibeon. For it was of Yahweh to harden their hearts to come against Israel in battle, that He might utterly destroy them as He commanded Moses.

Joshua also cut off the Anakim from the hill country—from Hebron, Debir, Anab, and all the mountains of Judah and Israel. He utterly destroyed them with their cities. None remained except in Gaza, Gath, and Ashdod.

So Joshua took the whole land according to all that Yahweh spoke to Moses, and gave it for an inheritance to Israel according to their tribal divisions. The land had rest from war.

---

🧭 **Context**

After Israel's southern victories, the northern Canaanite kings unite under Hazor—bringing overwhelming numbers and superior military technology. God speaks before the battle: "Don't be afraid." Victory is promised before a sword is raised. Joshua obeys with precision—destroying enemies completely, burning chariots, hamstringing horses. The campaign is long and thorough. Israel takes the land exactly as God commanded. The result: rest from war.

---

👨‍👧‍👦 **F.U.L.F.I.L.L.E.D. — Father's Charge**

**Fearless in Obedience**
Joshua faces overwhelming odds—a coalition with superior technology and numbers like sand. Yet he doesn't hesitate. Why? Because God spoke first. Courage isn't the absence of fear—it's obedience despite it. When God's word is clear, we move. As fathers, we teach our children that we don't need to see the path; we need to trust the Commander.

**Unwavering Execution**
Joshua hamstrings horses and burns chariots—deliberately destroying assets that could benefit Israel. Why? Because God said so. Obedience isn't selective. We don't keep the convenient parts of God's command and ignore the costly ones. As fathers, we model full obedience, not partial compliance.

**Long-Term Commitment**
"Joshua made war a long time with all those kings." Victory wasn't instant—it was a grinding campaign requiring sustained faithfulness. Fatherhood is the same: a long obedience in the same direction. We don't sprint; we march. Our children need to see that obedience to God isn't a moment—it's a lifetime.

**Foundation for Rest**
The land had rest from war because Joshua finished the job. He didn't leave enemies to rise again. That's our charge as fathers—to fight the battles our children shouldn't have to. To eliminate spiritual threats. To establish a foundation of faith so solid they can build their lives on it without fear.

Your children are watching how you fight. Are you fearless when God commands? Unwavering in obedience? Committed for the long haul? Clearing ground for their future?

Don't just survive the battles—win them completely. Your children's rest depends on your faithfulness today.

---

⛏️ **Application**

• **Identify the coalition.** What overwhelming challenge are you facing? What feels impossible? Name it. Then ask: Has God already spoken on this? If He has, fear is irrelevant—obey.

• **Destroy the chariots.** What advantage are you clinging to that God told you to surrender? What compromise looks strategic but violates His command? Hamstring it. Burn it. Full obedience.

• **Commit to the long campaign.** What spiritual battle requires years, not days? Pray with your children daily. Disciple them consistently. Fight the culture relentlessly. Don't quit when it's hard.

• **Clear the ground.** What spiritual threat in your home needs to be eliminated now so your children can build their lives on solid ground? Destroy it utterly.

---

🙏 **Prayer**

*Father, give me the courage of Joshua—not the absence of fear, but obedience despite it. When You speak, let me move without hesitation. Help me trust Your promises more than my circumstances. Give me strength to obey fully, not selectively—to destroy what You command destroyed, even when it seems wasteful. Sustain me for the long campaign of fatherhood. Let my faithfulness today clear ground for my children's rest tomorrow. Make me a father who finishes what You've commanded. In Jesus' name, Amen.*

---

⚓ **Helm Command**

**"Fear not. Obey fully. Fight long. Finish completely."**

📖 **Psalm 75 — God Will Judge with Equity**

We give thanks to you, O God; we give thanks, for your name is near. We recount your wondrous deeds.

At the set time that I appoint I will judge with equity. When the earth totters, and all its inhabitants, it is I who keep steady its pillars.

I say to the boastful, 'Do not boast,' and to the wicked, 'Do not lift up your horn; do not lift up your horn on high, or speak with haughty neck.'

For not from the east or from the west and not from the wilderness comes lifting up, but it is God who executes judgment, putting down one and lifting up another.

For in the hand of the Lord there is a cup with foaming wine, well mixed, and he pours out from it, and all the wicked of the earth shall drain it down to the dregs.

But I will declare it forever; I will sing praises to the God of Jacob. All the horns of the wicked I will cut off, but the horns of the righteous shall be lifted up.

---

Your marriage stands not on your perfection, but on His faithfulness. Your children are formed not by flawless parenting, but by a father who points them to the One who judges with equity.

As a citizen, you witness arrogance and injustice daily. The boastful strut. The wicked prosper—for a season. But God's set time is fixed. He will judge. Your calling isn't to panic or retaliate, but to give thanks that His name is near, to recount His wondrous deeds, and to trust His timing.

Tonight, release the weight of outcomes you cannot control. The cup is in His hand, not yours. Sleep in the confidence that the God who steadies the pillars will steady your home, your heart, and your nation.
