📖 Ezra 5

Then the prophet Haggai and Zechariah the son of Iddo, prophets, prophesied to the Jews who were in Judah and Jerusalem, in the name of the God of Israel, who was over them.
So Zerubbabel the son of Shealtiel and Jeshua the son of Jozadak rose up and began to build the house of God which is in Jerusalem; and the prophets of God were with them, helping them.
At the same time Tattenai the governor of the region beyond the River and Shethar-Boznai and their companions came to them and spoke thus to them: “Who has commanded you to build this temple and finish this wall?”
Then, accordingly, we told them the names of the men who were constructing this building.
But the eye of their God was upon the elders of the Jews, so that they could not make them cease till a report could go to Darius. Then a written answer was returned concerning this matter.
This is a copy of the letter that Tattenai sent: The governor of the region beyond the River, and Shethar-Boznai, and his companions, the Persians who were in the region beyond the River, to Darius the king.
(They sent a letter to him, in which was written thus) To Darius the king: All peace.
Let it be known to the king that we went into the province of Judea, to the temple of the great God, which is being built with heavy stones, and timber is being laid in the walls; and this work goes on diligently and prospers in their hands.
Then we asked those elders, and spoke thus to them: “Who commanded you to build this temple and to finish these walls?”
We also asked them their names to inform you, that we might write the names of the men who were chief among them.
And thus they returned us an answer, saying: “We are the servants of the God of heaven and earth, and we are rebuilding the temple that was built many years ago, which a great king of Israel built and completed.
But because our fathers provoked the God of heaven to wrath, He gave them into the hand of Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon, the Chaldean, who destroyed this temple and carried the people away to Babylon.
However, in the first year of Cyrus king of Babylon, King Cyrus issued a decree to build this house of God.
Also, the gold and silver articles of the house of God, which Nebuchadnezzar had taken from the temple that was in Jerusalem and carried into the temple of Babylon—those King Cyrus took from the temple of Babylon, and they were given to one named Sheshbazzar, whom he had made governor.
And he said to him, ‘Take these articles; go, carry them to the temple site that is in Jerusalem, and let the house of God be rebuilt on its former site.’
Then the same Sheshbazzar came and laid the foundation of the house of God which is in Jerusalem; but from that time even until now it has been under construction, and it is not finished.”
Now therefore, if it seems good to the king, let a search be made in the king’s treasure house, which is there in Babylon, whether it is so that a decree was issued by King Cyrus to build this house of God at Jerusalem, and let the king send us his pleasure concerning this matter.

🧭 Context Summary

The Jews have resumed the arduous task of rebuilding the Temple after a long period of stagnation, facing immediate political opposition from the regional governors. Despite the intimidation tactics and legal challenges posed by Tattenai and his companions, the work continues under the divine protection and providence of the LORD. The prophets Haggai and Zechariah provide the necessary spiritual encouragement, reminding the people that their authority comes from the King of Kings, not the Persian crown. This chapter illustrates how God’s sovereign plan advances even through the bureaucratic machinery of earthly empires, ensuring His house is built according to His timing.

🗺️ Briefing Summary

Ezekiel delivers a prophetic parable using the imagery of an eagle and a cedar to illustrate the political and spiritual fate of Judah. The passage contrasts the temporary security sought through human alliances with the enduring sovereignty of the Lord over His chosen people. It serves as a stern warning against trusting in earthly powers rather than remaining faithful to the covenant. The imagery highlights how God’s people were uprooted from their land due to their unfaithfulness and reliance on foreign kings.

Again, when a wicked man turns away from the wickedness which he committed, and does what is lawful and right, he preserves himself alive.
Because he considers and turns away from all the transgressions which he committed, he shall surely live; he shall not die.

Cast away from you all the transgressions which you have committed, and get yourselves a new heart and a new spirit. For why should you die, O house of Israel?
For I have no pleasure in the death of one who dies,” says the Lord GOD. “Therefore turn and live!”

🦅 This Day in American History — July 9

On July 9, 1776, General George Washington had the newly arrived Declaration of Independence read to his Continental Army on the parade grounds of Lower Manhattan, that the men facing a mighty enemy might know the great cause for which they would fight and perhaps die. On the same day in 1850, President Zachary Taylor, the old soldier whom the nation had lately raised to its highest office, was taken by sudden illness, and the Republic submitted to the loss as Millard Fillmore quietly took up the office. The one steeled men for a long fight and the other bowed before the providence that numbers a man's days, and a Steadfast citizen learns to stand firm in the cause and to trust the God who holds both the soldier's courage and the leader's appointed end.

🌾 Reflection for a Man at Home and in Community

As a HAPPY husband, brother, you must recognize that the world offers many fragile refuges, from wealth to status, but only the Lord is the tried stone upon which your household can stand secure. Your wife looks to you not for the shifting sands of cultural trends, but for the steadfastness of a man who has anchored his soul in the sure foundation of Christ. You provide true safety not by shielding her from all trouble, but by leading her with the courage of one who knows that the Lord Himself is her shield and her song. Let your home be a place where the truth of His word is the only refuge you acknowledge, teaching your family to trust in His promises rather than in the fleeting comforts of this age.

As a FULFILLED father, you bear the solemn duty of instructing your children in the ways of the Lord, for they too must learn to stand firm when the storms of life rage against them. You are called to be a man of righteousness and salvation, modeling for your sons the discipline of a soldier and the wisdom of a sage. Do not let them rely on the lies of this world for their security, but point them daily to the precious cornerstone who alone saves. Your instruction must be clear and direct, stripping away the thorns of confusion so that they may see the clear path of obedience, knowing that their father’s love is rooted in the fear of the Lord and the desire for their eternal good.

As a RESOLUTE citizen, you understand that your primary allegiance is to the Kingdom of Heaven, and you must live with the gravity of one who awaits the day of the Lord’s vengeance and justice. The world may mock your convictions or seek to undermine your authority, but you stand firm on the rock of Scripture, unshaken by the political winds of the hour. You serve your nation with integrity and courage, not as a partisan, but as a man of God who knows that all authority is ordained by Him. Let your life be a testimony of sober faithfulness, demonstrating that a true man finds his strength in submission to God’s law and his peace in the assurance of His sovereign grace.

🙏 Prayer from the Stateroom

Father, we thank You for the solid rock of Your Word, which stands firm when all else fades. By the power of Your Holy Spirit, grant us the grace to build our lives on this sure foundation, that we may stand firm in our duties to You, our families, and our community. Give us the courage to reject the lies of this age and to trust wholly in Your salvation. In the name of Jesus Christ, my Lord and Commander, we pray. Amen.

🛡️ Watch Charge: Plant your feet on the solid rock of Christ’s authority and let the storm of divine judgment wash away every fragile idol you have trusted for security.
