📖 **Scripture: Proverbs 4**

Hear, ye children, the instruction of a father, and attend to know understanding. For I give you good doctrine, forsake ye not my law. For I was my father's son, tender and only beloved in the sight of my mother. He taught me also, and said unto me, Let thine heart retain my words: keep my commandments, and live. Get wisdom, get understanding: forget it not; neither decline from the words of my mouth. Forsake her not, and she shall preserve thee: love her, and she shall keep thee. Wisdom is the principal thing; therefore get wisdom: and with all thy getting get understanding. Exalt her, and she shall promote thee: she shall bring thee to honour, when thou dost embrace her. She shall give to thine head an ornament of grace: a crown of glory shall she deliver to thee.

Hear, O my son, and receive my sayings; and the years of thy life shall be many. I have taught thee in the way of wisdom; I have led thee in right paths. When thou goest, thy steps shall not be straitened; and when thou runnest, thou shalt not stumble. Take fast hold of instruction; let her not go: keep her; for she is thy life.

Enter not into the path of the wicked, and go not in the way of evil men. Avoid it, pass not by it, turn from it, and pass away. For they sleep not, except they have done mischief; and their sleep is taken away, unless they cause some to fall. For they eat the bread of wickedness, and drink the wine of violence.

But the path of the just is as the shining light, that shineth more and more unto the perfect day. The way of the wicked is as darkness: they know not at what they stumble.

My son, attend to my words; incline thine ear unto my sayings. Let them not depart from thine eyes; keep them in the midst of thine heart. For they are life unto those that find them, and health to all their flesh. Keep thy heart with all diligence; for out of it are the issues of life. Put away from thee a froward mouth, and perverse lips put far from thee. Let thine eyes look right on, and let thine eyelids look straight before thee. Ponder the path of thy feet, and let all thy ways be established. Turn not to the right hand nor to the left: remove thy foot from evil.

---

🧭 **Context**

Proverbs 4 is the most concentrated fathering passage in all of Scripture. Solomon doesn't just command his son to pursue wisdom — he traces the chain of transmission: *"I was my father's son... he taught me also."* Wisdom passed from grandfather to father to son. This is the great inheritance — more valuable than land, money, or reputation.

The chapter sets two paths before every young soul: the path of the just, which shines brighter and brighter toward noonday, and the path of the wicked, which is darkness so thick the stumbling man cannot even identify what tripped him. There is no neutral ground. Every child you raise is walking one road or the other.

Solomon's instruction to his son is the distillation of a father's highest calling: *Guard your heart. Guard your eyes. Guard your mouth. Guard your feet.* Wholeness comes when all four align with wisdom.

---

👨‍👧‍👦 **F.U.L.F.I.L.L.E.D. Father — Raising Sons and Daughters in the Way of Wisdom**

**Faithfulness — "He taught me also, and said unto me..."**
Your sons didn't ask to be born. They didn't choose you. God appointed you as the first voice to speak wisdom into their souls. Faithfulness means you show up — not occasionally, not when it's convenient, but relentlessly. The greatest sermon your sons will ever hear is the one your life preaches from Monday to Saturday. Be the father who taught — not the one who meant to.

**Unity — "Get wisdom... get understanding..."**
A house divided stumbles in the dark. When father and mother speak the same wisdom, the same values, the same vision — their children stand on solid ground. Make sure what you're passing down is unified. Your sons need to hear the same truth from your lips and your wife's. Contradictions in the home create confusion in the child. Unite on what matters most: fear God, pursue wisdom, walk in the light.

**Legacy — "I was my father's son... he taught me..."**
Solomon knew he was a link in a chain. Your children are not endpoints — they are conduits. What you deposit in them today will flow into their children's children. Teach them to think generationally. You are not just raising a son or daughter; you are planting an orchard you'll never fully taste. Plant well. Prune faithfully. Trust the harvest to God.

**Focus — "Let thine eyes look right on... let thine eyelids look straight before thee."**
In a world designed to scatter attention, the godly father teaches his children to *lock in.* Distractions are not neutral — they are the enemy's primary weapon. Teach your sons to keep their eyes ahead. Teach your daughters to fix their gaze on what is true, noble, and good. The path narrows when eyes wander. Help them build the discipline of singular focus before the world fragments them.

**Integrity — "Keep thy heart with all diligence; for out of it are the issues of life."**
The heart is the command center. Everything — courage, cowardice, love, lust, truth, deception — flows from it. You can't sanitize what comes out without guarding what goes in. This is your most urgent assignment: help your children guard the gate of their hearts. Teach them what to feed on. Watch what they watch. Curate what enters their inner life. A guarded heart is the source of a guarded life.

**Love — "Wisdom is the principal thing; therefore get wisdom: and with all thy getting get understanding."**
What do you love most? You will pursue it relentlessly and pass that pursuit to your children. Love wisdom above comfort. Love truth above popularity. Love God above everything. And when your sons and daughters watch you — what will they see you chasing? Your loves are contagious. Let the right ones spread.

**Lordship — "Enter not into the path of the wicked... turn from it and pass away."**
A father who walks closely with the Lord can see the enemy's roads before his children reach the on-ramp. Lordship means Christ is King in your home — not tradition, not culture, not the world's drumbeat. When Christ is Lord, you have the authority to say "Turn away from that road" — not out of fear, but out of discernment purchased through submission. Walk under His authority so your children can walk under yours.

**Endurance — "The path of the just is as the shining light, that shineth more and more unto the perfect day."**
The righteous man doesn't peak early. He grows brighter. Each decade of faithfulness adds luminosity. Your children are watching whether the faith holds. They're watching whether you get better with age or just more tired. Show them what enduring looks like — a man whose light increases, whose character deepens, who runs and doesn't stumble because wisdom holds his feet. Endurance is the proof that the foundation is real.

**Direction — "Ponder the path of thy feet, and let all thy ways be established."**
You can't lead where you haven't pondered. A father who doesn't think about where he's going will take his household in circles. Take time — with God, with your journal, with your wife — to ponder your family's path. Where are you headed? What are the five-year goals spiritually? Where do your children's gifts point? Direction is a fathering act. Don't drift. Steer.

---

⛏️ **Application**

This week, pick one of the four guards from Proverbs 4 — heart, eyes, mouth, feet — and open a conversation with your sons and daughters about it. Ask them:

*"Where do you feel pulled off the right path right now?"*

Don't lecture. Listen first. Then speak as Solomon speaks — not as a distant authority, but as a man who was once a tender son himself, who received these words from his own father, who knows the road.

Pass it on. That's the whole job.
