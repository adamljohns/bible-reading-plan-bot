Morning Wisdom

The Lord calls His saints to rest in the certainty of His justice while awaiting the final execution of His wrath.

📖 Revelation 15

Then I saw another sign in heaven, great and marvelous: seven angels having the seven last plagues, for in them the wrath of God is complete. And I saw something like a sea of glass mingled with fire, and those who have the victory over the beast, over his image and over his mark and over the number of his name, standing on the sea of glass, having harps of God. They sing the song of Moses, the servant of God, and the song of the Lamb, saying: “Great and marvelous are Your works, Lord God Almighty! Just and true are Your ways, O King of the saints! Who shall not fear You, O Lord, and glorify Your name? For You alone are holy. For all nations shall come and worship before You, For Your judgments have been manifested.” After these things I looked, and behold, the temple of the tabernacle of the testimony in heaven was opened. And out of the temple came the seven angels having the seven plagues, clothed in pure bright linen, and having their chests girded with golden bands. Then one of the four living creatures gave to the seven angels seven golden bowls full of the wrath of God who lives forever and ever. The temple was filled with smoke from the glory of God and from His power, and no one was able to enter the temple till the seven plagues of the seven angels were completed.

🧭 Context Summary

John is granted a vision of the heavenly temple opened in preparation for the final outpouring of God’s wrath upon the earth. The scene depicts the victorious saints gathered on a sea of glass, singing the songs of Moses and the Lamb in worship of God’s righteous judgments. This moment serves as the solemn prelude to the seven bowls of wrath, emphasizing that God’s justice is perfect and His holiness is absolute. The passage reminds us that the final defeat of evil is not chaotic, but a righteous execution of divine law.

🛡️ Reflection for a REAL MAN — Engage Consistently

Notice who sings on the sea of glass: "those who have the victory over the beast, over his image and over his mark and over the number of his name." That victory was not won in one heroic afternoon. The mark was pressed on men at the market and at work, day after day, and these men refused it day after day. That is how a man wins in his own house too. Not with bursts of zeal after a men's retreat that fade by the next month, but with thousands of ordinary mornings in which he shows up for God, for his wife and for his children.

They sing "the song of Moses, the servant of God." Think of Moses' life: forty years in the wilderness with a grumbling people, interceding for them, judging their disputes from morning to evening, climbing the mountain again and again. Moses was not great because of one moment at the Red Sea. He was faithful over God's house year after year. A husband and father is called to the same steady work. Maria needs a man whose presence she can count on, not one who is warm in one season and absent the next. Your sons need a father who is the same man on Tuesday that he was on Sunday.

The seven angels come out "clothed in pure bright linen, and having their chests girded with golden bands." Heaven's servants are dressed for duty before the task begins. A man engages consistently the same way: he is girded before the day starts. The early hour in the Word, the daily check-in with a brother, the nightly prayer over Shiloh, the regular evening with Maria, the steady call to the men of USMC Ministries. None of it is dramatic. All of it, done every day, is how a man keeps his house.

⛏️ Personal Application
  • Fix one daily appointment with Maria this week (coffee in the morning, a walk after dinner) and keep it every day, even when it is inconvenient.
  • Pray over Shiloh at bedtime tonight and every night this week, and send Gideon or Boaz one word of encouragement each day.
  • Set a regular weekly time with a brother at Spotswood or in USMC Ministries and put it on the calendar for the next month.
🙏 Prayer

Father, I thank You that You are the King of saints, whose ways are just and true. Grant me the courage to hold fast to Your truth, like the saints upon the sea of glass. By the power of Your Holy Spirit, give me the discipline to show up for my wife, my children and my brothers every day, not in bursts, and the humility to submit to Your sovereign will. Let my life reflect Your justice and my heart rest in Your righteous judgments. In Jesus' name, I pray. Amen.

🛡️ Watch Charge: Keep watch over the sea of glass mingled with fire, guarding your soul against the beast’s image until God’s wrath is fully spent.

First Watch — The Husband's Post

Brother, man your post: as you keep watch over the spiritual integrity of your home, remember that the enemy’s primary tactic is subtle corruption disguised as truth.

📖 2 Peter 2

But there were also false prophets among the people, even as there will be false teachers among you, who will secretly bring in destructive heresies, even denying the Lord who bought them, and bring on themselves swift destruction. And many will follow their destructive ways, because of whom the way of truth will be blasphemed. By covetousness they will exploit you with deceptive words; for a long time their judgment has not been idle, and their destruction does not slumber. For if God did not spare the angels who sinned, but cast them down to hell and delivered them into chains of darkness, to be reserved for judgment; and did not spare the ancient world, but saved Noah, one of eight people, a preacher of righteousness, bringing in the flood on the world of the ungodly; and turning the cities of Sodom and Gomorrah into ashes, condemned them to destruction, making them an example to those who afterward would live ungodly; and delivered righteous Lot, who was oppressed by the filthy conduct of the wicked (for that righteous man, dwelling among them, tormented his righteous soul from day to day by seeing and hearing their lawless deeds)— then the Lord knows how to deliver the godly out of temptations and to reserve the unjust under punishment for the day of judgment, and especially those who walk according to the flesh in the lust of uncleanness and despise authority. They are presumptuous, self-willed. They are not afraid to speak evil of dignitaries, whereas angels, who are greater in power and might, do not bring a reviling accusation against them before the Lord. But these, like natural brute beasts made to be caught and destroyed, speak evil of the things they do not understand, and will utterly perish in their own corruption, and will receive the wages of unrighteousness, as those who count it pleasure to carouse in the daytime. They are spots and blemishes, carousing in their own deceptions while they feast with you, having eyes full of adultery and that cannot cease from sin, enticing unstable souls. They have a heart trained in covetous practices, and are accursed children. They have forsaken the right way and gone astray, following the way of Balaam the son of Beor, who loved the wages of unrighteousness; but he was rebuked for his iniquity: a dumb donkey speaking with a man’s voice restrained the madness of the prophet. These are wells without water, clouds carried by a tempest, for whom is reserved the blackness of darkness forever. For when they speak great swelling words of emptiness, they allure through the lusts of the flesh, through lewdness, the ones who have actually escaped from those who live in error. While they promise them liberty, they themselves are slaves of corruption; for by whom a person is overcome, by him also he is brought into bondage. For if, after they have escaped the pollutions of the world through the knowledge of the Lord and Savior Jesus Christ, they are again entangled in them and overcome, the latter end is worse for them than the beginning. For it would have been better for them not to have known the way of righteousness, than having known it, to turn from the holy commandment delivered to them. But it has happened to them according to the true proverb: “A dog returns to his own vomit,” and, “a sow, having washed, to her wallowing in the mire.”

🗺️ Briefing Summary

Peter warns the church that false teachers will infiltrate the congregation, denying the Lord who redeemed them and leading many astray through greed and arrogance. He reminds believers that the Sovereign God judges sin swiftly, having condemned the wicked cities of old while preserving the righteous, Lot, from their destruction. This passage serves as a stern warning to husbands to guard their hearts against the subtle lies of the culture and to remain steadfast in the truth of Christ’s authority.

❤️ Reflection for Your Wife — H.A.P.P.Y. Husband — Abiding

Peter calls the false teachers "wells without water." They promised much and had nothing to give, because they had cut themselves off from "the Lord who bought them." A husband who does not abide in Christ becomes the same thing in his own home. He may still lead, provide and talk about the faith, but Maria comes to the well and finds it dry. You cannot give her what you are not receiving. Abiding in Christ, staying daily in His Word and in prayer, is not private religion; it is how you have anything to give your wife.

Look at what the men in this chapter could not do. They had "eyes full of adultery and that cannot cease from sin," and they "have forsaken the right way and gone astray." Their failure was a failure to remain. They had escaped the pollutions of the world and then went back, "again entangled in them and overcome." Abiding is the opposite: a husband who stays. He stays in Christ when temptation pulls. He stays in his marriage covenant when feelings run low. He stays present at home instead of disappearing into work, screens or his own thoughts.

Then there is Lot, who "tormented his righteous soul from day to day by seeing and hearing their lawless deeds," and Peter's comfort: "the Lord knows how to deliver the godly out of temptations." The world will press on your marriage every day. Your safety and Maria's do not rest on your strength but on remaining close to the Lord who delivers. So abide together. Open the Word with her. Pray with her. Let her see a husband whose roots go down into Christ, so that when she comes to you for strength, the well is full.

⛏️ Personal Application
  • Get into the Word and prayer before you speak to Maria this morning, so you meet her with something to give.
  • Read a short passage with Maria tonight and pray together before sleep, and keep the habit all week.
  • Put the phone away when you walk in the door tonight and stay present with her for the evening, in the room and in the conversation.
🙏 Prayer

Father, I thank You for the gift of Maria and for the sacred responsibility You have given me to lead her in the fear of the LORD. Forgive me for the times I have failed to guard my heart against the subtle lies of this age. By the power of Your Holy Spirit, keep me abiding in Christ and remaining in Your Word, so that I have something to give Maria and never forsake the right way. Protect my marriage from the corruption of the world, and let my headship reflect the sacrificial love of Christ. In the name of Jesus Christ, I pray. Amen.

🛡️ Watch Charge: Brother, guard your heart against the subtle rot of heresy that denies your Sovereign, lest you join the many who follow the path to swift destruction.

Second Watch — The Father's Charge

Brother, take up the charge: hold fast to the promise that the Lord’s patience is a call to repentance, not a license for delay.

📖 2 Peter 3

Beloved, I now write to you this second epistle (in both of which I stir up your pure minds by way of reminder), that you may be mindful of the words which were spoken before by the holy prophets, and of the commandment of us, the apostles of the Lord and Savior, knowing this first: that scoffers will come in the last days, walking according to their own lusts, and saying, “Where is the promise of His coming? For since the fathers fell asleep, all things continue as they were from the beginning of creation.” For this they willfully forget: that by the word of God the heavens were of old, and the earth standing out of water and in the water, by which the world that then existed perished, being flooded with water. But the heavens and the earth which are now preserved by the same word, are reserved for fire until the day of judgment and perdition of ungodly men. But, beloved, do not forget this one thing, that with the Lord one day is as a thousand years, and a thousand years as one day. The Lord is not slack concerning His promise, as some count slackness, but is longsuffering toward us, not willing that any should perish but that all should come to repentance. But the day of the Lord will come as a thief in the night, in which the heavens will pass away with a great noise, and the elements will melt with fervent heat; both the earth and the works that are in it will be burned up. Therefore, since all these things will be dissolved, what manner of persons ought you to be in holy conduct and godliness, looking for and hastening the coming of the day of God, because of which the heavens will be dissolved, being on fire, and the elements will melt with fervent heat? Nevertheless we, according to His promise, look for new heavens and a new earth in which righteousness dwells. Therefore, beloved, looking forward to these things, be diligent to be found by Him in peace, without spot and blameless; and consider that the longsuffering of our Lord is salvation—as also our beloved brother Paul, according to the wisdom given to him, has written to you, as also in all his epistles, speaking in them of these things, in which are some things hard to understand, which untaught and unstable people twist to their own destruction, as they do also the rest of the Scriptures. You therefore, beloved, since you know this beforehand, beware lest you also fall from your own steadfastness, being led away with the error of the wicked; but grow in the grace and knowledge of our Lord and Savior Jesus Christ. To Him be the glory both now and forever. Amen.

🗺️ Field Notes

Peter reminds us that time is in the hands of the Sovereign Lord, who delays judgment to offer mercy to the elect. The certainty of the final day should not breed fear in the believer, but rather a holy urgency to live in purity and godliness. We are called to look past the chaos of the present age to the stability of the new creation where righteousness dwells. This passage anchors our hope not in the stability of the world, but in the faithfulness of the God who promises to renew all things.

👨‍👦 Reflection for Your Children — F.U.L.F.I.L.L.E.D. Father — Encouraging

Count how often Peter calls his readers "beloved" in this short chapter. He is warning them about scoffers, fire and judgment, and still he keeps calling them beloved. He writes to stir up their "pure minds by way of reminder," assuming the best about them, and he even credits them with their "own steadfastness" while warning them not to lose it. That is how an encouraging father speaks: he names the good he sees before he names the danger.

Your children hear plenty of correction from a world that is quick to tear down. They need a father who builds up. Gideon at twenty is making his own way as a man; tell him specifically where you see God at work in him: his diligence, his honesty, a hard choice he made well. Boaz at fifteen hears mostly about what he did wrong; catch him doing right and say so where others can hear it. Shiloh at five lights up at her father's praise; tell her what you love about the way God made her, and let her hear you thank God for her by name. Peter says the Lord "is longsuffering toward us," patient with slow growth. Be patient with theirs, and say out loud that you can see it coming. A father who only reports failures teaches his children to expect them; a father who names grace teaches them to look for it.

Peter closes with "grow in the grace and knowledge of our Lord and Savior Jesus Christ." Growth is slow and often invisible to the one doing the growing. Part of your work is to be the man who sees it and names it. One true word of encouragement from a father can carry a child through a hard season for years.

⛏️ Personal Application
  • Write Gideon a short note or text this week naming one specific grace of God you see in him as a man.
  • Catch Boaz doing something right this week and praise him for it plainly, in front of the family.
  • Each night this week, tell Shiloh one thing you love about how God made her, and thank God for her aloud where she can hear.
🙏 Prayer

Father, I come before You as Your servant and the head of this house. Give me eyes to see Your grace at work in my children and a tongue quick to name it. You have been longsuffering toward me; make me patient with Gideon, Boaz and Shiloh as they grow. By the power of Your Holy Spirit, guard me from words that tear down, and let my words build them up and point them always to the Savior. Keep my heart humble and my authority aligned with Your Word. Through Christ my Savior, I pray. Amen.

🛡️ Watch Charge: Wait with holy patience for the LORD’s precise timing, knowing His delay is mercy, not neglect, as you keep watch over your soul.

Third Watch — The Citizen's Stand

Brother, stand firm: Stand firm in the truth of the gospel against the creeping shadows of false doctrine and moral decay.

📖 Jude

Jude, a bondservant of Jesus Christ, and brother of James, To those who are called, sanctified by God the Father, and preserved in Jesus Christ: Mercy, peace, and love be multiplied to you. Beloved, while I was very diligent to write to you concerning our common salvation, I found it necessary to write to you exhorting you to contend earnestly for the faith which was once for all delivered to the saints. For certain men have crept in unnoticed, who long ago were marked out for this condemnation, ungodly men, who turn the grace of our God into lewdness and deny the only Lord God and our Lord Jesus Christ. But I want to remind you, though you once knew this, that the Lord, having saved the people out of the land of Egypt, afterward destroyed those who did not believe. And the angels who did not keep their proper domain, but left their own abode, He has reserved in everlasting chains under darkness for the judgment of the great day; as Sodom and Gomorrah, and the cities around them in a similar manner to these, having given themselves over to sexual immorality and gone after strange flesh, are set forth as an example, suffering the vengeance of eternal fire. Likewise also these dreamers defile the flesh, reject authority, and speak evil of dignitaries. Yet Michael the archangel, in contending with the devil, when he disputed about the body of Moses, dared not bring against him a reviling accusation, but said, “The Lord rebuke you!” But these speak evil of whatever they do not know; and whatever they know naturally, like brute beasts, in these things they corrupt themselves. Woe to them! For they have gone in the way of Cain, have run greedily in the error of Balaam for profit, and perished in the rebellion of Korah. These are spots in your love feasts, while they feast with you without fear, serving only themselves. They are clouds without water, carried about by the winds; late autumn trees without fruit, twice dead, pulled up by the roots; raging waves of the sea, foaming up their own shame; wandering stars for whom is reserved the blackness of darkness forever. Now Enoch, the seventh from Adam, prophesied about these men also, saying, “Behold, the Lord comes with ten thousands of His saints, to execute judgment on all, to convict all who are ungodly among them of all their ungodly deeds which they have committed in an ungodly way, and of all the harsh things which ungodly sinners have spoken against Him.” These are grumblers, complainers, walking according to their own lusts; and they mouth great swelling words, flattering people to gain advantage. But you, beloved, remember the words which were spoken before by the apostles of our Lord Jesus Christ: how they told you that there would be mockers in the last time who would walk according to their own ungodly lusts. These are sensual persons, who cause divisions, not having the Spirit. But you, beloved, building yourselves up on your most holy faith, praying in the Holy Spirit, keep yourselves in the love of God, looking for the mercy of our Lord Jesus Christ unto eternal life. And on some have compassion, making a distinction; but others save with fear, pulling them out of the fire, hating even the garment defiled by the flesh. Now to Him who is able to keep you from stumbling, And to present you faultless Before the presence of His glory with exceeding joy, To God our Savior, Who alone is wise, Be glory and majesty, Dominion and power, Both now and forever. Amen.

🛰️ Situation Report

The epistle of Jude serves as an urgent alarm for the faithful to recognize the subtle infiltration of false teachers who distort the grace of God into license. Brother, you are called to contend for the faith once delivered, not by engaging in worldly arguments, but by standing firm in the truth of Christ’s lordship. The passage warns that those who defy authority and indulge in fleshly desires face certain judgment, reminding us that God is both just and merciful. Your duty is to build yourself up in the most holy faith and keep yourself in the love of God while showing mercy to those who are wavering.

🛡️ Reflection for a R.E.S.O.L.U.T.E. Citizen — Responsible

Fredericksburg (local)
Jude says that "certain men have crept in unnoticed." Things creep in where no one owns the watch. A Responsible citizen owns his corner of Fredericksburg: his street, his block, his church's front door. He knows when a zoning change for his neighborhood is on the city council agenda. He notices the neighbor's boy who is drifting, the vacant house drawing trouble, the storm drain clogged before the next hard rain. He does not wait for city hall to notice first. At Spotswood Baptist he does not assume the pastors will catch everything; he takes his own share of the watch and reports what he sees.

Virginia (state)
Jude describes men who are "clouds without water, carried about by the winds; late autumn trees without fruit." They promise rain and bring none. Responsibility in the Commonwealth is the opposite: the man who actually does the thing. He reads the sample ballot before Virginia's elections instead of voting by party label alone. He knows who his delegate and state senator are and how they voted. He corrects his own errors on his state taxes before anyone asks. He serves when a jury summons comes instead of hunting for a way out. Words cost nothing; fruit is what the Lord inspects.

United States (nation)
"But you, beloved, building yourselves up on your most holy faith." Jude puts the duty squarely on the reader: "keep yourselves in the love of God." National decline is real, but you will not answer for the whole nation; you will answer for your part of it. "And on some have compassion, making a distinction; but others save with fear, pulling them out of the fire." The veteran in crisis down the road, the young man USMC Ministries meets who is halfway into the fire: that is your share of the nation's rescue. Own it, and stop waiting for Washington to do what God has handed to you.

⛏️ Personal Application
  • Pull up the agenda for the next Fredericksburg city council meeting and read every item that touches your neighborhood.
  • Learn the names and recent votes of your Virginia delegate and state senator this week, and write them down where you will see them.
  • Contact one struggling veteran or young man in the area this week and take one concrete step to help him, beyond praying from a distance.
🙏 Prayer

Father, I thank You for the gift of Your Son, Christ Jesus, who is my only Master and Lord. I confess that I often leave to others the duties You have placed in my own hands, and I ask that You keep me from stumbling. By the power of Your Holy Spirit, grant me the grace to own my corner of this city, to keep watch where You have stationed me, and to contend for the faith with clarity and courage. Let my life be a testament to Your glory, bearing fruit and not only words, and pulling others from the fire with compassion. In the name of the risen Lord Jesus, I pray. Amen.

🦅 This Day in American History — December 15

On December 15, 1791, Virginia cast the deciding vote that gave force to the Bill of Rights, enshrining in the Constitution the freedoms of conscience, speech, and worship that no government may rightly take away. On the same day in 1864, General George Thomas loosed his army upon General Hood's forces at Nashville, breaking the Confederate Army of Tennessee in one of the war's most complete victories. A Responsible citizen takes up his own share in guarding the liberties his fathers secured and answers the call when they must be defended, knowing that the God who is the author of freedom requires faithful men to keep it.

Evening Peace

The day’s labors conclude, and the soul turns to the One who holds all authority in heaven and on earth.

That Your way may be known on earth, Your salvation among all nations.

🌾 Reflection for a Man of God

As a HAPPY husband, brother, you recognize that your domestic peace is not self-generated but flows from the grace of God shining upon your household. Just as the psalmist petitions for the Lord’s face to shine, so must you lead your wife in the light of His Word, ensuring that your home is a sanctuary of truth and love. Your joy is rooted in the certainty that God is faithful to provide for your flock and to guard your union against the storms of this world.

As a FULFILLED father, you see your role as a steward of the covenant, tasked with passing down the knowledge of the Lord to your children. The blessing of God is not merely for your comfort but for the instruction of your seed, that they may know the way of the Lord and fear Him. You stand as a tangible witness to their young eyes, demonstrating that the God of Scripture is real, active, and worthy of awe, guiding their steps with the same equity He shows to the nations.

As a RESOLUTE citizen, you acknowledge that your authority and your duty are subordinate to the King of Kings. God judges with equity and guides the nations, meaning your civic responsibilities are performed under His sovereign gaze. You do not seek to build a kingdom of your own but to serve in your sphere with integrity, knowing that all power belongs to Him and that your labor in the public square is an act of worship when done for His glory.

🙏 Prayer

Father, I thank You for the grace that has sustained me through this day. I ask that Your face would continue to shine upon my home, my family, and my community, that Your way may be known in the earth. By the power of Your Holy Spirit, grant me the strength to lead with humility, to love with sacrifice, and to hold fast to the truth of Your Word. Keep me vigilant against the snares of the enemy and anchor my heart in Your eternal promises. For the sake of Christ our King, I pray. Amen.
