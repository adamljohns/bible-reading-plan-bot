⚓ MORNING WISDOM — Friday, April 10, 2026

📖 Scripture — Proverbs 3:3-8

Never let loyalty and faithfulness leave you. Bind them around your neck; write them on the tablet of your heart. Then you will find favor and a good name in the sight of God and man.

Trust in the LORD with all your heart and do not lean on your own understanding. In all your ways acknowledge Him, and He will make your paths straight.

Do not be wise in your own eyes; fear the LORD and turn away from evil. It will be healing to your body and refreshment to your bones.

🧭 Context

Solomon writes to his son — and to every man after him — about the twin anchors of loyalty and faithfulness. These aren't abstract virtues. In the ancient world, a man's loyalty determined whether he ate or starved, whether his family was protected or abandoned. The Hebrew word "chesed" here carries the weight of covenant love — the kind that stays when staying costs something.

🛡️ REAL MAN — L: LOYAL

A loyal man doesn't shift with the wind. He is the same man on Friday night that he is on Sunday morning. Loyalty to God anchors every other loyalty — to his wife, his children, his brothers, his word. The world rewards cleverness. God rewards faithfulness. A man who trusts his own understanding will eventually outsmart himself. A man who trusts the LORD builds something that outlasts him.

⛏️ Application

• Examine one relationship this week where your loyalty has been inconsistent — a friend you've neglected, a commitment you've let slide. Take one concrete step today to restore it.
• Before making any decision today, pause and ask: "Am I leaning on my own understanding or seeking the LORD's direction?"
• Write Proverbs 3:3 somewhere you'll see it — your phone lock screen, a note on the dash, the bathroom mirror. Let it confront you all day.
• Tonight, ask your wife or a close brother: "Where have you seen me be most loyal? Where do I fall short?" Listen without defending.

🙏 Prayer

Father, forgive me for the times I have trusted my own cleverness more than Your wisdom. Burn loyalty into my character — not the fair-weather kind, but the covenant kind that stays when it costs me something. Make me a man whose word means something, whose presence is reliable, and whose heart is anchored in You alone. In Jesus' name. Amen.

⚓ Helm Command: "Loyalty is not a feeling. It is a decision renewed every morning. Stay the course."

📖 **Scripture: Judges 14** — Samson's Marriage and the Riddle

💍 **H.H.A.A.P.P.Y. Husband — ABIDING in the Marriage**

Samson is the cautionary tale every husband needs to sit with. He had supernatural strength, divine calling, and the Spirit of the LORD — and he still lost his wife before the honeymoon was over. Why? Because strength without presence is just force. Power without abiding is just violence.

An abiding husband stays. Not just physically — any man can sleep under the same roof — but emotionally, spiritually, with his full attention. Samson kept secrets from everyone: his parents, his wife, even from himself about who he really was. A marriage built on hidden things is a house with no foundation.

When your wife asks you a hard question — when she presses in, when she weeps before you — that is not manipulation. That is a woman trying to reach a man who has walled himself off. Abiding means you stay in the conversation when it gets uncomfortable. You open the locked rooms. Your wife is not the enemy — she is the one God gave you to walk beside. But she cannot walk beside a ghost.

The opposite of abiding is drifting. And drifting always looks harmless until you look up and realize you've lost everything.

⛏️ Application: Put the phone down tonight — 15 minutes of undivided attention. Share one thing you've been keeping to yourself. Name your drift. Pray together before bed.

🙏 Prayer: Lord God, forgive me for the times I have been physically present but spiritually absent. Teach me to abide — not just in my marriage, but in You. Root me so deeply in Your presence that my wife never has to wonder where I am. In Jesus' name. Amen.

⚓ Helm Command: "Abiding is not passive. It is the daily, deliberate choice to be fully present where God has planted you. Stay rooted. Stay open. Stay."
