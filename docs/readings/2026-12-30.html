Morning Wisdom

Brother, hold fast to the eternal counsel of the Lord before the day’s battles begin.

📖 Proverbs 8

Does not wisdom cry out, And understanding lift up her voice?
She takes her stand on the top of the high hill, Beside the way, where the paths meet.
She cries out by the gates, at the entry of the city, At the entrance of the doors:
“To you, O men, I call, And my voice is to the sons of men.
O you simple ones, understand prudence, And you fools, be of an understanding heart.
Listen, for I will speak of excellent things, And from the opening of my lips will come right things;
For my mouth will speak truth; Wickedness is an abomination to my lips.
All the words of my mouth are with righteousness; Nothing crooked or perverse is in them.
They are all plain to him who understands, And right to those who find knowledge.
Receive my instruction, and not silver, And knowledge rather than choice gold;
For wisdom is better than rubies, And all the things one may desire cannot be compared with her.
“I, wisdom, dwell with prudence, And find out knowledge and discretion.
The fear of the LORD is to hate evil; Pride and arrogance and the evil way And the perverse mouth I hate.
Counsel is mine, and sound wisdom; I am understanding, I have strength.
By me kings reign, And rulers decree justice.
By me princes rule, and nobles, All the judges of the earth.
I love those who love me, And those who seek me diligently will find me.
Riches and honor are with me, Enduring riches and righteousness.
My fruit is better than gold, yes, than fine gold, And my revenue than choice silver.
I traverse the way of righteousness, In the midst of the paths of justice,
That I may cause those who love me to inherit wealth, That I may fill their treasuries.
“The LORD possessed me at the beginning of His way, Before His works of old.
I have been established from everlasting, From the beginning, before there was ever an earth.
When there were no depths I was brought forth, When there were no fountains abounding with water.
Before the mountains were settled, Before the hills, I was brought forth;
While as yet He had not made the earth or the fields, Or the primal dust of the world.
When He prepared the heavens, I was there, When He drew a circle on the face of the deep,
When He established the clouds above, When He strengthened the fountains of the deep,
When He assigned to the sea its limit, So that the waters would not transgress His command, When He marked out the foundations of the earth,
Then I was beside Him as a master craftsman; And I was daily His delight, Rejoicing always before Him,
Rejoicing in His inhabited world, And my delight was with the sons of men.
“Now therefore, listen to me, my children, For blessed are those who keep my ways.
Hear instruction and be wise, And do not disdain it.
Blessed is the man who listens to me, Watching daily at my gates, Waiting at the posts of my doors.
For whoever finds me finds life, And obtains favor from the LORD;
But he who sins against me wrongs his own soul; All those who hate me love death.”

🧭 Context Summary

The passage presents Wisdom personified as a divine attribute existing before creation, calling out to all mankind. She contrasts her eternal, righteous value against the fleeting and insufficient nature of material wealth. The text establishes that true authority and justice flow from her, and that the fear of the Lord is the necessary starting point for all understanding.

🛡️ Reflection for a REAL MAN — Accept Responsibility

"To you, O men, I call, And my voice is to the sons of men." Wisdom cries out "by the gates, at the entry of the city," where the men sat to govern and judge. She is speaking to the men who answer for the city. "By me kings reign, And rulers decree justice. By me princes rule." To rule is to be responsible. Under God you rule a small realm, your home, and you will answer for it. A ruler cannot blame his people for the condition of the kingdom he governs.

"Pride and arrogance and the evil way And the perverse mouth I hate." Pride is what keeps a man from saying, "This is on me." It shifts the blame. Adam pointed at the woman, and through her at God Himself (Genesis 3). Pride says the marriage is cold because she is distant, the boys are disrespectful because of their friends, the money is tight because of the economy. Wisdom says you are the head, so start with what you have done and what you have left undone.

"But he who sins against me wrongs his own soul." The man who refuses to own his sin wrongs himself first and then everyone under him. "Blessed is the man who listens to me, Watching daily at my gates, Waiting at the posts of my doors." The blessed man is a sentry who owns the watch he was given. Your post is Maria, Gideon, Boaz, Shiloh, and the men you lead. With the year closing, take stock of what is yours and own it before God.

⛏️ Personal Application
  • Pick the one recurring conflict in your home and tell Maria what part of it is yours, without mentioning any part that is hers.
  • Apologize to one of your children today for a specific failure of yours as their father, with no "but" attached.
  • Before the new year, write down everything God has put under your charge, wife, children, ministry, money, and body, and beside each one name one failure you own and one step you will take.
🙏 Prayer

Father, I come before Your throne of grace, acknowledging that all wisdom and strength flow from You alone. Forgive me for the times I have sought my own counsel rather than submitting to the fear of the LORD. By the power of Your Holy Spirit, grant me the prudence to rule my home with justice, the humility to own its condition without blaming anyone under me, and the courage to hate evil with all my heart. Let my life reflect the enduring wealth of righteousness, that my family may see Your glory in my leadership. In Jesus' name, I pray. Amen.

🛡️ Watch Charge: Calibrate your tongue on the anvil of truth so that every word you utter is straight and free from the crookedness of wickedness.
0700

First Watch — The Husband's Post

Brother, man your post: as you guard your household, let the psalmist’s call to bless the Lord shape your morning devotion before you address your wife, Maria.

📖 Psalm 103

A Psalm of David. Bless the LORD, O my soul; And all that is within me, bless His holy name!
Bless the LORD, O my soul, And forget not all His benefits:
Who forgives all your iniquities, Who heals all your diseases,
Who redeems your life from destruction, Who crowns you with lovingkindness and tender mercies,
Who satisfies your mouth with good things, So that your youth is renewed like the eagle’s.
The LORD executes righteousness And justice for all who are oppressed.
He made known His ways to Moses, His acts to the children of Israel.
The LORD is merciful and gracious, Slow to anger, and abounding in mercy.
He will not always strive with us, Nor will He keep His anger forever.
He has not dealt with us according to our sins, Nor punished us according to our iniquities.
For as the heavens are high above the earth, So great is His mercy toward those who fear Him;
As far as the east is from the west, So far has He removed our transgressions from us.
As a father pities his children, So the LORD pities those who fear Him.
For He knows our frame; He remembers that we are dust.
As for man, his days are like grass; As a flower of the field, so he flourishes.
For the wind passes over it, and it is gone, And its place remembers it no more.
But the mercy of the LORD is from everlasting to everlasting On those who fear Him, And His righteousness to children’s children,
To such as keep His covenant, And to those who remember His commandments to do them.
The LORD has established His throne in heaven, And His kingdom rules over all.
Bless the LORD, you His angels, Who excel in strength, who do His word, Heeding the voice of His word.
Bless the LORD, all you His hosts, You ministers of His, who do His pleasure.
Bless the LORD, all His works, In all places of His dominion. Bless the LORD, O my soul!

🗺️ Briefing Summary

David calls the church to remember the specific benefits of redemption, focusing on God’s character as a compassionate Father who forgives and heals. The passage contrasts the fleeting, fragile nature of human life with the eternal, stable mercy of the Almighty. It establishes that our standing before God is not based on our merit, but on His sovereign grace and paternal pity. This truth anchors the husband’s duty to lead his home with grace, mirroring the mercy he has received.

❤️ Reflection for Your Wife — H.A.P.P.Y. Husband — Adoring

"Bless the LORD, O my soul, And forget not all His benefits." David counts God's kindnesses one by one, and his heart swells into praise. Adoration grows by remembering. Marriages cool when a husband forgets, when he quits noticing what his wife has poured into his life: the children she bore him, the nights she waited up, the faith she has kept beside him through hard years. Adoring Maria begins with refusing to forget.

"Who crowns you with lovingkindness and tender mercies." Look at how God loves His people. He does not merely put up with them; He crowns them. Christ loves His church that way and gave Himself for her (Ephesians 5:25). And "As far as the east is from the west, So far has He removed our transgressions from us." He keeps no ledger against His bride. A husband who keeps a running list of his wife's failings cannot delight in her. Clear the record as yours has been cleared, and then crown her with tender affection.

"So that your youth is renewed like the eagle's." The God who renews youth can renew delight in a long marriage. Romance is not only for the early years. Pursue Maria as you did when you were courting her: notes, time alone, touch that is not only a prelude, words that tell her she is lovely to you. The Song of Solomon is in your Bible for a reason. Let the last days of this year be days when Maria knows, without having to guess, that her husband still delights in her.

⛏️ Personal Application
  • Write down ten specific ways Maria has blessed your life this year and read them to her tonight.
  • Plan and lead a New Year's Eve moment alone with Maria, whether a walk, a meal, or a prayer, and tell her plainly what you love about her.
  • Throw out whatever mental list you have been keeping of her failings, and greet her today with affection and no agenda.
🙏 Prayer

Father, I praise You for Your boundless mercy and Your righteous judgment. You who forgive iniquity and heal my diseases, grant me the grace to delight in my wife Maria, to crown her with tender affection, and to love her with the same compassion and grace that You have shown to me. By the power of Your Holy Spirit, help me to remember that I am dust, yet I am loved by the King of kings. Let my home be a place where Your covenant mercy is displayed through my leadership and my love. In the name of Jesus Christ, I pray. Amen.

🛡️ Watch Charge: Steady your heart on the mercy of the LORD and lead your house with grace.

Second Watch — The Father's Charge

Brother, take up the charge: The dawn of a new day calls us to hold fast to the truth that the King of Glory sustains His kingdom and His people with unshakeable faithfulness.

📖 Psalm 145

A Praise of David. I will extol You, my God, O King; And I will bless Your name forever and ever.
Every day I will bless You, And I will praise Your name forever and ever.
Great is the LORD, and greatly to be praised; And His greatness is unsearchable.
One generation shall praise Your works to another, And shall declare Your mighty acts.
I will meditate on the glorious splendor of Your majesty, And on Your wondrous works.
Men shall speak of the might of Your awesome acts, And I will declare Your greatness.
They shall utter the memory of Your great goodness, And shall sing of Your righteousness.
The LORD is gracious and full of compassion, Slow to anger and great in mercy.
The LORD is good to all, And His tender mercies are over all His works.
All Your works shall praise You, O LORD, And Your saints shall bless You.
They shall speak of the glory of Your kingdom, And talk of Your power,
To make known to the sons of men His mighty acts, And the glorious majesty of His kingdom.
Your kingdom is an everlasting kingdom, And Your dominion endures throughout all generations.
The LORD upholds all who fall, And raises up all who are bowed down.
The eyes of all look expectantly to You, And You give them their food in due season.
You open Your hand And satisfy the desire of every living thing.
The LORD is righteous in all His ways, Gracious in all His works.
The LORD is near to all who call upon Him, To all who call upon Him in truth.
He will fulfill the desire of those who fear Him; He also will hear their cry and save them.
The LORD preserves all who love Him, But all the wicked He will destroy.
My mouth shall speak the praise of the LORD, And all flesh shall bless His holy name Forever and ever.

🗺️ Field Notes

This psalm stands as a fortress of praise, reminding us that God’s sovereignty is not distant but intimately involved in the preservation of His saints. For a father, it is a solemn charge to ensure that the next generation knows the unsearchable greatness of their Creator. The text emphasizes that God is near to those who call on Him in truth, offering a solid foundation for our daily walk. We must recognize that while He preserves those who love Him, His justice remains absolute against all wickedness.

👨‍👦 Reflection for Your Children — F.U.L.F.I.L.L.E.D. Father — Intentional

David writes, "One generation shall praise Your works to another, And shall declare Your mighty acts." That handoff does not happen by accident. Faith that reaches the next generation is carried there on purpose, by fathers who decide what they will tell, when, and how. Drift is the default. A man who makes no plan for his children's souls still shapes them, but by whatever happens to fill the hours: screens, schedules, and the culture's own catechism. David says, "Every day I will bless You." He chose the rhythm before the day arrived.

With the year nearly over, this is the week to think deliberately about each child. Gideon is twenty; your aim for him now is not control but counsel and friendship, so decide what you want to pass on to him this coming year as one man to another, and when you will make the time. Boaz at fifteen is about three years from leaving home; ask what he must know, believe, and be able to do before then, and build the year around it. Shiloh at five is at the very start; decide which Scriptures, prayers, and songs she will carry with her by the time she turns six.

Psalm 145 is full of telling: "They shall utter the memory of Your great goodness," "They shall speak of the glory of Your kingdom," "To make known to the sons of men His mighty acts." Make that telling deliberate. Write down the stories of God's faithfulness in your own life, from your years as a Marine, from your marriage, from the founding of USMC Ministries, and plan when each child will hear them. A father with a plan still needs grace at every turn, but he will not wake up one day to find the years gone and the handoff never made.

⛏️ Personal Application
  • Before New Year's Day, write one page for each child: where they are now, what you want to see grow in them in 2027, and one specific thing you will do toward it.
  • Put a recurring time with each child on your 2027 calendar, a monthly breakfast with Gideon, a weekly drive with Boaz, a nightly Bible story with Shiloh.
  • Choose one story of God's faithfulness from your own life and tell it to the family at supper this week, on purpose.
🙏 Prayer

Father, I come before You as Your unworthy servant, asking for the grace to lead my house with integrity and courage. By the power of Your Holy Spirit, keep me from drifting through my children's years and give me a deliberate plan for each one: counsel and friendship for Gideon, a clear aim for Boaz before he leaves home, and the first words of faith for Shiloh. Help me declare Your mighty acts to the next generation on purpose and not by accident. Protect my family from the evil one and keep them in Your truth. Through Christ my Savior, I pray. Amen.

🛡️ Watch Charge: Raise the banner of praise high over your household to signal to the next generation that the LORD’s greatness remains unsearchable.

Third Watch — The Citizen's Stand

Brother, stand firm: The church stands not as a fortress of stone, but as a living army of believers offering their daily lives as holy service.

📖 Romans 12

I beseech you therefore, brethren, by the mercies of God, that you present your bodies a living sacrifice, holy, acceptable to God, which is your reasonable service. And do not be conformed to this world, but be transformed by the renewing of your mind, that you may prove what is that good and acceptable and perfect will of God. For I say, through the grace given to me, to everyone who is among you, not to think of himself more highly than he ought to think, but to think soberly, as God has dealt to each one a measure of faith. For as we have many members in one body, but all the members do not have the same function, so we, being many, are one body in Christ, and individually members of one another. Having then gifts differing according to the grace that is given to us, let us use them: if prophecy, let us prophesy in proportion to our faith; or ministry, let us use it in our ministering; he who teaches, in teaching; he who exhorts, in exhortation; he who gives, with liberality; he who leads, with diligence; he who shows mercy, with cheerfulness. Let love be without hypocrisy. Abhor what is evil. Cling to what is good. Be kindly affectionate to one another with brotherly love, in honor giving preference to one another; not lagging in diligence, fervent in spirit, serving the Lord; rejoicing in hope, patient in tribulation, continuing steadfastly in prayer; distributing to the needs of the saints, given to hospitality. Bless those who persecute you; bless and do not curse. Rejoice with those who rejoice, and weep with those who weep. Be of the same mind toward one another. Do not set your mind on high things, but associate with the humble. Do not be wise in your own opinion. Repay no one evil for evil. Have regard for good things in the sight of all men. If it is possible, as much as depends on you, live peaceably with all men. Beloved, do not avenge yourselves, but rather give place to wrath; for it is written, “Vengeance is Mine, I will repay,” says the Lord. Therefore “If your enemy is hungry, feed him; If he is thirsty, give him a drink; For in so doing you will heap coals of fire on his head.” Do not be overcome by evil, but overcome evil with good.

🛰️ Situation Report

Brother, you stand in the gap for your household and your community, called to a service that transcends mere duty. Paul reminds us that true strength comes not from conforming to the world’s standards, but from a mind renewed by the truth of God. We are distinct members of one Body, each called to serve with the specific grace God has apportioned to us.

🛡️ Reflection for a R.E.S.O.L.U.T.E. Citizen — Enduring

Fredericksburg (local)
Paul calls the church "one body in Christ, and individually members of one another," each with gifts to use: "he who leads, with diligence; he who shows mercy, with cheerfulness." Bodies grow slowly, and so do towns. Fredericksburg's lasting institutions, its churches, its food bank, its youth leagues, its civic boards, were built by people who put in decades of unglamorous service. The Enduring citizen does not look for a quick win in his city. He finds his place in the body and serves there for years, "not lagging in diligence." Spotswood Baptist and the ministries it supports in town need men who will still be at it ten years from now.

Virginia (state)
Paul tells believers to be "rejoicing in hope, patient in tribulation." Civic life in Virginia will test that patience. Good bills die in committee, bad ones pass, and a decade of work can be undone in a single session in Richmond. Men who burn hot and quit cannot build anything that lasts. The Enduring citizen in the Commonwealth takes the long view: he keeps showing up, keeps writing, keeps voting, keeps raising up the next set of men to do the same, and leaves the outcome to God across many sessions, not one.

United States (nation)
Romans 12 ends, "Do not be overcome by evil, but overcome evil with good." That is a long campaign, not a single battle. The nation is not renewed by one election, one law, or one viral moment. It is renewed slowly, by citizens who "Repay no one evil for evil," who keep doing good in their own corner year after year, and who pass the habit on. A Marine knows the difference between a raid and a campaign. Citizenship is a campaign. Plan to be at it for the rest of your life, and to leave men behind you who will carry it on.

⛏️ Personal Application
  • Pick one Fredericksburg institution, a ministry at Spotswood Baptist, the food bank, a youth league, a city board, and commit to serve there through all of 2027, not just once.
  • Mark every 2027 Fredericksburg city council meeting and Virginia election date on your calendar now, so showing up is planned rather than remembered.
  • Invite one younger man this week to join you in that work, so the service outlasts your own years.
🙏 Prayer

Father, I thank You for the mercy that has brought me to this point of standing. I ask that You grant me the strength to present my body as a living sacrifice, holy and acceptable in Your sight. By the power of Your Holy Spirit, make me patient in tribulation and not lagging in diligence, willing to serve my city for years and not only for a season. Teach me to overcome evil with good across a lifetime, to build in Fredericksburg what will outlast me, and to hand the work on to the men who come after. In the name of the risen Lord Jesus, I pray. Amen.

🛡️ Watch Charge: Strip the rot of this age from your timber and burn your days as a living offering on the altar of His will.
🦅 This Day in American History — December 30

On December 30, 1813, British troops and their allies put the frontier village of Buffalo to the torch, leaving its hardy settlers homeless in the dead of winter amid the hardships of that frontier war. On the same day in 1853, James Gadsden signed the treaty at Mexico City securing for the United States the desert lands that would round out the southern border of Arizona and New Mexico. An Enduring citizen recalls how the people of Buffalo rebuilt from the ashes and how a young nation kept building toward frontiers it would take generations to settle, trusting that the God who sustains a people through fire and loss will yet establish the work of their hands.

Evening Peace

The day’s exertions yield to the quiet assurance that the Lord has kept you and your house.

📖 Psalm 114

When Israel went out of Egypt, The house of Jacob from a people of strange language,
Judah became His sanctuary, And Israel His dominion.
The sea saw it and fled; Jordan turned back.
The mountains skipped like rams, The little hills like lambs.
What ails you, O sea, that you fled? O Jordan, that you turned back?
O mountains, that you skipped like rams? O little hills, like lambs?
Tremble, O earth, at the presence of the Lord, At the presence of the God of Jacob,
Who turned the rock into a pool of water, The flint into a fountain of waters.

🌾 Reflection for a Man of God

As a HAPPY husband, brother, you find your rest in the same God Who brought His people out of bondage into the promised land. Your home is not merely a shelter from the storm, but a sanctuary established by Divine decree. Just as the Lord made Judah His sanctuary and Israel His dominion, He calls you to rule your household with a gentle but firm hand, mirroring Christ’s headship over the church. Your marriage is a covenant of protection and provision, a tangible sign of the gospel where you lead your wife not by force, but by the sacrificial love that secures her peace.

As a FULFILLED father, you recognize that the same Power Who parted the Red Sea and turned back the Jordan is the Author of your lineage. You do not raise your children in the chaos of the world, but in the order of His Word. The mountains of your discipline and the hills of your instruction are meant to skip and dance under the weight of His grace. You teach your sons that strength is not for self-aggrandizement, but for the defense of the weak and the upholding of truth. Your fatherhood is a stewardship of the covenant, ensuring that your house knows the God of Jacob, Who turns the hardest rock of sin into a fountain of living water.

As a RESOLUTE citizen, you stand firm in the knowledge that the earth trembles at the presence of the Lord. Your service in the Corps and your life in the republic are anchored in the sovereignty of God, Who holds all nations in His hand. You do not seek the applause of men, for your citizenship is in heaven, and your earthly duties are performed as unto the Lord. Let the world see your steadiness, your integrity, and your unwavering loyalty to the Constitution and the Creed. You are a man of action, yet your heart rests in the One Who commands the elements, knowing that your labor in the Lord is not in vain.

🙏 Prayer

Father, I thank You for the deliverance You have wrought in my life, bringing me from the slavery of sin into the liberty of Your grace. I ask for the strength to lead my household with wisdom and courage, and to raise my children in the nurture and admonition of the Lord. By the power of Your Holy Spirit, make me steadfast in my duties, unshaken by the turmoil of the age, and faithful to the covenant promises. Guard my heart from pride and my hands from sloth, that I may serve You with reverence and godly fear. For the sake of Christ our King, I pray. Amen.
