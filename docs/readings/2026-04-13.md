## 🛡️ 2nd Watch — Father's Charge (1100)
**📖 1 Samuel 8 · KJV**

### Scripture
And it came to pass, when Samuel was old, that he made his sons judges over Israel. Now the name of his firstborn was Joel; and the name of his second, Abiah: they were judges in Beersheba. And his sons walked not in his ways, but turned aside after lucre, and took bribes, and perverted judgment.

Then all the elders of Israel gathered themselves together, and came to Samuel unto Ramah, and said unto him, Behold, thou art old, and thy sons walk not in thy ways: now make us a king to judge us like all the nations.

But the thing displeased Samuel, when they said, Give us a king to judge us. And Samuel prayed unto the LORD. And the LORD said unto Samuel, Hearken unto the voice of the people in all that they say unto thee: for they have not rejected thee, but they have rejected me, that I should not reign over them.

And Samuel told all the words of the LORD unto the people that asked of him a king. And he said, This will be the manner of the king that shall reign over you: He will take your sons, and appoint them for himself, for his chariots, and to be his horsemen. And he will take your daughters to be confectionaries, and to be cooks, and to be bakers. And he will take your fields, and your vineyards, and your oliveyards, even the best of them, and give them to his servants. He will take the tenth of your sheep: and ye shall be his servants.

Nevertheless the people refused to obey the voice of Samuel; and they said, Nay; but we will have a king over us; that we also may be like all the nations. And the LORD said to Samuel, Hearken unto their voice, and make them a king.

### Context
Samuel — last of the judges, faithful from boyhood — appointed his sons Joel and Abiah as judges, but they turned aside after lucre. The elders demanded a king "like all the nations." God told Samuel the painful truth: they were rejecting God as King.

### F.U.L.F.I.L.L.E.D. Reflection
- **F — Faith Modeled:** Samuel modeled hearing God's voice and obeying. Your children watch whether your faith is Sunday performance or Monday reality.
- **U — Understanding the Risk:** Even a righteous father can raise wayward sons. Urgency, not comfort.
- **L — Letting Go of Control:** Trust God who is bigger than your parenting.
- **F — Fighting for Their Hearts:** Speak truth in love, then let them choose.
- **I — Identity Over Imitation:** Teach them their identity is rooted in whose they are.
- **L — Legacy Isn't Guaranteed:** A good name must be chosen by each generation.
- **L — Listening to God's Voice:** Pray first. React second.
- **E — Entrusting Them to God:** Your children belong to God before they belong to you.
- **D — Discipline of Presence:** Stay in the fight even when they're running.

### Helm Command
You are not the king of your household. God is. Point your children to the true King, warn them of every counterfeit, and trust the One who called them before you ever held them.
🌙 Evening Peace — From Pastor John Wesley Graves

📖 **Psalm 92**
A psalm. A song. For the Sabbath day.
It is good to praise the LORD and make music to your name, O Most High,
proclaiming your love in the morning and your faithfulness at night,
to the music of the ten-stringed lyre and the melody of the harp.
For you make me glad by your deeds, LORD; I sing for joy at what your hands have done.
How great are your works, LORD, how profound your thoughts!
Senseless people do not know, fools do not understand,
that though the wicked spring up like grass and all evildoers flourish, they will be destroyed forever.
But you, LORD, are forever exalted.
For surely your enemies, LORD, surely your enemies will perish; all evildoers will be scattered.
You have exalted my horn like that of a wild ox; fine oils have been poured on me.
My eyes have seen the defeat of my adversaries; my ears have heard the rout of my wicked foes.
The righteous will flourish like a palm tree, they will grow like a cedar of Lebanon;
planted in the house of the LORD, they will flourish in the courts of our God.
They will still bear fruit in old age, they will stay fresh and green,
proclaiming, “The LORD is upright; he is my Rock, and there is no wickedness in him.”

🛡️ **Evening Reflection**
Brothers, as the day draws to a close, we are reminded by Psalm 92 of the steadfast faithfulness of our God. Just as the righteous are planted in the house of the Lord to flourish, so too are we called to thrive in our various roles. As **husbands**, let us reflect on how we have cherished and honored our wives, proclaiming our love to them as we proclaim God's love in the morning. Have we been a source of strength and joy, making our homes a place where true peace can be found?

As **fathers**, consider the seeds we are planting in the lives of our children. Are we guiding them towards the courts of our God, nurturing them to be fresh and green, bearing fruit even in their youth and old age? Our profound thoughts and deeds, like the Lord's, should reflect wisdom and care in their upbringing.

And as **citizens**, we are called to stand upright, proclaiming the Lord's righteousness in a world that often seems senseless. Though evildoers may flourish for a season, their end is certain. Let us be unwavering in our testimony, rooted firmly in Christ, our Rock, knowing there is no wickedness in Him. Tonight, let us rest in His exaltation, trusting that His works, though sometimes mysterious, are always profound and ultimately for our good.

**Night Orders:**
*   **Reaffirm:** Remind your family of your love and commitment before sleep.
*   **Reflect:** Consider one area where you can better align your actions with God's righteousness tomorrow.
*   **Rest:** Cast your anxieties upon the Lord and find peace in His eternal presence.

🙏 **Prayer**
Heavenly Father, we thank You for Your unwavering love and faithfulness throughout this day. As we prepare for rest, we pray for Your peace to guard our hearts and minds. Strengthen us, Lord, to be righteous husbands, fathers, and citizens, flourishing in Your presence and proclaiming Your goodness to all. May our lives be a testament to Your uprightness, our Rock in whom there is no wickedness. Amen.

Pastor John Wesley Graves
