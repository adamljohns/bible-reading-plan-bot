Morning Wisdom

The Lord prepares a man for service by granting him the wisdom to seek His face with unyielding resolve.

📖 Ezra 7

The LORD moved the heart of Cyrus king of Persia to make a proclamation throughout all his kingdom, and also to put in writing: Thus says King Cyrus: The LORD God of heaven has given me all the kingdoms of the earth, and He has charged me to build Him a house at Jerusalem which is in Judah. Who is there among you of all His people? May his God be with him, and let him go up to Jerusalem which is in Judah and build the house of the LORD God of Israel (He is the God) who is in Jerusalem. And whoever is left in any place where he dwells, let the men of his place support him with silver and gold, with goods and livestock, besides the freewill offering for the house of God that is in Jerusalem.

Then the heads of the fathers’ houses of Judah and Benjamin, and the priests and the Levites, with everyone whose spirit God had stirred, went up to Jerusalem to build the house of the LORD which is in Jerusalem. Now all those around them supported them with vessels of silver, with gold, with goods, with livestock, and with precious objects, besides all that was willingly offered.

King Cyrus also brought out the vessels of the house of the LORD which Nebuchadnezzar had taken from Jerusalem and had put in the house of his gods. And Cyrus king of Persia brought them out by the hand of Mithredath the treasurer, and he counted them to Sheshbazzar, the prince of Judah. And this was the number of them: thirty basins of gold, a thousand basins of silver, twenty-nine knives, thirty bowls of gold, silver bowls of a second kind, four hundred and ten other vessels, and all the vessels of gold and silver were three thousand. The whole of the gold and silver vessels was five thousand four hundred. All these did Sheshbazzar bring up with them of the captivity who were brought up from Babylon to Jerusalem.

🧭 Context Summary

Ezra 7 opens with the divine sovereignty over the Persian empire, demonstrating that the LORD uses even pagan rulers to fulfill His redemptive purposes for His people. The text highlights the return of the sacred vessels and the mobilization of the remnant, showing that God stirs the hearts of His elect to obey His call despite the daunting odds. This section establishes the historical and spiritual foundation for Ezra’s subsequent mission, proving that the restoration of Jerusalem is entirely the work of the LORD’s gracious hand.

🛡️ Reflection for a REAL MAN — Divine Sovereignty

Brother, you must understand that your life is not a series of random accidents, but a meticulously ordered narrative under the thumb of the Almighty. Just as the LORD stirred the heart of Cyrus to issue a decree that seemed impossible to the faithful, He has stirred your heart to stand firm in your covenant duties. You are a man of war and family, and you may often feel surrounded by enemies or overwhelmed by the weight of provision. Yet, the same God who moved the king of Persia is your Commander, and He does not lose control of the battlefield or the home front.

When you face obstacles that appear insurmountable, remember that the LORD is working behind the scenes, aligning the hearts of men and the circumstances of history for the good of His people. Do not falter in your resolve because the world seems hostile or indifferent to your faithfulness. Your strength does not come from your own strategic brilliance, but from the fact that the LORD God of heaven has given all things into His Son’s hands, and He is fighting for you.

Walk up to the task God has set before you with boldness, knowing that He has charged you to build His house in your life and in your community. Support your brethren with your resources and your strength, just as the surrounding nations supported the builders. Trust that the LORD will bring out the treasures of His grace for you, even when you must start with nothing but faith. Stand firm, brother, for the LORD is with you, and He will complete the work He has begun in you.

📖 Ezekiel 23

The word of the LORD came to me: Son of man, there were two women, daughters of the same mother. They became prostitutes in Egypt, engaging in prostitution from their youth, when their breasts were fondled and their virgin bosoms caressed. The older was named Oholah and the younger Oholibah, and they were Mine. They gave themselves to prostitution and each had lovers. Oholah was Samaria and her lovers the Assyrians, who flaunted her indecency. They deployed men from their ranks as lovers, wearing blue, commanders and officers, all of them desirable young men, and riding on horses. She gave herself as a prostitute to all the elite of the Assyrians and defiled herself with all the idols of those she loved. She did not give up the prostitution she began in Egypt, for on that night she relished her lovers, those whose genitals were like those of donkeys and whose emission was like that of horses. You will be disgusted by the evil you have practiced and by the idols you have detested. Oholibah saw all this and was more depraved in her lustful ways and prostitution than her sister. She gave herself to all the lovers of her choice, to those she had courted. She defiled herself with their idols and became unclean in their presence. She even forgot the days of her youth and violated her covenant with Me. You will be disgusted by her nakedness, for her shameless breasts and womb will be exposed. I will expose her vagina to her lovers, to all who she slept with, and to all the idols she loved. I will gather against her all her lovers, all who she loved, and all those she hated; I will expose her nakedness to them, and they will see her entirely naked. I will inflict punishment on her for the times she went to the pagan shrines and entered those places where she brought her perfume and her incense. Then you will ask, ‘When did I become so diseased and abominable?’ You will look upon your lovers’ genitals as an abominable thing. And Oholibah went further in her prostitution than the others. She saw men depicted on a wall, figures of Chaldeans portrayed in red, girded with belts around their waists, with flowing turbans on their heads; all of them looked like officers, resembling the appearance of the Babylonians of Chaldea, the land of their origin. As soon as she saw them, she was captivated by their images and sent messengers to them in Chaldea for her lovers to come to her. The Babylonians came to her, to the bed of love, and defiled her with their prostitution, and she was polluted by them and she turned away from them in disgust. When she gave herself over to prostitution, she revealed her nakedness; so I turned away from her as I had turned away from her sister. Yet she indulged in even more prostitution, recalling the days of her youth when she was a prostitute in Egypt. There she lusted after her lovers, whose genitals were like those of donkeys and whose emission was like that of horses. So you longed for the lewdness of your youth, when in Egypt your bosom was caressed and your young breasts fondled. Therefore, Oholibah, this is what the Sovereign LORD says: I will stir up your lovers against you, those you turned away from in disgust, and I will bring them against you from everywhere. They will bring against you the Chaldeans, all the Babylonians, the people of Puth and Lud and Shoa, all the Assyrians with them, desirable young men, commanders and officers, all of them prosperous riders wearing helmets. They will come against you with swords, swords and chariots and horsemen and chariots of horror, all of them arrayed for war. I will turn over to them the prize of your property, and they will put an end to your shameful prostitution and take your ill-gotten gains. You will be left naked and exposed. The shame of your prostitution will be revealed. Your lewdness and promiscuity have brought this on you, because you pursued your lovers and defiled yourself with all their idols. And you have followed the way of your sister; so I will put her cup in your hand. This is what the Sovereign LORD says: You will drink your sister’s cup, the cup full and overflowing; it will bring scorn and derision, for it holds a cup of devastation and desolation, the cup of your sister Samaria. You will be filled with drunkenness and sorrow, the cup of devastation and desolation, the cup of your sister Samaria. You will be filled full, but not with wine. You will be desolate and devastated, the cup of terror and ruin, the cup of your sister Jerusalem. This is what the Sovereign LORD says: Because you have forgotten Me and cast Me behind your back, you must bear the consequences of your lewdness and prostitution. The LORD said to me: Son of man, will you judge Oholah and Oholibah? Declare to them their detestable practices, for they committed adultery with men who lived far away, whose bodies were like those of donkeys and whose emission was like that of horses. They slept with all the worst kind of men, like harlots, and they accepted payment. But in the case of an adulteress who replaces strangers, yet gives no payment, while these men give their payment to one another and have lewd relations with her. Therefore, Oholah and Oholibah, this is what the Sovereign LORD says: I will punish you with lewd acts and bring upon you all the usual penalties for adultery. I will bring a mob against them to give them over to terror and plunder. The mob will stone them and cut them down with their swords. They will kill their sons and daughters and burn down their houses. For I will declare that they will be punished with the penalties for prostitution, and adultery will be charged against them, because they are adulteresses and their hands are full of blood.

🗺️ Briefing Summary

The prophet Ezekiel uses a visceral allegory of two sisters to illustrate the unfaithfulness of Israel and Judah to their Covenant Lord. The text describes their spiritual adultery as a reckless pursuit of foreign alliances and idols, resulting in severe divine judgment. This passage serves as a stark warning against the soul-deadening consequences of compromising with the world.

❤️ Reflection for Your Wife — H.A.P.P.Y. Husband — Protecting

Brother, this passage is a terrifying mirror. It shows us that spiritual death begins not with a grand rebellion, but with a gradual, seductive compromise. The sisters were not corrupted overnight; they were worn down by familiarity, by the "fondling" of lesser affections, until they forgot their original Husband. As a husband, you are called to be the guardian of the spiritual integrity of your home. You must watch for the subtle encroachments of the world that seek to distract Maria and your children from their devotion to the LORD.

Your protection is not merely physical, though that is vital. It is the active, vigilant defense of the covenant heart. You must identify the "lovers" of this age—the ideologies, the entertainments, the alliances—that promise satisfaction but deliver only shame. You must tear them down before they take root in the soil of your family’s soul. Do not let the "blue uniforms" of secular culture charm your household into a false sense of security.

To protect is to lead with discernment. It is to recognize when a compromise, however small, is actually a breach of the covenant. You must be the wall that stands between the holy intimacy of your marriage and the defiling influences of the world. You do this not out of fear, but out of a fierce love for the truth and for those you are sworn to keep safe.

  • Conduct a weekly inventory of the media and influences entering your home, asking if they honor or hinder the glory of God.
  • Establish clear, unapologetic boundaries with secular alliances that demand compromise of biblical truth.
  • Engage in direct, verbal instruction with Maria regarding the dangers of spiritual drift, reinforcing their primary covenant bond.

Second Watch — The Father's Charge

The Lord speaks through the prophet to remind His people that His judgments are deliberate, sober, and inescapable.

📖 Ezekiel 24

In the ninth year of my exile, in the tenth month, on the tenth day of the month, the word of the Lord came to me: Son of man, write down this name of the day, this very day. The king of Babylon has laid siege to Jerusalem this very day. Therefore, son of man, set forth a pot, set it on the fire and put water in it. Put into it the pieces of meat, every good piece, the thigh and the shoulder; fill it with choice bones. Take the finest of the flock and pile the wood under it; boil the pieces in it and cook the bones in it. For thus says the Lord God: Blessed is the pot, and blessed are the fish in it! But do not take the bones out of it; let them go down into the fire and be burned. For thus says the Lord God: Woe to the bloody city, to the pot that has rust on it and whose rust has not gone out of it! Take the pieces out of it, one by one, without casting lots. For the blood has been poured out in the midst of it; it has been put on the bare rock; it has not been poured on the ground to cover it with dust. That it may cause wrath to come up to take vengeance; I have set her blood on the bare rock, that it might not be covered. Therefore thus says the Lord God: Woe to the bloody city! I too will make the pile great. Heap up the wood, kindle the fire, boil the meat well, mix in the spices, and let the bones be burned. Then set it empty on the coals, that it may become hot and its copper may burn, that its filthiness may be melted in it, that its rust may be consumed. It has frustrated efforts and its great rust has not gone out of it, even by fire. Your filthiness is in your uncleanness. Because I have cleansed you and you were not cleansed, you shall not be cleansed from your filthiness anymore, till I have satisfied My fury against you. I the Lord have spoken it; it shall come to pass and I will do it. I will not spare, nor will I pity, nor will I repent, according to your ways and according to your deeds, says the Lord God. They shall execute judgments on you according to your ways, and your abominations shall be on you. Then you shall know that I am the Lord.

🗺️ Field Notes

Ezekiel acts out a grim prophecy while his own wife dies, teaching Jerusalem that their destruction is not a surprise to God. The imagery of the boiling pot illustrates how God will sift through His people, removing the good while leaving the corrupt to burn. This scene reveals that divine judgment is precise, leaving no room for excuse or escape from His righteous wrath.

Shiloh, my little one, even in your youth, you must know that God sees all things. He watches over you with a father’s eye. Learn to obey, for the path of the upright is like the shining light that grows brighter and brighter.

🛰️ Situation Report

Ezekiel pronounces judgment upon the nations surrounding Israel, not merely as political entities but as proud powers that mocked God’s people and defiled His sanctuary. The text strips away their confidence, revealing that their strength is vanity before the Sovereign Judge. For the Christian soldier, this passage underscores that God defends His honor and His people with absolute finality.

🛡️ Reflection for a R.E.S.O.L.U.T.E. Citizen — Steadfast

Fredericksburg (local)
Brother, look to the banks of the Rappahannock. In the winter of 1862, the Union army faced a Confederate line entrenched on high ground that seemed impregnable. The order to assault was given, and men walked into a hail of fire with tragic consequences. Yet, in that darkness, we see the reality of earthly warfare: it is messy, costly, and often futile when driven by human pride. But consider the men who held the line at Marye’s Heights. They stood firm, not knowing the outcome, but knowing their duty. Steadfastness is not the absence of fear or the guarantee of victory; it is the refusal to break covenant under pressure. As you stand in your local church and community, you are called to hold the line of truth when culture profanes the sanctuary of the family. You do not retreat when the enemy mocks your faith; you stand, knowing the Lord fights for those who are faithful.

Virginia (state)
The Commonwealth of Virginia was born from the soil of covenant and conflict. From the first settlers who sought to establish a godly order in the wilderness to the patriots who drafted the Virginia Declaration of Rights, this land has seen the tension between human liberty and divine authority. When the state turns against biblical morality, the Christian citizen must remain steadfast in his allegiance to the higher Law. The history of Virginia teaches us that political power is transient, but the Word of the Lord endures. You must be like the sturdy oaks of the Shenandoah Valley, rooted deep in Scripture, unshaken by the winds of secularism that sweep through our government buildings. Your loyalty to your state does not excuse compromise; it demands that you be a salt preservative, standing firm against the decay of moral relativism.

United States (nation)
The United States was founded upon the premise that all men are created by a Creator and endowed with certain unalienable rights. This is a biblical truth, not a philosophical abstraction. When the nation forgets its debt to God, it invites the judgment that Ezekiel describes. Yet, God is merciful and preserves a remnant. As a citizen of this republic, your steadfastness is your contribution to its preservation. You uphold the rule of law, you honor the flag, and you defend the weak, all while pointing your family and neighbors to the true King. Do not let the noise of political division distract you from your primary duty: to be a man of God in a land that is slowly forgetting Him. Stand firm, brother, for the foundation of the nation rests on the bedrock of biblical justice.

🦅 This Day in American History — July 11

On July 11, 1804, Alexander Hamilton, the principal author of the Federalist Papers and a man of profound biblical faith, died in a duel with Aaron Burr. While the duel itself was a tragic failure of Christian brotherhood and a sin of pride, Hamilton’s life remains a testament to the power of a mind and spirit dedicated to establishing a just republic. He spent his final hours reading Scripture and receiving communion, demonstrating a steadfast commitment to his faith even in the face of death. His legacy reminds us that our duty to God and country transcends our earthly reputation.

On July 11, 1911, the U.S. Navy established the first aviation school at Pensacola, Florida, marking a pivotal step in the development of naval air power. This event reflects the American spirit of innovation and the willingness to master new tools for the defense of liberty. It serves as a reminder to the Christian soldier that we must be diligent in learning and mastering the skills God has given us to protect the weak and uphold justice. Just as these early aviators trained with discipline and courage, we must train our spiritual lives with equal rigor.

🌾 Reflection for a Man at Home and in Community

As a HAPPY husband, you recognize that true security is not found in the shifting sands of worldly alliances or the hollow promises of self-reliance. Just as Judah sought deliverance from Egypt instead of the LORD, we often look to our careers, our finances, or even our own strength to save us. But you, brother, know that your wife and children are safest when their provider trusts entirely in the LORD. Do not let fear drive you to compromise your covenant duties. Instead, stand firm in the truth that the LORD is your sanctuary and your shield. Your peace in the marriage bed is rooted in the peace of your soul, which is anchored in Christ alone.

As a FULFILLED father, you see the danger of leading your children into a rebellion that mirrors the world’s pattern. The children of Israel added sin to sin by refusing to acknowledge the LORD as holy. You must guard your household against this same spirit. Teach your sons that strength is not measured by independence from God, but by submission to His will. Let your home be a place where the LORD is regarded as holy, where His name is revered, and where His authority is accepted without grumbling. Your fatherhood is a stewardship of faith, and you must ensure that your legacy is one of reverence for the LORD of hosts, not a legacy of human pride.

As a RESOLUTE citizen, you understand that the world calls holiness foolishness and labels dependence on God as weakness. To the rebellious, the LORD is a stone of stumbling. You, brother, do not fear the ridicule of those who have lost their way. You have been called to be holy as He is holy. In your community, your witness is your integrity. Let your actions declare that the LORD is your dread and your refuge. When the world trembles, you stand firm, not because you are strong, but because you serve the Sovereign LORD who holds all history in His hands. Your resolve is quiet, steady, and unshakable.
