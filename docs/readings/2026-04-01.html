Morning Wisdom

The battle for the soul is won or lost in the quiet moments of choosing between the world’s whisper and the LORD’s command.

📖 Proverbs 1

The proverbs of Solomon the son of David, king of Israel:
To know wisdom and instruction, To perceive the words of understanding,
To receive the instruction of wisdom, Justice, judgment, and equity;
To give prudence to the simple, To the young man knowledge and discretion—
A wise man will hear and increase learning, And a man of understanding will attain wise counsel,
To understand a proverb and an enigma, The words of the wise and their riddles.
The fear of the LORD is the beginning of knowledge, But fools despise wisdom and instruction.
My son, hear the instruction of your father, And do not forsake the law of your mother;
For they will be a graceful ornament on your head, And chains about your neck.
My son, if sinners entice you, Do not consent.
If they say, “Come with us, Let us lie in wait to shed blood; Let us lurk secretly for the innocent without cause;
Let us swallow them alive like Sheol, And whole, like those who go down to the Pit;
We shall find all kinds of precious possessions, We shall fill our houses with spoil;
Cast in your lot among us, Let us all have one purse”—
My son, do not walk in the way with them, Keep your foot from their path;
For their feet run to evil, And they make haste to shed blood.
Surely, in vain the net is spread In the sight of any bird;
But they lie in wait for their own blood, They lurk secretly for their own lives.
So are the ways of everyone who is greedy for gain; It takes away the life of its owners.
Wisdom calls aloud outside; She raises her voice in the open squares.
She cries out in the chief concourses, At the openings of the gates in the city She speaks her words:
“How long, you simple ones, will you love simplicity? For scorners delight in their scorning, And fools hate knowledge.
Turn at my rebuke; Surely I will pour out my spirit on you; I will make my words known to you.
Because I have called and you refused, I have stretched out my hand and no one regarded,
Because you disdained all my counsel, And would have none of my rebuke,
I also will laugh at your calamity; I will mock when your terror comes,
When your terror comes like a storm, And your destruction comes like a whirlwind, When distress and anguish come upon you.
“Then they will call on me, but I will not answer; They will seek me diligently, but they will not find me.
Because they hated knowledge And did not choose the fear of the LORD,
They would have none of my counsel And despised my every rebuke.
Therefore they shall eat the fruit of their own way, And be filled to the full with their own fancies.
For the turning away of the simple will slay them, And the complacency of fools will destroy them;
But whoever listens to me will dwell safely, And will be secure, without fear of evil.”

🧭 Context Summary

Solomon, the son of David, opens his collection of wisdom by establishing its divine source and urgent necessity. He contrasts the fleeting, destructive allure of sinful companions with the enduring, protective grace of parental instruction rooted in the fear of the LORD. The passage serves as a stern warning that the path of wickedness, while seductive in its promise of gain, ultimately leads only to self-destruction. This foundational chapter demands that a man anchor his identity and decisions in the reverence of God rather than the approval of his peers.

🛡️ Reflection for a REAL MAN — Rejects Evil Counsel

Brother, you stand at the intersection of two kingdoms daily. One offers you the warmth of the crowd, the promise of easy gain, and the validation of your own desires. The other demands your submission to the authority of the LORD and the discipline of His Word. Solomon writes to his son with the urgency of a father who knows the cost of error. He does not ask for your preference; he demands your refusal. To be a godly man is to recognize that the "lot" you throw in with the wicked is not shared prosperity, but shared ruin.

The world will always offer you a shortcut to significance. It will whisper that the rules of covenant faithfulness are too rigid, too restrictive, and too costly for the life you want to live. It will tell you that your father’s instruction is outdated, that the authority of Scripture is a chain rather than a shield. But you know, brother, that the net spread in sight of a bird is vain, yet the snare laid for your soul is real. You must not walk in their way. You must keep your foot from their paths. This is not cowardice; it is the highest form of courage. It is the resolve to stand alone rather than fall with the many.

Your strength as a husband and father does not come from your ability to conform to the culture, but from your ability to resist it. You protect your home by first protecting your heart from the enticements of sin. When you reject evil counsel, you declare that the LORD is your portion, your treasure, and your commander. You choose the garland of grace over the garland of gold. You choose the long obedience of faith over the short pleasure of sin. This is the discipline of a real man.

⛏️ Personal Application
  • Identify one area of your life where peer pressure or cultural norms are challenging your biblical convictions, and establish a clear boundary against it.
  • Audit your daily influences; remove or limit exposure to media, conversations, or associations that normalize compromise with sin.
  • Review your family instructions with your wife and children, reinforcing that the fear of the LORD is the foundation of your household’s safety and prosperity.
  • When tempted to consent to the "easy way" of the world, pause and recall the fate of those who lie in wait for their own blood.

By the power of Your Holy Spirit, give me the discernment to recognize the snare and the courage to flee from it. First Watch — The Husband's Post

The Lord raises up deliverers in His sovereign time, calling His servants to step out in faith even when the odds seem insurmountable.

📖 Judges 4

When Ehud was dead, the children of Israel again did evil in the sight of the LORD.
So the LORD sold them into the hand of Jabin king of Canaan, who reigned in Hazor. The commander of his army was Sisera, who dwelt in Harosheth Hagoyim.
And the children of Israel cried out to the LORD; for Jabin had nine hundred chariots of iron, and for twenty years he had harshly oppressed the children of Israel.
Now Deborah, a prophetess, the wife of Lapidoth, was judging Israel at that time.
And she would sit under the palm tree of Deborah between Ramah and Bethel in the mountains of Ephraim. And the children of Israel came up to her for judgment.
Then she sent and called for Barak the son of Abinoam from Kedesh in Naphtali, and said to him, “Has not the LORD God of Israel commanded, ‘Go and deploy troops at Mount Tabor; take with you ten thousand men of the sons of Naphtali and of the sons of Zebulun;
and against you I will deploy Sisera, the commander of Jabin’s army, with his chariots and his multitude at the River Kishon; and I will deliver him into your hand’?”
And Barak said to her, “If you will go with me, then I will go; but if you will not go with me, I will not go!”
So she said, “I will surely go with you; nevertheless there will be no glory for you in the journey you are taking, for the LORD will sell Sisera into the hand of a woman.” Then Deborah arose and went with Barak to Kedesh.
And Barak called Zebulun and Naphtali to Kedesh; he went up with ten thousand men under his command, and Deborah went up with him.
Now Heber the Kenite, of the children of Hobab the father-in-law of Moses, had separated himself from the Kenites and pitched his tent near the terebinth tree at Zaanaim, which is beside Kedesh.
And they reported to Sisera that Barak the son of Abinoam had gone up to Mount Tabor.
So Sisera gathered together all his chariots, nine hundred chariots of iron, and all the people who were with him, from Harosheth Hagoyim to the River Kishon.
Then Deborah said to Barak, “Up! For this is the day in which the LORD has delivered Sisera into your hand. Has not the LORD gone out before you?” So Barak went down from Mount Tabor with ten thousand men following him.
And the LORD routed Sisera and all his chariots and all his army with the edge of the sword before Barak; and Sisera alighted from his chariot and fled away on foot.
But Barak pursued the chariots and the army as far as Harosheth Hagoyim, and all the army of Sisera fell by the edge of the sword; not a man was left.
However, Sisera had fled away on foot to the tent of Jael, the wife of Heber the Kenite; for there was peace between Jabin king of Hazor and the house of Heber the Kenite.
And Jael went out to meet Sisera, and said to him, “Turn aside, my lord, turn aside to me; do not fear.” And when he had turned aside with her into the tent, she covered him with a blanket.
Then he said to her, “Please give me a little water to drink, for I am thirsty.” So she opened a jug of milk, gave him a drink, and covered him.
And he said to her, “Stand at the door of the tent, and if any man comes and inquires of you, and says, ‘Is there any man here?’ you shall say, ‘No.’ ”
Then Jael, Heber’s wife, took a tent peg and took a hammer in her hand, and went softly to him and drove the peg into his temple, and it went down into the ground; for he was fast asleep and weary. So he died.
And then, as Barak pursued Sisera, Jael came out to meet him, and said to him, “Come, I will show you the man whom you seek.” And when he went into her tent, there lay Sisera, dead with the peg in his temple.
So on that day God subdued Jabin king of Canaan in the presence of the children of Israel.
And the hand of the children of Israel grew stronger and stronger against Jabin king of Canaan, until they had destroyed Jabin king of Canaan.

🗺️ Briefing Summary

This narrative details the Lord’s sovereign orchestration of Israel’s deliverance from the iron chariots of Canaan through the unlikely instruments of a prophetess and a hesitant warrior. Deborah commands Barak to march with faith, yet Barak’s reluctance shifts the primary glory of the victory away from him to a woman, illustrating that the Lord determines the manner of His triumphs. The passage underscores that human strength, whether in numbers or military hardware, is subordinate to the will of the Creator.

❤️ Reflection for Your Wife — H.A.P.P.Y. Husband — Protecting

Brother, look closely at the command given to Barak. He was ordered to march against a formidable enemy, yet he hesitated, demanding a visible guarantee of support before he would commit his life and his men to the field. As a husband, you are called to be the spiritual and physical defender of your household, standing in the breach for Maria and your children. But like Barak, you must not wait for perfect conditions or visible signs of assurance before you take up your post. The Lord has already given you the victory in the cross; your duty is to march in obedience, trusting that He will fight for you as He fights for His Church.

Your role is not to control the outcome, but to faithfully execute the command given to you by your Commander. When you protect Maria, you are not merely shielding her from harm, but creating a space where she can flourish in her God-ordained role, secure in the knowledge that her husband is leaning on the LORD. This protection is rooted in humility, acknowledging that your strength is nothing apart from His grace. You must lead with courage, not because you are fearless, but because you trust the One who holds the future.

Do not let the fear of failure or the weight of responsibility paralyze your faith. Barak’s hesitation cost him the glory, but it did not stop the LORD from saving His people. Your job is to be the man who steps forward, sword in hand and heart fixed on the LORD, trusting that He will establish your home and preserve your wife. Stand firm, brother, for the battle belongs to the LORD, and your faithful obedience is your shield.

Gideon, at nineteen, is a man who must learn to trust the LORD's plan over his own understanding. Boaz, at fourteen, is in the formative years where he must see his father's faithfulness to the LORD in every decision. Shiloh, at five, watches you closely and will mimic the reverence you show to the LORD, as you guide her toward godly womanhood, modesty, and feminine flourishing.

Your primary duty is to guide your family toward the LORD, not just to provide for their physical needs. You must model a life that honors the LORD in your work, your speech, and your discipline. When you make decisions, you must do so with prayer and reliance on the LORD's wisdom. Your sons need to see that you value the LORD's commands above cultural trends or personal preference.

Let your children see that you protect them by keeping them close to the LORD. You must instruct them in the ways of the LORD daily, just as Deborah instructed Israel. Your authority is a stewardship from the LORD, and you will give an account to Him for how you use it. Do not shy away from the hard tasks of leadership, for the LORD is with you when you walk in His truth.

🛰️ Situation Report

The nation has fallen into a cycle of sin, oppression, and subsequent cries for deliverance, leaving the people broken and hidden in fear. Gideon, a man of humble standing and self-doubt, is confronted by the Divine Warrior who calls him to a task far beyond his natural capacity. The encounter shifts from a moment of terror to one of profound grace, establishing a new identity for a reluctant leader.

As a citizen of this state, you are called to be a witness in the public square, speaking truth with courage and clarity. Your trustworthiness as an American citizen is rooted in your loyalty to the Creator who endowed certain unalienable rights. You must advocate for a society that honors God, recognizing that true liberty is found only in submission to His will. Stand firm, brother, for the Lord is with you.

O LORD God of hosts, How long will You be angry Against the prayer of Your people?
You have fed them with the bread of tears, And given them tears to drink in great measure.
You have made us a strife to our neighbors, And our enemies laugh among themselves.
Restore us, O God of hosts; Cause Your face to shine, And we shall be saved!
You have brought a vine out of Egypt; You have cast out the nations, and planted it.
You prepared room for it, And caused it to take deep root, And it filled the land.
The hills were covered with its shadow, And the mighty cedars with its boughs.
She sent out her boughs to the Sea, And her branches to the River.
Why have You broken down her hedges, So that all who pass by the way pluck her fruit?
The boar out of the woods uproots it, And the wild beast of the field devours it.
Return, we beseech You, O God of hosts; Look down from heaven and see, And visit this vine
And the vineyard which Your right hand has planted, And the branch that You made strong for Yourself.
It is burned with fire, it is cut down; They perish at the rebuke of Your countenance.
Let Your hand be upon the man of Your right hand, Upon the son of man whom You made strong for Yourself.
Then we will not turn back from You; Revive us, and we will call upon Your name.
Restore us, O LORD God of hosts; Cause Your face to shine, And we shall be saved!

🌾 Reflection for a Man at Home and in Community

As a HAPPY husband, brother, you recognize that the church is a vineyard cultivated by the Lord, and your role is to tend the soil of your home with the same fidelity that God shows to His elect. Just as the vine in Egypt was brought out by divine power, your marriage is a covenantal bond established by grace, not merely human affection. You must protect this union from the boars of the world that seek to ravage your peace, standing firm in the truth that your headship is a stewardship of the Lord’s authority. Do not let the scorn of neighbors or the weariness of daily life break the walls of your covenant, for your faithfulness is a witness to the Gospel.

As a FULFILLED father, brother, you see that the Lord leads His people like a flock, and you are called to lead your children with the same gentle yet firm guidance. The psalmist cries out for the Lord to stir up His might, and you must do the same in your household by establishing order and doctrine. Your duty is to plant the seed of the Word in the hearts of your children, ensuring they take root in the truth of the Reformed faith. When the world seeks to uproot them, you must be the stock that Your right hand has planted, strong in faith and ready to defend the faith once delivered to the saints.

As a RESOLUTE citizen, brother, you acknowledge that true restoration comes only from the God of hosts, not from the shifting tides of political culture. The vine suffered because its walls were broken down, yet the Lord remains enthroned between the cherubim, sovereign over all nations. You serve your country with honor, but your ultimate allegiance is to the Kingdom of Christ. Let your hand be upon the man of Your right hand, the Son of Man, and let your public life reflect the stability of one who knows his final Judge. Stand resolute in your convictions, knowing that the Lord restores those who call upon His name.

🙏 Prayer from the Stateroom

Father, Shepherd of Israel, we thank You for the vine You have planted in our lives and for the covenant blessings bestowed upon our households. By the power of Your Holy Spirit, strengthen us to lead with grace, to love with truth, and to stand firm against the ravages of a fallen world. Restore our faces to Your light, that we may walk in obedience to Your commands and fulfill our duties with joy and reverence. In the name of Jesus Christ, my Lord and Commander, who is the true Son of Man and the Head of the Church, we pray. Amen.
