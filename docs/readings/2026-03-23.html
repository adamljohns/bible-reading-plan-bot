Morning Wisdom

Discipline the restless heart before the world’s seduction steals your vigilance and your virtue.

📖 Proverbs 23:19-35

Hear, my son, and be wise; And guide your heart in the way.
Do not mix with winebibbers, Or with gluttonous eaters of meat;
For the drunkard and the glutton will come to poverty, And drowsiness will clothe a man with rags.
Listen to your father who begot you, And do not despise your mother when she is old.
Buy the truth, and do not sell it, Also wisdom and instruction and understanding.
The father of the righteous will greatly rejoice, And he who begets a wise child will delight in him.
Let your father and your mother be glad, And let her who bore you rejoice.
My son, give me your heart, And let your eyes observe my ways.
For a harlot is a deep pit, And a seductress is a narrow well.
She also lies in wait as for a victim, And increases the unfaithful among men.
Who has woe? Who has sorrow? Who has contentions? Who has complaints? Who has wounds without cause? Who has redness of eyes?
Those who linger long at the wine, Those who go in search of mixed wine.
Do not look on the wine when it is red, When it sparkles in the cup, When it swirls around smoothly;
At the last it bites like a serpent, And stings like a viper.
Your eyes will see strange things, And your heart will utter perverse things.
Yes, you will be like one who lies down in the midst of the sea, Or like one who lies at the top of the mast, saying:
“They have struck me, but I was not hurt; They have beaten me, but I did not feel it. When shall I awake, that I may seek another drink?”

🧭 Context Summary

Solomon warns his son against the deceptive allure of intoxication, which promises pleasure but delivers only ruin and shame. The passage illustrates how sensory attraction blinds the wise man to the inevitable consequences of sin, leading to spiritual and physical decay. It serves as a stark contrast to the value of wisdom, showing that the path of folly is a trap that ensnares the unwary. The father urges his son to keep his heart focused on the righteous way, avoiding the company of those who have lost control.

🛡️ Reflection for a REAL MAN — Guard Your Senses

Brother, the enemy does not always attack with a sword; often, he attacks with a seductive smile and a sparkling cup. You must understand that sin begins in the eyes and ends in the spirit’s captivity. When you lower your guard against the small compromises of pleasure, you open the door to a slavery that no amount of strength can break. A man who cannot master his appetites cannot master his duties.

The text describes a gradual descent: first comes the look, then the curiosity, and finally the obsession that blinds the mind to pain. This is the same trap that ensnares many good men who think they can handle the world’s vices without being consumed by them. You must reject the lie that you can indulge without consequence. Your eyes must be fixed on what is pure and true, for anything else will bite like a serpent.

As a father and a husband, your strength is reserved for protecting your covenant family, not for satisfying your own fleeting whims. Discipline your gaze. Deny your flesh its false comforts. Let your heart be set on the way of the LORD, for that is the only path that leads to life and honor.

⛏️ Personal Application
  • Inspect your media consumption and remove any content that feeds lust or laziness from your life.
  • Establish a strict boundary on alcohol or entertainment that prevents you from losing self-control.
  • Pray daily for the grace to deny the flesh and walk in the Spirit.
  • Lead your family in evening devotions to set a tone of reverence over revelry.
🙏 Prayer

Father, You who see all things and know the secrets of the heart, I confess that my eyes are often drawn to vanity. Forgive me for the times I have sought comfort in creation rather than in the Creator. By the power of Your Holy Spirit, gird my loins with truth and set a watch over my mouth and eyes. Give me the discipline to reject the fleeting pleasures of sin and to embrace the lasting joy of obedience. Strengthen me to be the head of my house, leading my wife and children in the fear of the LORD. In the name of Jesus Christ, my Lord and Commander, I pray. Amen.

🛡️ Watch Charge: Steady your gaze against the seductive glint of the cup and shatter the illusion that comfort is safety before the spirit is enslaved.

First Watch — The Husband's Post

Stand firm in the strength of the Almighty as you lead your household with the same courage God commanded for His ancient people.

📖 Joshua 1

After the death of Moses the servant of the LORD, it came to pass that the LORD spoke to Joshua the son of Nun, Moses’ assistant, saying:
“Moses My servant is dead. Now therefore, arise, go over this Jordan, you and all this people, to the land which I am giving to them—the children of Israel.
Every place that the sole of your foot will tread upon I have given you, as I said to Moses.
From the wilderness and this Lebanon as far as the great river, the River Euphrates, all the land of the Hittites, and to the Great Sea toward the going down of the sun, shall be your territory.
No man shall be able to stand before you all the days of your life; as I was with Moses, so I will be with you. I will not leave you nor forsake you.
Be strong and of good courage, for to this people you shall divide as an inheritance the land which I swore to their fathers to give them.
Only be strong and very courageous, that you may observe to do according to all the law which Moses My servant commanded you; do not turn from it to the right hand or to the left, that you may prosper wherever you go.
This Book of the Law shall not depart from your mouth, but you shall meditate in it day and night, that you may observe to do according to all that is written in it. For then you will make your way prosperous, and then you will have good success.
Have I not commanded you? Be strong and of good courage; do not be afraid, nor be dismayed, for the LORD your God is with you wherever you go.”
Then Joshua commanded the officers of the people, saying,
“Pass through the camp and command the people, saying, ‘Prepare provisions for yourselves, for within three days you will cross over this Jordan, to go in to possess the land which the LORD your God is giving you to possess.’ ”
And to the Reubenites, the Gadites, and half the tribe of Manasseh Joshua spoke, saying,
“Remember the word which Moses the servant of the LORD commanded you, saying, ‘The LORD your God is giving you rest and is giving you this land.’
Your wives, your little ones, and your livestock shall remain in the land which Moses gave you on this side of the Jordan. But you shall pass before your brethren armed, all your mighty men of valor, and help them,
until the LORD has given your brethren rest, as He gave you, and they also have taken possession of the land which the LORD your God is giving them. Then you shall return to the land of your possession and enjoy it, which Moses the LORD’s servant gave you on this side of the Jordan toward the sunrise.”
So they answered Joshua, saying, “All that you command us we will do, and wherever you send us we will go.
Just as we heeded Moses in all things, so we will heed you. Only the LORD your God be with you, as He was with Moses.
Whoever rebels against your command and does not heed your words, in all that you command him, shall be put to death. Only be strong and of good courage.”

🗺️ Briefing Summary

Joshua steps into the void left by Moses, tasked with leading Israel into the Promised Land. The charge is clear: move forward in obedience to the Law while resting in the unyielding presence of the LORD. Victory is not found in tactical superiority but in faithful adherence to God’s command.

❤️ Reflection for Your Wife — H.A.P.P.Y. Husband — Providing

Brother, look to Joshua as you look to your role as the head of this home. God does not ask you to provide merely for material needs but to provide the spiritual direction and security that only a man anchored in Scripture can offer. Just as Joshua was commanded to meditate on the Law day and night, you must saturate your mind with the Word so that your decisions for Maria and the children are not driven by the shifting winds of culture but by the solid rock of God’s truth. Your provision is your protection; by keeping your eyes on the text, you shield your family from the chaos of the world. You must be strong not in your own might but in the might of the Almighty, knowing that He goes before you into every battle you face for their souls.

Brother, do not shrink from the weight of this command. The world tells you to be soft and passive, but God commands you to be strong and very courageous. This courage is the foundation of your provision because it allows you to stand firm when the enemy attacks your family’s peace. When you lead with the authority of Scripture, you provide Maria with the security of knowing her husband is under the authority of Christ. You are the gatekeeper of the home’s spiritual climate, and your diligence in the Word determines the safety of your post.

⛏️ Personal Application
  • Read Joshua 1 aloud to Maria at breakfast, explaining how God’s promise of presence is the basis for your leadership.
  • Memorize Joshua 1:9 this week to anchor your heart against fear when making difficult financial or disciplinary decisions.
  • Audit your family’s spiritual resources to ensure you are providing the means for regular church attendance and Bible study.
  • Pray specifically for the courage to enforce godly boundaries in your home without apology or compromise.
🙏 Prayer from the Stateroom

Father, I thank You for the command to be strong and courageous, not in my own strength but in Yours. Grant me the grace to meditate on Your Word day and night, that my leadership over Maria and our children may be rooted in Your truth alone. By the power of Your Holy Spirit, give me the fortitude to protect this home from the encroaching darkness of this age, that I may provide a stable and godly foundation for my family. Keep my feet on the path of obedience, that I may prosper in the work You have assigned to me. In the name of Jesus Christ, my Lord and Commander, I pray. Amen.

🛡️ Watch Charge: Rise from your post and cross the Jordan into the inheritance God has promised, leading your household with the same courageous obedience that defined Joshua’s march.

Second Watch — The Father's Charge

Brother, the night watch demands vigilance, just as the father’s heart must remain alert to the spiritual state of his house.

📖 Joshua 2

Now Joshua the son of Nun sent out two men from Acacia Grove to spy secretly, saying, “Go, view the land, especially Jericho.” So they went, and came to the house of a harlot named Rahab, and lodged there.
And it was told the king of Jericho, saying, “Behold, men have come here tonight from the children of Israel to search out the country.”
So the king of Jericho sent to Rahab, saying, “Bring out the men who have come to you, who have entered your house, for they have come to search out all the country.”
Then the woman took the two men and hid them. So she said, “Yes, the men came to me, but I did not know where they were from.
And it happened as the gate was being shut, when it was dark, that the men went out. Where the men went I do not know; pursue them quickly, for you may overtake them.”
(But she had brought them up to the roof and hidden them with the stalks of flax, which she had laid in order on the roof.)
Then the men pursued them by the road to the Jordan, to the fords. And as soon as those who pursued them had gone out, they shut the gate.
Now before they lay down, she came up to them on the roof,
and said to the men: “I know that the LORD has given you the land, that the terror of you has fallen on us, and that all the inhabitants of the land are fainthearted because of you.
For we have heard how the LORD dried up the water of the Red Sea for you when you came out of Egypt, and what you did to the two kings of the Amorites who were on the other side of the Jordan, Sihon and Og, whom you utterly destroyed.
And as soon as we heard these things, our hearts melted; neither did there remain any more courage in anyone because of you, for the LORD your God, He is God in heaven above and on earth beneath.
Now therefore, I beg you, swear to me by the LORD, since I have shown you kindness, that you also will show kindness to my father’s house, and give me a true token,
and spare my father, my mother, my brothers, my sisters, and all that they have, and deliver our lives from death.”
So the men answered her, “Our lives for yours, if none of you tell this business of ours. And it shall be, when the LORD has given us the land, that we will deal kindly and truly with you.”
Then she let them down by a rope through the window, for her house was on the city wall; she dwelt on the wall.
And she said to them, “Get to the mountain, lest the pursuers meet you. Hide there three days, until the pursuers have returned. Afterward you may go your way.”
So the men said to her: “We will be blameless of this oath of yours which you have made us swear,
unless, when we come into the land, you bind this line of scarlet cord in the window through which you let us down, and unless you bring your father, your mother, your brothers, and all your father’s household to your own home.
So it shall be that whoever goes outside the doors of your house into the street, his blood shall be on his own head, and we will be guiltless. And whoever is with you in the house, his blood shall be on our head if a hand is laid on him.
And if you tell this business of ours, then we will be free from your oath which you made us swear.”
Then she said, “According to your words, so be it.” And she sent them away, and they departed. And she bound the scarlet cord in the window.
They departed and went to the mountain, and stayed there three days until the pursuers returned. The pursuers sought them all along the way, but did not find them.
So the two men returned, descended from the mountain, and crossed over; and they came to Joshua the son of Nun, and told him all that had befallen them.
And they said to Joshua, “Truly the LORD has delivered all the land into our hands, for indeed all the inhabitants of the country are fainthearted because of us.”

🗺️ Field Notes

Rahab, a Gentile woman of questionable reputation, demonstrates profound faith by aligning herself with the people of the LORD despite her cultural background. Her actions reveal that God’s covenant promises extend beyond ethnic boundaries to all who fear Him and trust in His power. The scarlet cord becomes a type of the blood of Christ, marking those who are saved by grace through faith rather than works.

👨‍👦 Reflection for Your Children — F.U.L.F.I.L.L.E.D. Father — Faithful

Gideon, Boaz, Shiloh, listen closely to your father’s charge tonight. In the military, we learn that a man’s word is his bond, but in the Kingdom of God, faithfulness is the bedrock of our character. Your mother and I are called to be faithful stewards of this household, not merely providing for your physical needs but guarding your spiritual souls. Just as Rahab trusted the spies with her life, you must learn to trust the LORD with your fears, your future, and your hearts.

Brother, you are building a legacy that will outlast your uniform. Faithfulness means showing up for prayer when you are tired, speaking truth when it is costly, and loving your wife when it is difficult. It means teaching Gideon to lead with humility, Boaz to study with diligence, and Shiloh to believe with wonder, cultivating her as a godly young woman marked by gentleness, modesty, and feminine flourishing. We do not hide the truth of God’s Word; we display it plainly, even as Rahab displayed the scarlet cord in her window for all to see.

⛏️ Personal Application
  • Establish a consistent evening family devotional time to read Scripture and pray together.
  • Speak a specific word of affirmation and biblical truth to each son individually before bed.
  • Demonstrate faithfulness to your wife in public and private, showing the boys what a godly marriage looks like.
  • Teach the boys the importance of keeping their word to their peers and to God.

Third Watch — The Citizen's Stand

The Israelites stand at the edge of the Jordan, waiting for the Ark to part the waters and lead them into the Promised Land.

📖 Joshua 3

Then Joshua rose early in the morning; and they set out from Acacia Grove and came to the Jordan, he and all the children of Israel, and lodged there before they crossed over.
So it was, after three days, that the officers went through the camp;
and they commanded the people, saying, “When you see the ark of the covenant of the LORD your God, and the priests, the Levites, bearing it, then you shall set out from your place and go after it.
Yet there shall be a space between you and it, about two thousand cubits by measure. Do not come near it, that you may know the way by which you must go, for you have not passed this way before.”
And Joshua said to the people, “Sanctify yourselves, for tomorrow the LORD will do wonders among you.”
Then Joshua spoke to the priests, saying, “Take up the ark of the covenant and cross over before the people.” So they took up the ark of the covenant and went before the people.
And the LORD said to Joshua, “This day I will begin to exalt you in the sight of all Israel, that they may know that, as I was with Moses, so I will be with you.
You shall command the priests who bear the ark of the covenant, saying, ‘When you have come to the edge of the water of the Jordan, you shall stand in the Jordan.’ ”
So Joshua said to the children of Israel, “Come here, and hear the words of the LORD your God.”
And Joshua said, “By this you shall know that the living God is among you, and that He will without fail drive out from before you the Canaanites and the Hittites and the Hivites and the Perizzites and the Girgashites and the Amorites and the Jebusites:
Behold, the ark of the covenant of the Lord of all the earth is crossing over before you into the Jordan.
Now therefore, take for yourselves twelve men from the tribes of Israel, one man from every tribe.
And it shall come to pass, as soon as the soles of the feet of the priests who bear the ark of the LORD, the Lord of all the earth, shall rest in the waters of the Jordan, that the waters of the Jordan shall be cut off, the waters that come down from upstream, and they shall stand as a heap.”
So it was, when the people set out from their camp to cross over the Jordan, with the priests bearing the ark of the covenant before the people,
and as those who bore the ark came to the Jordan, and the feet of the priests who bore the ark dipped in the edge of the water (for the Jordan overflows all its banks during the whole time of harvest),
that the waters which came down from upstream stood still, and rose in a heap very far away at Adam, the city that is beside Zaretan. So the waters that went down into the Sea of the Arabah, the Salt Sea, failed, and were cut off; and the people crossed over opposite Jericho.
Then the priests who bore the ark of the covenant of the LORD stood firm on dry ground in the midst of the Jordan; and all Israel crossed over on dry ground, until all the people had crossed completely over the Jordan.

🛰️ Situation Report

The people of God are poised on the brink of their inheritance, yet they must first submit to the discipline of waiting and the authority of the priesthood. Joshua commands consecration, reminding the brethren that the presence of the Ark signifies the living God who fights their battles. This moment requires total reliance on Divine power, not human strength, to breach the natural barriers standing between them and their destiny.

🛡️ Reflection for a R.E.S.O.L.U.T.E. Citizen — Steadfast

Fredericksburg (local)
Brother, as you walk the streets of Fredericksburg, remember that the ground beneath your feet is holy because the God of Israel is present. The crossing of the Jordan was not a casual stroll but a solemn entry into a new covenant reality. Just as the priests had to stand firm in the riverbed while the waters halted, you must stand firm in your faith when the currents of culture try to sweep you away. Do not look to the shifting sands of public opinion for your stability, but fix your eyes on the Ark that leads the way. Your steadiness is not in your own willpower, but in the unmovable nature of the Lord who commands the waters.

Virginia (state)
The state of Virginia was founded on the principle that civil authority derives from God and must uphold His law. When the tribes crossed the Jordan, they left the wilderness of chaos to enter a land ordered by Divine decree. Similarly, the fathers and magistrates of this commonwealth are called to maintain a godly order in their households and communities. You are the priestly head of your home, responsible for keeping the Ark of the Covenant—the Word of God—central and visible. When the waters of moral relativism rise in our state, your steadfastness in upholding biblical manhood and the sanctity of marriage serves as a dam against the flood of sin.

United States (nation)
The United States was established under the conviction that all men are created by God and are accountable to Him. The crossing of the Jordan marked the end of wandering and the beginning of responsible stewardship over a land promised by the Creator. As a citizen of this republic, you are called to be a steady pillar in the body politic, reflecting the order and justice of Heaven. Your duty is not to overthrow the structures God has permitted, but to fill them with men of courage and conviction. By standing firm in the truth of Scripture, you help preserve the liberty and virtue that allow the Gospel to flourish in this nation.

  • Stand firm in your role as the spiritual head of your household, leading your family in evening worship regardless of external pressures or your own fatigue.
  • Speak the truth of God’s sovereignty to your fellow Marines and neighbors, correcting the narrative of chaos with the hope of Divine providence.
  • Maintain your physical and spiritual discipline, recognizing that a steady hand and a pure heart are essential tools for protecting the weak and providing for the home.

🦅 This Day in American History — March 23

On March 23, 1775, before the Second Virginia Convention gathered in St. John's Church in Richmond, Patrick Henry cast down his fears with the immortal cry, 'Give me liberty, or give me death!' and swung the colony toward armed defense. On the same day in 1965, Gus Grissom and John Young rode Gemini 3 into orbit on America's first two-man flight and steered their craft by hand among the stars, daring an untried frontier. A Steadfast citizen draws courage from the orator who staked his life on liberty and the astronauts who staked theirs on the unknown, resolving to hold his post whatever it costs, for the God who keeps him is faithful.

Evening Peace

The day’s labor ends, and we turn our eyes to the King who establishes justice in the home and the nation.

📖 Psalm 72

A Psalm of Solomon. Give the king Your judgments, O God, And Your righteousness to the king’s Son.
He will judge Your people with righteousness, And Your poor with justice.
The mountains will bring peace to the people, And the little hills, by righteousness.
He will bring justice to the poor of the people; He will save the children of the needy, And will break in pieces the oppressor.
They shall fear You As long as the sun and moon endure, Throughout all generations.
He shall come down like rain upon the grass before mowing, Like showers that water the earth.
In His days the righteous shall flourish, And abundance of peace, Until the moon is no more.
He shall have dominion also from sea to sea, And from the River to the ends of the earth.
Those who dwell in the wilderness will bow before Him, And His enemies will lick the dust.
The kings of Tarshish and of the isles Will bring presents; The kings of Sheba and Seba Will offer gifts.
Yes, all kings shall fall down before Him; All nations shall serve Him.
For He will deliver the needy when he cries, The poor also, and him who has no helper.
He will spare the poor and needy, And will save the souls of the needy.
He will redeem their life from oppression and violence; And precious shall be their blood in His sight.
And He shall live; And the gold of Sheba will be given to Him; Prayer also will be made for Him continually, And daily He shall be praised.
There will be an abundance of grain in the earth, On the top of the mountains; Its fruit shall wave like Lebanon; And those of the city shall flourish like grass of the earth.
His name shall endure forever; His name shall continue as long as the sun. And men shall be blessed in Him; All nations shall call Him blessed.
Blessed be the LORD God, the God of Israel, Who only does wondrous things!
And blessed be His glorious name forever! And let the whole earth be filled with His glory. Amen and Amen.

🌾 Reflection for a Man at Home and in Community

As a HAPPY husband, you recognize that your authority is not your own to wield, but a stewardship borrowed from the King of Kings. Your home is a small kingdom where righteousness must reign, and your joy is found in submitting your will to the Lord’s design for your wife. You do not rule by tyranny, but by the sacrificial love that mirrors Christ’s own covenant keeping. When you lay down your preferences for the good of your household, you reflect the character of the One who gave Himself for His Bride.

As a FULFILLED father, you bear the weight of blessing your children with the fear of the Lord. Your legacy is not built on wealth or status, but on the spiritual prosperity you cultivate in their hearts. You stand as a shield against the oppression of this world, teaching them that true strength is found in humility before God. Your discipline is an act of love, your provision a testament to His faithfulness, and your presence a daily sermon on the reliability of the Almighty.

As a RESOLUTE citizen, you look to the ultimate Judge who will defend the afflicted and crush the oppressor. Your service in the military was a shadow of the greater war you fight for righteousness in the public square. You do not seek the applause of men, but the approval of the One whose glory fills the earth. Your integrity is your armor, your truth your sword, and your peace your testimony to a nation in need of the Prince of Peace.
