Morning Wisdom

The courage to stand firm in faith requires the discipline to plan with wisdom and the humility to seek God’s hand in every detail.

📖 Nehemiah 2

And it came to pass in the month of Nisan, in the twentieth year of King Artaxerxes, when wine was before him, that I took the wine and gave it to the king. Now I had never been sad in his presence before.
Therefore the king said to me, “Why is your face sad, since you are not sick? This is nothing but sorrow of heart.” So I became dreadfully afraid,
and said to the king, “May the king live forever! Why should my face not be sad, when the city, the place of my fathers’ tombs, lies waste, and its gates are burned with fire?”
Then the king said to me, “What do you request?” So I prayed to the God of heaven.
And I said to the king, “If it pleases the king, and if your servant has found favor in your sight, I ask that you send me to Judah, to the city of my fathers’ tombs, that I may rebuild it.”
Then the king said to me (the queen also sitting beside him), “How long will your journey be? And when will you return?” So it pleased the king to send me; and I set him a time.
Furthermore I said to the king, “If it pleases the king, let letters be given to me for the governors of the region beyond the River, that they must permit me to pass through till I come to Judah,
and a letter to Asaph the keeper of the king’s forest, that he must give me timber to make beams for the gates of the citadel which pertains to the temple, for the city wall, and for the house that I will occupy.” And the king granted them to me according to the good hand of my God upon me.
Then I went to the governors in the region beyond the River, and gave them the king’s letters. Now the king had sent captains of the army and horsemen with me.
When Sanballat the Horonite and Tobiah the Ammonite official heard of it, they were deeply disturbed that a man had come to seek the well-being of the children of Israel.
So I came to Jerusalem and was there three days.
Then I arose in the night, I and a few men with me; I told no one what my God had put in my heart to do at Jerusalem; nor was there any animal with me, except the one on which I rode.
And I went out by night through the Valley Gate to the Serpent Well and the Refuse Gate, and viewed the walls of Jerusalem which were broken down and its gates which were burned with fire.
Then I went on to the Fountain Gate and to the King’s Pool, but there was no room for the animal under me to pass.
So I went up in the night by the valley, and viewed the wall; then I turned back and entered by the Valley Gate, and so returned.
And the officials did not know where I had gone or what I had done; I had not yet told the Jews, the priests, the nobles, the officials, or the others who did the work.
Then I said to them, “You see the distress that we are in, how Jerusalem lies waste, and its gates are burned with fire. Come and let us build the wall of Jerusalem, that we may no longer be a reproach.”
And I told them of the hand of my God which had been good upon me, and also of the king’s words that he had spoken to me. So they said, “Let us rise up and build.” Then they set their hands to this good work.
But when Sanballat the Horonite, Tobiah the Ammonite official, and Geshem the Arab heard of it, they laughed at us and despised us, and said, “What is this thing that you are doing? Will you rebel against the king?”
So I answered them, and said to them, “The God of heaven Himself will prosper us; therefore we His servants will arise and build, but you have no heritage or right or memorial in Jerusalem.”

🧭 Context Summary

Nehemiah, serving as cupbearer to the Persian king Artaxerxes, receives permission to return to Jerusalem to rebuild its ruined walls. After a brief prayer and a strategic audience with the king, he travels to Judah under the king’s protection. Upon arrival, he conducts a covert nighttime inspection of the damaged fortifications before rallying the people to the task of restoration.

🛡️ Reflection for a REAL MAN — Strategic Faith

Brother, true spiritual warfare is not merely a matter of emotional zeal; it is a disciplined exercise of faithfulness in the details. Nehemiah did not rush into the breach with empty hands or blind courage. He first prayed to the God of heaven, acknowledging that the outcome rests in the Almighty’s sovereign hand. Then he acted with the prudence of a seasoned commander, securing the necessary letters of passage and timber from the king. This is the way of the godly man: he trusts the LORD for the victory, but he prepares his tools and plans his steps with wisdom.

You see, brother, God often works through the means He provides. Nehemiah did not despise the resources of the Gentile king, for he recognized the good hand of his God upon him. He utilized the authority given to him to protect the work of restoration. As a father and a husband, you must similarly steward the resources, opportunities, and authority God has placed in your hands. Do not neglect the practical preparations for your family’s spiritual and physical safety. Faith without works, and works without wisdom, is no faith at all.

When Nehemiah arrived, he did not immediately shout his plans from the rooftops. He inspected the damage privately, assessing the reality of the situation before mobilizing the troops. This is a lesson in leadership. A man of God assesses the terrain of his heart and his home with honesty. He does not ignore the broken walls of his own character or the vulnerabilities of his household. He faces the reality of the breach, whether it be financial, moral, or relational, and then he calls his people to rise up and build.

⛏️ Personal Application
  • Review your family’s spiritual and physical security needs with prayerful precision this week.
  • Secure the necessary resources or permissions for your household’s provision without anxiety.
  • Conduct a honest, private assessment of the "broken walls" in your own character or home.
  • Rally your family to a specific, God-glorifying task with clear instructions and encouragement.

🗺️ Briefing Summary

Ezekiel 38 depicts the dramatic confrontation between the gathered host of Gog and the people of God. The passage emphasizes that the enemy’s confidence is entirely misplaced because the LORD commands the nations. God declares His purpose to act decisively against these forces to demonstrate His holiness to the world. This vision assures the believer that no coalition of evil can ultimately thwart the Divine plan.

🦅 This Day in American History — July 16

On July 16, 1945, the first atomic device was detonated before dawn in the New Mexico desert at the Trinity site, a fearsome unleashing of nature's hidden power that sobered even the men who made it. On the same day in 1969, Apollo 11 rose from Cape Kennedy on a pillar of fire, bearing Armstrong, Aldrin, and Collins on the long voyage to the Moon. The one revealed the terrible reach of man's knowledge and the other its noblest ambition, and a Steadfast citizen presses on in his own calling with sober reverence, remembering that every power and every height belongs first to the God who set the heavens in their order.

📖 Isaiah 35

The wilderness and the wasteland shall be glad for them, And the desert shall rejoice and blossom as the rose;
It shall blossom abundantly and rejoice, Even with joy and singing. The glory of Lebanon shall be given to it, The excellence of Carmel and Sharon. They shall see the glory of the LORD, The excellency of our God.
Strengthen the weak hands, And make firm the feeble knees.
Say to those who are fearful-hearted, “Be strong, do not fear! Behold, your God will come with vengeance, With the recompense of God; He will come and save you.”
Then the eyes of the blind shall be opened, And the ears of the deaf shall be unstopped.
Then the lame shall leap like a deer, And the tongue of the dumb sing. For waters shall burst forth in the wilderness, And streams in the desert.
The parched ground shall become a pool, And the thirsty land springs of water; In the habitation of jackals, where each lay, There shall be grass with reeds and rushes.
A highway shall be there, and a road, And it shall be called the Highway of Holiness. The unclean shall not pass over it, But it shall be for others. Whoever walks the road, although a fool, Shall not go astray.
No lion shall be there, Nor shall any ravenous beast go up on it; It shall not be found there. But the redeemed shall walk there,
And the ransomed of the LORD shall return, And come to Zion with singing, With everlasting joy on their heads. They shall obtain joy and gladness, And sorrow and sighing shall flee away.

🌾 Reflection for a Man at Home and in Community

As a HAPPY husband, you are called to be the steady ground upon which your wife can rest, just as the Lord provides a highway for the redeemed. Your home must be a sanctuary of peace where the chaotic winds of the world cannot breach the walls of your covenant love. You do not merely coexist with your spouse; you lead her toward the joy that is found only in the LORD, ensuring that your daily actions reflect the steadfastness of your commitment to her spiritual and physical well-being.

As a FULFILLED father, you recognize that your strength is not for your own glory but for the protection and provision of your household. The Lord commands you to strengthen the weak hands and make firm the feeble knees, a duty that begins in the nursery and extends to the boardroom. You teach your children the Way of Holiness not by your own perfection, but by pointing them to the Ransomed who saves, modeling a masculinity that is gentle in discipline and fierce in defense of the truth.

As a RESOLUTE citizen, you walk the path of holiness with your eyes fixed on Zion, knowing that your ultimate allegiance is to the Kingdom of God. The world may offer you the fleeting applause of men, but you seek the everlasting joy that belongs to the redeemed. You stand firm against the lions of cultural decay, not with the noise of political outrage, but with the quiet, unshakeable confidence of a man who knows his Commander has already secured the victory.

🙏 Prayer from the Stateroom

Father, I thank You for the peace of this evening and for the steadfast love that has sustained me through the day. By the power of Your Holy Spirit, renew my mind and fortify my heart, that I may lead my household with wisdom and courage. Grant me the grace to love my wife with Christ-like devotion, to raise my children in the fear of the LORD, and to serve my neighbors with integrity. Keep me steadfast in my duties, that I may walk the Way of Holiness without stumbling. In the name of Jesus Christ, my Lord and Commander, I pray. Amen.
