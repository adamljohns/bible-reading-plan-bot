Morning Wisdom

The Lord’s judgments are terrible, yet they are just, calling His people to flee the coming wrath and cling to His mercy.

📖 Revelation 16

Then I heard a loud voice from the temple saying to the seven angels, “Go and pour out the bowls of the wrath of God on the earth.” So the first went and poured out his bowl upon the earth, and a foul and loathsome sore came upon the men who had the mark of the beast and those who worshiped his image. Then the second angel poured out his bowl on the sea, and it became blood as of a dead man; and every living creature in the sea died. Then the third angel poured out his bowl on the rivers and springs of water, and they became blood. And I heard the angel of the waters saying: “You are righteous, O Lord, The One who is and who was and who is to be, Because You have judged these things. For they have shed the blood of saints and prophets, And You have given them blood to drink. For it is their just due.” And I heard another from the altar saying, “Even so, Lord God Almighty, true and righteous are Your judgments.” Then the fourth angel poured out his bowl on the sun, and power was given to him to scorch men with fire. And men were scorched with great heat, and they blasphemed the name of God who has power over these plagues; and they did not repent and give Him glory. Then the fifth angel poured out his bowl on the throne of the beast, and his kingdom became full of darkness; and they gnawed their tongues because of the pain. They blasphemed the God of heaven because of their pains and their sores, and did not repent of their deeds. Then the sixth angel poured out his bowl on the great river Euphrates, and its water was dried up, so that the way of the kings from the east might be prepared. And I saw three unclean spirits like frogs coming out of the mouth of the dragon, out of the mouth of the beast, and out of the mouth of the false prophet. For they are spirits of demons, performing signs, which go out to the kings of the earth and of the whole world, to gather them to the battle of that great day of God Almighty. “Behold, I am coming as a thief. Blessed is he who watches, and keeps his garments, lest he walk naked and they see his shame.” And they gathered them together to the place called in Hebrew, Armageddon. Then the seventh angel poured out his bowl into the air, and a loud voice came out of the temple of heaven, from the throne, saying, “It is done!” And there were noises and thunderings and lightnings; and there was a great earthquake, such a mighty and great earthquake as had not occurred since men were on the earth. Now the great city was divided into three parts, and the cities of the nations fell. And great Babylon was remembered before God, to give her the cup of the wine of the fierceness of His wrath. Then every island fled away, and the mountains were not found. And great hail from heaven fell upon men, each hailstone about the weight of a talent. Men blasphemed God because of the plague of the hail, since that plague was exceedingly great.

🧭 Context Summary

John records the seventh and final series of divine judgments poured out upon the earth during the end times. These bowls represent the fullness of God’s wrath against the unrepentant world and the forces of evil. The passage highlights the absolute sovereignty of God in history, as He controls nature, nations, and the final fate of all humanity. It serves as a sobering warning that judgment is inevitable for those who reject the Lord, yet it also offers comfort to the faithful who know their God is just.

🛡️ Reflection for a REAL MAN — Accept Responsibility

Again and again in this chapter, men under judgment respond the same way. They "blasphemed the name of God who has power over these plagues; and they did not repent." They "blasphemed the God of heaven because of their pains and their sores, and did not repent of their deeds." When the hail fell, they cursed God for that too. They blamed everyone but themselves. That is the posture of a man who refuses responsibility: when the consequences arrive, he is angry at God, at his wife, at his boss or at his circumstances, and he never once looks hard at his own deeds.

In the middle of the bowls, Christ Himself speaks: "Blessed is he who watches, and keeps his garments, lest he walk naked and they see his shame." The words reach back to Eden. Adam sinned, saw that he was naked, hid, and then blamed. A man who keeps his garments watches over his own life and owns what is his to own. He does not wait to be exposed before he admits his sin. He watches his eyes, his tongue, his temper and his time, and when he fails, he says so before anyone has to drag it out of him.

You are the watchman of your house. If Maria is weary, ask what part of that weight is yours to carry. If one of your sons is drifting, ask what you have modeled and what you have neglected. If a brother in USMC Ministries has slipped through the cracks, ask whose job it was to call him. God does not hand the head of a house an excuse; He hands him a charge. A man who accepts responsibility repents of his deeds, gives God the glory, and goes back to work.

⛏️ Personal Application
  • Recall the last time something went wrong at home and you blamed someone else; go to that person today and own your part without adding a "but."
  • Ask Maria tonight what you have left on her shoulders that is yours to carry, and take it back this week.
  • Sit down with Gideon this week, ask him plainly where you have failed him as a father, and listen without defending yourself.
🙏 Prayer

Father, I acknowledge Your righteous judgments and Your sovereignty over all nations. By the power of Your Holy Spirit, give me the grace to own my deeds without blaming You or anyone else, and to keep my garments clean in the sight of the world. Strengthen me to be steadfast in my roles as a husband, a father, and a man of God, that I might not be shaken by the storms of this age. In Jesus' name, I pray. Amen.

🛡️ Watch Charge: Inspect your heart for the mark of the beast and flee the gathering storm before the bowl of judgment is poured out.

First Watch — The Husband's Post

Brother, man your post: begin your day by walking in the light of His truth before you face the shadows of the world.

📖 1 John 1

That which was from the beginning, which we have heard, which we have seen with our eyes, which we have looked upon, and our hands have handled, concerning the Word of life— the life was manifested, and we have seen, and bear witness, and declare to you that eternal life which was with the Father and was manifested to us— that which we have seen and heard we declare to you, that you also may have fellowship with us; and truly our fellowship is with the Father and with His Son Jesus Christ. And these things we write to you that your joy may be full. This is the message which we have heard from Him and declare to you, that God is light and in Him is no darkness at all. If we say that we have fellowship with Him, and walk in darkness, we lie and do not practice the truth. But if we walk in the light as He is in the light, we have fellowship with one another, and the blood of Jesus Christ His Son cleanses us from all sin. If we say that we have no sin, we deceive ourselves, and the truth is not in us. If we confess our sins, He is faithful and just to forgive us our sins and to cleanse us from all unrighteousness. If we say that we have not sinned, we make Him a liar, and His word is not in us.

🗺️ Briefing Summary

John addresses the early believers with a stern but loving correction regarding the nature of true fellowship. He establishes that walking in the light of God’s truth is the only path to genuine communion with Him and with other believers. The passage demands absolute honesty about our sinful condition and our total dependence on Christ’s atoning work for cleansing.

❤️ Reflection for Your Wife — H.A.P.P.Y. Husband — Adoring

Listen to how John speaks of Christ: "which we have seen with our eyes, which we have looked upon, and our hands have handled." This is not a man describing an idea. It is a man who looked long at the One he loved and is still full of it decades later. Adoration works that way. It lives on attention. A husband who stops looking at his wife, really looking, soon stops delighting in her. Maria is not part of the furniture of your house. Look at her the way a man looks at what he treasures.

John writes "that your joy may be full," and he ties that joy to fellowship, a shared life with the Father, the Son and one another. Marriage is the closest fellowship a man has on earth, and God means it to be full of joy, not only duty. Ephesians 5:25 calls you to love Maria as Christ loved the church, and that love pursued its bride, gave itself for her, and delights in her. Pursue Maria the way you did when you were winning her: notes, time alone, a hand at her back, words of praise spoken where she can hear them.

Then John shows how fellowship stays alive: "if we walk in the light as He is in the light, we have fellowship with one another, and the blood of Jesus Christ His Son cleanses us from all sin." Christ does not love His bride by keeping a ledger of her faults; He cleanses and keeps on cleansing. Delight cannot survive in a man who keeps score or hides his own sin. Walk in the light, forgive quickly, confess quickly, and your delight in her will grow rather than fade.

⛏️ Personal Application
  • Look Maria in the eye this morning and tell her she is beautiful and that you are glad she is your wife.
  • Write her a short note today naming something you love about her, and leave it where she will find it.
  • Plan time alone with her this week, just the two of you, with no agenda but enjoying her company.
🙏 Prayer

Father, I thank You for the light of Your truth that exposes all darkness. Forgive me for the times I have walked in pretense and hidden my sin. By the power of Your Holy Spirit, give me a heart that delights in Maria and pursues her with joy, as Christ loves His church. Purify my heart and my home, that my fellowship may be with You and with Your Son, Jesus Christ. In the name of Jesus Christ, I pray. Amen.

🛡️ Watch Charge: Shatter the glass of your self-deception and let the raw light of His presence expose the sin you have tried to hide.

Second Watch — The Father's Charge

Brother, take up the charge: hold fast to the truth that anchors your soul against the shifting sands of this age.

📖 1 John 2

My little children, these things I write to you, so that you may not sin. And if anyone sins, we have an Advocate with the Father, Jesus Christ the righteous. And He Himself is the propitiation for our sins, and not for ours only but also for the whole world. Now by this we know that we know Him, if we keep His commandments. He who says, “I know Him,” and does not keep His commandments, is a liar, and the truth is not in him. But whoever keeps His word, truly the love of God is perfected in him. By this we know that we are in Him. He who says he abides in Him ought himself also to walk just as He walked. Brethren, I write no new commandment to you, but an old commandment which you have had from the beginning. The old commandment is the word which you heard from the beginning. Again, a new commandment I write to you, which thing is true in Him and in you, because the darkness is passing away, and the true light is already shining. He who says he is in the light, and hates his brother, is in darkness until now. He who loves his brother abides in the light, and there is no cause for stumbling in him. But he who hates his brother is in darkness and walks in darkness, and does not know where he is going, because the darkness has blinded his eyes. I write to you, little children, Because your sins are forgiven you for His name’s sake. I write to you, fathers, Because you have known Him who is from the beginning. I write to you, young men, Because you have overcome the wicked one. I write to you, little children, Because you have known the Father. I have written to you, fathers, Because you have known Him who is from the beginning. I have written to you, young men, Because you are strong, and the word of God abides in you, And you have overcome the wicked one. Do not love the world or the things in the world. If anyone loves the world, the love of the Father is not in him. For all that is in the world—the lust of the flesh, the lust of the eyes, and the pride of life—is not of the Father but is of the world. And the world is passing away, and the lust of it; but he who does the will of God abides forever. Little children, it is the last hour; and as you have heard that the Antichrist is coming, even now many antichrists have come, by which we know that it is the last hour. They went out from us, but they were not of us; for if they had been of us, they would have continued with us; but they went out that they might be made manifest, that none of them were of us. But you have an anointing from the Holy One, and you know all things. I have not written to you because you do not know the truth, but because you know it, and that no lie is of the truth. Who is a liar but he who denies that Jesus is the Christ? He is antichrist who denies the Father and the Son. Whoever denies the Son does not have the Father either; he who acknowledges the Son has the Father also. Therefore let that abide in you which you heard from the beginning. If what you heard from the beginning abides in you, you also will abide in the Son and in the Father. And this is the promise that He has promised us—eternal life. These things I have written to you concerning those who try to deceive you. But the anointing which you have received from Him abides in you, and you do not need that anyone teach you; but as the same anointing teaches you concerning all things, and is true, and is not a lie, and just as it has taught you, you will abide in Him. And now, little children, abide in Him, that when He appears, we may have confidence and not be ashamed before Him at His coming. If you know that He is righteous, you know that everyone who practices righteousness is born of Him.

🗺️ Field Notes

John addresses the believer with the tenderness of a father and the authority of an apostle, reminding us that grace does not license sin but liberates us from it. The passage draws a sharp line between those who claim to know God and those who actually walk in His commands, a distinction that defines our covenant walk. As a father, you must hold this tension: offering mercy to the fallen while demanding righteousness from the house.

👨‍👦 Reflection for Your Children — F.U.L.F.I.L.L.E.D. Father — Discipling

John writes the way a father disciples his children: "My little children, these things I write to you, so that you may not sin. And if anyone sins, we have an Advocate with the Father, Jesus Christ the righteous." Notice the order. He holds up the standard and in the same breath points to the Savior. That is the heart of discipling your children. You are not merely training well-behaved kids; you are teaching sinners where to run. Every rule in your house should end at the cross, with the Advocate who is "the propitiation for our sins."

John then speaks to each season of life: little children, young men, fathers. Disciple each of yours where they are. Shiloh at five is one of John's little children: teach her that her sins can be forgiven for His name's sake and that she can know the Father, through simple Bible stories, short prayers, and the catechism one question at a time. Boaz at fifteen is close to John's young men, who "are strong, and the word of God abides in you." Put the Word in his own hands: read a book of the Bible with him, welcome his hard questions, and show him how to fight "the lust of the flesh, the lust of the eyes, and the pride of life." Gideon at twenty is a young man in full; disciple him now as a brother in Christ rather than a pupil, by talking doctrine with him, praying with him, and asking what God is teaching him.

"He who says he abides in Him ought himself also to walk just as He walked." Children learn to follow Christ by watching a man who follows Him. "Therefore let that abide in you which you heard from the beginning." What you put in them now (the gospel, the Scriptures, the habits of prayer and worship) is what will still abide in them when you are no longer there to remind them.

⛏️ Personal Application
  • Begin a short catechism with Shiloh this week, one question and answer at bedtime each night.
  • Start reading 1 John with Boaz this week, one chapter a sitting, and let him ask whatever he wants about it.
  • Ask Gideon what God has been teaching him lately, and tell him honestly what He has been teaching you.
🙏 Prayer

Father, I come before You as a servant who has been entrusted with the care of Your flock in my home. Forgive me for the times I have been lax in teaching Your Word, and for the times I have handed my children rules without pointing them to Christ. Grant me the wisdom to disciple Gideon, Boaz, and Shiloh each according to their season, that what they hear from the beginning may abide in them. By the power of Your Holy Spirit, make me walk as Jesus walked, so that my children learn to follow Him by watching me follow Him. Keep my heart soft toward You and my teaching true to Your Word. Through Christ my Savior, I pray. Amen.

🛡️ Watch Charge: Run to the Advocate who is your propitiation, for the righteous Judge is also your merciful Defender when you falter.

Third Watch — The Citizen's Stand

Brother, stand firm: The watch is long, but the hope of Christ’s appearing keeps the sentinel alert and the heart steady.

📖 1 John 3

Behold what manner of love the Father has bestowed on us, that we should be called children of God! Therefore the world does not know us, because it did not know Him. Beloved, now we are children of God; and it has not yet been revealed what we shall be, but we know that when He is revealed, we shall be like Him, for we shall see Him as He is. And everyone who has this hope in Him purifies himself, just as He is pure. Whoever commits sin also commits lawlessness, and sin is lawlessness. And you know that He was manifested to take away our sins, and in Him there is no sin. Whoever abides in Him does not sin. Whoever sins has neither seen Him nor known Him. Little children, let no one deceive you. He who practices righteousness is righteous, just as He is righteous. He who sins is of the devil, for the devil has sinned from the beginning. For this purpose the Son of God was manifested, that He might destroy the works of the devil. Whoever has been born of God does not sin, for His seed remains in him; and he cannot sin, because he has been born of God. In this the children of God and the children of the devil are manifest: Whoever does not practice righteousness is not of God, nor is he who does not love his brother. For this is the message that you heard from the beginning, that we should love one another, not as Cain who was of the wicked one and murdered his brother. And why did he murder him? Because his works were evil and his brother’s righteous. Do not marvel, my brethren, if the world hates you. We know that we have passed from death to life, because we love the brethren. He who does not love his brother abides in death. Whoever hates his brother is a murderer, and you know that no murderer has eternal life abiding in him. By this we know love, because He laid down His life for us. And we also ought to lay down our lives for the brethren. But whoever has this world’s goods, and sees his brother in need, and shuts up his heart from him, how does the love of God abide in him? My little children, let us not love in word or in tongue, but in deed and in truth. And by this we know that we are of the truth, and shall assure our hearts before Him. For if our heart condemns us, God is greater than our heart, and knows all things. Beloved, if our heart does not condemn us, we have confidence toward God. And whatever we ask we receive from Him, because we keep His commandments and do those things that are pleasing in His sight. And this is His commandment: that we should believe on the name of His Son Jesus Christ and love one another, as He gave us commandment. Now he who keeps His commandments abides in Him, and He in him. And by this we know that He abides in us, by the Spirit whom He has given us.

🛰️ Situation Report

The Apostle John addresses the tension between our present identity as God’s adopted sons and the hostility of a world that knows not the Father. He reminds you, brother, that the hope of future glorification is not a passive wish but an active force for moral purification. The Christian life is defined by this radical separation from the patterns of the devil and a zealous pursuit of righteousness.

🛡️ Reflection for a R.E.S.O.L.U.T.E. Citizen — Engaged

Fredericksburg (local)
John's line cuts against the armchair: "let us not love in word or in tongue, but in deed and in truth." Plenty of Christian men love Fredericksburg in tongue only. They complain about the city council at the dinner table and never set foot in city hall. An Engaged citizen shows up. He attends a council meeting and signs up for public comment. He goes to the school board when Fredericksburg City Public Schools sets policy that touches families. He serves in the neighborhoods around Spotswood Baptist instead of driving past them. Love for a city that never leaves the house is love in word only.

Virginia (state)
"But whoever has this world’s goods, and sees his brother in need, and shuts up his heart from him, how does the love of God abide in him?" Engagement in the Commonwealth means keeping your eyes open and your heart unshut. It means knowing what is on the ballot in every Virginia election, primaries included. It means answering a jury summons, testifying before a General Assembly committee when a bill touches the vulnerable, and saying yes when asked to serve on a local board. The brother in need is often reached through civic machinery that Christian men have walked away from.

United States (nation)
"By this we know love, because He laid down His life for us. And we also ought to lay down our lives for the brethren." You did a version of that once, in uniform. Engagement at the national level is quieter but no less real: voting in every federal election, writing your congressman, keeping informed on what Congress does to veterans and families, and putting USMC Ministries into the national conversation with truth and charity. "Do not marvel, my brethren, if the world hates you." Engage anyway.

⛏️ Personal Application
  • Put the next Fredericksburg city council meeting on your calendar, read the agenda, and attend, ready to speak in public comment if an item calls for it.
  • Confirm your Virginia voter registration, note the next election date, and send one note to a Fredericksburg school board member, either thanks or a concern.
  • Meet one concrete need in the neighborhood around Spotswood Baptist this week (a meal, a ride, a repair), in deed and in truth.
🙏 Prayer

Father, I thank You for the great love by which You have adopted me as Your child. Forgive me for loving my city in word and in tongue while staying home when it needed me. By the power of Your Holy Spirit, open my heart to the needs of my neighbors and make me present where decisions are made: at the council table, at the ballot box, in the jury box, and at my neighbor's door. Let me love in deed and in truth, and lay down my comfort for the brethren as Christ laid down His life for me. Preserve this nation and my family under the shadow of Your wings. In the name of the risen Lord Jesus, I pray. Amen.

🦅 This Day in American History — December 16

On December 16, 1773, the men of Boston, resolved against taxation laid upon them without their consent, boarded three ships in the harbor and heaved their chests of tea into the cold tide, a bold stroke that set the colonies on the road to independence. On the same day in 1944, the Germans hurled their last great offensive through the snowbound Ardennes, and American soldiers, surprised and surrounded, dug in and held until the line was restored at fearful cost. An Engaged citizen draws strength from both the defiance of Boston harbor and the grit of the Ardennes and refuses to watch his own day from the sidelines, trusting that the God who hates injustice steels the hearts of free men to resist the oppressor.

Evening Peace

The day’s labor yields to the quiet watch, where the soul finds its true rest in the sovereignty of God.

📖 Isaiah 49:1-7

“Listen, O coastlands, to Me, And take heed, you peoples from afar! The LORD has called Me from the womb; From the matrix of My mother He has made mention of My name. And He has made My mouth like a sharp sword; In the shadow of His hand He has hidden Me, And made Me a polished shaft; In His quiver He has hidden Me.” “And He said to me, ‘You are My servant, O Israel, In whom I will be glorified.’ Then I said, ‘I have labored in vain, I have spent my strength for nothing and in vain; Yet surely my just reward is with the LORD, And my work with my God.’ ” “And now the LORD says, Who formed Me from the womb to be His Servant, To bring Jacob back to Him, So that Israel is gathered to Him (For I shall be glorious in the eyes of the LORD, And My God shall be My strength), Indeed He says, ‘It is too small a thing that You should be My Servant To raise up the tribes of Jacob, And to restore the preserved ones of Israel; I will also give You as a light to the Gentiles, That You should be My salvation to the ends of the earth.’ ” Thus says the LORD, The Redeemer of Israel, their Holy One, To Him whom man despises, To Him whom the nation abhors, To the Servant of rulers: “Kings shall see and arise, Princes also shall worship, Because of the LORD who is faithful, The Holy One of Israel; And He has chosen You.”

🌾 Reflection for a Man of God

As a HAPPY husband, brother, you find your identity not in the applause of the world but in the faithful calling of your Creator. Just as the servant in Isaiah was formed and named before his work began, your role as head of your household is a sacred trust given by God, not a burden imposed by culture. You lead with the confidence of one who knows his worth is secured by Christ, allowing you to love your wife with a steady, self-giving grace that reflects the covenant loyalty of the LORD.

As a FULFILLED father, you see in this text the sobering reality that spiritual labor can feel like vanity when the results are not immediately visible. Yet, you are called to train your children in the way they should go, not for your own glory, but because the LORD holds the reward. Your discipline, your provision, and your instruction are not in vain; they are the sharp sword of truth you wield in the shadow of His hand, shaping the next generation for His glory even when the harvest is hidden from your eyes.

As a RESOLUTE citizen, you recognize that your service often places you among the despised or the overlooked, much like the servant who was abhorred by nations. Do not shrink from this calling, brother. The same God who chose the lowly and the rejected to bring salvation to the ends of the earth has chosen you to stand firm in your community. Your authority is derived from His election, and your strength is found in submitting to His will, knowing that kings and princes will one day bow before the faithfulness of the Holy One.

🙏 Prayer

Father, I thank You for the privilege of serving under Your sovereign command. I confess that I often measure my worth by visible results rather than Your faithful calling. Forgive me for the vanity that creeps into my duties as a husband, a father, and a citizen. By the power of Your Holy Spirit, strengthen me to bear the weight of my responsibilities with joy and endurance, trusting that my labor in the Lord is never in vain. Guard my heart against pride and my hands against sloth, that I may reflect Your glory in my home and my community. For the sake of Christ our King, I pray. Amen.
