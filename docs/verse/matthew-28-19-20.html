Romans 1:16-17

All authority first. Then go. Make disciples of the nations, baptize them into the Name, teach them to keep what He said — and He is with you to the end.

Go, and make disciples of all nations, baptizing them in the name of the Father and of the Son and of the Holy Spirit, teaching them to observe all things that I commanded you. Behold, I am with you always, even to the end of the age.” Amen.

— World English Bible (public domain)

— King James Version (public domain)

πορευθέντες οὖν μαθητεύσατε πάντα τὰ ἔθνη, βαπτίζοντες αὐτοὺς εἰς τὸ ὄνομα τοῦ πατρὸς καὶ τοῦ υἱοῦ καὶ τοῦ ἁγίου πνεύματος, διδάσκοντες αὐτοὺς τηρεῖν πάντα ὅσα ἐνετειλάμην ὑμῖν· καὶ ἰδοὺ ἐγὼ μεθ’ ὑμῶν εἰμι πάσας τὰς ἡμέρας ἕως τῆς συντελείας τοῦ αἰῶνος.

1. The Text and Its Words

Verse 18 is the engine. “All authority in heaven and on earth has been given to me.” The commission is not a pep talk to a shrinking club. It is an order from the risen King. “Therefore” in verse 19 leans on that authority. If He does not have it, the church has no business at the nations. If He does, sitting still is the strange thing.

  • μαθητεύωmatheteuō — "make disciples" · Strong's G3100

    To make a disciple, not merely to collect decisions. The KJV’s “teach” is the old English for this, and the margin even says “make disciples.” The main verb is not “go.” Going is the participle — as you go. The command is to make learners of Jesus among all the nations. A baptized crowd that was never taught is not this verse. A taught circle that never goes is not this verse either.

  • ἔθνοςethnos — "nations" · Strong's G1484

    Nation, people. In the plural it is the Gentiles, the families of the earth. Genesis 12:3 already promised that all those families would be blessed in Abraham. Isaiah 49:6 said it was too light a thing to raise up Israel only — the servant would be a light to the nations. The mountain in Galilee is that promise with a risen voice.

  • βαπτίζωbaptizō — "baptizing" · Strong's G907

    To dip, plunge, immerse. The participles “baptizing” and “teaching” unpack how disciples are made. Baptism is not a club handshake. It is into the name — one name, three persons. You do not baptize a man into your brand. You put him under the Name that now claims him.

  • ὄνομαonoma — "name" · Strong's G3686

    In that world a name is character and authority, not a label. “In the name of the Father and of the Son and of the Holy Spirit” is one Name. Calvin will press that. You are not sprinkled with three gods. You are claimed by the one God who has been made known as Father, Son, and Spirit.

  • διδάσκω / τηρέωdidaskō / tēreō — "teaching / observe" · Strong's G1321, G5083

    Teach them to observe — to keep, to guard, to hold fast — all that He commanded. Not a greatest-hits list. Not the parts that fit the age. Discipleship that stops at the water is unfinished. Discipleship that never gets wet is also unfinished. The two participles belong to the same verb.

  • ἐγώ εἰμιegō eimi — "I am" · Strong's G1510

    “Lo, I am with you always, even unto the end of the age.” Joshua heard “I will be with you.” Isaiah 41:10 said the same. The risen Christ takes that old promise in His own mouth. The commission is not “you go, I watch.” It is “you go, I remain.” The presence is as long as the age. You do not outlast Him. You do not outrun Him either.

2. Where It Sits

Matthew ends on a mountain in Galilee, as Jesus said He would. Some worship. Some doubt. He comes near anyway and speaks. Authority first — given, not seized. Then the therefore. The book that opened with “God with us” (1:23) closes with “I am with you always.” The frame is the point. The King who was born under a foreign census now sends the church past every border that census could name.

This is not a leftover mission slogan stapled to a biography. The whole Gospel has been moving here: Gentiles in the genealogy, magi at the house, a centurion’s faith, a Canaanite woman’s cry, the temple cleansed for the nations. Now the risen Lord makes it a standing order. Acts will show the first steps — repent and be baptized, the Name, the teaching of the apostles. Revelation will show the end: the Spirit and the Bride saying Come, the dwelling of God with man. Matthew 28 is the middle of that road, spoken as a command.

Notice what He does not say. He does not say make converts of your own kind. He does not say baptize them into a personality. He does not say teach them your preferences. All nations. The Name. All that I commanded. The church that shrinks any of those three has already edited the King.

3. What the Witnesses Saw

Calvin — on the embassy

Calvin reads the sentence as the definition of the apostles’ embassy, and he will not let later teachers invent a different one:

The words in which Christ defined their embassy are sufficiently clear, “Go ye, therefore, and teach all nations, teaching them to observe all things whatsoever I have commanded you”

Calvin, Institutes IV.8 (Beveridge 1845)

The church does not get a second commission when the culture shifts. The embassy is this. Teach what He commanded. Not what the age will applaud.

Calvin — on the Name

He also hears the baptismal formula as a confession of the one God in three persons:

And there cannot be a doubt that our Saviour wished to testify, by a solemn rehearsal, that the perfect light of faith is now exhibited, when he said, “Go and teach all nations, baptising them in the name of the Father, and of the Son, and of the Holy Spirit,”

Hence it plainly appears, that the three persons, in whom alone God is known, subsist in the Divine essence.

Calvin, Institutes I.13 (Beveridge 1845)

One name. Three persons. That is why a church can baptize without becoming tritheists, and why she cannot baptize into a vague “higher power” without ceasing to be His.

4. Confessional Anchor

1689 London Baptist Confession · Chapter 2, Paragraph 3

In this divine and infinite Being there are three subsistences, the Father, the Word or Son, and Holy Spirit, of one substance, power, and eternity, each having the whole divine essence, yet the essence undivided: the Father is of none, neither begotten nor proceeding; the Son is eternally begotten of the Father; the Holy Spirit proceeding from the Father and the Son; all infinite, without beginning, therefore but one God, who is not to be divided in nature and being, but distinguished by several peculiar relative properties and personal relations; which doctrine of the Trinity is the foundation of all our communion with God, and comfortable dependence on him.

Baptist Catechism · Question 97

Q. What is baptism?
A. Baptism is an ordinance of the New Testament instituted by Jesus Christ, to be to the party baptized a sign of his fellowship with him, in his death, burial, and resurrection; of his being grafted into him; of remission of sins; and of his giving up himself to God through Jesus Christ, to live and walk in newness of life.

Baptist Catechism · Question 100

Q. How is baptism rightly administered?
A. Baptism is rightly administered by immersion, or dipping the whole body of the party in water, into the name of the Father, and of the Son, and of the Holy Spirit, according to Christ’s institution, and the practice of the apostles, and not by sprinkling or pouring of water, or dipping some part of the body, after the tradition of men.

Question 100 →

The confession will not let the Name become a slogan, and it will not let baptism become a mood. The catechism puts the same formula in the water that Jesus put in the commission. Make disciples. Plunge them into that Name. Teach them to walk in newness of life. The risen Christ did not ask the church to invent a milder rite for a milder age.

5. For the Man Reading This

This pair of verses closes the Unashamed memorize pack — with Romans 1:16–17 and 1 Peter 3:15. Paul: do not be ashamed of the gospel. Peter: be ready with a reason. Jesus: go make disciples of the nations. One pack, one nerve. If you carry the first two and never obey the third, you have a private religion with public language.

Start with the man in front of you. All nations includes your house, your street, and the nations you will never see. You are not the apostle to the ethnē by yourself. You are a disciple under orders. Teach your sons what He commanded. Open your mouth where you actually live. Support the going you cannot do with the going you can.

Do not separate the water from the teaching. A baptism with no follow-through is a wet decision. A study with no baptism is a class. Jesus joined them. If you lead men, lead them into the Name and into the commands, not into your personality.

Work as a man who is not alone. “I am with you always” is not a poster for anxious nights only. It is the working condition of the commission. You will be tired. You will be ignored. You will be opposed. He does not wait at the finish line. He is with you on the ordinary day you open the Word and tell the truth.

The age has an end. The presence does not run out before that. Make disciples until then. That is the work. Amen is not a flourish. It is the church saying the King’s last word is a good one.
