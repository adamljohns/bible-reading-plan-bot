H5835 · Hebrew · Old Testament
עֲזָרָה
ʻăzârâh
Pronounced az-aw-raw'
an inclosure

Definition

Strong's defines H5835 as an inclosure; also a border. Strong's gives the derivation as from H5826 (עָזַר) in its original meaning of surrounding. The King James translators rendered it court, settle. In the tagged KJV the word carries this number 6 times.

Usage in Scripture

Tracing H5835 through the Old Testament shows 6 tagged occurrences in the Old Testament, falling in 2 Chronicles, Ezekiel. The verses below are not selected for effect: each one carries this Hebrew number in the KJV tagging itself, so the reader can check the claim against the text rather than taking it on trust.
