G3941 · Greek · New Testament
πάροικος
pároikos
having a home near

Definition

Strong's defines G3941 as having a home near, i.e. (as noun) a by-dweller (alien resident). Strong's gives the derivation as from G3844 (παρά) and G3624 (οἶκος). The King James translators rendered it foreigner, sojourn, stranger. In the tagged KJV the word carries this number 4 times.

Usage in Scripture

Tracing G3941 through the New Testament shows 4 tagged occurrences in the New Testament, falling in Acts, Ephesians, 1 Peter. The verses below are not selected for effect: each one carries this Greek number in the KJV tagging itself, so the reader can check the claim against the text rather than taking it on trust.
