G3664 · Greek · New Testament
ὅμοιος
homoios
Adjective
like; of the same kind

Definition

The Greek adjective homoios means like — of the same kind, not a cheaper copy. KJV renders it like. In the kingdom parables the word is the hinge: the kingdom of heaven is like a mustard seed, leaven, treasure, a merchant, a net, a householder. The comparison is not decoration. It is how Jesus teaches a man what the kingdom actually is.

Usage & Theological Significance

Matthew 11:16 asks, whereunto shall I liken this generation? It is like unto children sitting in the markets. Matthew 13 then stacks the word: the kingdom of heaven is like to a grain of mustard seed (13:31), like unto leaven (13:33), like unto treasure hid in a field (13:44), like unto a merchant man (13:45), like unto a net (13:47), and like unto a man that is an householder (13:52). Matthew 20:1 keeps the same hinge: the kingdom of heaven is like unto a man that is an householder. The adjective will not let a man treat the kingdom as a slogan. It is like these things, or he has not yet heard the parable.

Key Bible Verses

Matthew 11:16 But whereunto shall I liken this generation? It is like unto children sitting in the markets, and calling unto their fellows,
Matthew 13:31 Another parable put he forth unto them, saying, The kingdom of heaven is like to a grain of mustard seed, which a man took, and sowed in his field:
Matthew 13:33 Another parable spake he unto them; The kingdom of heaven is like unto leaven, which a woman took, and hid in three measures of meal, till the whole was leavened.
Matthew 13:44 Again, the kingdom of heaven is like unto treasure hid in a field; the which when a man hath found, he hideth, and for joy thereof goeth and selleth all that he hath, and buyeth that field.
Matthew 13:45 Again, the kingdom of heaven is like unto a merchant man, seeking goodly pearls:
Matthew 13:47 Again, the kingdom of heaven is like unto a net, that was cast into the sea, and gathered of every kind:
Matthew 13:52 Then said he unto them, Therefore every scribe which is instructed unto the kingdom of heaven is like unto a man that is an householder, which bringeth forth out of his treasure things new and old.
Matthew 20:1 For the kingdom of heaven is like unto a man that is an householder, which went out early in the morning to hire labourers into his vineyard.
