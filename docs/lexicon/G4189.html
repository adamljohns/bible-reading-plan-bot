G4189 · Greek · New Testament
πονηρία
Ponēría
Noun
Wickedness / Malice

Definition

Ponēria denotes active, deliberate wickedness—not passive wrongdoing but malice with intent to corrupt.

Usage & Theological Significance

Mark 7:22: Jesus lists ponēria among evils from the heart. Ephesians 6:12: spiritual forces of wickedness.

Key Bible Verses

Mark 7:22 Wickedness, deceit, sensuality.
Ephesians 6:12 Spiritual forces of evil.

Related Words

G4190 — Ponēros G2549 — Kakia
