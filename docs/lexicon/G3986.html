G3986 · Greek · New Testament
πειρασμός
Peirasmós
Noun
Temptation / Trial

Definition

Peirasmos has dual meaning: (1) temptation to sin, (2) trial testing faith. Context determines which.

Usage & Theological Significance

James 1:2–4: count trials as joy. Matthew 6:13: 'Lead us not into temptation.' Christ endured testing (Hebrews 4:15).

Key Bible Verses

James 1:2 Count it joy when you meet trials.
Matthew 6:13 Lead us not into temptation.

Related Words
