G3900 · Greek · New Testament
παράπτωμα
Paraptōma
Noun
Transgression / Trespass

Definition

From para (beside) and piptō (to fall), paraptōma means a fall beside or slip sideways—a deviation from God's path.

Usage & Theological Significance

Paul uses paraptōma in Romans 5:15–20: Adam's single trespass brought condemnation; Christ's obedience brought justification.

Key Bible Verses

Romans 5:15 But the free gift is not like the trespass.
Ephesians 2:1 Dead in trespasses and sins.

Related Words

G266 — Hamartia G458 — Anomia
