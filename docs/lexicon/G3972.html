G3972 · Greek · New Testament
Παῦλος
Paûlos
meaning the same)

Definition

Strong's defines G3972 as (little; but remotely from a derivative of G3973 (παύω), meaning the same); Paulus, the name of a Roman and of an apostle. Strong's gives the derivation as of Latin origin. The King James translators rendered it Paul, Paulus. In the tagged KJV the word carries this number 160 times.
