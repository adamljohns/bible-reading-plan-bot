H3423 · Hebrew · Old Testament
יָרַשׁ
yârash
Verb (primitive root)
Pronounced yaw-rash'
to occupy

Definition

Strong's defines H3423 as to occupy (by driving out previous tenants, and possessing in their place); by implication, to seize, to rob, to inherit; also to expel, to impoverish, to ruin. Strong's gives the derivation as or יָרֵשׁ; a primitive root. The King James translators rendered it cast out, consume, destroy, disinherit, dispossess, driveing out. In the tagged KJV the word carries this number 204 times.
