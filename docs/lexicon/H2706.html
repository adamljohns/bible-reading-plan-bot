H2706 · Hebrew · Old Testament
חֹק
Choq
Noun, masculine
Statute, appointed portion

Definition

The Hebrew noun choq is an enactment, and from there an appointment — Strong's extends it to appointments of time, space, quantity, labour and usage. The KJV renders it statute, decree, ordinance, law, custom, due, bound and measure. The root idea is something inscribed and therefore fixed. A choq can be a moral statute God lays on Israel or the fixed ration a workman is owed, and the two senses are not far apart: both are portions cut out in advance and not open to renegotiation by whoever finds them inconvenient.

Usage & Theological Significance

Kings uses the word to set the terms of the covenant with David's line. Solomon is promised long life if he will walk in God's ways to keep His statutes and His commandments as his father David did walk (1 Kings 3:14), and the same condition is restated when the temple is finished (1 Kings 9:4). Solomon's own prayer asks that God would incline our hearts unto him, to keep his statutes (1 Kings 8:58), and that the people's heart be perfect with the LORD to walk in them (1 Kings 8:61) — the request is for the will to keep them, not merely for the text of them. The books then use the same word to write the obituary of the northern kingdom: they rejected his statutes and his covenant (2 Kings 17:15), and the statutes and ordinances they were commanded to observe for evermore were abandoned (2 Kings 17:37). Chronicles keeps the promise side in view, recalling the covenant confirmed to Jacob for a statute (1 Chronicles 16:17), and David's charge to Solomon to take heed to fulfil the statutes and judgments (1 Chronicles 22:13). The lesson Kings draws is unsentimental: a statute that is genuinely fixed does not stop being fixed when a nation stops keeping it.

Key Bible Verses

1 Kings 3:14
And if thou wilt walk in my ways, to keep my statutes and my commandments, as thy father David did walk, then I will lengthen thy days. (KJV)
1 Kings 9:4
And if thou wilt walk before me, as David thy father walked, in integrity of heart, and in uprightness, to do according to all that I have commanded thee, and wilt keep my statutes and my judgments: (KJV)
1 Kings 8:58
That he may incline our hearts unto him, to walk in all his ways, and to keep his commandments, and his statutes, and his judgments, which he commanded our fathers. (KJV)
1 Kings 8:61
Let your heart therefore be perfect with the LORD our God, to walk in his statutes, and to keep his commandments, as at this day. (KJV)
2 Kings 17:15
And they rejected his statutes, and his covenant that he made with their fathers, and his testimonies which he testified against them; and they followed vanity, and became vain, and went after the heathen that were round about them, concerning whom the LORD had charged them, that they should not do like them. (KJV)

And the statutes, and the ordinances, and the law, and the commandment, which he wrote for you, ye shall observe to do for evermore; and ye shall not fear other gods. (KJV)

And hath confirmed the same to Jacob for a law, and to Israel for an everlasting covenant, (KJV)

Then shalt thou prosper, if thou takest heed to fulfil the statutes and judgments which the LORD charged Moses with concerning Israel: be strong, and of good courage; dread not, nor be dismayed. (KJV)
