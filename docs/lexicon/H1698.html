H1698 · Hebrew · Old Testament
דֶּבֶר
deber
Pronounced deh'-ber
a pestilence

Definition

Strong's defines H1698 as a pestilence. Strong's gives the derivation as from H1696 (דָבַר) (in the sense of destroying). The King James translators rendered it murrain, pestilence, plague. In the tagged KJV the word carries this number 48 times.

Usage in Scripture

Tracing H1698 through the Old Testament shows 48 tagged occurrences in the Old Testament, falling in 2 Samuel, 1 Kings, 1 Chronicles, 2 Chronicles. The verses below are not selected for effect: each one carries this Hebrew number in the KJV tagging itself, so the reader can check the claim against the text rather than taking it on trust.
