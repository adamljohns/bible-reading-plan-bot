H8354 · Hebrew · Old Testament
שָׁתָה
Shatah
Verb
To drink

Definition

The Hebrew verb shathah means to drink, literally or figuratively, and Strong's glosses it simply as to imbibe. The KJV renders it drink, drunkard, banquet, and — in the idiom where drinking stands for the whole of feasting — assuredly and surely. Its plainness is the point: Hebrew narrative reaches for eating and drinking when it wants to show what a man is actually like, because nobody performs at a table the way they perform in public.

Usage & Theological Significance

The books of Samuel use the word to expose people. Uriah refuses it as a matter of honour, protesting that the ark and Israel and Judah abide in tents, and asking whether he should then go into his house to eat and to drink while his fellow soldiers lie in the open field (2 Samuel 11:11). David's answer is to make him drunk, and Uriah still will not go home (2 Samuel 11:13), so the drinking measures the soldier and condemns the king in the same scene. Nathan's parable turns on it too: the poor man's one little ewe lamb ate of his own meat and drank of his own cup and lay in his bosom (2 Samuel 12:3), and the intimacy of that shared cup is what makes the theft monstrous. Later, when three mighty men break through the Philistine garrison to fetch water from the well of Bethlehem, David would not drink it but poured it out unto the LORD, saying it was the blood of the men that went in jeopardy of their lives (2 Samuel 23:16-17). Refusing the drink is how he honours them. Barzillai, eighty years old, says he can no longer taste what he eats or drinks (2 Samuel 19:35), and the loss of the pleasure marks the end of his usefulness at court.

Key Bible Verses

2 Samuel 11:11
And Uriah said unto David, The ark, and Israel, and Judah, abide in tents; and my lord Joab, and the servants of my lord, are encamped in the open fields; shall I then go into mine house, to eat and to drink, and to lie with my wife? as thou livest, and as thy soul liveth, I will not do this thing. (KJV)
2 Samuel 11:13
And when David had called him, he did eat and drink before him; and he made him drunk: and at even he went out to lie on his bed with the servants of his lord, but went not down to his house. (KJV)
2 Samuel 12:3
But the poor man had nothing, save one little ewe lamb, which he had bought and nourished up: and it grew up together with him, and with his children; it did eat of his own meat, and drank of his own cup, and lay in his bosom, and was unto him as a daughter. meat: Heb. morsel (KJV)
2 Samuel 23:16
And the three mighty men brake through the host of the Philistines, and drew water out of the well of Bethlehem, that was by the gate, and took it, and brought it to David: nevertheless he would not drink thereof, but poured it out unto the LORD. (KJV)
2 Samuel 23:17
And he said, Be it far from me, O LORD, that I should do this: is not this the blood of the men that went in jeopardy of their lives? therefore he would not drink it. These things did these three mighty men. (KJV)
2 Samuel 19:35
I am this day fourscore years old: and can I discern between good and evil? can thy servant taste what I eat or what I drink? can I hear any more the voice of singing men and singing women? wherefore then should thy servant be yet a burden unto my lord the king? (KJV)
