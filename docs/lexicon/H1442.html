H1442 · Hebrew · Old Testament
גָּדַף
gâdaph
Verb (primitive root)
Pronounced gaw-daf'
to hack

Definition

Strong's defines H1442 as to hack (with words), i.e. revile. Strong's gives the derivation as a primitive root. The King James translators rendered it blaspheme, reproach. In the tagged KJV the word carries this number 7 times.

Usage in Scripture

Tracing H1442 through the Old Testament shows 7 tagged occurrences in the Old Testament, falling in 2 Kings, Psalms, Isaiah, Ezekiel. The verses below are not selected for effect: each one carries this Hebrew number in the KJV tagging itself, so the reader can check the claim against the text rather than taking it on trust.
