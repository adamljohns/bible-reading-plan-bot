Pastor Maxie Brock serves as the senior pastor at Oakton Baptist, where he preaches verse-by-verse through the Scriptures and shepherds the congregation week to week. His preaching is plain, his heart is for souls, and his door is open to anyone wrestling with the claims of the gospel.

Body Life

How We Grow Together

The Christian life isn't lived alone. These are the regular rhythms by which Oakton's congregation knows, serves, and prays for one another throughout the week.

CM

Children's Ministry

Nursery through 5th grade — age-graded Sunday classes and a children's worship moment during the main service.

SM

Student Ministry

Middle and high schoolers gather midweek for study, fellowship, and serious teaching from God's Word.

SG

Small Groups

Smaller mixed-age groups meet through the week in homes for Bible study, prayer, and shared meals.

MS

Missions & Outreach

We support a slate of vetted SBC missionaries and partner annually with local outreach efforts in Fairfax County.

What We Believe

Our Confession

Oakton Baptist Church affirms the historic Christian faith summarized in the Baptist Faith and Message 2000, the confessional document of the Southern Baptist Convention.

Scripture

The Bible is the inspired, inerrant, and authoritative Word of God — our supreme rule for faith and practice.

The Gospel

Salvation is by God's grace, through faith in Christ alone — His life, death, and resurrection for sinners.

The Church

The local church is shepherded by qualified pastors and deacons, ordering its life around the Word and the ordinances.

Baptism & the Supper

Baptism by immersion follows a credible profession of faith; the Lord's Supper is observed in faithful remembrance.

A note on confession: Oakton Baptist is a confessional church. Our preaching, teaching, and discipline are bounded by the Baptist Faith and Message 2000. We affirm the full text of that confession and we are happy to walk through any of its articles with anyone who wants to understand where we stand on a particular question.

Plan a Visit

We'd love to meet you this Sunday. Wear what's comfortable, bring the kids, and grab a coffee in the lobby before service. Look for the welcome desk near the main entrance — someone will say hello and help you find your way around.

If it'd help to chat with the pastor beforehand, or if you'd like a tour during the week, call the church office and we'll set it up.
