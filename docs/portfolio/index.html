Church Web-Build Samples

Three sample sites built by Uniting, Serving, Mentoring & Counseling Ministries for small US churches with active social media but no working website. Each demo was modeled on a real congregation in the MOOP Church Directory, with the goal of showing how different traditions and aesthetics translate to a clean, fast, mobile-friendly web presence.

These are sample sites — none of the churches shown have commissioned this work yet. The intent is to show prospective congregations what their own site could look like.

About the offer

USMC Ministries ("Uniting, Serving, Mentoring & Counseling Ministries") offers small, faithful US churches a clean, durable web presence built around their actual congregation — service times, address, pastor, beliefs, and the Facebook page they already maintain. Each site is single-page, mobile-first, fast, and yours to keep.

The three demos above each represent a different visual direction. A real build for your church would start from a 10-minute phone call, a one-page intake form, and a draft you'd review before anything goes live.

  1. Reply & we talk. A short call or email exchange about your church — service times, pastor, beliefs, what photos you have, what tone fits your congregation.
  2. Choose a direction. Pick one of the three styles above, or describe a different one. The samples are starting points, not the only options.
  3. I draft, you review. A draft of your site, hosted at a private URL like the samples above, with the demo banner on top. You review and request changes.
  4. Go live. Final version published either at a domain you own (preferred) or, if you don't have one yet, at a permanent home in our directory.

The aim is service to the local church, not a sales funnel. If your church already has a working web presence and is happy with it, the Lord be praised — keep it. This offer exists for the small congregation that has been meaning to get a site up for years and never quite gotten there.
