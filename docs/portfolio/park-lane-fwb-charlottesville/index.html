Who We Are

A Free Will Baptist Family in Charlottesville

Park Lane Free Will Baptist Church is a member congregation of the National Association of Free Will Baptists, a denomination rooted in the Wesleyan-Arminian Baptist tradition. We hold to the orthodox doctrines of the historic Christian faith — the deity of Christ, salvation by grace through faith, the inspiration and authority of Scripture — while emphasizing the sincere universal offer of the gospel and human responsibility before God.

Our congregation has worshipped on Park Lane West for many years. We are a multi-generational church: grandparents who have been members for half a century, parents raising children in the faith, and young families finding their first church home. The pews are not packed every Sunday — they don't need to be. The Lord adds to the church such as should be saved.

"We are a plain people. We sing the old hymns. We preach the whole Bible. We pray for one another. And we believe, with the apostle Paul, that Christ Jesus came into the world to save sinners — of whom we are chief."

What We Believe

Our Doctrine in Brief

Park Lane Free Will Baptist Church holds to the doctrines summarized in the National Association of Free Will Baptists' Treatise of the Faith and Practices.

The Scriptures

The Holy Bible — Old and New Testaments — is the inspired Word of God, our infallible rule for faith and conduct.

The Trinity

One eternal God, existing in three Persons — Father, Son, and Holy Spirit — equal in essence and glory.

The Person & Work of Christ

Jesus Christ, fully God and fully man, died for the sins of the world, rose bodily from the dead, and reigns now and forever.

Salvation by Grace

Salvation is by grace through faith in the finished work of Christ — a free gift, not earned by works, received by repentance and belief.

The Church & the Ordinances

The local church gathers around the Word and the ordinances — baptism by immersion and the Lord's Supper.

Christian Living

The believer is called to a life of holiness, Christlike love, faithful witness, and perseverance in the grace of God.

On the Name "Free Will Baptist"

Free Will Baptists believe that the gospel is for all — that "whosoever will may come" — and that the grace of God is freely offered to every sinner. We are free grace Baptists: salvation cannot be earned, and we cannot save ourselves. And we are free will Baptists: each person is summoned to respond to the gospel in repentance and faith. These two truths — God's free grace and the sinner's responsible response — are held together at the cross.
