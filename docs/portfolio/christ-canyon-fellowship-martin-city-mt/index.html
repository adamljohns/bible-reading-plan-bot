A Word from the Elders

Christ Canyon Fellowship is a small, confessing, Reformed-Baptist congregation gathered in the canyon country just south of Glacier National Park. We sing the old hymns, preach the whole counsel of God, and believe the Christian faith is best handed down from one generation to the next inside a covenant family.

We are a small church on purpose. Our elders know every family by name, our prayer list fits on one printed page, and the same hands that bake the communion bread also stack the chairs after service. We are not a destination church and we do not aspire to be one. What we offer to anyone who would worship with us is the ordinary means of grace — the read and preached Word, the sacraments rightly administered, and the warm fellowship of a body of believers who have learned to bear with one another.

If you are visiting the Flathead Valley — whether you came up for the trout, the trails, or for relatives in Hungry Horse, Coram, or Columbia Falls — we would be honored to have you in the pew next to us this Lord's Day. Pull up around back, walk in the side door, and ask the first deacon you see for a seat. We will find you one.

Our Story

Christ Canyon Fellowship was planted by a small core of Flathead-area believers who, after years of driving into Kalispell or Whitefish for Sunday morning, became persuaded that this end of the valley needed its own confessing congregation. We began with seven families meeting in a member's home for a year of prayer, study, and the formation of an eldership.

By God's good providence, we now meet in a rented hall on 3rd Avenue South in Martin City, with our own membership covenant, our own deacons, and our own modest but growing library of Reformed and Puritan works. Our Sunday school for children meets concurrently with the morning service; our Sunday evening study works through one book of the Bible at a time, no shortcuts.

We are honored to be cross-listed with the 9Marks church network — a fellowship of confessional Baptists committed to expositional preaching, biblical theology, the gospel, conversion, evangelism, membership, discipline, discipleship, and biblical leadership. That partnership has been a real encouragement to our small body, and it shapes how we think about church health in this valley.

What We Confess

Scripture

The 66 books of the Old and New Testaments are the inspired, inerrant, and infallible Word of God — the sole final authority for all faith and practice.

Gospel

Sinners are justified by grace alone, through faith alone, in Christ alone — His substitutionary death and bodily resurrection the only ground of our peace with God.

Worship

The regulative principle shapes our gathered worship — Scripture read, preached, prayed, sung, sealed in baptism and the Lord's Supper. Plain and ordinary, by design.

Leadership

The local church is governed by a plurality of qualified male elders, served by deacons, and made up of regenerate, baptized members who have walked a known path to the table.

Family

Marriage is the lifelong covenant of one man and one woman. Fathers are the primary disciplers of their children; mothers the primary keepers of the home. We honor both callings.

Mission

The local church is the engine of the Great Commission. Our members are sent into the Flathead Valley as ordinary Christians witnessing to ordinary neighbors with the ordinary gospel.

Our full Statement of Faith is available on request — we are happy to walk through it line by line with any interested family.
