Who We Are

A small, plain Baptist church that has been gathering on Water Street since the 19th century.

Memorial Baptist is part of the Southern Baptist Convention of Virginia (SBCV). We affirm the Baptist Faith and Message 2000 as a faithful summary of biblical doctrine, and our preaching ministry is shaped by the conviction that the whole counsel of Scripture is profitable for teaching, reproof, correction, and instruction in righteousness.

Our congregation is intentionally small. We know each other's names, share each other's burdens, and gather around an unhurried table on Wednesday evenings. We are not trying to be the largest church in Caroline County; we are trying to be a faithful one.

A Note on Our Pulpit Search

Memorial Baptist is currently between settled pastors. Our previous interim, Pastor Patrick Allen, finished his service with us in 2026 and has been called elsewhere. We are now in a season of prayer and patient searching for the man the Lord would call to this pulpit.

In the meantime, faithful supply pastors fill the pulpit each Lord's Day, and the work of the church — Bible study, prayer, fellowship, missions — continues unbroken. We covet your prayers.

— The deacons of Memorial Baptist Church

What We Believe

  • The Bible is the inspired, inerrant, and authoritative Word of God.
  • Salvation is by grace alone, through faith alone, in Christ alone — to the glory of God alone.
  • Baptism by immersion is for those who have believed on the Lord Jesus Christ for salvation.
  • The Lord's Supper is observed in remembrance of Christ's death, until He comes.
  • The local church is shepherded by qualified pastors and deacons, after the pattern given in 1 Timothy and Titus.
  • Marriage is the covenant union of one man and one woman, given by God for the good of His people.
A full statement of our doctrine is found in the Baptist Faith and Message 2000, the confessional document of the Southern Baptist Convention, which Memorial Baptist Church publicly affirms.
