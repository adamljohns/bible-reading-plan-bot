📝 Assessment
Capitol Hill Baptist Church is not merely a church — it is an institution that has shaped American evangelical ecclesiology for thirty years. Dr. Mark Dever, who has served as Senior Pastor since 1994, literally wrote the book on healthy church practice (Nine Marks of a Healthy Church). CHBC is the prototype of what expository preaching, elder-led polity, meaningful church membership, and Gospel-centered culture look like when done exceptionally well. Founded in 1878 just blocks from the U.S. Capitol, CHBC preaches verse-by-verse through entire books of the Bible, maintains strict membership accountability, and has planted and trained hundreds of churches through 9Marks Ministries. For any Christian man — especially military or government professionals in DC — CHBC is one of the most significant church opportunities in America.
