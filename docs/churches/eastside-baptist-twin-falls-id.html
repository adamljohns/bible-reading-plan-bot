Assessment
Eastside Baptist Church in Twin Falls, Idaho is a 1689 Confessional Reformed Baptist church with a plurality of elders, age-integrated worship, and a robust confessional foundation. Founded in 1972, this church holds to the full 1689 London Baptist Confession including the appendix on baptism. The elder-led structure (Thompson, Martinez) with deacons reflects biblical governance. Their adoption of resolutions on Freemasonry, age-segregation, and women's conscription demonstrates a church willing to take stands on cultural issues from Scripture — not hiding behind neutrality. Men's Book Study and age-integrated model mean men are formed as household leaders, not shuffled into a separate program. Four services per week (Sunday AM, Sunday PM, Sunday School, Wednesday evening) indicates a serious, committed congregation. This is an all-GREEN church — rare and worthy of attention for any Reformed Baptist in the Pacific Northwest.
