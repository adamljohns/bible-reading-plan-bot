Church Scorecard

Ten linked questions examine doctrine, order, discipleship, mission, and Kingdom Alignment.

Christology
Jesus is fully God and fully man, risen Lord, and the only way to the Father.Scripture
The written Word is true, sufficient, and the church’s final authority.Men's Discipleship
Men are intentionally formed to know Scripture, lead sacrificially, and disciple others.Soteriology
Salvation is by grace alone through faith alone in Christ alone, producing repentance.Gender / Biblical Design
God created humanity male and female; the church honors that good design.Leadership Structure
Qualified male elders shepherd under Christ with plurality and accountability.Preaching Style
Preaching explains and applies the Word in context, centering Christ.Mission Clarity
The church makes disciples through gospel proclamation, baptism, teaching, holiness, and mission.Kingdom Alignment
The public witness is ordered under Christ’s reign — gospel, Word, holiness, and Great Commission — on earth as in heaven.Accountability Structure
A pastor is answerable to qualified elders and meaningful external or denominational discipline.
