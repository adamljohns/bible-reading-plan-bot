Kingdom Alignment Zones

Four zones summarize the strength or danger of a church’s public witness.

Green Zone
A public witness bearing the marks of the Kingdom of Light: true gospel, Scripture as final authority, holiness, repentance, and the Great Commission.Caution Zone
Public evidence is thin, mixed, or adjacent to compromise. Examine carefully; this is not a declaration of apostasy.Red Zone
Very compromised — avoid as a default church home. Clear rival-kingdom markers are public.Black Zone
Avoid. Clear public evidence shows affirming homosexuality, a false gospel, or a totally apostate posture.

How to Use This

Open each teaching page before treating a rating as a verdict. Every page includes primary sources and a correction path.
