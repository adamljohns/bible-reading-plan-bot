Assessment
Shiloh Baptist Church (New Site) and Shiloh Baptist (Old Site) are sister congregations sharing common roots stretching back to the pre-Civil War African Baptist Church of Fredericksburg. Where Old Site (801 Sophia St) has drifted toward social-gospel framing, New Site presents a cleaner mission statement: 'Glorifying God, making disciples, and serving humanity' — with 'making disciples' as the explicit Great Commission anchor. Rev. Keenan Thomas arrived in October 2022 with a Howard University / Drew Seminary education and a practical ministry background at Alfred Street Baptist (one of the largest and most Word-focused Black Baptist churches in the country). His foundational scripture — 1 Peter 4:11, speaking 'the very words of God' — reflects a high view of Scripture in praxis. The honest concern is Drew Theological Seminary, which is a theologically progressive Methodist institution — not the formation environment that produces expository complementarians. However, a man's seminary doesn't always define his ministry convictions. The 15+ ministries and Wednesday Bible study suggest an active, Word-engaged congregation. Worth visiting and hearing Rev. Keenan preach before drawing conclusions.
