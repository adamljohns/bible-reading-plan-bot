📝 Assessment
Bayside Baptist Church is an established Baptist congregation in Virginia Beach, affiliated with the Baptist General Association of Virginia (BGAV) and the Norfolk Baptist Association. Important distinction: BGAV is the CBF-aligned Virginia Baptist body (moderate to progressive), distinct from the SBC of Virginia (SBCV). This means Bayside may be more open on women in ministry and less doctrinally conservative than SBC churches. Dr. John Nixon's recent departure (noted in 2022 Baptist News) means current pastoral leadership should be confirmed before visiting. The church maintains Sunday School programming, childcare, and community ministry. For men seeking a theologically clear conservative Baptist church, BGAV affiliation warrants caution and an in-person investigation to determine where this specific congregation lands doctrinally.
