📝 Assessment
Grace Presbyterian Church is a PCA congregation started in 2002 to glorify God and serve the families of King George County. The Presbyterian Church in America is among the most theologically sound denominations in the United States — holding to the Westminster Confession of Faith, inerrancy, male-only elders, and Reformed soteriology. For Moop's priorities, a PCA church in King George is a strong find. The congregation appears small but committed. Highly recommended for investigation.
