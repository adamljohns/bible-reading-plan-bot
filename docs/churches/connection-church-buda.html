📝 Assessment
The Connection Church is a growing, multi-campus non-denominational church in the Buda/San Marcos area of Texas. Lead Pastor Cole Lance holds a doctorate in missions and evangelism from Southwestern Baptist Theological Seminary — a generally orthodox institution — which suggests sound theological training even if the church's public-facing statements are light on doctrine. The church's branding centers on community connection and life change rather than doctrinal distinctives. For a man seeking a theologically rigorous home, this church would require in-person investigation: attend a service, review sermon content, and request their statement of faith. Their Southwestern Baptist roots are encouraging, but the non-denominational label with limited doctrinal transparency warrants caution.
