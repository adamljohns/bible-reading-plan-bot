📝 Assessment
Remnant Church RVA is a young, urban church in downtown/midtown Richmond with two campuses and a strong contemporary worship identity. The church is listed as SBCV affiliated but presents publicly as non-denominational. Historically Remnant was an Acts 29 church and experienced controversy around 2018 with documented reports of controlling/cult-like pastoral behavior. Current leadership structure, accountability, and pastoral team composition need direct investigation. On the positive side: male-only lead pastors confirmed by visitors, clear gospel preaching documented, Great Commission language on their website. Visit one Sunday, ask about leadership accountability structure, and evaluate. Could be excellent or still working through institutional problems.
