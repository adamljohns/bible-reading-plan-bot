Assessment
Faith Bible Fellowship Church of York is a Reformed, evangelical, baptistic church in the BFC denomination. Pastor Wesley Bunting leads a solid elder-governed congregation committed to the doctrines of grace and expository preaching. The church's mission — 'joyfully build Christ's kingdom, household by household' — reflects a family-centered, Great Commission focus. Elder Alex Kocman's role with ABWE gives the church strong international missions DNA. Two caution notes: men's discipleship is not confirmed as a dedicated program (verify in person), and the church holds a complementarian rather than patriarchal position on gender, with a female deacon serving on the board. Doctrinally very strong. Worth visiting.
