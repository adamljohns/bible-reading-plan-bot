📝 Assessment
Washington National Cathedral is an architectural masterpiece and a significant national landmark — but from a USMC Ministries theological alignment standpoint, it is firmly not recommended. The Episcopal Church (TEC) has abandoned biblical authority on human sexuality, ordains women as bishops and priests, performs same-sex marriages, and its leadership uses the pulpit for progressive political advocacy. Bishop Mariann Budde's nationally publicized January 2025 inaugural sermon was a political statement, not a Gospel proclamation. The Cathedral has hosted interfaith services including Islamic prayers. The beauty of the building and the grandeur of the liturgy should not be confused with theological fidelity. This is included in the directory for situational awareness — military and government personnel in DC will be invited to events here. Know what you're walking into.
