📝 Assessment
Tabernacle Baptist Church is a Fan District congregation with over 120 years of Richmond history, located on Grove Avenue near Meadow Street. The church received a CBF Ministries Council Grant for affordable housing work — the Cooperative Baptist Fellowship (CBF) is the progressive Baptist body that split from the SBC in 1991 explicitly over inerrancy and women's ordination. BGAV/CBF dual affiliation places Tabernacle at the most progressive end of the Baptist spectrum. The mission framing centers 'God's abundance through the gift of community' with explicit social justice ministry over Gospel proclamation. Sterling Severns may be a sincere and pastoral minister, but the denominational and cultural framework here is incompatible with biblical complementarianism, inerrancy, and Great Commission-first mission. Not recommended.
