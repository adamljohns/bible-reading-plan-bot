  • שָׁנַן shanan · And thou shalt teach them diligently · Strong's H8150

    Shanan (שָׁנַן) means to whet or sharpen (as a sword or arrow), and by extension to teach sharply and repeatedly — to inculcate by diligent repetition. It appears about 9 times in the OT.
