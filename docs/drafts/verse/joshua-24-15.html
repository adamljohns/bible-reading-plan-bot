Verse Study · Joshua

Joshua 24:15

The verse is not a wall plaque. It is a commander putting his own house on the table after the people have already been told they cannot serve the Lord casually.

If it seems evil to you to serve the LORD, choose today whom you will serve; whether the gods which your fathers served that were beyond the River, or the gods of the Amorites, in whose land you dwell; but as for me and my house, we will serve the LORD.”— World English Bible (public domain)

And if it seem evil unto you to serve the LORD, choose you this day whom ye will serve; whether the gods which your fathers served that were on the other side of the flood, or the gods of the Amorites, in whose land ye dwell: but as for me and my house, we will serve the LORD.— King James Version (public domain)

1. The Text and Its Words

Shechem. The tribes are assembled. Joshua has just retold the whole story in the first person of God: Abraham taken from beyond the River, Egypt, the sea, the wilderness, the land they did not plant. Verse 14 is the charge: fear the LORD, serve Him in sincerity and truth, put away the gods. Verse 15 is the fork.

Notice what Joshua does not do. He does not say the choice is between the LORD and a neutral private life. He names two existing altars — the ancestral gods beyond the River, and the local gods of the Amorites. If the LORD seems evil to serve, you still serve someone. The only open question is which master.

Then he stops speaking for the crowd. As for me and my house. The commander will not wait for a consensus to decide the worship of his own roof.

  • בָּחַר bachar · “choose” · Strong’s H977

    Bachar means to choose, select, or prefer — always with the connotation of careful examination and deliberate decision. It occurs about 170 times in the OT.

    “This day” keeps the verb from becoming a lifetime mood. The choice is dated. Humans use bachar here; the same root is used of God’s choosing.

  • עָבַד avad · “serve” · Strong’s H5647

    Avad means to work, serve, labor, or worship. It appears about 290 times in the OT.

    Serve is not attend. It is labor under a master. Joshua is not asking who they admire. He is asking whose work they will do.

  • בַּיִת Bayith · “my house” · Strong’s H1004

    Bayith means house, household, dwelling, or temple. Appearing over 2,000 times, it covers physical buildings, family lineages ('house of David'), and the temple as God's dwelling place.

    The house is not a slogan and it is not only a building. It is the people under Joshua’s roof. He does not say “as for me, in my heart.” He says the household will serve. A man can have a private preference and a pagan house. This verse will not call that faithfulness.

    The people then swear they will serve the LORD (24:16–18). Joshua’s next sentence is not congratulations. “You cannot serve the LORD, for He is a holy God” (24:19). The plaque-reading of verse 15 that skips 24:19 has not heard the commander.

2. Where It Sits

Joshua 24 is covenant renewal at the end of the book, not a pep talk at the beginning. The land is possessed. The danger is the same danger Deuteronomy 6 named: fullness, leftover gods, a people who will talk loyalty and keep household idols.

Verse 14: put away the gods. Verse 15: choose today. Verses 16–18: the people’s eager yes. Verses 19–20: Joshua refuses cheap yeses — He is holy, He is jealous, forsaking Him will not be a light thing. Verses 22–27: they become witnesses against themselves; a stone is set up; the words are written in the book of the law.

So “me and my house” is not the end of the scene. It is the commander’s personal stake inside a public covenant that he will not let become a chant.

3. What the Witnesses Saw

The Reformed household reading is right as far as it goes: a man is responsible for the worship under his roof. The verse will not let him hide behind “they will decide when they are older” as if the house had no head.

The other half is less often quoted. Joshua does not trust a swift confession. Holiness and jealousy are the next words. A house that says verse 15 and keeps foreign gods in the midst (24:23) has not chosen. It has decorated.

I am not hanging a verbatim Henry quote on this page this turn. The CCEL fetch for adjacent pages returned a loading shell, not a clean public-domain paragraph I could check word for word. The chapter already interprets the sentence: choose, then put the gods away, then live as a witness against yourself.

4. Confessional Anchor

But the acceptable way of worshiping the true God is instituted by himself, and so limited by his own revealed will, that he may not be worshiped according to the imagination and devices of men, nor the suggestions of Satan, under any visible representation, or in any other way not prescribed in the Holy Scriptures.

1689 LBCF, ch. 22 — Of Religious Worship and the Sabbath Day

Joshua 24:15 is that sentence at Shechem. The choice is not between tasteful spiritualities. It is between the LORD and gods already on the table. Worship invented beside Him is still avad — just aimed at the wrong master.

Religious worship is to be given to God the Father, Son, and Holy Spirit, and to him alone — not to angels, saints, or any other creature; and since the fall, not without a Mediator, nor in the mediation of any other but Christ alone.

1689 LBCF, ch. 22 — Of Religious Worship and the Sabbath Day

“Me and my house” is not a family brand. It is exclusive service.

5. For the Man Reading This

Do not hang this verse until you can name the gods still in the house.

Not statues. The ancestral ones — the way your fathers actually lived. The local ones — the Amorite options in the land you now occupy: the job, the feed, the appetite, the quiet idol that keeps the peace.

Bachar today, out loud, in front of the people who live under your roof. Then do verse 23: put them away. A man who loves the second half of 24:15 and will not do 24:14 is quoting a commander he will not follow.

And do not make your sons the voters. The sentence is me and my house. You lead the service of the house, or the house will serve whatever is already in it.
