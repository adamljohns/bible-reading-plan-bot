  • εἴδω eidō · seeing · Strong's G1492

    A primary verb used in two tenses with distinct meanings: the aorist eidon means 'I saw' (physical or visionary sight), and the perfect oida means 'I know' (knowledge gained through observation). This dual meaning reveals the Greek connection between seeing and knowing — what you have seen, you know.
