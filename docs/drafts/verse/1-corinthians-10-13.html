  • λαμβάνω Lambanō · There hath · Strong's G2983

    Lambanō (G2983) means to take, receive, or accept — often with the sense of actively taking hold of what is offered. It appears over 250 times and covers receiving gifts, power, commandments, and the Holy Spirit.

  • ἀνθρώπινος Anthropinos · such as is common to man · Strong's G442

    Paul uses anthropinos in 1 Corinthians 2:13 to contrast Spirit-taught wisdom with human wisdom: "not in words taught by human wisdom but taught by the Spirit." In Romans 6:19 he says "I am speaking in human terms, because of your natural limitations" — acknowledging the limits of human analogy when speaking of divine things. The word underscores Scripture's consistent theme: human standards and human wisdom, while real, are insufficient to grasp divine realities.
