1. The Text and Its Words

  • ἀδελφός adelphos · brethren · Strong's G80

    Literally 'from the same womb' — a brother by blood. In the NT, it expands to include spiritual brothers and sisters, fellow members of the faith community.

  • ἀληθής alēthēs · true · Strong's G227

    True in the sense of being unconcealed, real, and genuine — from a- (not) + lēthō (to escape notice). Alēthēs describes what corresponds to reality and what is dependable.

    [WRITE — what "true" is doing in this verse specifically — not a dictionary entry]

  • δίκαιος dikaios · are just · Strong's G1342

    Righteous, just, conforming to God's standard. Dikaios describes one who is in right standing — either through personal conduct or through God's declaration.

    [WRITE — what "are just" is doing in this verse specifically — not a dictionary entry]

  • Hagnos · are pure · Strong's G53

    The Greek adjective hagnos means pure, chaste, morally clean, or innocent. It occurs 8 times in the New Testament and describes a purity that is inward and motivational, not merely external ritual cleanness.

    [WRITE — what "are pure" is doing in this verse specifically — not a dictionary entry]

  • εὔφημος euphemos · are of good report · Strong's G2163

    Euphemos means 'of good reputation' or 'well spoken of' — things or persons spoken of with praise and approval. The word gives us the English 'euphemism,' though the NT meaning is straightforwardly positive: those things that have genuine good repute, that are rightly praised and honored.
