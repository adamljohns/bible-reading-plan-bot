    From G2095 (eu, well) and G1096 (ginomai, to become). Literally 'well-born.' Used both literally (of nobility) and figuratively (noble character, open-mindedness).

  • δέχομαι dechomai · received · Strong's G1209

    A Greek verb meaning to receive, accept, take, welcome. Unlike lambanō (which can mean 'to seize'), dechomai emphasizes deliberate, willing reception — actively welcoming what is offered.
