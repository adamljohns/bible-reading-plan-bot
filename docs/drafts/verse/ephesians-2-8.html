4. Confessional Anchor

This effectual call is of God's free and special grace alone, not from anything at all foreseen in man, nor from any power or agency in the creature. Man is wholly passive in it, being dead in sins and trespasses, until he is brought to life and renewed by the Holy Spirit; thereby he is enabled to answer this call and to embrace the grace offered and conveyed in it, and that by no less power than that which raised up Christ from the dead.
