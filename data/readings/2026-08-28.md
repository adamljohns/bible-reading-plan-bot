🸻 Context Summary
Solomon addresses the covenant people of Israel with stark contrasts between the conduct of the wicked and the character of the righteous. He emphasizes that true righteousness is not merely external compliance but an internal integrity that flows from the fear of the Lord. The passage warns against the seductive lure of unjust gain and the inevitable collapse of those who pursue it. It establishes that a man’s integrity in small matters determines his standing before God and his effectiveness in leadership.

🦅 This Day in American History — August 28
On August 28, 1565, the Spanish admiral Pedro Menendez sighted the Florida shore on the feast day of Saint Augustine and gave the place that name, soon planting there the settlement that endures as the oldest European city in the land. On the same day in 1830, Peter Cooper's little engine Tom Thumb proved on the rails of the Baltimore and Ohio that American steam could pull the freight of a continent, opening the railroad age. A Steadfast citizen lays foundations meant to outlast him as that first settlement did and presses forward the honest work of building as Cooper did, trusting the God who blesses patient labor to raise enduring things from small beginnings.
