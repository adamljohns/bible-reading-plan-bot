I love those who love me, and those who seek me diligently will find me. Riches and honor are with me, enduring wealth and righteousness. Solomon contrasts this divine Wisdom with fleeting worldly treasures, establishing it as the only foundation for a righteous life. The text underscores that God’s order is intentional, purposeful, and rooted in His eternal nature.

On May 8, 1846, General Zachary Taylor's outnumbered force stood firm on the prairie at Palo Alto and turned back a larger Mexican army by the speed of its mobile artillery, opening the Mexican-American War.
