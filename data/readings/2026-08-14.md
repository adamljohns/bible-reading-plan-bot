🧭 Context Summary
Solomon, the wisest man who ever lived, contrasts the eternal stability found in the fear of the LORD with the inevitable collapse of a life built on folly. He addresses the head of the household directly, reminding him that true strength is not found in physical might or political power, but in the disciplined application of divine truth. The passage warns that a man who ignores correction or embraces the seductive lies of the world will find his own house destroyed by the very hands he trusted to build it.
