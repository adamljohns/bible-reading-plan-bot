And four great beasts came up from the sea, each different from the other.
The first was like a lion, and had eagle’s wings. I watched till its wings were plucked off; and it was lifted up from the earth and made to stand on two feet like a man, and a man’s heart was given to it.
“And suddenly another beast, a second, like a bear. It was raised up on one side, and had three ribs in its mouth between its teeth. And they said thus to it: ‘Arise, devour much flesh!’

“I was watching in the night visions, And behold, One like the Son of Man, Coming with the clouds of heaven! He came to the Ancient of Days, And they brought Him near before Him.

I came near to one of those who stood by, and asked him the truth of all this. So he told me and made known to me the interpretation of these things:

🧭 Context Summary
Daniel receives a vision of four great beasts rising from the sea, representing successive earthly empires characterized by pride and violence. Amidst this turmoil, the court of heaven is convened, and the Ancient of Days takes His seat to execute judgment. The Son of Man is then given eternal authority, contrasting the temporary nature of human power with the everlasting kingdom of God. This passage assures the faithful that their ultimate King reigns supreme over all earthly rulers.

On June 30, 1775, the Continental Congress adopted the first Articles of War, binding Washington’s new army to discipline and lawful order, so that liberty would be defended by men under authority rather than by a mob. In that day there will be an altar to the LORD in the midst of the land of Egypt, and a pillar to the LORD at its border.
And it will be for a sign and for a witness to the LORD of hosts in the land of Egypt; for they will cry to the LORD because of the oppressors, and He will send them a Savior and a Mighty One, and He will deliver them.
Then the LORD will be known to Egypt, and the Egyptians will know the LORD in that day, and will make sacrifice and offering; yes, they will make a vow to the LORD and perform it.
And the LORD will strike Egypt, He will strike and heal it; they will return to the LORD, and He will be entreated by them and heal them.
In that day there will be a highway from Egypt to Assyria, and the Assyrian will come into Egypt and the Egyptian into Assyria, and the Egyptians will serve with the Assyrians.
In that day Israel will be one of three with Egypt and Assyria—a blessing in the midst of the land,
whom the LORD of hosts shall bless, saying, “Blessed is Egypt My people, and Assyria the work of My hands, and Israel My inheritance.”

🌾 Reflection for a Man at Home and in Community

As a HAPPY husband, you find your strength not in your own ability to control the chaotic winds of culture, but in the sovereign stability of your God. The text reminds you that the Lord rides on a swift cloud, commanding the very forces that seem to threaten your household. When the world around you stirs up confusion or when the idols of this age demand your allegiance, remember that your God is not distant. He is actively involved in the affairs of men, and His counsel stands firm against the shifting tides of public opinion. Your peace is anchored in the truth that He alone is the Lord of hosts, and no storm can arise without His permission and ultimate purpose.

As a FULFILLED father, you are called to lead your family with the same resolute trust that Pharaoh’s counselors lacked. The passage warns that human wisdom, when divorced from the fear of the Lord, becomes foolishness and leads to despair. Do not let the anxiety of the age infect your home. Instead, teach your children that the Lord strikes and heals, that His discipline is a sign of His covenant love. You are to be a pillar of clarity in your household, pointing your family away from the vain attempts to find security in worldly power and toward the only true refuge. Your authority is not for domination, but for sheltering your kin under the wings of the Almighty.

As a RESOLUTE citizen, you recognize that your ultimate allegiance is to the Kingdom of Heaven, even as you serve your nation with integrity. The prophecy speaks of a future where God blesses Egypt and Assyria, nations once hostile to His people, showing that His mercy extends beyond tribal boundaries. This should humble your heart and broaden your view. You do not fight for the supremacy of your own tribe or ideology, but you stand firm for the truth of God’s Word in the public square. You are a citizen of the city above, yet you fulfill your duties here with diligence and courage, knowing that your inheritance is secure in Christ, regardless of the political winds that blow.

🙏 Prayer from the Stateroom

Father, I thank You for Your sovereign rule over all nations and the affairs of men. Grant me the grace to stand firm in Your truth when the world around me stumbles into foolishness. By the power of Your Holy Spirit, strengthen me to lead my home with wisdom and courage, and to live as faithful witnesses in a land that often forgets You. Keep my hearts anchored in Your covenant love, and Give me the wisdom to distinguish between Your eternal will and the fleeting opinions of the age. In the name of Jesus Christ, my Lord and Commander, I pray. Amen.
