🦅 This Day in American History — July 4
On July 4, 1776, the Continental Congress adopted the Declaration of Independence at Philadelphia, pledging lives, fortunes, and sacred honor upon the truth that men are endowed by their Creator with unalienable rights. By the remarkable providence of God, on the same day fifty years later in 1826, John Adams and Thomas Jefferson, the document's great advocate and its author, both passed from this life within hours of each other, Jefferson at Monticello and Adams at Quincy, his last thoughts upon his old friend. That two founders should be summoned home on the jubilee of their labor reminds a Steadfast citizen that a man finishes his course and lays it down, but the cause he serves in faith endures long after him under the hand of the God who raised it up.
