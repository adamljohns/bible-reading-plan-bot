🦅 This Day in American History — July 13
On July 13, 1787, the Confederation Congress passed the Northwest Ordinance, the great charter of the frontier that opened an orderly road to statehood, secured the liberties of settlers, forbade slavery in the territory, and set aside land for schools because, as it declared, religion, morality, and knowledge are necessary to good government. On the same day in 1832, the explorer Henry Schoolcraft pressed through lakes and portages to stand at last at Lake Itasca, the true headwaters of the Mississippi he had long sought. The one laid a wise foundation for new commonwealths and the other reached the end of a long search, and a Steadfast citizen labors in the same spirit, building for generations he will not see and pressing on until the appointed work is finished, trusting the God who blesses faithful perseverance.
