🌅 0600 Morning Wisdom
The final chapter of Scripture calls the faithful soldier to hold fast to the eternal reality of the New Jerusalem.

📖 Scripture — Revelation 22
And he showed me a pure river of water of life, clear as crystal, proceeding from the throne of God and of the Lamb. In the middle of its street, and on either side of the river, was the tree of life, which bore twelve fruits, each tree yielding its fruit every month. The leaves of the tree were for the healing of the nations. And there shall be no more curse, but the throne of God and of the Lamb shall be in it, and His servants shall serve Him. They shall see His face, and His name shall be on their foreheads. There shall be no night there: They need no lamp nor light of the sun, for the Lord God gives them light. And they shall reign forever and ever. Then he said to me, “These words are faithful and true.” And the Lord God of the holy prophets sent His angel to show His servants the things which must shortly take place. “Behold, I am coming quickly! Blessed is he who keeps the words of the prophecy of this book.” Now I, John, saw and heard these things. And when I heard and saw, I fell down to worship before the feet of the angel who showed me these things. Then he said to me, “See that you do not do that. For I am your fellow servant, and of your brethren the prophets, and of those who keep the words of this book. Worship God.” And he said to me, “Do not seal the words of the prophecy of this book, for the time is at hand. He who is unjust, let him be unjust still; he who is filthy, let him be filthy still; he who is righteous, let him be righteous still; he who is holy, let him be holy still.” “And behold, I am coming quickly, and My reward is with Me, to give to every one according to his work. I am the Alpha and the Omega, the Beginning and the End, the First and the Last.” Blessed are those who do His commandments, that they may have the right to the tree of life, and may enter through the gates into the city. But outside are dogs and sorcerers and sexually immoral and murderers and idolaters, and whoever loves and practices a lie. “I, Jesus, have sent My angel to testify to you these things in the churches. I am the Root and the Offspring of David, the Bright and Morning Star.” And the Spirit and the bride say, “Come!” And let him who hears say, “Come!” And let him who thirsts come. Whoever desires, let him take the water of life freely. For I testify to everyone who hears the words of the prophecy of this book: If anyone adds to these things, God will add to him the plagues that are written in this book; and if anyone takes away from the words of the book of this prophecy, God shall take away his part from the Book of Life, from the holy city, and from the things which are written in this book. He who testifies to these things says, “Surely I am coming quickly.” Amen. Even so, come, Lord Jesus! The grace of our Lord Jesus Christ be with you all. Amen.

⸻
🧭 Context Summary
This closing chapter serves as the eternal conclusion to the biblical narrative, contrasting the cursed state of the world with the blessed reality of the New Jerusalem. It depicts the unbroken fellowship between the Father and the Son, who share their throne and pour out the river of life for the healing of the nations. The passage emphasizes the absolute finality of the separation between the righteous who wash their robes and the wicked who remain outside the city gates. It issues a solemn charge to the church to guard the integrity of God’s Word and to live in eager expectation of the King’s return.

⸻
🛡️ Reflection for a REAL MAN — Engage Consistently
Consider the tree of life: twelve fruits, "each tree yielding its fruit every month." No off-season, no dry spell, no month skipped. That is the picture of the man God wants at the head of a house. Not the man who blazes for a week after a men's retreat and then goes dark for a season, but the husband and father who is there at the table on an ordinary Tuesday. Maria and the children do not need a hero in bursts. They need a man who keeps bearing fruit.

Then the angel says, "he who is righteous, let him be righteous still; he who is holy, let him be holy still." Mark the word still. Men are good at sprints; a Marine knows how to surge. What breaks a man is the long middle: the daily fight against lust when no one is watching, the evening he would rather scroll than talk with Boaz, the week he skips his brothers because he is tired. The man who is filthy still got there one skipped day at a time. The man who is holy still got there the same way, walking the other direction.

"Blessed is he who keeps the words of the prophecy of this book." Keeping is a daily verb. Three times in this chapter Christ says He is coming quickly, and the man He finds faithful will be the one found at his post on an ordinary day, not the one who meant to get serious later. Show up today with your wife, your sons, your daughter, and your brothers. Then show up tomorrow. Then the day after that.

⸻
⛏️ Personal Application — Engage Consistently
• Fix a time tonight, and the same time every night this week, to sit with Maria for fifteen minutes with your phone in another room and hear about her day.
• Give Boaz ten minutes of undivided attention today, and put the same ten minutes on tomorrow's schedule before you go to bed.
• Name the hour you are most often tempted toward lust or numbing out, and arrange today for a brother to check on you at that hour every day this week.

By the power of Your Holy Spirit, make me a man who shows up for my wife and children every day, not in bursts, and who leads my household in the truth of Your Word. Keep me steadfast in my duties as a husband, father, and servant of Christ until He returns. In Jesus' name, I pray. Amen.

🕖 0700 First Watch — The Husband's Post

Brother, man your post: hold fast to the supremacy of Christ, for He alone is the foundation of your home and the authority over your life.

📖 Scripture — Hebrews 1

God, who at various times and in various ways spoke in time past to the fathers by the prophets, has in these last days spoken to us by His Son, whom He has appointed heir of all things, through whom also He made the worlds; who being the brightness of His glory and the express image of His person, and upholding all things by the word of His power, when He had by Himself purged our sins, sat down at the right hand of the Majesty on high, having become so much better than the angels, as He has by inheritance obtained a more excellent name than they. For to which of the angels did He ever say: “You are My Son, Today I have begotten You”? And again: “I will be to Him a Father, And He shall be to Me a Son”? But when He again brings the firstborn into the world, He says: “Let all the angels of God worship Him.” And of the angels He says: “Who makes His angels spirits And His ministers a flame of fire.” But to the Son He says: “Your throne, O God, is forever and ever; A scepter of righteousness is the scepter of Your kingdom. You have loved righteousness and hated lawlessness; Therefore God, Your God, has anointed You With the oil of gladness more than Your companions.” And: “You, LORD, in the beginning laid the foundation of the earth, And the heavens are the work of Your hands. They will perish, but You remain; And they will all grow old like a garment; Like a cloak You will fold them up, And they will be changed. But You are the same, And Your years will not fail.” But to which of the angels has He ever said: “Sit at My right hand, Till I make Your enemies Your footstool”? Are they not all ministering spirits sent forth to minister for those who will inherit salvation?

⸻

🗺️ Briefing Summary

The author of Hebrews establishes the absolute supremacy of Christ over all creation and all spiritual powers, including the angels. This passage dismantles any notion that Christ is merely a created being or a lesser deity, affirming His full deity and eternal sovereignty. For the husband, this truth provides the unshakeable basis for his own authority, which is derived solely from his submission to this King.

⸻

❤️ Reflection for Your Wife — H.A.P.P.Y. Husband — Abiding

"They will perish, but You remain." The heavens grow old like a garment; the Son does not. Here is the one Person in the universe who never changes and never leaves: "You are the same, And Your years will not fail." A husband who means to love his wife faithfully for decades has to draw from that source, because his own reserves wear thin like an old cloak.

Abiding starts in Christ before it ever reaches Maria. Jesus said the branch bears fruit only while it remains in the vine (John 15). If you are not in the Word and not praying, running on your own fumes, you will come home with nothing to give her but fatigue and short answers. God "has in these last days spoken to us by His Son." The husband who listens to the Son every morning has something real to carry home.

Then abide with her. Bodies age, feelings ebb, and seasons of marriage are folded up and changed. But a covenant husband remains. You do not drift off into work or the phone; you do not withdraw into silence after an argument; you stay present. Open the Word with Maria and let the Son's voice be the steady voice in your marriage. Your constancy toward her is a small reflection of the One whose years will not fail.

⸻

⛏️ Personal Application — Abiding

• Before you say much to Maria this morning, spend unhurried time alone in Hebrews 1, so you come to her having heard from Christ first.
• Read a short passage with Maria tonight and pray together, even briefly, and make it the habit of the week rather than the exception.
• If tension from a recent disagreement is still sitting between you, do not wait it out in silence; go back to her today and stay in the conversation until it is settled.

🙏 Prayer

Father, I thank You for the Son, whom You appointed heir of all things and through whom You made the worlds. Grant me the grace to lead my home with the humility of one who serves the King of kings. By the power of Your Holy Spirit, keep me abiding in Christ so that I have something to give Maria, and keep me present with her through every season, as faithful as the One who remains. Keep me faithful to the covenant duties You have assigned to me as a husband and father. In the name of Jesus Christ, I pray. Amen.

🕚 1100 Second Watch — The Father's Charge

Brother, take up the charge: hold fast to your covenant duty as you survey the day’s watch.

📖 Scripture — Hebrews 2

Therefore we must give the more earnest heed to the things we have heard, lest we drift away. For if the word spoken through angels proved steadfast, and every transgression and disobedience received a just reward, how shall we escape if we neglect so great a salvation, which at the first began to be spoken by the Lord, and was confirmed to us by those who heard Him, God also bearing witness both with signs and wonders, with various miracles, and gifts of the Holy Spirit, according to His own will? For He has not put the world to come, of which we speak, in subjection to angels. But one testified in a certain place, saying: “What is man that You are mindful of him, Or the son of man that You take care of him? You have made him a little lower than the angels; You have crowned him with glory and honor, And set him over the works of Your hands. You have put all things in subjection under his feet.” For in that He put all in subjection under him, He left nothing that is not put under him. But now we do not yet see all things put under him. But we see Jesus, who was made a little lower than the angels, for the suffering of death crowned with glory and honor, that He, by the grace of God, might taste death for everyone. For it was fitting for Him, for whom are all things and by whom are all things, in bringing many sons to glory, to make the captain of their salvation perfect through sufferings. For both He who sanctifies and those who are being sanctified are all of one, for which reason He is not ashamed to call them brethren, saying: “I will declare Your name to My brethren; In the midst of the assembly I will sing praise to You.” And again: “I will put My trust in Him.” And again: “Here am I and the children whom God has given Me.” Inasmuch then as the children have partaken of flesh and blood, He Himself likewise shared in the same, that through death He might destroy him who had the power of death, that is, the devil, and release those who through fear of death were all their lifetime subject to bondage. For indeed He does not give aid to angels, but He does give aid to the seed of Abraham. Therefore, in all things He had to be made like His brethren, that He might be a merciful and faithful High Priest in things pertaining to God, to make propitiation for the sins of the people. For in that He Himself has suffered, being tempted, He is able to aid those who are tempted.

⸻

🗺️ Field Notes

The text reminds us that the Son of God, though supreme, humbled Himself to become one of us, bridging the gap between the Creator and the creature. This great salvation is not a passive gift but requires our active, vigilant attention, lest we drift from the truth we have heard. Jesus, crowned with glory after suffering, now sanctifies His people, calling them brothers and sisters, thereby validating our identity as heirs of the covenant. We look to Him as the faithful High Priest who has tasted death for us, ensuring that our service to Him is grounded in His finished work.

⸻

👨‍👧 Reflection for Your Children — F.U.L.F.I.L.L.E.D. Father — Listening

Hebrews 2 opens with a command about hearing: "we must give the more earnest heed to the things we have heard, lest we drift away." Drift is what happens when the ears go dull. The salvation you hold "was confirmed to us by those who heard Him"; it came down to you through men who listened before they spoke. That is the order for a father too. Before you can hand the faith on to Gideon, Boaz, and Shiloh, you have to hear the Son yourself, and then you have to hear them. A father who only transmits and never receives loses his children by slow drift, not by one blowout. Earnest heed toward God and patient heed toward your children grow from the same root: the humility that assumes there is something worth hearing.

Look at how Christ came near: "in all things He had to be made like His brethren," so that He could aid those who are tempted. He did not shout orders from a distance; He entered the room. Listening is how a father enters the room. Gideon is twenty, a grown man, and what he needs from you now is less counsel and more a father who asks what he is wrestling with and then waits through the silence without reaching for a fix. Boaz at fifteen will tell you the truth only once he learns the first thing out of your mouth will not be a lecture; ask about his week, then ask the second question. Shiloh at five tells her whole heart in long, wandering stories; get down to her eye level and hear the whole thing. Christ says, "Here am I and the children whom God has given Me." Do not be too busy to hear the ones God has given you.

⸻

⛏️ Personal Application — Listening
• Call or sit down with Gideon this week and ask one open question about what he is carrying right now; hear his whole answer before you offer a single opinion.
• Take Boaz out for a burger, ask about the best and worst part of his week, and ask a follow-up question before you say anything that sounds like advice.
• At bedtime tonight, let Shiloh tell you a story about her day, and stay on the floor until she is finished.

🙏 Prayer

Father, I stand before You as Your servant, acknowledging that all authority comes from You. By the power of Your Holy Spirit, make me a man who gives earnest heed to Your Word first, and then gives patient heed to my children before I speak. Teach me to hear Gideon as a man, to hear Boaz without rushing to correct him, and to hear Shiloh without hurrying her, so that each of them knows their father listens and learns from that how gladly You hear them. Keep my heart aligned with Your Word, that I may never drift from the truth. Through Christ my Savior, I pray. Amen.

🕒 1500 Third Watch — The Citizen's Stand

Brother, stand firm: stand firm in the unshakable Kingdom while the earth around you trembles.

📖 Scripture — Hebrews 12:18-29

For you have not come to the mountain that may be touched and that burned with fire, and to blackness and darkness and tempest, and the sound of a trumpet and the voice of words, so that those who heard it begged that the word should not be spoken to them anymore. (For they could not endure what was commanded: “And if so much as a beast touches the mountain, it shall be stoned or shot with an arrow.” And so terrifying was the sight that Moses said, “I am exceedingly afraid and trembling.”) But you have come to Mount Zion and to the city of the living God, the heavenly Jerusalem, to an innumerable company of angels, to the general assembly and church of the firstborn who are registered in heaven, to God the Judge of all, to the spirits of just men made perfect, to Jesus the Mediator of the new covenant, and to the blood of sprinkling that speaks better things than that of Abel. See that you do not refuse Him who speaks. For if they did not escape who refused Him who spoke on earth, much more shall we not escape if we turn away from Him who speaks from heaven, whose voice then shook the earth; but now He has promised, saying, “Yet once more I shake not only the earth, but also heaven.” Now this, “Yet once more,” indicates the removal of those things that are being shaken, as of things that are made, that the things which cannot be shaken may remain. Therefore, since we are receiving a kingdom which cannot be shaken, let us have grace, by which we may serve God acceptably with reverence and godly fear. For our God is a consuming fire.

⸻

🛰️ Situation Report
The author of Hebrews contrasts the terrifying distance of the Old Covenant with the glorious nearness of the New, reminding the believer that his citizenship is anchored in a Kingdom that cannot be moved. This passage calls the faithful to recognize the supremacy of Christ’s blood over the old shadows and to hold fast to the unshakable reality of God’s eternal rule. As a soldier of the cross, you must distinguish between the temporary shaking of the world and the permanent stability of your Savior’s throne.

⸻

🛡️ Reflection for a R.E.S.O.L.U.T.E. Citizen — Enduring

Fredericksburg (local)
Israel at Sinai "could not endure what was commanded," and they begged for the voice to stop. You have come to something better: "the city of the living God," a kingdom "which cannot be shaken." That changes how you build in the city where God has put you. Fredericksburg has been shelled, occupied, and rebuilt, and the old steeples downtown have outlived every council that ever met on Princess Anne Street. The Enduring citizen in this city thinks past the next election. He gives his years to things that will still be here when he is gone: Spotswood Baptist as a church in the city, a youth league, a pregnancy center, a men's table through USMC Ministries. Things that are made will be shaken and removed. Put your labor into what remains.

Virginia (state)
Virginia's General Assembly traces its line to 1619, the oldest continuous lawmaking body in this hemisphere. It has outlasted a king, a civil war, and every man who ever sat in it. That kind of continuity is not an accident; it is built by men who take up a long work and hand it on. The text warns that God will shake "not only the earth, but also heaven," removing what is temporary "that the things which cannot be shaken may remain." The Enduring citizen in the Commonwealth does not pour all his civic energy into one session's fight and then quit in disgust. He learns how Richmond works, gets to know his delegate and state senator over years rather than through one angry email, and keeps at it through losing sessions as well as winning ones.

United States (nation)
The nation turns two hundred and fifty this year, and the temptation is to treat every national season as the last one. Hebrews takes a longer view. The things "that are made" will be removed; one kingdom will remain, and you are "receiving" it now. That frees you to work for the country without panic. Vote in every election, not only the dramatic ones. Serve when the jury summons comes. Raise up younger men who will still be voting, serving, and praying for this country after you are gone. An enduring citizen plants trees whose shade he will never sit under, and he does it "with reverence and godly fear," because the God who outlasts every nation is watching how he builds.

⸻

⛏️ Personal Application — Enduring

• Find out this week what long-term work Spotswood Baptist is carrying in Fredericksburg, a ministry, a mission partner, a building need, and commit to back it for the whole coming year rather than with one gift.
• Look up your Virginia delegate and state senator, and send each a short note introducing yourself as a constituent they will be hearing from for years, not once.
• Ask one younger man in your circle to come with you to the next Fredericksburg city council meeting, so the habit of showing up outlasts you.

🙏 Prayer

Father, I thank You that my citizenship is not of this earth, but of the heavenly Jerusalem. Forgive me for the times I have sought stability in shifting sands rather than in Your unshakable Kingdom. Grant me the grace to worship You with reverence and godly fear, and to trust in the better blood of Jesus. By the power of Your Holy Spirit, give me patience to build for the long haul in Fredericksburg and in Virginia, laboring at things that will outlast me and handing the work on to the men who come after. When I grow weary of slow work, remind me that I am receiving a kingdom which cannot be shaken. In the name of the risen Lord Jesus, I pray. Amen.

🦅 This Day in American History — December 22
On December 22, 1775, the Continental Congress appointed Esek Hopkins to command the fledgling Continental Navy, sending the new nation's first ships to sea against the mightiest fleet on earth. On the same day in 1944, General Anthony McAuliffe and his men, encircled and outnumbered at Bastogne, met the enemy's call for surrender with the immortal reply of 'Nuts!' and held their ground until relief broke through. An Enduring citizen remembers that a navy and a free people are both built to outlast the men who start them, and he trusts the God who armed a young Republic against an empire to strengthen those who keep building for generations not yet born.

⸻

🌾 Reflection for a Man at Home and in Community

As a HAPPY husband, brother, you find your deepest satisfaction in the fact that the Lord who reigns over all nations is the same Lord who has covenanted with you and your wife. This Psalm reminds us that our hope is not in earthly powers, political schemes, or the fragile strength of men, but in the God of Jacob. Your headship is not a burden of self-reliance but a privilege of leading your household toward the Only One worthy of ultimate trust. When you lay down your armor at the door, remember that the true victory is already won by Christ, and your duty is to walk in that peace, providing for your family not just materially but spiritually, pointing them constantly to the King who does not fail.

As a FULFILLED father, you see in the text a tender image of the Creator who lifts the lowly and seats them with princes. Your role is to model this dependence on the LORD for your children, teaching them that their security does not rest in their own abilities or the shifting tides of culture. You are to be a man of action and prayer, showing your sons that godly strength is exercised in humility before God’s throne. By the power of the Holy Spirit, let your home be a place where the fatherless and the weak are upheld, reflecting the heart of the Father who cares for His children, so that your legacy is one of faithfulness and reverence for the Sovereign.

As a RESOLUTE citizen, you hold fast, knowing that the LORD will reign forever, while every earthly kingdom eventually passes away. This truth liberates you from the anxiety of controlling outcomes and empowers you to serve your community with integrity and courage. You do not fight for a temporal utopia but for the right order of a society that acknowledges the rule of God. Your service is an act of worship, conducted with the discipline of a soldier and the grace of a saint, trusting that the Lord protects the stranger and upholds the cause of the just, even when the world around you seems to reject Him.
