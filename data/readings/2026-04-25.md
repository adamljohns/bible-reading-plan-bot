🧭 Context Summary
These proverbs, originally collected by the men of Hezekiah, reveal that true sovereignty resides not in human might but in the absolute rule of God over the hearts of kings. The text juxtaposes the reliability of God’s providence with the foolishness of empty boasting and the diligence of the ant against the sloth of the sluggard. It establishes that a man’s domestic life is a primary theater of divine favor, where the covenant marriage serves as a blessing to be guarded and cherished. The passage calls the believer to align his personal conduct with the hidden purposes of God, recognizing that wisdom is found in submission to His order.

Your strength is to be stewarded, not squandered in the company of the ruthless or the idle. Guard your heart, brother, for out of it flow the issues of life for your family and your unit.

By governing your heart to love her with the love of Christ, you demonstrate to your children the reality of the Gospel. A man who rules his own house well, with a wife who respects him, lays a foundation of peace that stands against the storms of the world.
