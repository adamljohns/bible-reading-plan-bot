🦅 This Day in American History — May 19
On May 19, 1643, the Puritan colonies of Massachusetts Bay, Plymouth, Connecticut, and New Haven joined in the New England Confederation, the first union of English colonies on these shores, knit together for the defense of their people and the support of the church. On the same day in 1780, a mysterious darkness fell over New England at noon, and as many feared the end of the world had come, the Connecticut legislator Abraham Davenport answered the call to adjourn by saying that if it were indeed the Day of Judgment he chose to be found doing his duty.
