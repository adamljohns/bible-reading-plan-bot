🧭 Context Summary

The psalmist, a servant of the covenant, acknowledges that the LORD’s judgments are perfectly righteous and upright in their execution. This passage serves as a reminder that divine discipline is a sign of God’s faithful covenant love, not His rejection.

🗺️ Briefing Summary
Jeremiah confronts a people who have confused ritual observance with genuine righteousness, warning them that empty worship offers no protection against judgment.
