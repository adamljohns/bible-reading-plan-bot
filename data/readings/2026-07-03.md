🦅 This Day in American History — July 3
On July 3, 1863, the third day at Gettysburg ended in Pickett's Charge, when fifteen thousand Confederate soldiers crossed nearly a mile of open ground into Union fire and were broken at the stone wall, the high-water mark of the rebellion and the turning of the war.
