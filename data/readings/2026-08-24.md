🦅 This Day in American History — August 24
On August 24, 1587, the infant Virginia Dare was baptized into the church on Roanoke Island, among the first Christian sacraments offered up on the soil of this land, a seed of faith planted in a wilderness that would one day bear a Christian nation. On the same day in 1814, British soldiers put the White House and the Capitol to the torch, and only a sudden storm out of heaven kept the ruin from being total; the buildings rose again and the Republic endured. A Steadfast citizen is neither lifted up by the hope of his beginnings nor cast down by the fire of his trials, but stands fast through ashes and rebuilding alike, knowing the God who waters the first seed also sends the rain that saves His people.
