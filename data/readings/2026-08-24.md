🧭 Context Summary
Solomon concludes his instructions on practical wisdom by warning against the allure of the wicked and urging steadfast reliance on the LORD. He contrasts the secure, prosperous life of the wise with the sudden, violent destruction awaiting the arrogant and the lazy. The passage anchors the believer’s security not in circumstances, but in the sovereign care and discipline of the Father.

Stand firm, brother, for the LORD redeems the soul of His servants.
