🦅 This Day in American History — December 17
On December 17, 1777, the Comte de Vergennes informed the American envoys that France would recognize the independence of the United States, an alliance won by the blood of Saratoga that would prove decisive in the long fight for liberty. On the same day in 1903, two bicycle makers from Ohio, Orville and Wilbur Wright, lifted their flimsy machine off the sands at Kitty Hawk and held it aloft under a man's control, opening the age of flight by patient labor and dauntless trial. A Steadfast citizen perseveres as the Wrights did through every failure, and waits as the patriots did for help in God's appointed hour, confident that diligence and faith are never finally disappointed.
