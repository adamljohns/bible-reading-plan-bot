🧭 Context Summary

Asaph reflects on the constructive nature of divine discipline, recognizing that the Lord’s chastening was a merciful intervention to redirect his path. This passage serves as a testament to the transformative power of Scripture in the life of a believer who submits to God’s authority.

🦅 This Day in American History — June 9
On June 9, 1772, a band of Rhode Island men rowed out under cover of darkness and burned the British revenue schooner Gaspee where she lay aground in Narragansett Bay, standing firm against a Crown they judged to have overreached its lawful bounds. On the same day in 1732, King George II's privy council finalized the charter for Georgia, founding the last of the thirteen colonies under James Oglethorpe as a refuge for the worthy poor. A Steadfast citizen holds his ground against injustice as those Narragansett men did, and labors patiently to build something lasting as Oglethorpe did on that southern frontier, trusting the providence of God to establish the work of faithful hands.
