🌅 0600 Morning Wisdom

The morning light reveals the depth of our need for discernment in a world that prizes haste over holiness.

📖 Scripture — Proverbs 20

Wine is a mocker, strong drink a brawler, and whoever is led astray by it is not wise. Trust in the LORD with all your heart, and do not lean on your own understanding. In all your ways acknowledge Him, and He will make straight your paths. Do not say, “I will pay back evil”; wait for the LORD, and He will deliver you. The king of Israel by justice establishes the land, but a bribed official ruins it. The lamp of the LORD searches out the whole house of the wicked. The glory of young men is their strength, but the splendor of old men is their gray hair. If a ruler hears lies, all his officials become wicked.

⸻

🧭 Context Summary
Solomon, the wisest king to sit on the throne of Israel, turns his gaze from the royal court to the common soldier and laborer. He warns against the dulling influence of intoxicants that cloud the judgment required for righteous living. He then establishes the absolute sovereignty of God in directing the steps of those who seek His face. Finally, he contrasts the corruption of unjust leadership with the inherent dignity of age and experience.

⸻

🛡️ Reflection for a REAL MAN — Trusts the LORD

Brother, you stand in the breach daily, tasked with protecting your household and providing for their physical needs. The world around you screams for immediate solutions, for shortcuts to success, and for the validation of your own instincts. Yet Scripture commands you to reject the false comfort of wine and the arrogance of self-reliance. True manhood is not found in the strength of your arm, but in the humility of your spirit as you bow before the Sovereign.

You must recognize that your understanding is finite and often flawed by sin. When you lean on your own wisdom, you build on shifting sand. But when you acknowledge the LORD in every decision—whether in the boardroom or the nursery—He promises to direct your path. This is not a passive waiting, but an active, disciplined reliance on His covenant faithfulness. He is the One who searches the depths of your heart, knowing your motives even when you do not.

Do not seek revenge or try to control the outcomes of your interactions. The Lord is the Judge. Your duty is to walk in integrity, letting His wisdom guide your strength. As you age, let your gray hair become a crown of glory, not by accumulating wealth, but by accumulating a reputation for fearing the LORD. Lead your family with justice, not because you are perfect, but because you submit to the Perfect One.

⸏ Personal Application — Trusts the LORD

• Identify one area of your life where you have been leaning on your own understanding and deliberately pray for guidance before acting.
• Establish a daily rhythm of acknowledging the LORD in your work, stopping to offer brief prayers of dependence throughout the day.
• Refuse to engage in gossip or complaints about leadership, trusting that God is sovereign over the positions others hold.
• Invest time in mentoring younger men in your church, sharing how reliance on God has strengthened your own faith.

Brother, stand firm in the covenant of your household, knowing that your authority is derived solely from the King of Kings.

Rehoboam went to Jerusalem and built up the cities of Judah for defense. He strengthened their garrisons and put commanders in them, with supplies of food, oil, and wine. He put shields and spears in all the cities and made them very strong. So Judah and Benjamin remained loyal.

The priests and the Levites who were in all Israel presented themselves to him from all their places of residence. For the Levites left their suburbs and their possession and came to Judah and Jerusalem, because Jeroboam and his sons had cast them off from serving as priests to the LORD. And he appointed for himself priests for the high places and for the demons and for the calves that he had made. After this, from all the tribes of Israel, those who set their hearts to seek the LORD the God of Israel came to Jerusalem to sacrifice to the LORD the God of their fathers. So they strengthened the kingdom of Judah and made Rehoboam the son of Solomon strong, for three years they walked in the way of David and Solomon, for they sought the LORD the God of their fathers.

Rehoboam took to himself wives: Mahalath the daughter of Jerimoth the son of David, and Abihail the daughter of Eliab the son of Jesse. And he begot sons: Junius, Nejah, and Shamma; these were the sons of Abihail. And to Mahalath he begot sons: Jehiel, Zelmoth, and Zebadiah. ⸻

🗺️ Briefing Summary
King Rehoboam secures the physical and spiritual foundations of his realm by reinforcing the fortifications of Judah and Jerusalem. He gathers the faithful priests and Levites who have been rejected by the northern kingdom, thereby restoring proper worship and consolidating the loyalty of the southern tribes. Despite his initial success in strengthening the kingdom through military and religious means, Rehoboam eventually compromises his spiritual integrity by taking many wives and concubines, prioritizing political alliances and personal desire over the singular devotion required by the Law.

⸻

❤️ Reflection for Your Wife — H.A.P.P.Y. Husband — Providing

Brother, consider the diligence Rehoboam displayed in strengthening the cities of Judah. He did not merely rely on the walls built by his father; he actively reinforced the garrisons and stocked them with essential supplies. This is the posture of a godly husband toward his wife, Maria. You are called to be the active provider and protector of your household, ensuring that the physical and spiritual needs of your family are met with intentionality and strength. Your provision is not a passive allowance but a fortified defense against the hardships of this fallen world.

Just as Rehoboam gathered the priests who had remained faithful to the LORD, you must gather and nurture the spiritual life of your home. You provide the environment where faith is cultivated, where the Word is taught, and where worship is established. Your authority is exercised in the service of this spiritual provision, leading your wife and children to seek the LORD their God. You do not rule as a tyrant, but as a steward who secures the territory of your family’s soul, ensuring that no enemy of righteousness gains a foothold in your domestic sphere.

Yet, brother, beware the subtle trap of Rehoboam’s later years. He sought to strengthen his kingdom through the sheer number of his wives and concubines, diluting his focus and compromising his covenant. In your marriage to Maria, do not seek to multiply your comforts or your alliances at the expense of your singular devotion. True strength is found not in the accumulation of resources or relationships, but in the faithful, exclusive adherence to the covenant you have made before God. Protect the purity of your union, for it is the primary fortification of your lineage.

⸏ Personal Application — Providing

• Conduct a weekly inventory of the physical and spiritual resources in your home, ensuring that food, finances, and scripture are prioritized and accessible.
• Establish a regular time for teaching and prayer with Maria, reinforcing the spiritual garrison of your marriage through consistent engagement with the Word.
• Identify one area of physical protection or provision in your household that requires immediate reinforcement and take concrete steps to secure it this week.
• Audit your heart for any desire to seek validation or comfort outside of your covenant marriage, and confess this idolatry to God immediately.

🕚 1100 Second Watch — The Father's Charge

The Lord issues a solemn warning to His servants, reminding us that obedience to His specific command is the only path to true life and safety.

📖 Scripture — 1 Kings 13:1-34

At that time a man of God came from Judah to Bethel by the word of the LORD. Jeroboam was standing by the altar to make offerings. He cried out against the altar by the word of the LORD and said, “O altar, altar, thus says the LORD: Behold, a son shall be born to the house of David, Josiah by name, and he shall sacrifice on you the priests of the high places who make offerings on you, and human bones shall be burned on you.” On that day the man of God gave a sign, saying, “This is the sign that the LORD has enacted: Behold, the altar shall be torn apart, and the ash that is on it shall be poured out.” When the king heard the saying of the man of God, which he cried out against the altar at Bethel, Jeroboam stretched out his hand from the altar, saying, “Seize him.” But the hand that he stretched out against him dried up, so that he could not pull it back. For so it was commanded me by the word of the LORD, saying, ‘You shall eat no bread, nor drink water, nor turn back by the way that you came.’” So he went another way and did not return by the way that he came to Bethel. And he cried to the man of God who came from Judah, “Thus says the LORD: Because you have disobeyed the mouth of the LORD and have not kept the commandment that the LORD your God commanded you, but came back and ate bread and drank water in the place of which he said to you, ‘Eat no bread or drink water,’ your body shall not come to the tomb of your fathers.” And it happened, when he had eaten bread and drunk, that he saddled the donkey for him, the prophet who had brought him back. Then he went and found his body thrown down by the way, and the donkey and the lion standing by the body. The lion had not eaten the body nor torn the donkey. And the prophet took up the body of the man of God and laid it on the donkey and brought it back to the city to mourn and lament over him. Then he laid the body in his own tomb, and they mourned over him, saying, “Alas, my brother!” And after he had mourned over him, he said to his sons, “When I die, bury me in the grave where the man of God is buried. Also Jeroboam did not turn from his evil way, but made priests from the lowest of the people for the high places. Whoever wished, he consecrated him, and they became priests of the high places. And this thing was a sin to the house of Jeroboam, so as to cut it off and to destroy it from off the face of the earth.

🗺️ Field Notes
This passage contrasts the rigid obedience of the unnamed prophet of Judah with the tragic compromise of the old prophet in Bethel. The man of God faced immediate danger from King Jeroboam but remained steadfast because he honored the specific word he received. The old prophet, however, allowed his own desires to override the clear command of the Lord, leading to a fatal deception. This account serves as a sobering reminder that even religious leaders must submit wholly to the authority of Scripture without adding to or subtracting from it.

Teach them that God is not to be mocked, and that His commands are for their good and His glory.
