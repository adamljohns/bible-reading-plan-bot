🦅 This Day in American History — July 9
On July 9, 1776, General George Washington had the newly arrived Declaration of Independence read to his Continental Army on the parade grounds of Lower Manhattan, that the men facing a mighty enemy might know the great cause for which they would fight and perhaps die. On the same day in 1850, President Zachary Taylor, the old soldier whom the nation had lately raised to its highest office, was taken by sudden illness, and the Republic submitted to the loss as Millard Fillmore quietly took up the office. The one steeled men for a long fight and the other bowed before the providence that numbers a man's days, and a Steadfast citizen learns to stand firm in the cause and to trust the God who holds both the soldier's courage and the leader's appointed end.

For the black cummin is not threshed with a threshing sledge, Nor is a cartwheel rolled over the cummin; But the black cummin is beaten out with a stick, And the cummin with a rod.
Bread flour must be ground; Therefore he does not thresh it forever, Break it with his cartwheel, Or crush it with his horsemen.
This also comes from the LORD of hosts, Who is wonderful in counsel and excellent in guidance.

🌾 Reflection for a Man at Home and in Community

As a HAPPY husband, brother, you must recognize that the world offers many fragile refuges, from wealth to status, but only the Lord is the tried stone upon which your household can stand secure. Your wife looks to you not for the shifting sands of cultural trends, but for the steadfastness of a man who has anchored his soul in the sure foundation of Christ. You provide true safety not by shielding her from all trouble, but by leading her with the courage of one who knows that the Lord Himself is her shield and her song. Let your home be a place where the truth of His word is the only refuge you acknowledge, teaching your family to trust in His promises rather than in the fleeting comforts of this age.

As a FULFILLED father, you bear the solemn duty of instructing your children in the ways of the Lord, for they too must learn to stand firm when the storms of life rage against them. You are called to be a man of righteousness and salvation, modeling for your sons the discipline of a soldier and the wisdom of a sage. Do not let them rely on the lies of this world for their security, but point them daily to the precious cornerstone who alone saves. Your instruction must be clear and direct, stripping away the thorns of confusion so that they may see the clear path of obedience, knowing that their father’s love is rooted in the fear of the Lord and the desire for their eternal good.

As a RESOLUTE citizen, you understand that your primary allegiance is to the Kingdom of Heaven, and you must live with the gravity of one who awaits the day of the Lord’s vengeance and justice. The world may mock your convictions or seek to undermine your authority, but you stand firm on the rock of Scripture, unshaken by the political winds of the hour. You serve your nation with integrity and courage, not as a partisan, but as a man of God who knows that all authority is ordained by Him. Let your life be a testimony of sober faithfulness, demonstrating that a true man finds his strength in submission to God’s law and his peace in the assurance of His sovereign grace.
