🌅 Morning Wisdom
📖 Scripture — Revelation 4
After this I looked and saw a doorway open in heaven, and the first voice—clear and commanding like a
trumpet—said, “Come up here; I will show you what must take place.” In that moment I was in the
Spirit, and a throne stood in heaven with One seated upon it. The One seated had a brilliance like jasper
and carnelian, and around the throne was a rainbow with an emerald glow.
Encircling the throne were twenty-four thrones, and on them twenty-four elders, clothed in white and
crowned with gold. From the throne came flashes of lightning, rumblings, and peals of thunder. Seven
blazing torches were before the throne—these are the sevenfold Spirit of God. Before it was something
like a sea of glass, clear as crystal.
Around the throne, on each side, were four living creatures full of eyes, front and back: one like a lion,
one like an ox, one with a human face, and one like a flying eagle. Each had six wings; all around and
within they were covered with eyes. Day and night they never cease saying, “Holy, holy, holy is the
Lord God, the Almighty—the One who was, who is, and who is to come.”
Whenever the living creatures give glory, honor, and thanks to Him who sits on the throne, the twentyfour elders fall down before Him, worship the One who lives forever, and lay their crowns before the
throne, saying, “Worthy are You, our Lord and God, to receive glory and honor and power, because You
created all things; through Your will they existed and were created.”
🧭 Context Summary:
John is lifted into the control room of the universe. The vision centers on God’s sovereign throne,
uncreated holiness, and worthy rule. All created power—elders, crowns, and living beings—bows to the
Creator whose will sustains everything.
💪 A REAL MAN — LEADING COURAGEOUSLY
When heaven opens, leadership begins with worship. LEADING courageously means taking your
bearings from the throne, not from the noise. Set the tone at home by honoring God first, ordering your
day under His authority, and laying your “crowns” (status, preferences, pride) before Him. Courage isn’t
bluster; it’s steady obedience because the Almighty is worthy.
🙏 Prayer
Father, enthroned above every power, fix my eyes on Your holiness and worth.
By the power of Your Holy Spirit, make my leadership calm, clean, and courageous—shaped by
worship, not worry.
I ask this in the name of Jesus Christ, my Lord and Commander. Amen.

📖 Scripture — Genesis 7
The LORD said to Noah, “Enter the ark—you and your whole household—for in this generation I have
seen you as righteous before Me. Take seven pairs of every clean animal, a male with its mate, and one
pair of every animal that is not clean, a male with its mate; also seven pairs of the birds of the heavens,
male and female, to keep their offspring alive on the earth. In seven days I will send rain on the earth for
forty days and forty nights, and I will wipe out from the ground every living thing that I have made.”
Noah did everything the LORD commanded him.
Noah was six hundred years old when the floodwaters came on the earth. Noah, his sons, his wife, and
his sons’ wives went with him into the ark because of the waters of the flood. Of the clean animals and
of the animals that are not clean, of the birds and of everything that creeps on the ground—two by two,
male and female—went in to Noah into the ark, just as God had commanded him. After seven days, the
waters of the flood were on the earth. In the six-hundredth year of Noah’s life, in the second month, on
the seventeenth day of the month, on that very day all the fountains of the great deep burst open, and the
windows of the heavens were opened. The rain fell on the earth forty days and forty nights. On that same
day Noah and his sons—Shem, Ham, and Japheth—Noah’s wife, and the three wives of his sons with
them, entered the ark, they and every kind of animal according to its kind, every creeping thing that
creeps on the earth according to its kind, and every bird according to its kind—every winged creature.
They went into the ark to Noah, two by two of all flesh in which there was the breath of life. Those that
entered, male and female of all flesh, went in as God had commanded him. And the LORD shut him in.
The flood continued forty days on the earth; the waters increased and lifted up the ark so that it rose high
above the earth. The waters prevailed and increased greatly on the earth, and the ark moved on the
surface of the waters. The waters prevailed exceedingly on the earth so that all the high mountains under
the whole heaven were covered. The waters rose upward—fifteen cubits or so—and the mountains were
covered.
All flesh that moved on the earth perished: birds, livestock, wild animals, all swarming creatures that
swarm on the earth, and all mankind. Everything on dry land in whose nostrils was the breath of life
died. He blotted out every living thing that was on the face of the ground—man and beast and creeping
thing and birds of the heavens. Only Noah was left, and those who were with him in the ark. And the
waters prevailed on the earth for one hundred fifty days.

📅 Sunday, January 4 — 1100
⏱ Second Watch — The Father’s Post
📖 Scripture — Genesis 8:1–19
God turned His attention to Noah and to every creature with him. A strong wind swept over the earth and
the waters kept dropping—the underground springs were shut, the rain stopped, and the flood steadily
retreated. In the seventh month the ark settled upon the mountains of Ararat, and by the tenth month the
mountain tops came into view.
After forty days Noah opened the window of the ark. He released a raven that kept flying back and forth
until the land was dry. He sent out a dove to see whether the water had receded, but finding no place to
land she returned; Noah reached out and brought her back. Seven days later he sent the dove again, and
she returned that evening with a fresh olive leaf—Noah now knew the waters had gone down. Waiting
another seven days, he released her once more, and she did not return.
In Noah’s six-hundred-first year, on the first day of the first month, he removed the covering of the ark
and saw that the ground was drying; by the twenty-seventh day of the second month the earth was dry.
Then God said, “Come out of the ark—you, your wife, your sons, and their wives. Bring out every
living thing with you so they may swarm over the earth and be fruitful and multiply.” And Noah went
out with his family and all the creatures.
⸻
🗺 Briefing Summary
Waiting, watching, and obeying mark Noah’s fathering: patient intervals, careful observation, decisive
action when God speaks.
Reflection for Your Children (FULFILLED — faithful)
Your kids learn faithfulness by how you handle long waits. Model Noah’s pace—observe, test, verify,
and then move when God says move. Teach them that “God remembered” is enough to steady a family
when outcomes are slow.

⸻
Personal Application
• Build “seven-day checks”: set short review points before major family decisions.
• Teach patience: explain to the kids why you’re waiting and what signs you’re watching for.
• Practice obedience on command: when Scripture is clear, act the same day.
• Celebrate small olive leaves—name and thank God for every hint of His mercy.
⸻
🙏 Prayer
Father, You remember Your people and never forget Your promises. By the power of Your Holy Spirit,
steady my pace as a father—help me wait wisely, watch carefully, and move promptly when You speak.
In the name of Jesus Christ, my Lord and Commander, amen.
🧭 Helm Command: Hold a patient course today—set one “olive-leaf” checkpoint with your kids and
act on what God’s already made clear.

📅 Monday, January 5 — Morning Wisdom
Wisdom lifts her voice at the crossroads and invites men to trade impulse for sound judgment.
📖 Scripture — Proverbs 8:1–21
Does not Wisdom call out? Does Understanding raise her voice?
On the heights by the road, at the crossroads, at the gates and doorways of the city, she cries:
“To you, O men, I call; my voice is to the sons of Adam.
Learn prudence, you who are simple; gain understanding, you who lack sense.
Hear, for I speak what is noble; my lips open what is right.
My mouth tells the truth; wickedness is loathsome to my lips.
All the words of my mouth are righteous—nothing twisted or crooked is in them.
They are straight to the discerning and right to those who find knowledge.
Receive my instruction rather than silver, and knowledge rather than choice gold;
for Wisdom is better than rubies, and all you desire cannot compare with her.
“I, Wisdom, dwell with prudence; I find knowledge and sound discretion.
The fear of the Lord is to hate evil; I hate pride, arrogance, the evil way, and a perverse tongue.
Counsel is mine, and sound wisdom; I am understanding; I have strength.
By me kings reign and rulers decree what is just;
by me princes govern, and nobles—all who judge rightly.
I love those who love me, and those who seek me early find me.
Riches and honor are with me—enduring wealth with righteousness.
My fruit is better than gold, even fine gold, and my yield than choice silver.
I walk in the way of righteousness, on the paths of justice,
granting wealth to those who love me and filling their treasuries.”
Context Summary
Lady Wisdom stands in public places—where decisions are made—and offers straight, righteous speech. She ties
true prosperity to humility, the fear of the Lord, and just judgment, promising durable wealth that aligns with
righteousness.
Reflection — A REAL MAN: Managing faithfully
Managing faithfully means ordering your life under Wisdom’s voice. You audit inputs, steward resources, and
make just choices even when shortcuts glimmer. Wisdom promises strength, counsel, and durable gain to men
who hate pride and prize instruction above gold. Management isn’t micromanaging; it’s aligning plans, money,
and time with the fear of the Lord so your household and leadership run on righteousness.
Personal Application
• Put counsel before cash: schedule one decision you’ll make today only after prayer and wise input.
• Zero-base your time and money for the next 24 hours: every block and dollar gets a purpose.
• Reject pride at the door: speak what is right, refuse crooked words, and choose the just option even if it
costs.
🙏 Prayer
Father, fountain of wisdom and light, order my steps today.
By the power of Your Holy Spirit, cleanse my motives, steady my judgment, and teach my hands faithful
management.
I ask this in the name of Jesus Christ, my Lord and Commander. Amen.

All ahead one-third—plan, prioritize, and execute under Wisdom’s voice; no drift.
