📅 Monday, January 5 — Morning Wisdom
Wisdom lifts her voice at the crossroads and invites men to trade impulse for sound judgment.
📖 Scripture — Proverbs 8:1–21
Does not Wisdom call out? Does Understanding raise her voice?
On the heights by the road, at the crossroads, at the gates and doorways of the city, she cries:
“To you, O men, I call; my voice is to the sons of Adam.
Learn prudence, you who are simple; gain understanding, you who lack sense.
Hear, for I speak what is noble; my lips open what is right.
My mouth tells the truth; wickedness is loathsome to my lips.
All the words of my mouth are righteous—nothing twisted or crooked is in them.
They are straight to the discerning and right to those who find knowledge.
Receive my instruction rather than silver, and knowledge rather than choice gold;
for Wisdom is better than rubies, and all you desire cannot compare with her.
“I, Wisdom, dwell with prudence; I find knowledge and sound discretion.
The fear of the Lord is to hate evil; I hate pride, arrogance, the evil way, and a perverse tongue.
Counsel is mine, and sound wisdom; I am understanding; I have strength.
By me kings reign and rulers decree what is just;
by me princes govern, and nobles—all who judge rightly.
I love those who love me, and those who seek me early find me.
Riches and honor are with me—enduring wealth with righteousness.
My fruit is better than gold, even fine gold, and my yield than choice silver.
I walk in the way of righteousness, on the paths of justice,
granting wealth to those who love me and filling their treasuries.”
Context Summary
Lady Wisdom stands in public places—where decisions are made—and offers straight, righteous speech. She ties
true prosperity to humility, the fear of the Lord, and just judgment, promising durable wealth that aligns with
righteousness.
Reflection — A REAL MAN: Managing faithfully
Managing faithfully means ordering your life under Wisdom’s voice. You audit inputs, steward resources, and
make just choices even when shortcuts glimmer. Wisdom promises strength, counsel, and durable gain to men
who hate pride and prize instruction above gold. Management isn’t micromanaging; it’s aligning plans, money,
and time with the fear of the Lord so your household and leadership run on righteousness.
Personal Application
• Put counsel before cash: schedule one decision you’ll make today only after prayer and wise input.
• Zero-base your time and money for the next 24 hours: every block and dollar gets a purpose.
• Reject pride at the door: speak what is right, refuse crooked words, and choose the just option even if it
costs.
