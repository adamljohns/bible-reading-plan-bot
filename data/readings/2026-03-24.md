🌅 0600 Morning Wisdom

Brother, the morning air is clear, and Your Word stands as the unshakable ground beneath your boots.

📖 Scripture — Proverbs 24:15-22

Do not lie in wait, O my son, against the dwelling of the righteous; do not plunder his house;
For the righteous falls seven times and rises again, but the wicked stumble in time of trouble.
Do not rejoice when your enemy falls, and let not your heart be glad when he stumbles;
Lest the LORD see it and it be evil in His sight, and He turn away His wrath from him.
Do not fret because of evildoers, be not envious of the wicked;
For there will be a future hope for the wicked man, but the lamp of the wicked will be put out.
My son, fear the LORD and the king, and do not join with those who do otherwise,
For disaster will rise suddenly against them, and who knows the ruin that comes from them?

⸻

🧭 Context Summary

Solomon addresses his son with the gravity of a father instructing a son on the path of life. He warns against the temptation to exploit the vulnerable righteous and to take delight in the downfall of enemies, for such hearts are opposed to the LORD’s justice. The passage contrasts the temporary triumph of the wicked with the ultimate destruction of their light, urging the believer to maintain reverence for God and civil authority rather than succumbing to envy or malice.

⸻

🛡️ Reflection for a REAL MAN — Rejects Envy

Brother, the battlefield of the heart is often more treacherous than any foreign soil. When you see the wicked prosper or your enemies stumble, the flesh cries out for satisfaction in their ruin. But a man of God does not wait for the righteous to fall so he may plunder their inheritance. You are called to stand firm, knowing that the righteous may fall seven times, yet by Your grace, he rises again. Your strength is not in the defeat of others but in your own reliance on the LORD.

To envy the wicked or rejoice in their fall is to invite the LORD’s displeasure. It is a subtle sin of the heart that says, "My justice is served by their pain." But the lamp of the wicked will be put out, and their future is ruin. As a husband and father, you must guard your heart against this poison. You do not need to watch for the downfall of others to validate your own standing. Your calling is to fear the LORD and the king, maintaining a posture of holy reverence in all things.

Do not fret over the apparent success of evildoers. Your hope is not in the shifting sands of worldly power but in the sure promise of the LORD. By rejecting envy, you walk in the wisdom that preserves your soul and your household. Stand firm, brother, in the truth that the LORD sees all, and His wrath is turned away from those who trust in Him. Your duty is to remain upright, fearing God and honoring the authorities He has ordained, regardless of the chaos around you.

So Joshua called the twelve men whom he had prepared from the people of Israel, one from each tribe, and Joshua said to them, Pass on before the ark of the LORD your God into the middle of the Jordan and each of you take up a stone on his shoulder, according to the number of the tribes of the people of Israel, to serve as a sign among you. In the future, when your children ask you, What do these stones mean to you? then you shall tell your children that Israel passed through this Jordan on dry ground. For the LORD your God dried up the waters of the Jordan before you until you crossed over. 🗺️ Briefing Summary
Joshua obediently sets up a memorial of twelve stones to mark the miraculous crossing of the Jordan River. This tangible reminder serves as a catechetical tool for future generations to understand how the LORD parted waters for His people. The act transforms a military victory into a sacred memory of divine faithfulness.

🕚 1100 Second Watch — The Father's Charge

The Lord commands His people to remember their covenant identity before they engage in the battle of Canaan.

📖 Scripture — Joshua 5
At that time the LORD said to Joshua, Make flint knives and circumcise the Israelites again. Joshua made flint knives and circumcised the Israelites at Gibeath-Haaralith. This is why Joshua did it: All who came out had been circumcised, but all who were born in the wilderness during the journey had not been circumcised. The Israelites had moved about in the wilderness forty years until all the men who were of military age had died, since they had not obeyed the LORD. For the LORD had sworn to them that they would not see the land he had promised to give us, a land flowing with milk and honey, and the LORD had sworn to scatter them in the wilderness. But their children, whom he raised up in their place, these the circumcised, for they had not obeyed the LORD. So Joshua circumcised their sons. Now while they were being circumcised, the LORD said to Joshua, Today I have rolled away the reproach of Egypt from you. So the place has been called Gilgal to this day. On the evening of the fourteenth day of the month, while camped at Gilgal on the plains of Jericho, the Israelites celebrated the Passover. The day after the Passover, that very day, they ate some of the produce of the land: unleavened bread and roasted grain. The manna stopped the day after they ate this food from the land; there was no longer any manna for the Israelites, but that year they ate the produce of Canaan.

⸻

🗺️ Field Notes
Joshua obeys the ancient sign of the covenant, re-establishing the boundary between the holy people and the nations. The miraculous provision of manna ceases as the people begin to eat the fruit of the Promised Land, signaling that God’s faithfulness has brought them from wandering to possession. This transition marks the end of their wilderness testing and the beginning of their inheritance.

On the seventh day you shall march around the city seven times, and the priests shall blow the trumpets. 🛰️ Situation Report
Brother, the siege of Jericho was not won by tactical superiority or brute force, but by the rigid adherence to a divine command that defied conventional military wisdom. The Israelites were required to march in silence for six days, enduring the psychological strain of waiting on the Lord’s timing. On the seventh day, their obedience culminated in a shout of faith that dismantled the strongest defenses of the enemy. This is the pattern of the Christian walk: we do not fight for victory, but we fight from a victory already secured by Christ.

Truly God is good to Israel, to those who are pure in heart. But as for me, my feet had almost stumbled; my steps had nearly slipped. For I was envious of the arrogant when I saw the prosperity of the wicked. For they have no pain; they are strong and healthy. They are not like others in their troubles; they are not plagued like most people. Therefore pride is their necklace; violence covers them as a garment. Is there knowledge in the Most High? Behold, these are the wicked; and always at ease, they increase in riches. Surely in vain I have kept my heart pure and washed my hands in innocence. All day long I have been plagued and am reproached every morning. If I had spoken thus, behold, I would have betrayed the generation of your children. When I thought how to understand this, it seemed to me a wearisome task, till I went into the sanctuary of God; then I discerned their end. Your resolve is not in your own might, but in the enduring faithfulness of your Creator.
