🧭 Context Summary
Solomon presents Wisdom as a divine personification calling out to the sons of men in the public squares. This passage establishes that true wisdom is not merely intellectual but is rooted in the fear of the LORD and results in righteous living. It stands in stark contrast to the foolishness that leads to destruction, offering instead a path of justice and enduring honor. The Lord desires His people to choose this higher treasure over the fleeting allure of material wealth.
