🧭 Context Summary
Nehemiah leads the reconstruction of Jerusalem’s walls while navigating severe internal strife among the returned exiles. The wealthy nobles and officials have exploited their poorer brothers by charging interest on loans and seizing property during a time of famine. This economic abuse threatens to undo the spiritual and physical rebuilding of the community, creating a crisis of faith and fellowship. Nehemiah confronts this injustice with immediate authority, demanding restitution to preserve the unity of God’s people.
