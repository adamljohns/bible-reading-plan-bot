🧭 Context Summary
The Bride speaks here of her exclusive belonging to the Beloved, framing their relationship within the context of covenantal fidelity. She describes love not merely as an emotion but as a powerful, unquenchable force that surpasses even the severity of death. This passage underscores the absolute value of spiritual union, where mutual desire and protective zeal define the relationship. The imagery of the seal and the fire illustrates a bond that is both permanent and passionate, reflecting the divine commitment God has for His people.

🦅 This Day in American History — May 12
On May 12, 1780, after a long siege, General Benjamin Lincoln was forced to surrender Charleston and more than three thousand men to the British, the heaviest American defeat of the entire Revolution. On the same day in 1949, the Soviet Union admitted defeat and lifted its eleven-month blockade of West Berlin, broken at last by an Anglo-American airlift that had fed two million people from the sky. The bitter surrender and the patient triumph both teach the Steadfast citizen to hold fast through reversal and siege alike, for the God who permits the dark day also rewards those who do not let go their resolve.
