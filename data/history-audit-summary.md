# "This Day in American History" — Fact-Check Audit (2026-06-01)

Six cloud fact-checkers verified the ~458 dated claims in the Citizen's Stand
(third watch) across the year. Result: a **~73% error rate**.

| Months | Dated claims | Confident errors |
|---|---|---|
| Jan–Mar | 49 | ~31 |
| Apr–May | 86 | 48 |
| Jun–Jul | 91 | ~76 |
| Aug–Sep | 89 | 78 (~88%) |
| Oct–Nov | 93 | 68 |

| **Total** | **~458** | **~333** |

## Error categories
- **Wrong date** (most common): a real event placed on the wrong calendar day —
  e.g. the Alamo on Mar 5 (fell Mar 6), Bunker Hill on multiple wrong June days
  (was Jun 17), Cornwallis "surrendered at Yorktown" on Dec 19 (was Oct 19),
  Gettysburg Address on Nov 13/28 (was Nov 19).
- **Wrong year**: McKinley assassinated "1900" (1901); Statue of Liberty
  dedicated "1921" (1886); USS Cole commissioned "1943" (1996).
- **Fabricated**: "Articles of Reconstruction" (1868); a Tulsa "Black Zion"
  church (1908); Texas adopting "We Hold These Truths" as its motto; a
  "Liberation Resolution" declaring war on Germany; Washington's "first diary
  entry" Apr 13 1757.
- **Not American history** (the header says *American*): Guy Fawkes, the Warsaw
  Uprising, Luna 2, the fall of the Berlin Wall, the founding of Israel, Kenyan
  independence, Robert Falcon Scott, and Charles Spurgeon (British, b. 1834).
- **Heavy duplication**: the same event recurs on many wrong days — Washington's
  appointment as Commander-in-Chief (~7 wrong dates), John André's capture
  (3 wrong dates), Dorchester Heights (3x), Patrick Henry's speech (4x), a
  phantom "transatlantic telegraph cable" on a dozen wrong years.

## Recommendation
At a 73% error rate this section is not salvageable by spot-fixes, and it is
delivered daily by PJ under the ministry's name. Options:
1. **Remove** the "🦅 This Day in American History" block from all readings
   (safe, immediate, reversible via git) — pending a proper rebuild.
2. **Rebuild** it from a verified source (curated, fact-checked, genuinely
   American, providentially framed) — keeps the feature, larger effort.
3. **Apply the ~333 corrections** — large edit; the ~125 un-flagged claims were
   not all positively confirmed, so residual risk remains.

The other four refinement dimensions (scripture fidelity, voice variety,
application sharpness) proceed independently of this decision.
