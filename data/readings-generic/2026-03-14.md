Solomon continues his instruction on the practical outworking of wisdom in daily life, contrasting the prudent man with the fool. The text serves as a sobering reminder that our actions have eternal weight and that God’s judgment is both precise and righteous.

Sanctify our homes, that they may be places of joy and reverence, and empower our fathers to lead with grace and truth. Keep our feet from slipping, and let our lives be a living sacrifice, holy and acceptable to You. In the name of Jesus Christ, my Lord and Commander, we pray. Amen.
