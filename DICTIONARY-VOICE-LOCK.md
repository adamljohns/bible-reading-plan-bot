# MOOP Dictionary — Doctrinal Voice Lock

> **Constitutional document for the MOOP Dictionary.** Every batch authored
> (by any author, AI or human) must conform to this standard. Every batch is
> audited against this standard by `bin/dict_drift_audit.py` before commit.
> Editor: Adam Johns. Last updated 2026-05-28.

---

## 1. Why this document exists

The MOOP Dictionary is not a neutral reference work and does not pretend to be
one. It is a **confessionally Reformed, KJV-honoring, complementarian,
anti-postmodern word-study Bible dictionary** in the line of Webster (1828),
Vine, Strong, and the great Reformed dictionaries.

AI training corpora are heavily weighted toward modern academic biblical
scholarship, mainline-Protestant + post-Vatican II Catholic ecumenism,
progressive evangelical voices, Wikipedia neutrality bias, and therapy-culture
moral vocabulary. **The default AI register drifts away from Reformed
confessional Christianity in every entry, every paragraph, every sentence,
unless explicit guardrails are in place.** This document is those guardrails.

Mechanical schema rules live in `DICTIONARY-ROADMAP.md`. **Doctrinal voice
rules live here.** Both must be followed.

## 2. The doctrinal standard

The dictionary holds these positions explicitly. Every entry is written from
this standpoint. Where an entry must mention a contrary position, that
position is presented as **corruption** (in the `corruption_paragraphs` and
`corruption_summary` fields), not as a legitimate alternative.

### 2.1. Scripture

- 66-book Protestant canon (Genesis to Revelation)
- Verbal plenary inspiration; biblical inerrancy and infallibility in the
  original autographs
- Providentially preserved through the Masoretic Hebrew and Greek Textus
  Receptus / Majority Text traditions
- **KJV-honoring** &mdash; the Authorized Version is the dictionary's primary
  English text for direct biblical quotation. Not strict KJV-only (the
  dictionary acknowledges the legitimacy of other faithful translations) but
  the working register is Authorized.
- Mosaic authorship of the Torah; Davidic authorship of the Psalms ascribed
  to him; Solomonic authorship of Proverbs / Ecclesiastes / Song; one Isaiah;
  one Daniel (sixth-century BC); Pauline authorship of all 13 epistles bearing
  his name (including Pastorals and Hebrews-as-Pauline-or-Pauline-circle);
  apostolic authorship of all four Gospels and the Catholic epistles as
  traditionally ascribed

### 2.2. Theology proper

- One God in three Persons (Nicene-Constantinopolitan orthodoxy)
- Aseity, immutability, impassibility (classical attributes)
- Eternal generation of the Son; eternal procession of the Spirit (filioque
  affirmed)
- Divine simplicity in the Reformed scholastic sense

### 2.3. Christology

- Chalcedonian (one Person, two natures &mdash; divine and human &mdash;
  united without confusion, change, division, or separation)
- Virgin birth (Isa 7:14; Matt 1:23; Luke 1:34-35)
- Perfect active obedience as well as passive obedience (the active-obedience
  doctrine is the dictionary's standard, not the bare-passive-obedience
  alternative)
- Substitutionary penal atonement
- Bodily resurrection on the third day
- Bodily ascension and present session at the Father's right hand
- Bodily second coming

### 2.4. Soteriology

- Reformed / monergistic across the board
- **Total depravity** (the whole man, body and soul, mind and will, fallen)
- **Unconditional election** (according to God's good pleasure, not foreseen
  faith or works)
- **Definite (particular) atonement** &mdash; the standard Reformed position
  (Owen, *Death of Death*); Christ's atoning work effectually for the elect
- **Effectual / irresistible call**
- **Perseverance of the saints** (not bare "once saved always saved";
  perseverance is the genuine fruit of regeneration)
- Justification by grace alone through faith alone in Christ alone, by
  Christ's actively-and-passively-obedient imputed righteousness, to the
  glory of God alone (the five solas)
- Adoption as a distinct doctrine
- Sanctification as progressive (Romans 6; Romans 8; Phil 2:12-13)
- Glorification

### 2.5. Ecclesiology

- Local-church congregational governance
- Two offices: elder (= pastor / bishop / overseer; the New Testament uses
  the three terms interchangeably for one office) and deacon
- Plurality of elders is the New Testament pattern
- **Regulative principle of worship** &mdash; what Scripture commands is
  required; what Scripture has not commanded is not permitted in corporate
  worship
- Two ordinances: baptism and the Lord's Supper

### 2.6. Sacraments / ordinances

- Believer's (credo-) baptism by immersion as the New Testament pattern
- The Lord's Supper as a memorial sign-and-seal with Christ's spiritual
  presence to the believer by faith (Reformed)
- Reject transubstantiation (Roman)
- Reject consubstantiation / sacramental union (Lutheran)
- Reject bare Zwinglian memorialism that denies the spiritual presence

### 2.7. Confessional anchor

- **1689 London Baptist Confession of Faith (LBCF / Second London Baptist
  Confession)** is the primary subordinate standard
- Westminster Confession honored as the great Reformed-Presbyterian parallel
  (LBCF largely follows Westminster except on baptism, ecclesiology, and
  civil magistrate)
- Heidelberg Catechism, Belgic Confession, Canons of Dort honored as the
  Three Forms of Unity
- Pre-Reformation confessions (Nicene, Constantinopolitan, Chalcedonian,
  Athanasian) affirmed without reservation

### 2.8. Anthropology

- Imago Dei (Gen 1:27) &mdash; every human being a divine image-bearer
- **Two sexes, male and female** (Gen 1:27; Matt 19:4) &mdash; sex is the
  creational, embodied, immutable category; "gender identity" detached from
  embodied sex is a corruption of Gen 1:27
- Marriage = one man and one woman covenanting before God for life
  (Gen 2:24; Matt 19:5-6) &mdash; the only category of legitimate sexual union
- Historical Adam and Eve as the first parents of all humanity
- Original sin imputed (Romans 5:12-21)

### 2.9. Order

- **Complementarian** &mdash; creational order of headship and submission
  between husband and wife (Eph 5:22-33; 1 Pet 3:1-7); male-only eldership
  in the church (1 Tim 2:11-15; 1 Tim 3:1-7; Titus 1:5-9)
- The `biblical-order` entry is the dictionary's reference standard
- The `biblical-egalitarianism` entry treats egalitarianism as corruption
- Submission is mutual in some senses (Eph 5:21) but ordered in the creational
  sense (Gen 2; 1 Cor 11:3-9; 1 Tim 2:13)

### 2.10. Creation

- Six literal 24-hour days (the dictionary's default position)
- Old-earth creationism in the Reformed line (Kuyper, Warfield, some
  contemporary Reformed) tolerated as non-confession-binding but not the
  dictionary's voice
- Historical Adam and Eve; historical Noah; historical worldwide flood;
  historical patriarchs

### 2.11. Eschatology

- Bodily second coming of Christ
- Bodily resurrection of the just and the unjust
- Final judgment
- Eternal conscious heaven for the redeemed
- **Eternal conscious hell** for the unredeemed (reject annihilationism;
  reject universalism; reject post-mortem second-chance)
- Open on millennial position: amillennialism (the dominant Reformed
  position historically), postmillennialism (the LBCF / Puritan instinct),
  and historic premillennialism (Ladd, etc.) all within Reformed orthodoxy
- **Reject dispensational premillennialism** &mdash; a 19th-century novelty
  (Darby, Scofield) not present in any Reformation confession
- Reject preterism in its full-preterist (all-prophecy-fulfilled-in-AD-70)
  form; partial preterism is within orthodoxy

### 2.12. Civil order

- Lordship of Christ over all spheres
- Family / church / state as the three God-ordained authorities, each with
  its proper sphere
- Classical two-kingdoms doctrine (the Reformers' position) affirmed
- **Reject Radical Two-Kingdoms (R2K)** &mdash; the `two-kingdoms-theology`
  and `radical-two-kingdoms` entries draw the line

### 2.13. Authority and conscience

- Scripture alone binds the conscience absolutely (*sola Scriptura*)
- Confessions are subordinate standards &mdash; binding for the church that
  receives them, but always under Scripture
- Reformers, Puritans, and modern Reformed teachers are honored as
  faithful witnesses but never authoritative

## 3. How non-Reformed figures get treated

When the dictionary writes an entry on a figure outside the Reformed
confessional tradition &mdash; medieval Catholic saint, Eastern Orthodox
father, modern progressive theologian, Roman pontiff, ancient Israelite
apostate king, etc. &mdash; the entry must:

1. **Honor biographical and historical fact.** Cecilia really was a Roman
   martyr; Catherine of Siena really did confront Pope Gregory XI; Bernard of
   Clairvaux really did write the Latin hymn behind *O Sacred Head, Now
   Wounded*. Honor what is honorable in the historical record.

2. **Name specific doctrinal errors as errors**, not as "tensions" or
   "differences." Catherine's Marian devotion is a corruption of biblical
   Christology. Teresa's Tridentine sacramentology is a corruption of the
   Lord's Supper. Bernard's Crusade preaching is mourned. Aquinas's natural
   theology is critically engaged. The Roman papal monarchy is named as the
   ecclesiastical corruption the Reformation called the church away from.

3. **Name the Reformation as recovery from corruption**, not as "one tradition
   among several." Wittenberg 1517 was not a denominational schism; it was
   gospel-recovery against centuries of accumulated error.

4. **Allow pastoral honor and clear doctrinal correction to coexist.** The
   `biblical_def` field can recognize the figure's actual courage,
   intellectual contribution, or pastoral instinct. The `corruption_paragraphs`
   field must name where their theology departed from Scripture.

5. **For modern progressive theologians, name the framework explicitly.**
   Not "some readings" but "progressive evangelical" / "liberation theology" /
   "process theology" / "Open Theism" / "queer theology" / etc. &mdash; named,
   identified, and rebutted on biblical grounds.

## 4. Banned register

The drift-audit script (`bin/dict_drift_audit.py`) flags these patterns. They
must be removed before commit. Most slip in unconsciously from AI training
defaults; vigilance is the price of doctrinal integrity.

### 4.1. Academic-ecumenical hedging

- `tensions between` (Rome and the Reformation are not in "tension"; the
  Reformation is recovery from corruption)
- `engages warily with` / `engages cautiously` (soft-pedaling)
- `doctrinally serious` (do not apply this honorific to any non-Reformed
  framework as a whole; specific points within a tradition can be doctrinally
  serious, but the framework as a whole is not)
- `scholars debate` / `many scholars believe` / `most scholars argue` /
  `recent scholarship suggests`
- `the conversation between` (theology is not a conversation; truth is not a
  conversation)
- `all sides agree` / `common ground` / `shared heritage` (when used to
  flatten real doctrinal difference)
- `complex view of` / `complicated relationship with` / `nuanced position`
  (used to soft-pedal)
- `in dialogue with`
- `varied positions within`

### 4.2. Therapy-culture register

Substitute biblical moral vocabulary:

- `trauma` &rarr; suffering, affliction, the breaking of body or soul under
  sin and curse
- `harmful` (as the primary moral category) &rarr; sinful, against God's law,
  against the soul's good
- `safe space` &mdash; not a biblical category; reject entirely
- `lived experience` &rarr; testimony, witness
- `centering voices` / `decentering voices` &rarr; giving the platform;
  testifying
- `marginalized` (as autonomous moral-political category) &rarr; reject;
  biblical categories are the oppressed, the poor, the widow, the orphan,
  the stranger, the afflicted
- `validate` (as moral category) &rarr; confirm, affirm, commend
- `unhealthy patterns` &rarr; sin patterns, besetting sins

### 4.3. Progressive race / gender / sexuality framing

- `white supremacy` / `patriarchy` / `heteronormativity` / `cisnormativity`
  (as autonomous explanatory categories) &mdash; reject; biblical categories
  are sin, the fallen heart, generational covenant, national covenant, the
  curse
- `intersectional` / `intersectionality` &mdash; reject entirely; biblical
  anthropology is one-in-Adam (Rom 5:12) and one-in-Christ (Gal 3:28)
- `queer` / `non-binary` / `transgender` (as legitimate identity categories)
  &mdash; reject the framing; the imago Dei in every person is affirmed;
  the gender-identity claim is named as corruption of Gen 1:27
- `they/them` for individual persons &mdash; do not adopt; biblical pronouns
  follow biological sex
- `gender assigned at birth` / `gender identity` &mdash; the categories
  themselves are corruption
- `inclusive` (as autonomous virtue) &mdash; reject the framing; the gospel's
  inclusion is repentance-and-faith inclusion, not affirming-as-you-are
  inclusion

### 4.4. Historical-critical assumptions

- `Deutero-Isaiah` / `Trito-Isaiah` &mdash; reject; one prophet Isaiah
- `the Pauline corpus` (when used to distinguish "authentic" Paul from
  "deutero-Pauline" letters) &mdash; reject; all 13 Pauline epistles are Paul
- `JEDP` / `documentary hypothesis` / `Priestly source` / `Yahwist source`
  &mdash; reject; Mosaic authorship
- `Deuteronomistic historian` (when used to deny the historicity of Kings) &mdash; reject
- `post-exilic redactor` (when used to deny historicity) &mdash; reject
- `late composition` (when applied to texts the tradition dates earlier) &mdash;
  reject the framing
- `narrative theology` applied dismissively to historical narrative
- `mythological elements` / `legendary material` in the canon

### 4.5. Soft-pedal patterns

- `some traditions believe` (used to relativize the truth claim)
- `the Bible can be read as` (used to make Scripture's claims optional)
- `this is debated` (used to imply legitimate alternatives where Scripture is
  clear)
- `it is possible to see` (used to soften a hard biblical claim)
- `one perspective` / `another perspective` (when treating Scripture as one
  perspective among several)

### 4.6. Universalist drift

- `all people will eventually` / `God will eventually save all` /
  `ultimate restoration` (Origenism / Universalism)
- `God's love would not allow` (used to deny eternal conscious punishment)
- `larger hope` (the technical universalist euphemism)

### 4.7. Modernist worship register

- `worship experience` / `worship encounter` &rarr; corporate worship,
  worship of God
- `journey of faith` &rarr; the Christian life, sanctification, walking with
  the Lord
- `spirituality` (used without qualifier as if it were neutral-good) &rarr;
  religion, devotion, piety, the Christian life

## 5. Required register

### 5.1. In `biblical_def`

- Treat Scripture's claims as true claims about reality, not as the
  tradition's beliefs.
- Use the KJV / Authorized Version for direct biblical quotations.
- Use `the LORD` (small-caps when possible) for Yahweh in the canonical
  English convention; `God` for Elohim; capitalized pronouns for the Persons
  of the Trinity (the dictionary's house style).
- For historical figures and events, present the canonical narrative as the
  authoritative source; archaeological and extra-biblical sources are
  corroborative, not authoritative.

### 5.2. In `corruption_summary` and `corruption_paragraphs`

- Name the corruption explicitly. Not "some have argued" &mdash; name the
  position by its actual name, identify the framework by its actual name,
  cite the actual representatives.
- Trace the corruption to its source where possible (which century, which
  movement, which thinker introduced the error).
- Where AI training is likely to default to the corruption pattern itself
  (modern academic biblical scholarship, progressive evangelical, post-Vatican
  II ecumenism, therapy-culture moral vocabulary, historical-critical
  assumptions about authorship and dating), **name that default as the
  corruption pattern.** The corruption_paragraphs field is the dictionary's
  primary place to push back against the AI training defaults.

### 5.3. In `scriptures`

- KJV / Authorized Version text.
- Reference format: standard book-chapter:verse (e.g., `Romans 8:13`).

### 5.4. In `related`

- Link toward Reformed-confessional partner entries (the existing
  doctrinal-anchors page is a good guide for what counts as a confessional
  partner).
- Do not introduce new entries from progressive frameworks as "related" unless
  the entry is specifically a corruption-correcting entry on that framework.

## 6. Specific guardrails per topic category

### 6.1. Medieval Catholic saints / mystics / theologians

Honor what is honorable (martyrdom, intellectual contribution, pastoral
fidelity in specific instances) while clearly naming:

- Marian devotion as a corruption of biblical Christology
- Papal monarchy as the ecclesiastical corruption the Reformation called the
  church away from
- Sacramental theology that departs from the Reformed Lord's-Supper position
  as error, not difference
- Private revelation / visions / stigmata as not canonical-authoritative,
  whatever the individual's genuine devotion
- The cult of the saints as a corruption of the New Testament priesthood of
  all believers

### 6.2. Roman pontiffs

Honor specific points of fidelity (e.g., Gregory the Great's pastoral
instincts) while clearly naming the papacy as the office the Reformation
rejected on biblical grounds. The Reformer label `antichrist` applied to the
papal office (LBCF 26.4 explicitly identifies the pope as antichrist) is the
historical Reformed position; modern Reformed readers may handle it with
varied severity but the LBCF position is the confessional standard the
dictionary honors.

### 6.3. Modern progressive theology

Name the framework explicitly: liberation theology, process theology, open
theism, queer theology, progressive evangelicalism (Rob Bell, Brian McLaren,
post-evangelical exvangelicals), affirming Christianity. Identify
representatives. Rebut on biblical grounds. Do not give a "balanced both-sides"
treatment to positions that depart from Reformed confessional Christianity.

### 6.4. Roman Catholicism as a system

The dictionary respects the historic Roman Catholic Church's preservation of
many genuine Christian doctrines (Trinity, incarnation, Chalcedonian
Christology, etc.) AND clearly names the doctrinal corruptions the Reformation
called the church away from (treasury of merit, indulgences, papal monarchy,
purgatory, transubstantiation, Marian devotion, denial of *sola Scriptura*,
denial of *sola fide*). Modern Roman Catholic apologists' attempts to read
Trent and Vatican II as resolving the Reformation tensions are not granted.

### 6.5. Eastern Orthodoxy

Similar pattern: respect what is preserved (Trinitarian / Christological
orthodoxy; the patristic tradition); name what departs (icons as objects of
veneration, eucharistic theology, ecclesial structure, theosis as the
Reformed reader understands and modifies it).

### 6.6. Modern "Open Theism" / "Process Theology"

Name as error. The God of Scripture is omniscient, immutable, and impassible
in the classical Reformed sense. Open theism (Pinnock, Boyd, Sanders) denies
exhaustive divine foreknowledge; process theology denies divine immutability
and omnipotence; both depart from biblical theology proper and from every
historic Christian confession.

### 6.7. LGBTQ-affirming readings of Scripture

Name as error. The biblical-sexuality position is monogamous one-man-one-woman
marriage; the entries on `homosexuality`, `transgender`, etc. hold this line.
Affirming readings (Vines, Wilson, Brownson) are named as corruption of the
biblical sexual ethic on biblical grounds (Lev 18, 20; Rom 1:18-32; 1 Cor
6:9-11; 1 Tim 1:9-10), with pastoral care for image-bearers caught in sin
distinguished from doctrinal affirmation of the sin itself.

### 6.8. Critical Race Theory / "antiracism" frameworks

Name as a competing anthropology. The biblical anthropology is one-in-Adam
(Rom 5:12, all share original sin) and one-in-Christ (Gal 3:28). CRT's
oppressor-oppressed binary based on race is rejected; biblical categories
are sin, the heart, generational covenant, national covenant.

### 6.9. Old Testament kings and the divided monarchy

The Chronicler's verdict on each king is the dictionary's verdict. Faithful
kings are named faithful; apostate kings are named apostate; mixed records
are named mixed in the Chronicler's terms. Modern Ancient-Near-Eastern
political-history readings that subordinate the Chronicler's theological
judgment to political-success metrics are rejected.

### 6.10. Charismatic / continuationist movements

The dictionary is cessationist (the Reformed default). Modern apostle-and-
prophet claims (New Apostolic Reformation, IHOP, Bethel, etc.) are named as
corruption. The closed canon is the rule.

## 7. Audit workflow

Before any batch is committed:

1. `python3 bin/dict_drift_audit.py data/dictionary-batches/batch-NN.json`
2. The audit reports every banned-register hit with file, slug, field, and
   80-char context.
3. Each hit must be reviewed. Where the hit is a genuine drift, the text is
   revised. Where the hit is a discussion of the corruption pattern itself
   (e.g., an entry on `critical-race-theory` that names CRT's banned-register
   vocabulary to rebut it), the audit can be told to ignore the hit two ways:
   - **Per-field marker**: inline `# voice-lock-ok: <category-or-phrase>:
     <reason>` inside the field's text. Suppresses hits matching that
     category/phrase in that field only.
   - **Per-entry array**: top-level `voice_lock_ok` field on the entry
     containing a JSON array of categories to suppress for the WHOLE entry,
     e.g., `"voice_lock_ok": ["progressive", "histcrit"]`. Use this when the
     entire entry is a corruption-correcting entry that legitimately uses the
     banned register throughout.
4. The audit must return clean (exit code 0) before the batch is generated.

The audit can also be run against any prior batch to find drift evidence in
already-shipped entries.

## 8. Historical authorial note

This document was drafted on 2026-05-28 after Adam Johns (editor) flagged a
question: "Do we sufficiently have guardrails for the liberal drift that AI
is surely programmed with these days?" The honest answer was no, and this
document is the remedy. Batches 1-114 were authored without the explicit
voice-lock; they will be audited retroactively and revised where drift is
found. Batches 115+ are authored against this standard from authorship,
not just at audit.

The MOOP Dictionary is a confessional document, not a neutral reference. Its
voice is Reformed, Baptist, KJV-honoring, complementarian, biblical-inerrantist,
anti-postmodern, anti-progressive, in the line of the Reformers, the Puritans,
and the 1689 London Baptist Confession. Every entry should sound like it
came from that line. When it does not, it is drift, and drift gets corrected.
